## Click-by-click: signing in and finding the right place to post

### Facebook Marketplace
1. Open facebook.com (or the Facebook app) and sign in with Angad's existing Facebook account — don't create a new one.
2. Click the **Marketplace** icon (storefront icon) in the left sidebar, or go to facebook.com/marketplace.
3. Click **Create new listing**.
4. Choose **Item for sale** — Marketplace doesn't have a dedicated "rental" listing type, so list rentals under **Home Goods** or **Other** and say "FOR RENT" at the start of the title and the first line of the description.
5. Fill in the title, price, category, condition (pick "Used — like new"), and description from the listing text in `facebook-marketplace.md`.
6. Upload the photo from the path given with that listing.
7. Set **Location** to St. Charles, IL (or Chicago, if you want wider reach — you can run both as separate listings since they're different locations).
8. Click **Next**, then **Publish**.

### Nextdoor
1. Go to nextdoor.com and sign in (or create a free business account if Angad doesn't have one yet — use the business name "Beyond Chiavari").
2. Click **For Sale & Free** or, if a Business Page exists, post from that page so it shows our name and phone.
3. Click **+ Create post**.
4. Paste the title and body from `nextdoor.md`.
5. Attach the photo from the path given.
6. Choose the neighborhoods to post to — start with St. Charles, Geneva, Batavia, then expand to Naperville, Wheaton and Chicago neighborhoods as allowed.
7. Click **Post**.

### Craigslist
1. Go to chicago.craigslist.org (Chicago covers the whole metro, including St. Charles and the western suburbs).
2. Click **post to classifieds** near the top.
3. Choose **services offered** for the bundle/package posts, or **for sale > household** for straightforward item rentals. (Craigslist doesn't have an event-rental category — "services" fits a package with delivery/setup best.)
4. Pick **Chicago** as the metro, then the closest sub-area (e.g., **west chicagoland** for St. Charles/Geneva/Batavia, or a Chicago neighborhood for city listings).
5. Enter the title, then paste the body from `craigslist.md` into the posting box.
6. Add the phone number — Craigslist will ask whether to show it; choose "show phone number" so people can call or text.
7. Upload the photo from the path given.
8. Click **continue**, confirm the preview, then **publish**.
9. Craigslist emails a confirmation link — click it or the post won't go live.

## Reposting / renewing (so listings don't go stale)

- **Facebook Marketplace:** listings don't expire fast, but they sort by recency. Every 5-7 days, open the listing and click **Mark as sold then relist**, or use **Share to Marketplace group** again to bump visibility — don't just delete and repost the same text (Facebook flags that as spam).
- **Nextdoor:** posts stay visible for about a week before they fall off the feed. Repost once a week, varying the first line slightly so it doesn't look like a duplicate.
- **Craigslist:** posts expire after about 30-45 days (sooner if flagged). Craigslist allows reposting a *deleted* listing once per 48 hours per category/area — delete the old one first, then repost with slightly different wording in the title. Don't post the same item in two areas at once; that's the fastest way to get flagged.

## Platform rules to stay compliant (don't get flagged or banned)

- **One listing per item/bundle, one area at a time.** Posting the same thing in 5 Chicago-suburb Craigslist areas in one day reads as spam and risks a ban.
- **Don't copy-paste the exact same title/body across platforms.** Each file below already varies the wording for its platform's tone.
- **No duplicate reposts within Craigslist's cooldown window** (48 hours per category/area).
- **No phone-number spam in the title.** Put the phone number in the body only (Marketplace and Nextdoor hide listings with a phone number in the title).
- **Only real photos of our own items.** No stock photography borrowed from a vendor's site.
- **Don't link directly to `/quote` with pre-filled personal data** — link to the plain item page or `/quote` itself; never put the customer's name or any contact info in a URL.
