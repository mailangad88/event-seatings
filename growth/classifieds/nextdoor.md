# Nextdoor business posts

Nextdoor is neighbor-to-neighbor, so keep the tone warm and local — mention the towns, not just "Chicago area." Post from a Business Page if Angad has one set up; otherwise post to **For Sale & Free**. Pick 3-4 neighborhoods a day rather than all at once (Nextdoor limits how wide a single post reaches, and posting the same thing to every neighborhood in one day looks like spam).
