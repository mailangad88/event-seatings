# Craigslist posts (chicago.craigslist.org)

Craigslist doesn't have an event-rental category, so split these two ways:

- **Single items** (chairs, tables) → post under **for sale → household**.
- **Packages/bundles that include delivery and setup** → post under **services offered → event**.

Pick the sub-area closest to the renter: **west chicagoland** covers St. Charles/Geneva/Batavia/Aurora; use a Chicago neighborhood sub-area (e.g. **city of chicago**) for in-city reach, and **nw suburbs**/**sw suburbs** for Schaumburg/Naperville/Wheaton/Oak Brook. Post to one sub-area at a time — see the repost cadence in `README.md`.

## A note on Craigslist's email relay

Craigslist replies come through an anonymized relay email first. Reply from that same thread - don't ask the renter to email a personal address, and never put their real email or phone number into any other system until they've given it to you directly.
