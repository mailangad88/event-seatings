## Writing checklist
- Use the target phrase in the title and first paragraph, naturally.
- Write about 600–1,200 words with clear `##` headings.
- Include at least two internal links (a chair page, plus /quote or /quiz).
- Only include facts you can stand behind. No made-up statistics or venue claims.
- Add real photos once you have them. Original photos rank and convert better than stock.
