**Quick answer**

A 6-foot banquet table measures 72 x 30 inches and seats 6, or 8 if you use the ends. An 8-foot banquet table measures 96 x 30 inches and seats 8, or 10 with the ends. Choose 6-foot tables for small rooms and head tables, and 8-foot tables for long family-style rows, buffets and big guest counts. Floor-length cloths are 90 x 132 inches for a 6 ft table and 90 x 156 inches for an 8 ft table.

</div>

Rectangular banquet tables are the workhorse of Chicago-area events: church halls, park district rooms, school gyms, backyard tents and plenty of wedding venues. The two lengths look similar until you try to fit 40 people in a 30-foot room. This guide gives you the numbers you need, the layouts that work and the linen sizes that go with each.

## Banquet table size chart

<div class="table-wrap">

| Table | Size | Seats (sides only) | Seats (with ends) | Floor-length cloth | Lap-length cloth |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 ft | 72 x 30 in | 6 | 8 | 90 x 132 in | 60 x 102 in |
| 8 ft | 96 x 30 in | 8 | 10 | 90 x 156 in | 60 x 126 in |
| 6 ft seminar | 72 x 18 in | 3 (one side) | 3 | Skirting | n/a |
| 8 ft seminar | 96 x 18 in | 4 (one side) | 4 | Skirting | n/a |

</div>

Seats-with-ends numbers are the tight setup. Plated dinners with multiple glasses are comfortable at the sides-only count. For a full explanation of cloth sizes see our [tablecloth size chart](/blog/tablecloth-size-chart).

## When to pick a 6-foot table

- **Small rooms.** A 6 ft table is easier to place around columns, stages and doors.
- **Head tables for a small wedding party.** A 6 ft table seats 4 on one side, as long as chairs are spaced out.
- **Service tables.** Guest book, gifts, cake, DJ and escort cards all fit a 6 ft table. See our [cake, gift and guest book checklist](/blog/cake-gift-guest-book-table-checklist).
- **Kids tables.** Two 6 ft tables seat 12 kids with room for coloring. See [kids table ideas](/blog/kids-table-wedding-ideas).

## When to pick an 8-foot table

- **Long family-style dinners.** One 8 ft table seats 8 comfortably, and you can line up two or three in a row.
- **Buffets.** Eight feet gives each chafing dish 2 feet of table, so a four-dish line fits on one table. See [buffet table layouts](/blog/buffet-table-layouts).
- **Large, open rooms.** Fewer, longer tables mean fewer cloths and a cleaner floor plan.
- **Backyard parties and tents.** 8 ft tables are the standard in tent layouts.

## Placing banquet tables together

You can create a longer table by lining tables end to end. Two 8 footers become a 16-foot table that seats 16 to 18. Check three things before you do:

<ol class="flow">
<li><strong>Check the gap</strong> Tables meet with a seam. A runner or a full cloth hides it.</li>
<li><strong>Check the legs</strong> Legs sit inside each end, so seats near a seam may hit a leg.</li>
<li><strong>Check the width</strong> Two tables side by side make a 60-inch wide block that is too wide for conversation.</li>
<li><strong>Check the aisle</strong> Leave at least 4 feet behind chairs.</li>
</ol>

## Seating math for long rows

| Guests | Using 6 ft tables | Using 8 ft tables |
|---|---|---|
| 16 | 3 tables (18 seats) | 2 tables (16 seats) |
| 24 | 4 tables | 3 tables |
| 40 | 7 tables | 5 tables |
| 64 | 11 tables | 8 tables |

An 8 ft table gives each guest 24 inches of edge at 8 seats. A 6 ft table gives each guest 24 inches at 6 seats. That per-person space is the same; the difference is how many tables, cloths and aisles you manage.

## Chairs for banquet tables

Rectangular tables put chairs in straight rows, so style shows. A row of [cross-back chairs](/chairs/cross-back) reads as one clean line. [Garden folding chairs](/chairs/garden-folding) suit casual picnic setups. If your venue is a rented hall in Chicago, ask whether tables and chairs are in the hall's contract before you add rentals.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many people sit at a 6-foot banquet table?

A 6-foot banquet table seats 6 comfortably, 3 on each long side. You can fit 8 by seating guests at the ends, but that is tight for a plated meal.

### How many people sit at an 8-foot banquet table?

An 8-foot banquet table seats 8 comfortably, 4 per side, and up to 10 with end seats. Allow about 24 inches of table edge per guest.

### What size is an 8-foot banquet table?

It is 96 inches long by 30 inches wide and 30 inches tall. A few styles are 24 or 36 inches deep, so confirm with the rental company.

### What tablecloth fits a 6-foot banquet table?

A 90 x 132-inch cloth reaches the floor on a 30-inch table. A 60 x 102-inch cloth gives a shorter lap-length drop of about 15 inches.

### Are banquet tables the same as folding tables?

Most banquet tables are folding tables with metal or plastic legs, but premium rental tables can have heavier tops. See [folding vs heavy wood tables](/blog/folding-vs-heavy-wood-tables).

### Can I use banquet tables for a wedding?

Yes. Many Chicago weddings use banquet tables for head tables, buffets and long-table dinners. Dress them with floor-length linens and a runner to match round tables.
