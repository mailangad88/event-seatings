**Quick answer**

Choose plain white china if you want the safest, most flexible look: it suits every linen, food and color scheme. Choose gold-rim china for black-tie galas, winter weddings and ballroom receptions where metallic details already appear in the chairs, flatware or candles. Gold rims add polish but also add rules: they are not microwave safe and the trim can show wear, so confirm care and return conditions with the rental company.

</div>

Most couples searching for dinnerware in Chicago end up at the same decision: a plain white plate or one with a gold edge. It sounds minor, and in a 150-guest room it is not. The plate is the part of the table every guest touches. Here is how to choose without spending hours on samples.

## The case for plain white

White works with everything and reads as clean in photos. It lets the food, linens and flowers carry the color. It is also easier to replace: if the rental company runs short of a pattern, plain white is the most likely to be stocked in volume. For mixed settings, white is the best base for adding colored napkins or a patterned charger, as in [matching dinnerware to linens](/blog/matching-dinnerware-to-linens).

## The case for gold rim

A gold rim picks up candlelight and echoes gold flatware, gold chairs and metallic centerpieces. It signals formal without needing more décor. In a winter ballroom in downtown Chicago, a gold line on each plate ties the room together. It pairs well with [gold infinity chairs](/chairs/infinity) and with warm, low lighting.

The care point is real. Metallic trim can wear with harsh washing, which is why rental companies handle cleaning. Do not run rental gold-rim plates through your own dishwasher unless the company says so. Our [do you wash rental dishes](/blog/do-you-wash-rental-dishes) guide has details on what to do before returning them.

## Matching with flatware and glassware

If you pick gold rim, choose gold or champagne flatware, or plain stainless if you want a mix. Do not mix gold flatware with silver-rim plates, because the clash reads as an accident. With plain white, you can pick any flatware. If you have clear glassware, add a gold-banded glass only if the plate rim is gold too. Keep the metallic in one family so the table looks planned.

## Check the pattern in person

Photos of rental china vary on screen. The white can look cream, blue-white or bright. Ask for a sample plate and put it on your napkin and linen swatch. If you cannot visit in person, ask about return rules for a sample set. Set it under the lighting your venue will have, since warm light turns white to cream.

## A simple decision rule

If you want black-tie, a metallic palette or a winter ballroom, go gold rim. If you want a garden party, a barn, a rainbow of colors or a tighter budget, go plain white. If you are unsure, white is the lower-risk choice and you can add shine with chargers, which we discuss in [are charger plates worth it](/blog/are-charger-plates-worth-it).

## What caterers think

Most caterers work with either easily. The one practical difference is cleanup: they handle gold rims more gently, and some ask that gold-rim plates not go into high-heat dish machines. Mention your choice to your caterer early so the back-of-house crew knows how to handle it, and so the plates you rent fit the caterer's sheet pans and carts.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is gold-rim china more expensive to rent?

It is often priced higher than plain white because stock is more limited and the trim needs care. We do not publish dinnerware prices, so ask for both options in one quote. The difference varies by pattern and company.

### Can I mix gold-rim and white plates at one table?

You can, but do it on purpose. Use gold-rim chargers with white dinner plates, or white plates with gold flatware. Mixing different plates per course can look messy.

### Does gold rim go with silver flatware?

It can, but it is less common. Match the metal family when you can. If you use silver, plain white plates are safer.

### Is white china too plain for a wedding?

No. White is the most common wedding choice. Add interest with napkins, chargers, menus and flowers.

### Can gold rims go in the dishwasher?

Do not put rental china in your own dishwasher unless the rental company says it is safe. Harsh cycles can dull metallic trim. Follow the return instructions.

### What color linen works with gold-rim plates?

Ivory, white, navy, black and deep green show gold best. Bright colors can fight with it. Check a sample on your cloth first.
