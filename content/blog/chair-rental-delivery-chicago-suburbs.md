## What drives delivery cost

- **Distance and traffic.** A short run within the Fox Valley is simpler than a weekday afternoon delivery downtown
- **Timing.** Weekend, early-morning and late-night windows take more planning
- **Access.** Stairs, long carries, elevators and tight loading docks add labor
- **Setup.** Placing chairs to a floor plan takes longer than a stack-and-go drop
- **Separate trips.** A day-before delivery and a next-day pickup can mean two trips

## Full service vs. drop-off

| Option | What's included | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Full service | Delivery, placement, stacking and pickup | Weddings, galas, venues with tight windows |
| Drop-off | Chairs delivered stacked; you set them out | Backyard parties, smaller counts |
| Will-call | You pick up and return | Very small orders with a large vehicle |

## Suburban venue tips

- **Barns and farms:** confirm truck access and ground conditions, especially after rain
- **Backyards:** check gate widths, slopes and where the truck can park
- **Hotels and banquet halls:** ask for the vendor packet, dock times and COI wording. See [Chicago venue rules](/blog/chicago-venue-rules-chair-rentals)
- **Parks:** confirm permits with the park district first

## Frequently asked questions

### Do chair rental companies deliver to the Chicago suburbs?
Yes. Most full-service companies deliver, set up and pick up across the suburbs, with fees based on distance, timing and venue access.

### Does chair rental include setup?
Full-service rentals include placing chairs and stacking them for pickup. Drop-off service costs less but leaves setup to you, so check which your quote includes.

### How can I lower delivery costs?
Keep delivery and pickup within normal hours, give accurate access details up front, and avoid separate day-before and day-after trips when your venue allows same-day setup and strike.
