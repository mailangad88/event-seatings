The Windsor spindle chair is the quiet alternative: a rounded back of slim spindles, a sculpted seat and a country look that feels older and more personal. If you are planning a farmhouse wedding and want something less expected than the usual, this guide is for you.

## The chair, in plain terms

Our [Windsor spindle chair](/chairs/windsor) has a spindle back and a sculpted seat. It comes in antique white, walnut and sage. It is marked outdoor-friendly, does not stack and has an optional cushion.

What that means at an event:

- **Outdoor use is realistic**, with care. It is a good choice for a covered porch, a tent, a barn or a firm lawn.
- **Each chair is handled separately.** Plan labor and floor space for delivery and removal.
- **The finish matters more than usual.** Antique white shows scuffs and dirt more; walnut hides them.

## Choosing a color

| Finish | Look | Best with |

| Antique white | Soft, vintage, bright | Wildflowers, gingham, white barn interiors |
| Walnut | Dark, grounded | Pale linen, greenery, fall palettes |
| Sage | Muted green, calming | Cream, dried grasses, blush |

</div>

Sage Windsors are the least expected choice, and they work well with garden roses and linen. Antique white lightens a dim barn. Walnut disappears into wood and is a good value if you want the chair to stay quiet.

## Ground, weather and Chicago-area realities

Barns and orchards in the Chicago suburbs and collar counties often have gravel parking, grass lawns and uneven barn floors. Your chairs will meet all three.

1. **Soft turf.** Wood legs press into wet grass. Lay plywood or rental flooring for the chair rows, or choose a drier spot.
2. **Gravel.** Chairs wobble on loose stone. Avoid it for seating.
3. **Barn floors.** Old plank floors may be uneven. Place shims or choose a layout that avoids the worst boards.
4. **Weather.** Have a tent or indoor backup in case of rain. Our [barn wedding seating ideas](/blog/barn-wedding-seating-ideas) covers the rain plan, and [chairs on grass, sand and gravel](/blog/chairs-on-grass-sand-gravel) explains each surface.

## Tables and styling

A Windsor's spindles make it look busy from the side, so keep the table calm. Good pairings:

- Long farm tables with linen runners and low candles.
- Round tables with ivory floor-length cloths.
- Mason jars and lanterns, but not both with heavy florals.

Skip chair sashes. A small greenery sprig tied to a spindle is plenty. See [chair back decor](/blog/chair-back-decor-florals-and-signs).

## Windsor versus cross-back

Both are wood and both are rustic. The cross-back has a more modern, linear look and stacks, so it is cheaper to deliver in volume. The Windsor has a softer, more traditional shape and works better for a heritage or orchard feel. Compare them in [cross-back chair rental in Chicago](/blog/cross-back-chair-rental-chicago). Many couples use Windsors at the head table and family tables and cross-backs elsewhere.

## A sample farmhouse order

Here is how a 120-guest farm wedding might use Windsor chairs, as a planning example and not a recommendation for your event.

- **Ceremony:** 120 Windsors on the lawn in two sections with a center aisle, plus 4 spares.
- **Reception:** the same chairs move to twelve 60-inch rounds in the barn, 10 at a table.
- **Head table:** 10 Windsors in walnut for contrast, or a different style such as [velvet](/chairs/velvet-dining) for the couple.
- **Cushions:** optional pads for the family tables and anyone over 70.

Because Windsors do not stack, plan for two people to carry them in pairs, and ask about the path from the truck to the barn door. A gravel drive and a narrow barn entrance slow a crew more than the number of chairs does. Mention both when you request a quote.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are Windsor chairs good for a farmhouse wedding?

Yes. They are one of the best fits for barns, orchards and countryside venues, and they work outdoors on firm ground.

### How much do Windsor chairs cost to rent in Chicago?

Our estimate is $9 to $12 per chair. Delivery to rural and suburban venues is quoted by address.

### Can Windsor chairs go on grass?

They can, but wet turf lets the legs sink. Use plywood or flooring under the rows or choose a firmer area.

### Do Windsor chairs stack?

No. Plan labor for hand carrying and extra truck space.

### Are Windsor chairs comfortable?

Yes. The seat is sculpted, which is gentler than a flat plank. Add the optional cushion for a long dinner.

### What color Windsor chair should I choose?

Antique white for bright, vintage looks; walnut for a dark, grounded one; sage for a soft, unexpected tone.

### Can I use Windsor chairs for the ceremony and the reception?

Yes, and many farm weddings do. Plan the flip in advance; our guide to [ceremony vs reception chairs](/blog/ceremony-vs-reception-chairs) explains how.
