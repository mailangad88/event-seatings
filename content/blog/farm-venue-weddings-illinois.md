**Quick answer**

Farm venues near Chicago look effortless and need the most logistics. Ask about parking surface, restrooms, power, water, kitchen access, rain space and noise curfew, then rent what the farm does not supply: chairs, tables, linens, dinnerware, glassware, tent and lighting. Choose chairs with wide feet for grass and gravel, and plan a firm path for heels and wheelchairs.

</div>

A farm wedding or party near Chicago is a field, a driveway and an idea. Some farm venues are polished event sites with a finished barn, restrooms and a coordinator; others are working farms that rent a lawn and leave you the rest. Before you fall in love with the pictures, find out which kind you have. This guide is about the farm itself (for barn seating layouts, see our [barn wedding seating ideas](/blog/barn-wedding-seating-ideas)).

## Ask these questions before you sign

<div class="table-wrap">

| Area | Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Parking | Where, what surface, how many cars? | Gravel and grass drain slowly after rain |
| Restrooms | Flush or portable, how many? | Plan for guest count |
| Power | Which outlets, how many amps? | Lights, DJ, caterer, heat |
| Water | Is there a spigot for caterers? | Wash and prep |
| Weather | Is there an indoor space? | Rain and heat plan |
| Noise | What time must music stop? | Rural rules vary |
| Vendors | Open list or required list? | May include rentals |
| Access | Can a truck reach the site? | Delivery and teardown |

</div>

Get every answer in writing. If the farm requires its own rental company, ask for the list. If it allows outside vendors, send the venue the rental list and delivery plan early.

Draw this yourself on the venue's map. Mark where trucks unload, where the caterer works, where guests walk from parking to the ceremony and where the restrooms are. The best farm layouts keep the service path away from guests, and keep a firm path from parking to the seating for strollers, walkers and heels.

## Surfaces and chairs

Grass, gravel, packed dirt and old concrete all behave differently with 100 chairs. Thin legs sink; wide feet and flooring spread weight. Chairs on gravel tilt. Ask the venue about the surface before you pick chairs, and read our guide to [chairs on grass, sand and gravel](/blog/chairs-on-grass-sand-gravel). ## What farm venues usually do not include

- **Tables and chairs**: some provide them, many do not.
- **Kitchen**: your caterer may need a prep tent. See [catering prep tent and back of house](/blog/catering-prep-tent-and-back-of-house-space).
- **Lighting**: a field is dark. See [string light layouts](/blog/string-light-layouts-for-events).
- **Power**: you may need a generator. See [generator size for events](/blog/generator-size-for-events).
- **Climate control**: heat, fans or both.
- **Dance floor**: grass is uneven. See [outdoor dance floor on grass](/blog/outdoor-dance-floor-on-grass).
- **Trash**: farms need extra hauling plans.

## Rain and heat

A barn or indoor room is the backup. Without one, you need a tent. A farm field can be exposed to wind and sun, and tent anchoring depends on the ground; ask your tent vendor how they anchor in your soil. If the field has livestock or crops nearby, ask about bugs and smell, and see our [mosquito and bug guide](/blog/mosquitoes-and-bugs-outdoor-events).

## Delivery to a rural address

Rural delivery takes longer and needs clear directions. Ask the venue whether a box truck can reach the site, whether the driveway is too narrow, and what happens after rain. Share a pin and a contact number with the rental company, and confirm gate codes. ## Frequently asked questions

### What should I ask a farm wedding venue?

Ask about parking, restrooms, power, water, an indoor backup, noise rules, vendor policy and truck access. Get it in writing.

### Do farm venues provide chairs and tables?

Some do and many do not. Ask what is included and whether outside rentals are allowed.

### What chairs work on a farm field?

Chairs with wide feet or a plan for flooring. Wooden styles like cross-back and windsor fit the look.

### Do I need a tent at a farm venue?

Yes if there is no indoor backup. A tent also adds shade.

### How do I handle power at a farm?

Ask for the amps available and the outlet locations; you may need a generator.

### Are farm venues cheaper?

Not always. The venue fee may be lower, but rentals, restrooms, power and lighting add up. Request a full quote.

### Can I bring my own caterer to a farm venue?

Many allow it but may require licensed caterers. Ask the venue.
