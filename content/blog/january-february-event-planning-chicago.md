**Quick answer**

January in Chicago averages a 31.6°F high with 11.3 inches of snow, and February averages a 35.7°F high with 10.7 inches, per NWS normals at O'Hare. Sunset is about 4:43 pm in mid-January and 5:22 pm in mid-February. Book an indoor venue, order chairs and linens early, and leave a snow day in the schedule.

</div>

January and February are the quiet months for outdoor events, but they are busy for milestone birthdays, Valentine's gatherings, engagement parties, small weddings, corporate kickoffs and fundraisers. The Chicago winter is the reason most of these are indoors, and the reason delivery and setup need extra care.

## Winter weather in Chicago

At O'Hare, January averages a 31.6°F high and an 18.8°F low with 1.99 inches of precipitation and 11.3 inches of snow. February averages 35.7°F and 21.8°F with 1.97 inches and 10.7 inches of snow. Those are the coldest and snowiest months of the year, and wind off Lake Michigan makes the city feel colder than the number.

## Daylight

Sunset is about 4:43 pm on January 15, 5:04 pm on February 1 and 5:22 pm on February 15 (calculated for 2027). That is 40 minutes more daylight across the two months. Valentine's Day 2027 is Sunday, February 14. An afternoon party in February can finish in daylight, but an evening party starts in the dark.

## What winter events need

- **Indoor space with enough room for coats.** A cold-weather crowd arrives in bulky layers.
- **A dry entrance.** Mats, a coat check and a clear path.
- **Warm lighting.** The room should feel warm even when the sky is gray.
- **Backup dates.** Ask the venue about a snow date if guests travel.
- **Delivery planning.** See below.

### Rentals that feel warm

Winter is when velvet makes sense. A [velvet dining chair](/chairs/velvet-dining) in blush works for Valentine's, and navy or emerald suits a January gala. [Louis medallion chairs](/chairs/louis-medallion) feel formal for a milestone dinner. For more ideas, see our [winter wedding seating guide](/blog/winter-wedding-seating).

## Delivery in snow

A winter delivery plan needs three things: an access point that is shoveled and salted, a delivery window with a buffer and a person on site who can sign. Downtown buildings may require a certificate of insurance and a reserved freight elevator. Ask your venue for its loading rules early. Our [loading zones guide](/blog/chicago-event-delivery-parking-loading-zones) is a useful reference.

## Small events do well in winter

January and February are good months for events of 20 to 60 people. A smaller room is easier to heat, easier to light and easier to decorate. Chairs and tables for a 40-person dinner fit in a modest space. If you are planning a larger event, a tent is not practical in these months unless heat and flooring are included, which adds major cost and permits.

## Timing a winter day

A winter event runs on a tighter clock than a summer one. Guests will not linger on the sidewalk, so open the doors on time and have coats handled within minutes. Plan the toast and the first course around the early dark: a 5 pm start already feels like evening, and a 4 pm start feels like an afternoon party that turned into dinner. Ask the venue whether staff can be on site early to shovel and salt, and ask your photographer to plan indoor portraits, since outdoor shots will be brief and cold.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I have a wedding in Chicago in January or February?

Yes. Many couples choose a winter wedding for lower venue demand and a cozy feel. Plan indoors, include coat storage and leave extra time for snow.

### How cold is Chicago in January?

The average high at O'Hare is 31.6°F and the average low is 18.8°F.

### How much snow does Chicago get in January and February?

About 11.3 inches in January and 10.7 inches in February on average.

### What time is sunset in Chicago in February?

About 5:04 pm on February 1 and about 5:22 pm on February 15.

### Are rentals cheaper in winter?

We can't promise lower prices. Costs depend on the items, the date and the delivery. Request a quote to see the plan priced.

### Can I put up a tent in winter in Chicago?

It's possible with heat, flooring and sidewalls, but it's complex and costly. Most winter events use an indoor venue.

### How do I handle snow delays on the day of the event?

Build a buffer into the delivery window, shovel access points and keep contact numbers handy.

## A winter checklist

Pick an indoor space with parking close to the door. Count coats and plan racks. Choose warm lighting and linens. Confirm load-in times, freight elevator rules and any building insurance requirements. Ask for a weather plan in writing. Then finalize chairs, tables, linens, glassware and dinnerware in one order.
