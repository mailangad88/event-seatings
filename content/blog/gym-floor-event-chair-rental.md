**Quick answer**

Yes, rental chairs can usually go in a school or church gym, but most facilities ask you to protect the hardwood first. Expect to cover the event area with a floor cover such as a tarp, vinyl or carpet, make sure every chair and table leg has glides or caps, and roll carts on the cover instead of dragging anything. The school or church sets the rules, so get its floor policy in writing before you book rentals.

Gyms host more events than almost any other room: athletic banquets, school fundraisers, church dinners, reunions, rain-plan graduations, voting days and community nights. They're big, free or low cost, and close to home. The catch is the floor. A finished hardwood gym floor is expensive to refinish, and a single dragged chair or a loaded cart on bare wheels can leave marks that the custodian notices the next morning.

## What to ask the school or church first

Every facility has its own policy, and it usually lives with the athletic director, facilities manager or head custodian. Ask:

- **Is a floor cover required?** Some gyms own their own cover and lay it for you. Others ask the renter or the rental company to bring one.
- **What can touch the bare floor?** Some allow chairs with rubber or felt feet. Others want nothing on the hardwood at all.
- **How do deliveries come in?** Which door, whether there's a ramp, and whether carts can cross the floor before the cover is down.
- **When can you set up and when must you be out?** Gyms often have practices or games right before and after an event.
- **What's the occupancy for a seated event?** The number for tables and chairs is usually different from the number for bleachers.

## The three ways to protect the floor

1. **Cover the floor.** A gym floor cover, heavy tarp, vinyl or carpet over the whole event area protects against food, drinks, scuffs and chair legs at once. Tape seams with tape that won't leave residue.
2. **Protect the feet.** Every chair and table leg should have a glide or cap. Ask your rental company what feet its chairs have and whether they're in good condition.
3. **Roll, don't drag.** Chairs and tables arrive on carts. Bare or hard wheels can mark a floor, so carts should roll on the cover, and chairs should be carried into place, not slid.

## Making a gym feel like an event space

A gym is a big, bright, echoey room. A few choices change it quickly:

- **Use only part of the floor.** Pipe and drape or a row of tall plants can cut the room down to the size you need.
- **Turn down the overheads.** If the facility allows it, warm uplighting does more than anything else.
- **Mind the sound.** Linens, drape and a full room of guests all soften the echo. Put the speakers on the long wall, facing the guests.
- **Plan the bleachers.** Pushed-in bleachers make a clean backdrop, while pulled-out ones can seat a crowd for a program.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you put chairs on a gym floor?
Usually yes, but most schools and churches ask you to protect the hardwood first. That typically means a floor cover over the event area, glides or caps on every chair and table leg, and carts that roll on the cover rather than across the bare floor.

### What do you put on a gym floor for an event?
A gym floor cover, heavy tarp, vinyl or carpet over the whole event area. Many facilities own a cover and lay it for you. Others ask the renter or rental company to provide one, so ask before you book.

### Do rental chairs scratch hardwood floors?
They can if their feet are worn or missing, or if they're dragged. Ask your rental company about the feet on its chairs, carry chairs into place instead of sliding them, and keep carts on the floor cover.

### What kind of chairs are best for a school gym event?
Folding chairs are the most common choice because they're light, quick to set and easy to carry. For a fundraiser or banquet, a wood folding or statement chair at the head table makes the gym feel more like an event space.
