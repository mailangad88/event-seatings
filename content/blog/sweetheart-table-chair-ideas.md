You will be photographed at your sweetheart or head table more than almost anywhere else, so the chairs behind you matter.

## Ideas

- **A different chair from your guests.** The simplest way to mark the table as special. Cross-backs for guests and [velvet](/chairs/velvet-dining) for you, for example.
- **A statement chair or loveseat.** The [Royal Sweetheart Throne](/chairs/royal-throne) suits cultural celebrations, stages and mandaps.
- **Matching, upholstered seats.** The [Louis Medallion](/chairs/louis-medallion) brings a formal, French feel to a ballroom.

## Tips

1. Think about the backdrop. The chair should contrast with the wall, drape or floral behind it.
2. Keep comfort in mind. You may sit there for hours.
3. Check the dress. Wide skirts need seats with room and without arms that pinch.
