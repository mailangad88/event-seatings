**Quick answer**

Sweetheart table chairs should stand out from guest seating: velvet chairs, Louis chairs, throne chairs or rattan peacock chairs are popular. Rent just two, add matching signs or florals, and confirm the table height fits the chair, since throne chairs are wider and taller than standard dining chairs.

You will be photographed at your sweetheart or head table more than almost anywhere else, so the chairs behind you matter.

## Ideas

- **A different chair from your guests.** The simplest way to mark the table as special. Cross-backs for guests and [velvet](/chairs/velvet-dining) for you, for example.
- **A statement chair or loveseat.** The [Royal Sweetheart Throne](/chairs/royal-throne) suits cultural celebrations, stages and mandaps.
- **Matching, upholstered seats.** The [Louis Medallion](/chairs/louis-medallion) brings a formal, French feel to a ballroom.

## Tips

1. Think about the backdrop. The chair should contrast with the wall, drape or floral behind it.
2. Keep comfort in mind. You may sit there for hours.
3. Check the dress. Wide skirts need seats with room and without arms that pinch.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many chairs do I need for a sweetheart table?

Two. Some couples add a third chair nearby for a quick break or for photos with family.

### Do sweetheart chairs need to match the guest chairs?

No. A contrasting chair, such as velvet next to Chiavari guest chairs, makes the couple easy to find in photos.

### What table fits a throne chair?

Throne chairs are wide and tall, so use a 6-foot rectangular or a long sweetheart table with room behind for the chair backs.
