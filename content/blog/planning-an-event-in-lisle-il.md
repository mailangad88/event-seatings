Lisle sits along the I-88 corridor in DuPage County, with corporate offices, a small downtown and the Morton Arboretum on its edge. Events here are a mix of company gatherings and family parties. The key is to know who owns the space you want.

## Park district and village

The Lisle Park District has a rentals page for its facilities and shelters. The Village of Lisle code includes special events provisions, which matter for public streets and village property. For a park shelter, call the district. For a street event or a parade, call the village.

## The Morton Arboretum

The Morton Arboretum is one of the best-known spots in the area, and it sits in Lisle. It is operated as a private nonprofit with its own rules for events and rentals, so do not assume park district or village rules apply. If the Arboretum interests you, contact its events team directly and ask what is available.

## A Lisle example

For a 120-person company picnic in a shelter area, plan 15 rounds of 8 or a mix of rounds and long tables. [Garden folding chairs](/chairs/garden-folding) (about $5-7, an estimate) fit a casual picnic, and [cross-back chairs](/chairs/cross-back) (about $8-11) feel more polished.

## What to confirm with the district

Before you reserve a shelter, ask about maximum capacity, whether you can bring your own tables, what time you can start setup, and whether grills or amplified music are allowed. Write down the answers. A clear list helps your rental company size the crew and the truck. If you are coming from Chicago or another suburb, share the exact address so we can plan the route.

## Planning a company picnic in Lisle

Corporate picnics are common here, and they follow a pattern. Employers in Lisle's office parks often host summer events for staff and families, and the details are what make them work.

- **Headcount:** families add guests you do not expect. Ask for a range and plan for the high end.
- **Food service:** ask your caterer what they bring. Chafing dishes, serving tables and beverage stations are separate lines on a rental list.
- **Shade and cooling:** a tent over the food tables helps in July. Add fans if the shelter has power.
- **Kids and accessibility:** wide paths and a clear route to restrooms matter.
- **Cleanup:** parks usually require you to leave the space clean. Rent extra trash cans and plan a pickup window.

Because Lisle sits on a busy corridor, ask staff to arrive early. Parking around offices fills fast on weekdays, and a shuttle from a larger lot can save frustration. For help turning a headcount into a list, [see how many chairs you need](/blog/how-many-chairs-do-i-need).

## Frequently asked questions

### Where do I rent a shelter in Lisle?

From the Lisle Park District, which lists rentals on its website.

No. The Arboretum is a private nonprofit with its own event rules. Contact its events team.

### Does Lisle have special event rules?

The Village of Lisle code includes special event provisions. Ask the village about events on village property or streets.

### Is Lisle good for corporate events?

Yes, there are many offices and hotels nearby. Ask each venue what furniture is included.
