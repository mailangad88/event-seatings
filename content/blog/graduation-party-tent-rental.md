**Quick answer**

For a graduation party of 40 to 100 guests, rent a 20 x 30 to 30 x 40 ft frame tent, with open sides for airflow, a buffet along one wall and a mix of tables and lounge seating. Book your tent as soon as you know your date, because May and June weekends fill up around graduation season. Plan about 12 square feet per seated guest, or 7 per guest for a drop-in open house.

</div>

Graduation parties are different from weddings. Guests come and go, ages range from toddlers to great-grandparents, and the host often has just a few weeks of notice after the final date is set. They are also concentrated: every family in the Chicago area wants the same weekends in late May and early June. A tent makes the party weather-proof, and good planning makes it relaxed.

## Open house or seated meal?

The format decides the tent size.

<div class="table-wrap">

| Format | Guests at once | Space per guest | Tent for 80 invited |
|---|---|---|---|
| Open house, drop-in | About half of the guest list | 7 to 8 sq ft | 20 x 20 to 20 x 30 |
| Buffet with seating | 80 percent seated | 10 to 12 sq ft | 30 x 30 |
| Plated, seated | All at once | 12 sq ft | 30 x 40 |
| Seated with DJ | All at once | 15 sq ft | 30 x 45 to 30 x 60 |

</div>

See [what size tent do I need](/blog/what-size-tent-do-i-need) for the reasoning behind the numbers. Open houses are the common format for graduations, so many families do well with a smaller tent than the full guest list suggests.

## Timing and booking

Graduations cluster in the last weeks of May and the first weeks of June, which also overlaps with wedding season. Call tent companies as soon as the date is set. Permit lead time matters: Chicago triples its fee if the application comes less than 14 days before setup. See [Chicago tent permits](/blog/chicago-tent-permit) and [suburban tent permits](/blog/tent-permit-chicago-suburbs). For chairs, also read [when to book chair rentals](/blog/topic/budget-and-booking).

## Make it comfortable

May and June in Chicago can bring a cool, rainy day or a hot one. Plan for both:

- **Heat:** leave sides open, add fans, and put drinks near the entrance. See [cooling a tent in summer](/blog/cooling-a-tent-in-summer).
- **Rain:** have sidewalls available and a plan for the buffet. See [rain plan with a tent](/blog/rain-plan-with-a-tent).
- **Kids:** keep a clear path from the house, use floors on slopes and keep cords out of walkways.
- **Older guests:** shade, firm surfaces and chairs with a good seat height.

## What to put in the tent

A graduation party tent is usually one big living room. Think about:

1. **Buffet or food stations** on a long wall near the house.
2. **A drink table** apart from the food so lines do not stack up.
3. **Tables of 8** for family seating, with a few high-top tables for mingling.
4. **A photo display** of the graduate, with a table for cards and gifts.
5. **A cake or dessert table** with a nice backdrop.
6. **A slideshow** on a TV or projector, with power nearby.

## Chairs for the party

Guests rotate in and out, so stackable chairs that are easy to move are best. [Garden folding chairs](/chairs/garden-folding) are practical and comfortable on grass or patio. For a school-color event, a neutral [cross-back chair](/chairs/cross-back) lets decor do the talking. Count 60 to 70 percent of guests as seated at one time for an open house. ## Frequently asked questions

### What size tent do I need for a graduation party?

A 20 x 30 tent suits about 50 seated guests, and a 30 x 40 suits about 100. Open houses can use less because not everyone is there at once.

### How far ahead should I book a graduation tent?

As soon as you have your date. Late May and June weekends are busy for tent companies around Chicago. A few months ahead is safer than a few weeks.

### Do I need a permit for a graduation party tent?

If the tent is over 400 square feet in Chicago, yes, and many suburbs use a similar threshold. Check your village early.

### Can I put up a pop-up canopy instead?

A 10 x 10 canopy works for a small family party, but it is not a substitute for a tent at 40 or more guests. See [pop-up canopy vs rented tent](/blog/pop-up-canopy-vs-rented-tent).

### Do I need tables and chairs under the tent?

Yes, plan seating for at least 60 percent of guests. Buffet tables, a drink table and a gift table are extra.

### What if it rains on graduation day?

Bring sidewalls, keep the buffet inside the tent and have a plan for guest parking and paths. See [rain plan with a tent](/blog/rain-plan-with-a-tent).
