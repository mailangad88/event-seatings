**Quick answer**

Before tent stakes are driven into the ground in Illinois, utility lines should be located by calling 811 or JULIE at 800-892-0123, or by filing a request at illinois1call.com. According to the national 811 site, notice is two working days (weekends excluded) and no more than 10 calendar days before digging, and the ticket stays valid for 25 days. Ask your tent company whether it files the request. If your tent sits on ballast on pavement, no digging is needed.

</div>

Tent stakes are long, and they go into the same ground as gas lines, electric service, water lines and cable. Hitting one is expensive and can be dangerous. JULIE is Illinois's one-call notification system, and it is the step most hosts forget because their tent company assumes the homeowner handles it, while the homeowner assumes the tent company does. This guide clears that up. Rules and phone numbers can change, so confirm on the JULIE website.

1. **Request the locate.** Call 811 or 800-892-0123, or file online at illinois1call.com. The site has separate forms for homeowners and contractors.
2. **Give the details.** You provide the address, the work area and the type of work. A tent stake line is a work area.
3. **Wait for marks.** Utility companies send locators who mark their lines with paint or flags. Do not remove them.
4. **Stake clear of the marks.** Tent crews keep stakes away from the marked areas, following the utility's guidance on tolerance.
5. **Keep the ticket.** Your ticket number proves you called and stays valid for 25 days, not counting the day of notice, or until the marks become hard to see.

## Timing

Per 811beforeyoudig.com, the lead time is two working days, excluding weekends, and no more than 10 calendar days before excavation starts. For a Saturday event with Friday setup, a Wednesday request is cutting it close, so submit a week or so ahead and keep inside the 10-day window. ## Who is responsible?

It depends on your contract. The party that drives the stakes usually should make sure the locate is requested. Some tent companies file it automatically, and others expect the customer to do it. Ask in writing, and ask for the ticket number. Either way, the property owner remains the one living with the lines in the yard.

## Which utilities are not marked?

Be aware of what a locate may not cover. Utilities marked by JULIE are those owned by member companies. Private lines on your property, such as a sprinkler system, invisible dog fence, landscape lighting, a well or a septic line, might not be marked. Mark and tell the crew about them yourself, and tell the tent company. Ask a landscaper or your HOA if you are unsure.

## Chicago city limits

Inside the City of Chicago, utility locating may run through a different channel. We could not confirm the details for city addresses, so when you call 811 say that you are in Chicago and follow what they tell you. Your tent company has probably done this many times.

## What if you skip it?

Hitting a gas line can force an evacuation, and hitting a cable can cut service to a neighborhood. You may be responsible for repair costs. Responsible tent companies will want a locate on file before they stake. See [tent anchoring stakes vs ballast](/blog/tent-anchoring-stakes-vs-ballast) for a no-dig alternative.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I have to call 811 before putting up a tent?

If the tent will be staked into the ground, you should. Contact JULIE so utility lines are located before digging. Tents on ballast over pavement do not dig, but ask your company.

### How many days notice does JULIE need?

Two working days, excluding weekends, and no more than 10 calendar days before the work. The ticket stays valid for 25 days.

### What is the JULIE phone number?

Call 811 or 800-892-0123. You can also file at illinois1call.com.

### Does it cost anything to call JULIE?

We could not confirm a fee for homeowner requests on the pages we reviewed, and the 811 system is designed as a free public safety service. Confirm current terms on the JULIE site when you file.

### Who should call, the host or the tent company?

Ask your tent company. Many request it, but some require the customer to. Get the answer in writing, plus the ticket number.

### Will JULIE mark my sprinkler system?

Not necessarily. Private lines on your property may not be marked, so tell the crew where your sprinklers and dog fence are.
