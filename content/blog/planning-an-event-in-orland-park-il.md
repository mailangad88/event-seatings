## Check the village rules first

Orland Park publishes information on tent and canopy permits on its website. Requirements in Chicago-area villages usually depend on the tent's size, how long it is up and whether it has sidewalls, heaters or cooking inside. Ask the Village of Orland Park what applies to your tent, and whether an inspection is needed before guests enter. Ask your HOA, too. Read our [Chicago tent permit guide](/blog/chicago-tent-permit) for the questions to bring to any village office.

Call JULIE, the Illinois utility locating service, before stakes go into the lawn. Your tent installer should confirm this is done.

A frame tent can be staked into grass but needs weights on concrete or pavers. Wind rating matters; ask the tent company about how much wind the tent can handle and when to take guests inside.

## Lighting and heat

A tent in the afternoon sun can be hot. Open sidewalls, add fans and keep drinks cold. For evening, plan string lights across the ceiling and uplights on poles. If you plan heaters in a tent, ask the tent company about clearances and rules, because tent fabric and heaters demand specific safety steps. ## Grass, drainage and flooring

Tents often sit on lawns that drain slowly, especially in spring. Look for low spots and wet patches a week before the party. If the ground is soft, ask about tent flooring, which can be plywood, interlocking panels or a raised platform. Flooring keeps chair legs from sinking and protects guests in dress shoes. It also makes the tent feel like a room. It costs more than bare grass, so ask for a quote and decide whether it matters for your date.

Sidewalls help with wind and rain, but they trap heat. In summer, keep sides rolled up and use fans. In cool weather, close the sides and use approved heaters, following the tent company's safety rules. Place the buffet and bar near an outside edge so staff can restock without crossing the dance floor. Place the DJ or band where cables can reach power without crossing guest aisles. A tidy cord path matters in a Chicago-area crowd of guests in heels.

## Frequently asked questions

### How big a tent for 60 guests?

About 20 x 40 feet for a seated dinner at rounds. Add space if you have a dance floor or buffet inside.

### Do I need a tent permit in Orland Park?

Possibly. The village publishes tent permit information. Contact the building or fire department to confirm.

### How many chairs for 120 guests?

About 126, with extra seats for a head table or lounge.

### Can a tent go on a patio or driveway?

Yes, with weights instead of stakes. Ask the tent company about requirements.

### How early should the tent go up?

Often the day before, so tables and chairs can be set on event day. Confirm in your quote.

### What if the forecast calls for wind?

Ask the tent company for its wind limits, and have a plan to move indoors.
