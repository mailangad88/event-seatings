**Quick answer**

Book big-ticket rentals (tent, dance floor, specialty chairs) 9 to 12 months out for peak Chicago dates, and chairs, tables and linens 6 to 9 months out. Confirm the final count and details 2 to 4 weeks before the wedding, finalize delivery windows 1 week out, and plan pickup for the next morning. Peak season runs from late spring through October, when popular items and delivery slots go first.

</div>

Rentals feel like a last-minute task because they depend on the final guest count. But the most important choices, like which chair, whether you need a tent and who delivers, are made months earlier. Here is a timeline that works for most Chicago-area weddings, with a note on what changes for peak dates.

## 12 months out: venue and structure

After the venue and date are set, ask what is included. Tables and chairs are often supplied, and sometimes not. If you plan a tent, ask your venue and your village about permits and set a tentative date for a site visit. Tent crews and lighting companies book the most popular dates first. See [what size tent do I need](/blog/what-size-tent-do-i-need) and the [Chicago tent permit guide](/blog/chicago-tent-permit).

## 9 to 12 months out: chairs and tables

Pick a chair style and a table plan. Peak Saturdays in June, September and October are the first to go, and specialty chairs (velvet, Louis, throne) have limited quantities. Reserve with a deposit if the company offers one. Our guide on when to book event chair rentals in Chicago goes deeper on availability.

## 6 to 9 months out: linens, tableware, glassware

Choose linens and napkins after you know your table sizes and palette. Ask your caterer what dinnerware and glassware they provide. Order samples if you can. See the [tablecloth size chart](/blog/tablecloth-size-chart) and [how many glasses per guest](/blog/how-many-glasses-per-guest).

## 3 months out: floor plan

Create a floor plan with the venue showing the ceremony, cocktail hour, dinner, bar, dance floor and support tables. Include doors, columns and the loading route. This is the best time to find that a 72-inch round won't fit or that the bar belongs on the other wall.

## 1 month out: final count

Most rental companies need a final count 2 to 4 weeks before the date, with a deadline after which changes cost more or are not possible. Confirm what the count includes: guests, vendors, kids, head table and spares.

## Wedding week

<ol class="flow">
<li><strong>5 days before:</strong> confirm the delivery window, venue contact and loading dock.</li>
<li><strong>2 days before:</strong> check the weather and finalize the rain plan.</li>
<li><strong>Day before:</strong> tents and flooring go in; chairs and tables follow on the wedding morning.</li>
<li><strong>Wedding day:</strong> a point person walks the setup against the floor plan.</li>
<li><strong>Next morning:</strong> pickup. Bag linens, stack chairs, count dinnerware.</li>
</ol>

## What to bring to every vendor call

Keep one shared document with your date, venue address, guest count range, ceremony and reception start times, the loading route, and the venue contact. Add the floor plan as soon as you have one. Vendors can only quote well if they know about stairs, elevators, gravel parking lots and the time the room becomes available. A short list sent once saves a dozen back-and-forth emails, and it keeps every vendor working from the same numbers when the guest count changes.

Also write down who has authority to approve changes on the wedding day. A planner, a parent or a friend should be the single contact for the rental crew, so the couple is not answering questions in their wedding clothes.

## Short timelines

If the wedding is less than three months away, work in this order: venue details, chairs and tables, tent if needed, then linens and tableware. Call early and ask what is in stock for your date. Our last-minute chair rental guide covers the options. A shorter timeline mostly costs you choice, not quality.

## What drives lead time in Chicago

- **Peak season:** late spring through October.
- **Venue restrictions:** downtown buildings may need certificates of insurance and loading reservations well ahead.
- **Tents:** permits and utility locates take time. Call JULIE (811) before staking.
- **Weather plans:** a second delivery or a changed layout takes more crew time.

## Frequently asked questions

### How early should I book wedding rentals?

Book the tent and specialty items 9 to 12 months out and chairs and tables 6 to 9 months out. Peak weekends fill first, so earlier is safer for June, September and October.

### When do I need to give a final guest count?

Usually 2 to 4 weeks before the wedding. The exact deadline is in your rental contract, so check it when you book.

### Can I add chairs after the final count?

Sometimes. It depends on stock and the delivery schedule. Ask what the process is and whether it costs more.

### What if my wedding is in under three months?

Start with chairs and tables, then linens and tableware. You may have fewer choices, but most companies can help if you call early.

### When should I visit the venue with the rental company?

Around 3 to 6 months out, once you have a floor plan. A site visit catches issues with doors, ramps and loading.

### When is pickup?

Typically the morning after the wedding or the following business day. Confirm with your venue and rental company.
