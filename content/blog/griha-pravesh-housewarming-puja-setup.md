Griha pravesh is the Hindu ceremony for entering a new home for the first time. A priest leads prayers, usually to Ganesh and other deities, and often a havan, the sacred fire. Families also boil milk on the new stove and invite relatives and friends for a meal. Many Chicago families hold it in a new condo, a bungalow, a two-flat or a suburban house.

Customs differ by region, family and priest, so follow the plan your priest gives you. This guide covers the room, the seating, the smoke and the meal.

## Start with the priest

Ask the priest three things before you plan anything else.

1. **The date and time.** The priest usually chooses an auspicious time (muhurat). It can be early in the morning, so rentals must be in place the day before.
2. **How long the puja runs.** Most last 1 to 3 hours, and that decides how long guests sit.
3. **What the priest needs.** A low table or a mat for the puja items, a place for the havan kund, a water source and space to sit facing a particular direction. Some priests bring everything and some ask you to buy the samagri.

Then you can set the room, the guest count and the meal.

- **Clear the room.** Move big furniture out or against the wall. If the home is new, the room may already be empty, which helps.
- **Floor seating.** Lay clean rugs, dhurries or mats. Allow about 10 square feet per person, so a 15 x 20 foot room (300 sq ft) fits about 25 to 30 on the floor, less if the priest area takes space.
- **Chairs for elders.** Set 6 to 10 chairs along the walls, with arms if you can. Many older guests and anyone with a bad knee cannot sit on the floor for 2 hours. See [comfortable chairs for older guests](/blog/comfortable-chairs-for-older-guests) and [planning an event for older guests](/blog/planning-an-event-for-older-guests).
- **Shoes.** Guests take off shoes at the door. Set a rack or a mat and a small bench. See [coat racks for winter events](/blog/coat-racks-for-winter-events-chicago) if it is cold.
- **The doorway.** A toran of marigolds or mango leaves and a rangoli at the entrance are common. Keep the path clear.

## Havan smoke and smoke detectors

A havan is a small fire of wood, ghee and herbs. It makes smoke, and a new home's detectors are sensitive.

- **Open a window and run a fan** to move smoke outside. Do this before you light it.
- **Tell your building.** Condo and apartment buildings in Chicago often have a central fire alarm. A havan can set it off and bring the fire department. Check with your building manager or board before the day, and ask whether there are rules for open flame.
- **Do not disable a smoke detector without permission.** Some families cover or remove the detector in the puja room. Check with your building and your insurer first, and put it back right after.
- **Use a small kund.** A copper or metal havan kund on a metal tray, with sand or a base under it, protects the floor.
- **Keep water and a fire extinguisher nearby.** And keep fabric, garlands and kids away from the fire.
- **Ask about alternatives.** Some families use a small havan or a lamp and incense if smoke is a problem, and some priests will adjust. Ask the priest.

If you are in a rental or the home is new, check what your lease or the builder says about open flame.

## The meal after the puja

After the prayers, families serve prasad and a meal for all the guests. It is usually vegetarian and often catered or made by relatives.

- **Put the meal in a different room, or outside.** The puja room should stay clean and quiet, and floors with mats are hard to eat on.
- **Buffet or seated.** For 30 guests, a buffet on two 6-foot tables and a few round tables for eating works well. For 50 or more, add tables. See [buffet table layouts](/blog/buffet-table-layouts) and [how much food per person](/blog/how-much-food-per-person-for-a-party).
- **Plates and utensils.** Rent dinnerware if you do not want to wash for 40 people. See [dinnerware rentals](/rentals/dinnerware) and [catering equipment](/rentals/catering-equipment).
- **Chai and water.** A tea or coffee urn near the door is handy. See [how many coffee urns for a party](/blog/how-many-coffee-urns-for-a-party).
- **If it is cold,** keep the meal indoors, or use a tent and heaters in the yard. See [heating a tent in fall](/blog/heating-a-tent-in-fall).

## Small spaces and city homes

Many Chicago homes are small: a condo, a two-flat, a bungalow. Plan around that.

- Ask which room holds the most people and has a window.
- Use folding tables that fit through a narrow door or hallway. Measure before you order.
- Keep the elevator, stairs and the building's rules in mind. See [Chicago high-rise freight elevator and COI notes](/blog/chicago-high-rise-party-freight-elevator-coi).
- If the home has no space, consider a tent in the yard or a community hall for the meal. See [church hall and community center event planning](/blog/church-hall-and-community-center-event-planning).

## What most hosts find out too late

- **The date can move.** The muhurat depends on the priest and the home's details. Do not book rentals until the date is fixed, but ask for tentative holds.
- **Early start times.** Rental delivery is usually daytime. Ask for delivery the day before.
- **Count at delivery.** Count chairs and tables, and check the floor protection.
- **Measure doors and stairs.** Folding chairs fit anywhere. A round table top may not.
- **Smoke detectors.** This is the most common surprise. Check with your building early.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a griha pravesh puja?

It is a Hindu ceremony for entering a new home for the first time. A priest leads prayers, often with a havan, and the family invites guests for a meal. Customs vary by region and family.

### How many people can sit on the floor for a puja?

Plan about 10 square feet per person. A 15 x 20 foot room fits about 25 to 30 on the floor, less if the priest's area takes space. Add chairs along the walls for elders.

### Do I need chairs for a griha pravesh?

Yes, for elders and anyone who cannot sit on the floor. Plan 6 to 10 chairs for a typical gathering, with arms if possible. Place them along the wall with a view of the puja.

### Will a havan set off my smoke detector?

It can. Open a window, run a fan and tell your building before the day. Check with your building or insurer before covering a detector, and put it back right after.

### Where should I serve the meal after the puja?

In a different room or outside, so the puja area stays clean. Use a buffet on 6-foot tables with a few tables for eating. Rent dinnerware and an urn if you do not want to wash for the crowd.

### When should I rent tables and chairs for a griha pravesh?

After the priest sets the date, ask for delivery the day before. Book 4 to 6 weeks ahead if you can, and ask for a tentative hold while the date is set.
