## September weather: what to watch out for

September is comfortable. NWS normals for O'Hare give September an average high of 75.5°F and a low of 57.1°F, with about 3.19 inches of rain. Early September can still be hot, and late September evenings can be cool.

What to plan for:

- **Evening cool-down.** It is warm at 4 pm and jacket weather by 9 pm. A few heaters or an indoor space for the late part of the night keep guests comfortable.
- **Earlier sunsets.** Sunset moves from about 7:26 pm on September 1 to 7:02 pm by September 15 and 6:39 pm by September 28. Plan lighting from the start of dinner.
- **Less storm risk than summer, not zero.** Keep sidewalls and a rain plan.

## Venue demand and booking lead times in September

September and October are peak wedding months in the Chicago area, and September Saturdays are often booked a year or more ahead at popular venues. Our rule of thumb is to book tents, specialty chairs and dance floors 2 to 4 months ahead at minimum, and to reserve the venue first.

If the Saturdays you want are gone, Fridays and Sundays in September are still excellent. Labor Day Sunday is a popular choice because many guests have Monday off. ## Frequently asked questions

### Is September the best month for an outdoor event in Chicago?

It is one of the best. O'Hare averages a 75.5°F high and a 57.1°F low, with less heat and storm risk than summer. The trade-off is demand: venues and tents book early.

### When is Labor Day weekend in 2027?

Saturday, September 4 through Monday, September 6, 2027. Sunday events are popular because many guests have Monday off.

### What should I watch for around Mexican Independence Day in Chicago?

September 16 is a Thursday in 2027. Parades and celebrations in Pilsen and Little Village usually happen on nearby weekends, with street closures. Check before booking deliveries in those neighborhoods.

### How early should I book a September wedding venue in Chicago?

Popular venues are often booked a year or more ahead for September Saturdays. Book the venue first, then tents and specialty chairs 2 to 4 months ahead at minimum.

### What time is sunset in Chicago in September?

About 7:26 pm on September 1, 7:02 pm on September 15 and 6:39 pm on September 28 in 2027.
