**Quick answer**

For a holiday party at home, plan seating for about half your guests, two food stations, a separate bar, and a coat area that keeps winter gear out of the party. For 40 guests, rent about 20 chairs, 2 to 3 cocktail tables, a buffet table, a bar table and a coat rack with hangers. Spread food and drinks across two rooms so the crowd does not bunch in the kitchen.

</div>

Holiday parties in a Chicago-area home have three problems at once: too many people for the furniture, too many coats for the closet, and weather that makes any outdoor overflow a bad idea. A small rental order handles all three. The trick is knowing where each piece goes before it arrives.

## Start with the coats

In December every guest brings a coat, a hat and often boots. For 40 guests that is 40 coats, which will not fit in a hall closet. Plan for it.

A rolling garment rack holds a set number of hangers, so check the capacity when you order and add a second rack if you need it. A boot tray and a mat keep wet shoes from the hardwood. A spare bedroom with a bed works as a backup, but a rack by the door keeps traffic out of that room.

### Chairs
Pick chairs that blend into a living room. [Ghost chairs](/chairs/ghost), about $10 to $14, take up little visual space. [Velvet dining chairs](/chairs/velvet-dining), about $18 to $25, add color for an upscale party. [Cross-back chairs](/chairs/cross-back), about $8 to $11, suit a warm, rustic holiday look.

### Food and bar
Hot food needs chafing dishes and a way to keep them warm. See [how many chafing dishes you need](/blog/how-many-chafing-dishes-for-a-buffet). Set the bar in a different room from the food. If you want a hot cocoa or coffee corner, check [how many coffee urns](/blog/how-many-coffee-urns-for-a-party).

## A plan for the December crush

<ol class="flow">
<li><strong>Book early.</strong> December weekends fill first, so reserve rentals in October or early November.</li>
<li><strong>Clear the path.</strong> Move the coffee table and extra furniture to a bedroom the day before.</li>
<li><strong>Set up the day before if you can</strong>, so the day of is for food.</li>
<li><strong>Place the tree and lights</strong> away from the buffet so no one steps on a cord.</li>
<li><strong>Plan for snow:</strong> shovel the walk, and put down mats at the door.</li>
</ol>

## Chicago notes

Chicago winters can bring snow and ice, which affects delivery times and walkways. Tell the rental company if your driveway is steep or your street has parking restrictions, and clear a path for the truck. In the city, check the parking rules on your block, and in the suburbs ask your village about overnight parking. If you plan to use a tent for overflow, check heating and clearances; our [heating guide](/blog/heating-a-tent-in-fall) covers the basics. For string lights and candles, see [string light layouts](/blog/string-light-layouts-for-events).

## Set the mood without clutter

Holiday parties are decorated already: a tree, garlands, candles, a mantel. Rentals should stay quiet so the decor stands out. Choose neutral linens, such as white, ivory or deep green, and let your own ornaments do the talking. Keep the buffet simple, with a tablecloth that reaches the floor, and use cake stands and risers rather than extra tables.

Lighting matters in December, when it gets dark before 5 p.m. Dim overhead lights, add lamps and candles in safe holders, and avoid open flames near garlands. Battery candles are fine near trees.

Music should sit below conversation. A playlist set up before guests arrive saves you from fiddling with a phone all evening.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many chairs do I need for a holiday party at home?

Plan seats for about half your guests, counting your own furniture. A 40-guest party typically needs 18 to 22 rented chairs.

### Where do I put coats at a holiday party?

On a rolling rack near the front door, with a boot tray and a mat. A spare bedroom works as a backup.

### Should I rent glasses?

If you have fewer than three per guest, yes. Rented glasses mean you do not have to buy and wash a case of wine glasses.

### How do I stop the kitchen from getting crowded?

Put the bar and the food in other rooms, and place a few seats and cocktail tables in the kitchen's overflow area so guests spread out.

### What time should a holiday party start?

Late afternoon or early evening, 4 to 7 p.m., so people can leave before the roads freeze. Check weather forecasts if you are hosting in January.

### Can I host a holiday party outdoors in Chicago?

Only with a heated tent and a backup plan. Most holiday parties here work better indoors with a small outdoor space for guests who want air.
