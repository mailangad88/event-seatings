**Quick answer**

A proposal setup is a small stage for one moment: a backdrop, lighting, a table for two and maybe marquee letters. Pick a spot that stays warm and has power, then plan the order of the evening so the reveal happens when you are both there and the light looks good. In Chicago, winter sunsets come early, around 4:20 to 4:30 pm in December, so an indoor setup or an early evening plan is often the best choice.

Christmas, New Year's and Valentine's Day are three of the most popular nights to propose, and they all fall in the coldest part of a Chicago year. That changes the plan. You cannot count on a warm patio, and the sun is gone by late afternoon. The good news is that a proposal is a small job. You need a good spot, some light and a table, and you can rent most of it for one evening.

## Pick the date and the place first

For 2026 and 2027, the dates people choose most are Christmas Eve (Thursday, Dec 24, 2026), New Year's Eve and Valentine's Day (Sunday, Feb 14, 2027). Valentine's Day on a Sunday means restaurants are booked, so a home or small venue setup is easier to control.

- **At home.** Private and warm. You control the music, the lighting and who is in the next room.
- **A rented room.** A party room, loft or community space gives you more floor for a backdrop and a group of friends who come out after.
- **Outdoors.** A park or the lakefront looks great but the wind and cold are real. If you go outside, keep the moment short and have a warm room ready. Check with your park district about any rules for setups.

**Backdrop.** A flower wall, a draped panel or a simple arch. Keep it close to the table so the photos work. See our [flower wall guide](/blog/flower-wall-rental-guide) for sizes.

**Marquee letters.** Big lit letters spell "MARRY ME" or a name. Count the letters and check how each is powered, since lit letters need outlets or batteries. Our guide to [marquee letters](/blog/marquee-letters-for-events) covers sizes and power.

**Lighting.** Warm light is kinder in photos and feels softer in the room. String lights, uplights and candles work together. See [winter event lighting and sunset times](/blog/winter-event-lighting-chicago-sunset) before you pick the hour.

**A table for two.** A small round or square table, two chairs and a cloth. A 30-inch round or a 36-inch square is enough for two plates and a candle. Choose chairs that look good in photos and are comfortable for a long dinner. See [chairs for events](/rentals/chairs).

## Time the reveal

<ol class="flow">
<li><strong>Before they arrive.</strong> Test the lights and letters. Turn the room to its dim setting. Have your photographer or friend hidden and ready.</li>
<li><strong>The arrival.</strong> Walk them in from the door along the path of lights. Keep the first minute calm.</li>
<li><strong>The moment.</strong> Pick a spot near the table. Have the letters on and the music low enough to hear you.</li>
<li><strong>The photos.</strong> Spend 10 minutes on photos while the lights still look fresh.</li>
<li><strong>Dinner or the surprise.</strong> Sit at the table for two, or open the door to friends and family for a party.</li>
</ol>

## What most hosts find out too late

- **Check power before you design.** Lit letters, string lights and a speaker need outlets. Find them, and plan where cords run so no one trips.
- **Winter delivery needs a window.** Rental companies may have holiday hours or busy weekends. Book early for Christmas, New Year's and Valentine's weekends, and ask what time setup can happen.
- **Measure the doors.** Backdrops, panels and letters come in big pieces. Check doors, stairs and elevators in your building.
- **Candle rules vary by building.** Many rooms limit open flame. Use battery candles if you are not sure, and check with your building or venue.

## A winter plan B

If the plan was a park or patio, have a warm room ready for the same layout. A small heated space beats a cold outdoor setup because the lights and the sound hold up better indoors. If you want to use an outdoor spot for the proposal and then move inside, keep the indoor setup ready so you can go straight in. ## Frequently asked questions

### What do I need to set up a marriage proposal at home?

A backdrop or a clear wall, warm lighting, a table for two and a plan for the order of the evening. Add marquee letters or flowers if you want something big in the photos. Check that you have outlets for any lit pieces.

### How much space does a proposal setup need?

A small room is enough. A table for two needs about a 6 by 6 foot area, and the backdrop and photographer add a few more feet. If you want guests to join after, plan a second area for them.

### Can I propose on Christmas Eve or Valentine's Day?

Yes, many people do. These dates are busy, so book rentals early and expect holiday hours. Valentine's Day 2027 falls on a Sunday.

### Should I plan an outdoor proposal in winter?

You can, but keep it short and plan a warm room right after. Wind and cold affect lights, candles and sound. A heated indoor space is easier to control in a Chicago winter.

### How do I keep the proposal a surprise?

Give only the delivery crew the real details, and ask them to set up before the other person arrives. Stage friends in another room and use a signal for when to come in. Keep the plan out of group texts.
