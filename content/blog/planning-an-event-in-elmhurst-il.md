**Quick answer**

In Elmhurst, the Elmhurst Park District handles picnic permits, special use applications and facility rentals, including weddings in the Wilder Park formal garden and the Wilder Park Conservatory. It has a separate special use application for larger gatherings, so ask about the current guest-count threshold. The City of Elmhurst handles streets and public right-of-way.

Elmhurst is an inner-ring DuPage suburb with a downtown, a university, a strong park district and one of the best-known garden spots in the area. If you are marrying or hosting a party here, the Wilder Park grounds probably came up in your search. Here is how to plan around them.

## Park district first

The Elmhurst Park District publishes a Picnics and Permits page, a picnic permit application, and a Special Use Permit application. The park district's earlier form was titled for gatherings of 100 or more, so a larger group likely needs the special use application. Ask for the current threshold. It also offers weddings in the Wilder Park Formal Garden and facilities including the Wilder Park Conservatory.

## Space types

- **Wilder Park Formal Garden.** A wedding ceremony location. Ask the district about dates, capacity and rules.
- **Wilder Park Conservatory.** A district facility. Check what events it hosts.
- **Park picnic areas.** For graduations and reunions, via the picnic permit.
- **York Commons and other district facilities.** Check capacity.
- **City streets and plazas.** Elmhurst City Centre events fall under the City.
- **Private homes and halls.** Common for showers and receptions.

## Ceremony chairs and garden paths

Garden ceremonies need chairs that do not sink into soft ground and look good on photographs. Set rows 4 to 5 feet apart with a center aisle at least 4 feet wide, and hold the last row a few feet back from planting beds. See [ceremony vs reception chairs](/blog/ceremony-vs-reception-chairs). White folding chairs are the traditional choice, while [ghost chairs](/chairs/ghost) feel airy.

## Delivery from St. Charles

Elmhurst is east of St. Charles in inner DuPage County, with heavier traffic than the Fox Valley. Plan for I-290 and Route 83 congestion, and allow time for downtown parking. Garden areas may need hand-carrying along paths, so ask the district where vehicles can stop.

## Seasonal notes

The garden is at its best from late spring to early fall. Peak wedding dates book early, so ask the district about availability before you book anything else. Winter events move indoors.

## An Elmhurst example

A 60-guest garden ceremony needs 60 chairs in rows of 6 or 8 with an aisle. [Garden folding chairs](/chairs/garden-folding) (about $5-7, an estimate) are the budget pick; [cross-back chairs](/chairs/cross-back) (about $8-11) suit a rustic look.

## Rentals beyond chairs

A garden ceremony is only the first hour. Plan the rest of the day with these in mind.

- **Cocktail hour:** a few high tops with covers. See [how many cocktail tables](/blog/how-many-cocktail-tables-for-standing-reception).
- **Reception tables:** rounds of 8 or 10, with floor-length cloths.
- **Place settings:** plates, flatware and glasses per course. Our [place setting guide](/blog/place-setting-diagram-formal-dinner) shows a formal layout.
- **Weather backup:** a tent or an indoor room, decided a week out.

## Timeline for a garden wedding

Garden dates are popular, so work backward from the park district's availability.

1. **12 months out:** ask the Elmhurst Park District about available dates and what the ceremony fee covers.
2. **9 months out:** lock the reception venue and caterer.
3. **6 months out:** request a rental quote so you have a budget range.
4. **2 months out:** confirm the guest count and the table plan.
5. **2 weeks out:** send final counts and the load-in plan.

Ceremony times matter, because late-afternoon light in the garden is softer than midday light. Sunset is about 8:30 pm near the June solstice and about 7:00 pm in late March, so a 5 pm ceremony in spring still leaves daylight for photos.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a permit for a picnic in an Elmhurst park?

Yes, the Elmhurst Park District has a picnic permit application. For larger groups it has a separate special use application, so ask for the current guest-count threshold.

### Can I get married at Wilder Park?

The Elmhurst Park District offers weddings in the Wilder Park Formal Garden. Contact the district for dates, capacity and rules.

### Who handles streets and public plazas?

The City of Elmhurst. Ask it about permits for events that use City property.

### What is York Commons?

A district facility. Ask the park district what events it hosts.

### Do you deliver in Elmhurst?

Yes. First deliveries begin with the April 2027 Founding Season.

### What chairs suit a garden ceremony?

Garden folding chairs are the budget choice, and ghost or cross-back chairs look more finished.
