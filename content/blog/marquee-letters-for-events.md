**Quick answer**

Marquee letters come in common heights of 3, 4, 5 and 6 feet, and the right size depends on how far away guests will see them: bigger letters for farther views. A 4-foot set suits a photo backdrop, a 5 to 6-foot set suits a dance floor or entry, and a 3-foot set suits a table. Plan a flat, stable spot and a nearby outlet or battery option.

</div>

Light-up letters turn a plain wall into a photo moment. They also turn into tipping hazards if the base is flimsy or the cord crosses the aisle. This guide covers how big, how many, where to place them and what safety checks to ask for.

## Sizes and where they fit

Marquee letters are usually made of metal or wood, with bulbs mounted in the face. LED bulbs are standard now because they stay cool and draw little power. Popular heights are listed in the table. The viewing distances are rough planning guesses, not a standard, so ask your vendor.

<div class="table-wrap">

| Height | Best use | Typical viewing distance |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 2 ft | Table decor, cake table, gift table | 3 to 10 ft |
| 3 ft | Sweetheart table, photo corner | 10 to 20 ft |
| 4 ft | Photo backdrop, bar | 15 to 25 ft |
| 5 to 6 ft | Dance floor, entrance, stage | 25 to 40 ft |
| 7 ft and larger | Stage or outdoor signage | Ask the vendor |

## How many letters do you need?

Common sets:

- Initials (2 to 3 letters)
- "LOVE" or "MR & MRS" (4 to 6 pieces)
- Numbers for birthdays and anniversaries (2 or 3 pieces)
- Words like "BAR" or "PARTY"

Cost grows with the number of letters, because each is a separate rental that must be delivered, tested and set up. Ask for a quote with a spare bulb kit and a set-up fee, not just a price per letter. We do not publish prices for lighting, so [request a quote](/quote).

## Placement tips

1. **Pair with a backdrop.** A draped wall, greenery wall or curtain gives the letters contrast. White letters on a white wall disappear.
2. **Keep them out of traffic.** Place them where guests will not bump them: a corner, a wall or a stage.
3. **Check line of sight.** Letters should not block the head table, the DJ or the exit.
4. **Give photographers space.** Leave 6 to 8 feet in front for a group photo.
5. **Mind the bulb color.** Warm white bulbs match candles and string lights; see [lighting color temperature](/blog/lighting-color-temperature-events).

## Power and cords

Most large marquee letters plug in. A set of four may draw less than a toaster, but it needs a safe cord run.

- Plan a nearby outlet, ideally on a GFCI if outdoors.
- Use a cord cover across walkways.
- Ask about battery-powered letters for locations without outlets; see [battery-powered event lighting](/blog/battery-powered-event-lighting).
- Plug the set into one controller or power strip rated for the load.

See [cords on grass](/blog/cords-on-grass-outdoor-lighting-safety) for lawn setups and [extension cord safety](/blog/outdoor-extension-cord-safety-events).

## Stability

Tall letters are top-heavy.

- Ask for a base or stand rated for the height.
- Weight the base with sandbags or a ballast.
- On grass, use boards or a platform so the base does not sink.
- Outdoors, plan for wind. Large letters can act as a sail. Strap them to a frame or move them inside if the wind picks up.
- Do not place letters where guests lean on them.

## Using letters at different events

- **Weddings:** initials or LOVE at the dance floor or behind the sweetheart table.
- **Birthdays and milestones:** numbers (30, 50) near the photo backdrop.
- **Corporate events:** a company initial or event name near registration, along with [stage lighting](/blog/corporate-stage-lighting-basics).
- **Quinceañeras:** the guest's initial or the number 15.

Letters also combine with chairs: a gold [infinity chair](/chairs/infinity) or a clear [ghost chair](/chairs/ghost) in front of lit letters makes a polished photo spot.

## Chicago notes

Many Chicago venues limit what can be set on stages, floors and sidewalks, and some ask for insurance from vendors who deliver large items. Confirm loading dock access and freight elevator times, which matter for 6-foot letters in downtown buildings.

## Frequently asked questions

### What size marquee letters should I rent?

Choose by viewing distance. Four feet suits a photo backdrop, five to six feet suits a dance floor, and one to two feet suits tables.

### How many outlets do marquee letters need?

Often one, with all letters on one power strip. Ask the vendor for the total watts.

### Can marquee letters be used outdoors?

Some are rated for outdoor use. Ask the vendor, protect connections from water and secure them against wind.

### Do marquee letters get hot?

LED bulbs stay cool. Older incandescent bulbs get hot, so confirm the bulb type.

### Can I get battery-powered letters?

Some are available. Ask the vendor about battery life and brightness.

### How far in advance should I book?

Popular letter sets and numbers go quickly in peak wedding season. Ask early, and check [when to book](/blog/topic/budget-and-booking) for timing tips.
