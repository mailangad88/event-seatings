**Quick answer**

A loft party works best as three zones: a cocktail and lounge area, one dining or buffet area and a clear open space for dancing or mingling. Use the open floor plan to your advantage, work around columns rather than against them, and confirm freight elevator access and floor rules with the loft owner before you order. Plan about 6 to 8 square feet per standing guest and 10 to 12 per seated guest as a rule of thumb.

</div>

Chicago has plenty of converted warehouse space: brick walls, big windows, timber beams and concrete floors. A loft is a great canvas, and a tricky one. There is no ballroom to hide cords, no coat check, and the sound bounces off every hard surface. A little planning changes a loft party from echoing to intimate.

## Start with the room, not the guest list

Before you count chairs, get a floor plan of the space or measure it yourself. Write down the usable square footage, the position of columns, the freight elevator, restrooms and any permanent bar or kitchen. The dimension that decides everything is clear floor space after you subtract columns, stairs and walk lanes.

## Work around columns, not through them

A column in the middle of a room is a problem only if you pretend it is not there. Solutions that work:

- **Wrap a column with a cocktail table.** A high-top beside a column becomes a natural standing perch and uses dead space.
- **Seat the table so no guest looks at a column.** Place chairs on the side of the table that faces the room.
- **Keep walking lanes at least 4 to 5 feet wide** as a rule of thumb, wider near the bar and restrooms.
- **Use columns as signs.** Drape or hang the menu, escort cards or a welcome message.

## Hard floors, echoes and cords

Concrete and brick floors make everything louder. Sound levels rise when voices bounce, so lounge furniture, rugs and fabric help soak up noise. Heavy linens do too. Guests also notice cold: lofts can be colder than comfort in a Chicago winter, so ask the owner about heat and whether the space has a coat area.

Floors can be uneven. Chair legs wobble on seams, so test a few seats before guests arrive, and place wider-footprint chairs where the floor is rough. The [bentwood bistro chair](/chairs/bentwood) and [cross-back chair](/chairs/cross-back) both pair well with industrial interiors, and the [velvet dining chair](/chairs/velvet-dining) adds softness.

## Lighting a loft

Lofts usually have big windows and harsh overhead lighting. Dimmable ambient light does more than any decor. String lights across a ceiling, uplights on a brick wall and candles on tables change the room. Our [string light layout guide](/blog/string-light-layouts-for-events) explains the spacing. Check where the outlets are before you plan anything electric.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many people fit in a Chicago loft for a party?

Divide usable square feet by 6 to 8 for a standing party or 10 to 12 for a seated dinner. Subtract columns, stairs and service areas first. The venue's posted capacity is the legal limit, so never exceed it.

### What furniture works best in an industrial loft?

Simple, sturdy pieces: bentwood or cross-back chairs, farm or round tables and low sofas for lounge zones. Soft pieces like velvet balance the hard surfaces. Avoid anything too delicate for concrete floors.

### How do I keep a loft party from echoing?

Add soft surfaces: rugs, drapes, upholstered lounge furniture and full-length linens. Keep the speaker away from bare brick, and place the DJ so sound projects toward the crowd.

### Do I need a freight elevator for a loft rental delivery?

Usually, if the loft is above the ground floor. Ask the owner or manager whether reservations are required, how big the elevator is and which hours are allowed.

### Can I put a tent or heater in a loft?

Tents are for outdoor use, and open-flame or propane heaters are often banned indoors. Ask the owner and building management before you bring any heat source.

### How early should I order rentals for a loft party?

Order once you have the date and the access details. Summer and December weekends fill up in Chicago, so request a quote as early as you can.
