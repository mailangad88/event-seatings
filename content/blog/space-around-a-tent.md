**Quick answer**

As a rule of thumb, leave about 2 to 3 feet of clear space around a frame tent for anchors and crew access, and 5 to 10 feet around a pole tent for stakes and ropes. Add a path wide enough for guests (at least 3 to 5 feet) and a service route for the caterer. Your tent company will give the exact working footprint for their equipment, and you should measure your yard against that number, not the tent size alone.

</div>

A tent is bigger than its dimensions. The 20 x 30 tent that fits your yard on paper may not fit once you add stakes, ropes, walkways and the truck. Yard size is the number one reason tent orders change after the site visit. Measure first, and you can avoid it.

## Clearance by tent type

<div class="table-wrap">

| Item | Rule of thumb | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Frame tent, each side | 2 to 3 ft | Anchors, ballast and crew access |
| Pole tent, each side | 5 to 10 ft | Stakes and ropes angle outward |
| Walkway for guests | 3 to 5 ft | Entry and exit paths |
| Service path for caterer | 4 to 6 ft | Carts and food carriers |
| Overhead | Peak height plus trees clear | Branches and wires |
| Building or fence | Ask the company | Fire code and drip lines |

</div>

These are rules of thumb, not code. Fire codes in many places also keep cooking and heating away from tents, often 20 feet for open flame and cooking. Winnetka's tent form, for instance, asks for generators and cooking to be 20 feet from the tent. See [permits in the suburbs](/blog/tent-permit-chicago-suburbs). Chicago groups tents closer than 12 feet apart for area, so two tents placed near each other can trigger a permit. See [Chicago tent permits](/blog/chicago-tent-permit).

## How to measure your yard

1. **Walk the flat area** with a tape or a measuring wheel. Slopes may need a floor; see [tent flooring](/blog/tent-flooring-and-subfloors).
2. **Mark obstacles:** trees, planters, AC units, gas meters, fire pits, play sets and utility boxes.
3. **Note overhead:** branches and power lines.
4. **Draw it on graph paper** at 1 square = 1 foot, or use a free app.
5. **Add the working space** from the table, around the tent.
6. **Check gates:** the crew needs to carry in frame pieces. Measure the gate width and any narrow side yards.
7. **Take photos** and send them to the tent company for a site check.

## Small Chicago yards

City lots are narrow. A typical Chicago lot is 25 feet wide, which is why many city tents go in alleys, garages or shared courtyards. We did not verify lot sizes for this guide, so measure your own. Options for tight sites:

- **A narrower tent,** like 15 x 30 or a 10 x 30 along a fence.
- **A frame tent with ballast** on the patio, where no stakes are needed. See [stakes vs ballast](/blog/tent-anchoring-stakes-vs-ballast).
- **A driveway or parking pad.** See [tent on a driveway](/blog/tent-on-driveway-or-parking-lot).
- **A smaller guest count in the tent** and a lawn area for cocktails.

## What about neighbors?

A tent on your side of the property line still affects the neighbor's view and noise. Check setbacks and HOA rules. See [HOA and neighbors](/blog/hoa-and-neighbors-tent-party).

## Seating that fits the space

If space is tight, choose slim chairs and fewer larger tables. [Cross-back chairs](/chairs/cross-back) and [garden folding chairs](/chairs/garden-folding) have narrow footprints. A 72-inch round seats 10 in less floor area than two 60-inch rounds. See [what size tent you need](/blog/what-size-tent-do-i-need).

## Frequently asked questions

### How much room do I need around a party tent?

About 2 to 3 feet around a frame tent and 5 to 10 feet around a pole tent, as a rule of thumb, plus walkways. Ask your tent company for the exact number.

### How big a yard do I need for a 20 x 30 tent?

A flat, open area of around 30 x 40 feet is a reasonable target. Frame tents may fit in less, and pole tents need more.

### Can a tent touch a fence or house?

Usually not. Fire codes and anchoring need space, and the tent company will advise. Do not attach a tent to a building.

### How far from the house should a tent be?

Ask your tent company and your village. Cooking and heating devices have separate distance rules, often around 20 feet for open flame.

### Do I need a site visit?

It is wise. Many tent companies visit or ask for photos and measurements, which prevents surprises on setup day.

### What if my yard is too small?

Choose a narrower tent, use hard surfaces with ballast, use a driveway, or hold cocktail hour outside and dinner in the tent.
