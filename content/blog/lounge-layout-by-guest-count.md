**Quick answer**

Plan one lounge set (a sofa, two chairs, a low table) for every 25 to 30 guests, plus one highboy cocktail table for every 8 to 10 guests. That works out to about 2 sets for 50 guests, 4 for 100, 5 to 6 for 150 and 7 to 8 for 200. Each set needs about 9 by 7 feet of floor, with 3-foot walkways between groups.

</div>

A lounge area is easy to over-build. Couples scroll through photos of sprawling sectionals and then find the real room is 40 by 60 feet with a dance floor, a bar and a buffet competing for the same square footage. This guide gives you a size-by-size chart and a drawing to start from, so you can see how much lounge a room can hold before you rent a single sofa.

## The sizing chart

<div class="table-wrap">

| Guests | Lounge sets | Seats in lounge | Highboy tables | Lounge floor space |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 50 | 2 | 12 to 16 | 5 to 6 | About 150 sq ft |
| 100 | 4 | 24 to 32 | 10 to 12 | About 300 sq ft |
| 150 | 5 to 6 | 30 to 48 | 15 to 18 | About 400 sq ft |
| 200 | 7 to 8 | 42 to 64 | 20 to 24 | About 550 sq ft |

</div>

Treat these as starting numbers. They assume cocktails and the lounge share a room with the bar, and that the dining tables are set up in the same event. Space estimates include the sets and their immediate walkways, not the bar or dance floor.

## How to scale for your crowd

Adjust the baseline for who is coming.

- **Older guest list or a long cocktail hour:** move toward the high end of the seat count.
- **Lots of young adults or a standing-reception vibe:** use fewer sofas, more highboys.
- **Corporate cocktail reception without dinner:** you may want seating for 40 to 50 percent of guests because they stay longer.
- **Winter event:** coats and boots take up more room at entrances, so cluster lounge sets away from the door.

## Four rules that make a layout work

**Keep a clear lane.** Guests should be able to walk from the entrance to the bar and from the bar to the dance floor without passing between two sofas. Six feet is a comfortable width for a main lane.

**Cluster, do not scatter.** Putting every set in its own corner makes the room feel sparse. Group two or three sets so the room looks full and guests meet each other.

**Mix heights.** Low sofas, medium accent chairs and tall highboys create layers that look good from the door and in photos.

**Keep the dance floor visible.** If the lounge hides the dance floor, guests will not realize the party is happening. Place sets facing it, not blocking it.

For floor space around dining, our guide on [how many chairs you need](/blog/how-many-chairs-do-i-need) shows how to count every seating area in one pass.

## Matching the lounge to the room shape

- **Long and narrow:** place sets in a row along the long wall, with highboys in the opposite half.
- **Square:** a central lounge island works, but only if the walkway all the way around stays above 3 feet.
- **L-shaped:** put the lounge in the short leg so it feels like its own room.
- **Tent:** place the sets inside the tent edge and away from the door flaps. See [outdoor lounge rentals](/blog/outdoor-lounge-rentals-chicago) for weather planning.

## Checking the plan with the venue

Before you finalize, ask the venue for the room's dimensions, its allowed capacity and any rules on fabric-covered furniture, open flame and wall placement. Banquet halls, hotels and park district buildings across the Chicago suburbs often have their own layout limits, so get their requirements in writing. A short call with the venue manager now is cheaper than moving furniture during setup.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much space does a lounge area need?

About 75 to 80 square feet per set, including a small walkway. A 100-guest event with 4 sets needs around 300 square feet. Add more if you want rugs, plants or a photo backdrop.

### How many sofas do I need for 100 guests?

Four sofas or settees, one per set, plus 8 accent chairs. That seats about 24 to 32 guests, which is the usual 25 to 35 percent.

### Can I put the lounge in a different room?

Yes. A separate lounge room works well for hotels and large venues, and it takes pressure off the main space. Make sure signs and staff guide guests, and give the lounge its own bar or drink station.

### Do highboy tables count as lounge furniture?

They are part of the cocktail setup, but they do not provide seating. Count them separately and place them near the bar and at the room entrance.

### How early should I plan the lounge layout?

When you book your venue and rental list. Sofas and special pieces come from limited inventory, and a layout also tells you how many linens and chairs you need.

### Should the lounge match the dining chairs?

You do not need to match exactly, but a shared color or material makes the room feel designed. For example, a [velvet dining chair](/chairs/velvet-dining) at dinner can echo a velvet settee in the lounge.
