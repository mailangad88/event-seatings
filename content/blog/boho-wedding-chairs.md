A boho wedding is built on texture: woven materials, natural wood, dried grasses, layered fabrics. The chair is one of the easiest places to get that texture right.

## Our top picks

- **[Rattan Garden Chair](/chairs/rattan-garden).** The signature boho piece. Honey tones suit earthy palettes, and a white wash suits airy ones.
- **[Cane-Back Chair](/chairs/cane-back).** A woven panel with a vintage feel, good for estate gardens and collected-looking tables.
- **[Wishbone Chair](/chairs/wishbone).** Clean lines with a hand-woven cord seat, ideal for a more modern take on boho.

## Styling tips

- **Layer natural materials.** Linen napkins, ceramic plates and wooden chargers all echo the woven chair.
- **Choose a palette of three.** Terracotta, cream and sage is a classic. The chairs supply the neutral.
- **Mix a little.** Two chair styles in related tones can look intentional and relaxed. Three or more usually looks chaotic.

## Practical notes

Woven chairs are lighter and less stackable than acrylic or metal ones, which affects delivery and setup time. They also prefer covered or tented spaces in wet weather. Ask us about both when you request a quote.
