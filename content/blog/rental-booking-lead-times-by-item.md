<div class="answer-box">

**Quick answer**

Book tents, restroom trailers and large dance floors first, as soon as the date and place are set, because they are limited and need site checks. Book specialty chairs and heaters early for busy weekends. Linens, bounce houses and basic tables and chairs can wait a little longer, but not past about 4 to 6 weeks for a peak date. These are rules of thumb. Your rental company sets the real deadline.

</div>

Not everything on your list has the same clock. A folding chair is easy to find. A 40 by 80 tent on a June Saturday in the suburbs is not. This guide sorts each item by how early to call, and explains why some are harder than others. It adds to our timelines for [chair rentals](/blog/when-to-book-event-chair-rentals-chicago) and [linens](/blog/how-far-ahead-to-order-linens).

We do not have a magic number, and no rental company publishes one for every date. What we can tell you is what makes an item hard to get: how many exist, how often people want them on the same day, and how much work is needed before delivery.

## Lead times by item

These are rules of thumb for a peak Saturday. Off-peak dates and weekdays are easier, and big or unusual orders take longer.

<div class="table-wrap">

| Item | Rule of thumb for peak dates | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Tents | As soon as the date and site are set; many hosts book months ahead | Limited sizes, site walk, stakes or weights, permit check |
| Restroom trailers | Early; same time as the tent | Few units, need water, power and a flat spot |
| Dance floors | A few months for large sizes | Sized to the room, installed by a crew |
| Specialty chairs | 3 to 6 months for a large count | Smaller inventory than basic chairs |
| Heaters (fall) | Weeks to a couple of months | Everyone wants them on the first cold weekend |
| Linens in a set color | 2 to 3 months for large counts | Colors run out; see the [linen timeline](/blog/how-far-ahead-to-order-linens) |
| Bounce houses | 1 to 2 months | Fewer units on peak weekends |
| Basic chairs and tables | 4 to 8 weeks | Larger stock, but still tight at peak |

## Item by item

**Tents.** A tent is not a drop-and-go item. The company has to look at the ground, the space around it, stakes or weights, power and sometimes a permit. Ask early, and read [the Chicago tent permit guide](/blog/chicago-tent-permit). For sizes, see the [tent size guides](/blog/30x60-tent-seating-capacity-and-layout).

**Restroom trailers.** These need a flat spot, access for a truck, and often water and power. Our [restroom trailer guide](/blog/restroom-trailer-when-you-need-one) covers when you need one. Book it with your tent, not after.

**Dance floors.** The floor is sized to your guest count and the room. Check [dance floor size by guest count](/blog/dance-floor-size-by-guest-count) so your request is clear. Larger floors need a crew and a clear loading path.

**Specialty chairs.** Velvet, gold, rattan and throne styles come in smaller quantities than folding chairs. If you want 150 of a specific style, ask early. You can see our [chairs](/rentals/chairs) and our [chair rentals](/chairs).

**Heaters.** From October through the first cold snaps, heaters sell out. We cover how many you need in [patio heaters per guest](/blog/patio-heaters-how-many-per-guests). Ask what fuel is included.

**Linens.** A specific color in a large count, like 20 round tablecloths, can run out. Order napkins at the same time so they match.

**Bounce houses.** Needs a flat space, power and an anchor plan. Read [bounce house safety and space](/blog/bounce-house-rental-safety-and-space). Book early for graduation season and summer weekends.

## Peak Chicago weekends

These dates tend to be busy. They are not a promise, just a pattern.

- **Graduation weekends** in late spring and early June.
- **Memorial Day, July 4th and Labor Day weekends.**
- **Wedding season Saturdays** from late spring through early fall. Fall weekends are popular because of the colors.
- **The last Sunday in June** around the Chicago Pride Parade, when downtown and the north side are busy.
- **December Fridays and Saturdays** for holiday parties, with Chicago's early sunsets around 4:20 to 4:30 pm.
- **New Year's Eve and the week of Thanksgiving**, when rental company hours and delivery times change.

Holiday weeks also change schedules. A company may close on the holiday, shift deliveries a day earlier, or charge a late or early fee. See [late-night and early pickup fees](/blog/late-night-and-early-pickup-fees-event-rentals).

## What most hosts find out too late

- **Rental companies don't hold dates without a deposit.** A verbal "yes" does not reserve it. Check [how deposits work](/blog/rental-deposits-explained).
- **You can often add items later but not always.** Extra chairs after your RSVPs are likely fine. A second tent or more of a specific color may not be. Order the hard-to-find items first and fill in the rest.
- **The date is not the same as the delivery day.** A Saturday event might mean a Friday delivery and a Monday pickup, and that day can matter for booking.

## What to do if you are late

Call anyway and ask what is available. Be flexible on the style or the color. Ask about a Friday or Sunday date, or a different delivery time. [How to save money on event rentals](/blog/how-to-save-money-on-event-rentals) has ideas on flexibility. For the last-minute items, a [Chicago winter planning guide](/blog/chicago-winter-event-planning) helps with the cold-weather list.

## Frequently asked questions

### How far in advance should I book party rentals?

For a peak weekend, book tents and restroom trailers as soon as the date and place are set, and chairs, tables and linens a few months ahead. For quiet dates, a month or less can be fine. Large counts and special styles need more time.

### How far ahead should I book a tent?

As early as you can once the date and location are firm. A tent needs a site check, a plan for stakes or weights and often a permit review, which takes time. Many hosts book tents several months out for summer and fall events.

### Can I book rentals the week of the party?

Sometimes, for small orders of common items, if the company has stock and a truck. You will have fewer choices and may pay rush fees. Heaters, tents and specialty chairs are the hardest to find at the last minute.

### When should I book patio heaters in Chicago?

Book as soon as you know your fall or winter date, since heaters go quickly on the first cold weekends. Also ask about fuel and spare tanks when you book.

### Which Chicago weekends fill up first?

Graduation weekends, Memorial Day, July 4th and Labor Day weekends, wedding season Saturdays and December holiday Fridays and Saturdays. Check your rental company for the date you want.

### Do I need to book linens as early as chairs?

Often, a little later, but not too late. A specific color in a large count can sell out, so order a few months ahead for a peak date.
