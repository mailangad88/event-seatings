**Quick answer**

Each Chicago suburb sets its own tent rules, and the permit is usually handled by the village's building or fire department. Many start at 400 square feet: Oak Park requires a permit for tents over 400 square feet, and Winnetka asks for one for tents with sides over 400 square feet or open-sided tents over 700 square feet. Naperville has a dedicated tent permit page. Call your village's building department at least two weeks before your event, and ask your tent company to confirm who files.

</div>

Hosting a party in Oak Brook, Oak Park or Naperville means the City of Chicago's rules do not apply. Instead your village or city has its own code, often based on the International Fire Code, and its own forms. That is good news if you know where to look and a surprise if you do not. This guide shows real examples we checked, then gives you a process for your own town. Policies change, so always confirm with the municipality.

## What most suburban tent permits ask for

From the forms and guidelines reviewed, these are typical:

- **Tent size and site plan.** Oak Park, for instance, wants a site plan showing stakes, poles and cables and how the public is protected.
- **A flame-resistance certificate.** Oak Park asks for a cut sheet showing fire resistance, flame spread and smoke data. Winnetka's form asks for the manufacturer's fire resistance certificate.
- **Anchoring.** Winnetka's form says to use all anchor points, and that stakes and guy wires must not block exits.
- **Fire extinguishers and separation.** Winnetka's form lists one 5 lb ABC extinguisher per 1,500 square feet of floor space and 20 feet between open flames, cooking and the tent.
- **Heaters.** Winnetka prohibits portable outdoor gas-fired heaters inside the tent, and keeps them 5 feet away from the tent and exits. Electric heaters must be listed and plugged directly in. See [heating a tent in fall](/blog/heating-a-tent-in-fall).
- **Inspection.** Oak Park notes nothing should be concealed until a passing inspection is issued, so leave panels and anchors visible.

## How to find your village's rules

<ol class="flow">
<li><strong>Search</strong> the village website for "tent permit" or "temporary structure".</li>
<li><strong>Call</strong> the building department and the fire department. In some towns the fire department inspects.</li>
<li><strong>Ask the thresholds:</strong> square feet with and without sides, and height.</li>
<li><strong>Ask the lead time</strong> and whether setup can happen before the permit is issued.</li>
<li><strong>Ask who files:</strong> homeowner, tent company or caterer.</li>
<li><strong>Get it in writing</strong> and share it with your vendors.</li>
</ol>

## Special events and parks

If your event is in a park district facility, a forest preserve or a village-owned space, tent rules often sit on top of the facility's own permit. Naperville's page notes that special events can take Council approval and sometimes extra permits. Ask the park district or village event office before you pay a deposit. For the city side, read [Chicago tent permits](/blog/chicago-tent-permit). For venues, see our [Chicago venue rules for chair rentals](/blog/topic/chicago-planning).

## Common mistakes

- **Assuming the tent company handles it.** Some do and some do not. Ask in writing.
- **Counting only one tent.** Many codes combine tents placed close together. Chicago, for example, groups tents within 12 feet.
- **Planning a heater or grill under the tent.** These are the items most likely to fail an inspection.
- **Waiting too long.** Peak June and September weekends book up, and permit review takes time.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a permit for a backyard tent in the suburbs?

Often yes if the tent is over about 400 square feet, but it depends on the village. Oak Park and Winnetka both use 400 square feet as a starting point. Call your building department to confirm.

### How far ahead should I apply?

Plan on at least two weeks. Winnetka's form asks for 7 days before setup, Chicago triples its fee under 14 days, and Naperville special events can take much longer. Earlier is safer.

### Who handles the inspection?

It depends on the municipality. In some towns the building department inspects, in others the fire department. Oak Park directs people to call its inspection line to schedule.

### Can I use a propane heater under the tent?

Probably not. Winnetka's form prohibits portable outdoor gas-fired heaters inside the tent and many codes restrict open flame. Ask your village before you rent one.

### Do I need a permit for a 10 x 10 pop-up?

Usually not, because a 10 x 10 is 100 square feet, well below typical thresholds. Check if you are placing it on a public sidewalk or street, which can need separate approval.

### What if my tent crosses onto the public way?

Oak Park, for one, requires a permit for anything placed in the village right-of-way. Ask before you plan a tent over a sidewalk or in the street.
