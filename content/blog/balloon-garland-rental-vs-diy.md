**Quick answer**

A 5-foot garland suits a table, 8 to 10 feet suits a photo backdrop and 15 to 20 feet suits an arch or full wall. DIY saves money but takes an evening, a balloon artist saves time, and a frame rental works with your own balloons. Heat, wind and venue rules limit outdoor use.

A balloon garland is a long, organic line of balloons in mixed sizes, usually tied to a frame or tape. Rentals and installs have become common for birthdays, baby showers, graduations and quinceaneras around Chicago. The question is whether to rent or install, buy and DIY, and how long a garland you actually need.

## Rent, hire or DIY?

<div class="table-wrap">

| Option | What you get | Best for | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| DIY kit | Balloons, tape or string, you do the work | Small garlands, tight budgets | Time, tied fingers, uneven look |
| Balloon artist install | Designed and installed on site | Weddings, large backdrops | Needs lead time |
| Frame rental | Metal arch or stand only | Pairing with your own balloons | Balloons still needed |
| Foam or fabric garland | Reusable alternative | Eco-friendly events | Fewer color choices |

</div>

The actual cost depends on length, balloon count, colors, delivery and install. We do not publish prices for balloons, so request a quote from the balloon artist or rental company.

## How long should a garland be?

- **Table or cake backdrop:** 5 feet.
- **Photo backdrop:** 8 to 10 feet.
- **Arch over an entrance:** 10 to 20 feet depending on the width.
- **Full wall:** length of the wall, often 15 to 20 feet or more.

A rule many balloon artists use is that a 5-foot garland takes around 100 to 150 balloons in a mix of sizes, but this varies with style, so ask for a count in your quote.

## Weather and venue limits

Balloons hate heat and wind. Direct sun can make latex pop or fade, and cold can shrink it. In Chicago summer afternoons, an outdoor garland may need shade. In winter, balloons moved from a cold truck into a warm room expand and can pop. Many venues restrict balloons or helium, and parks may ban them for environmental or safety reasons. Never release balloons outdoors, since loose balloons become litter and can harm wildlife, and check with your venue and local park district about their rules.

## DIY tips if you go that route

Use a strip or a fishing line base, inflate balloons to varied sizes with a pump, and cluster them in fours before attaching. Start from the center and work out. Allow a full evening. Buy extra of each size because some will pop. If you plan a large arch, build the frame first and test it in the room where it will stand.

## Eco and alternatives

Latex is biodegradable, but foil balloons are not, and loose balloons can harm wildlife. Many venues prefer garlands that stay inside. Paper, fabric and foam alternatives are reusable. A [flower wall](/blog/flower-wall-rental-guide) or [pipe and drape](/blog/pipe-and-drape-uses-and-sizes) can give a similar backdrop without balloons.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Installing hours early in a hot room.
- Ordering a garland shorter than the backdrop.
- Using tape the venue forbids.
- Releasing balloons outside.


## Frequently asked questions

### How long should a balloon garland be?

Five feet for a table, 8 to 10 feet for a backdrop and 15 to 20 feet for an arch or wall. Match the length to the space it must fill.

### Is a balloon garland cheaper to DIY?

Usually the materials are cheaper, but it takes time and skill. A balloon artist saves time and gives a more finished look. Request quotes to compare.

### How far ahead should I install a balloon garland?

As close to the event as possible. Hot rooms and direct sun shorten how long balloons last.

### Can balloon garlands be outdoors?

Yes in shade with little wind. Direct sun and wind can pop or fade them. Check venue and park rules.

### Do venues allow balloons?

Many do but some restrict helium, tape or outdoor use. Ask your venue before you order.

### What is the best alternative to balloons?

Flower walls, pipe and drape, paper garlands or foam garlands give a similar backdrop and last longer.
