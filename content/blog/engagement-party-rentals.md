**Quick answer**

An engagement party can be standing cocktail style, a seated dinner or a casual backyard buffet. For a cocktail party, rent 1 highboy per 8 to 10 guests and seats for a third of the crowd. For a seated dinner, rent one chair per guest and a table per 8 to 10. Add a bar, a gift or card table and a photo spot, and choose a look lighter than the wedding.

Engagement parties come in more shapes than weddings: a rooftop cocktail hour in Chicago, a backyard barbecue in the suburbs, a family dinner in a banquet room or a house party. The right rental list depends on which one you pick, not on a single formula. This guide covers the three formats and what each one needs.

## Cocktail-style party

Best for 40 to 100 guests and 2 to 3 hours. Guests stand, so the room feels fuller and you need less furniture.

- **Highboys:** 1 per 8 to 10 guests, floor-length covered. See [how many cocktail tables](/blog/how-many-cocktail-tables-for-standing-reception).
- **Seats:** lounge pieces and bistro chairs for about a third of guests. See [lounge layouts by guest count](/blog/lounge-layout-by-guest-count).
- **Bar:** a bar front, glassware, ice tubs and napkins. Count with [how many glasses per guest](/blog/how-many-glasses-per-guest).
- **Food:** passed appetizers need small plates and napkins; a grazing table needs a 6- or 8-foot banquet table and a floor-length cloth.
- **Extras:** a gift or card table, a photo spot, a slideshow screen and a guest book.

Plan 6 to 8 square feet per guest. A 60-guest party fits comfortably in 400 to 500 square feet.

## Seated dinner

Best for 20 to 60 guests who know each other. Use 60-inch rounds of 8 or a long table.

- **Chairs:** one per guest. A lighter chair than your wedding style suits this occasion; try [bentwood](/chairs/bentwood) or [rattan](/chairs/rattan-garden).
- **Tables:** see [round table sizes](/blog/round-table-sizes-and-seating-48-60-66-72-inch).
- **Linens and dinnerware:** lap-length linens and simple plates. See [dinnerware sizes](/blog/dinnerware-sizes-buffet-plated-family-style).
- **A sweetheart table or seat of honor** if you plan toasts. See [sweetheart table ideas](/blog/sweetheart-table-chair-ideas).

## Backyard buffet

Best for 30 to 80 guests. This is the most flexible option and the one with the most logistics.

- **Seats and tables:** garden folding chairs and long banquet tables. See [banquet table sizes](/blog/banquet-table-sizes-6-ft-vs-8-ft).
- **Shade and lighting:** a tent or market umbrellas, string lights. See [backyard party lighting](/blog/backyard-party-lighting-ideas).
- **Buffet equipment:** chafing dishes, serving utensils, drink dispensers. See [how many chafing dishes](/blog/how-many-chafing-dishes-for-a-buffet).
- **Restrooms and trash:** plan them early. The [backyard wedding rental checklist](/blog/backyard-wedding-rental-checklist) has a full site list.

## Do not overbuild

An engagement party is not the wedding. A good rule is to keep rentals to what guests will actually use: a place to put a drink, a seat to rest and a spot for photos. Skip fancy chair covers, extra table decor and a full dance floor. Spend instead on food, a toast and a bar, and book rentals for one delivery and one pickup.

## Make room for the toast

Every engagement party has a toast, and the toast decides the layout. Leave a clear spot with a microphone where everyone can see, and keep the bar from blocking it. For a standing party, cluster the highboys around the edge and leave the center open. For a seated dinner, put the couple's seat where the toast giver can face the room. A small table with champagne flutes ready to pour saves the last-minute scramble; count one flute per adult guest and see [champagne flutes vs coupes](/blog/champagne-flutes-vs-coupes) if you are choosing between glass shapes.

Finally, plan where gifts go. Many engagement parties include a table for cards, and a locked box or a spot staffed by a family member is wise at larger parties.

## Timing in the Chicago area

Engagement parties often run 3 to 6 months before the wedding, in spring and summer when outdoor spaces and rooftops are open, and around holidays when families gather. Rooftops and downtown venues have load-in rules, so ask the building about elevator windows, and in the suburbs ask your village about noise and tents.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many chairs do I need for an engagement party?

For a cocktail party, seats for a third of guests. For a dinner or buffet, one chair per guest. Add one or two spares.

### What tables do I need?

Highboys for a standing party, rounds for a seated dinner, long tables for a buffet. Add a bar, gift and cake or dessert table.

### How many guests come to an engagement party?

It ranges from 20 to 100 or more depending on the families. Many couples invite both families, close friends and the wedding party.

### Do I need a tent?

Only if the party is outdoors and the weather is uncertain, or if you want a defined space. Small tents and market umbrellas help at smaller parties.

### Should engagement party decor match the wedding?

No. Use it as a preview of your colors, but keep it lighter and more casual.

### How far ahead should I book rentals?

Two to four weeks is typical for small parties, longer for peak months. Book as soon as you set the date and venue.
