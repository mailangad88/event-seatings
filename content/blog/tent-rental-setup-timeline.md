**Quick answer**

Book your tent as soon as you know your date, and at least 3 to 6 months ahead for a summer weekend. File the permit at least 14 days before setup (Chicago triples the fee after that), call JULIE (811) at least 2 working days before any stakes, and expect the tent to go up one to two days before your event. Takedown is usually the day after. Larger tents and floors take longer.

</div>

Tent rentals run on a backward calendar. The event date is fixed, and everything else, from the permit to the crew's arrival, counts down from it. Missing one step means a smaller tent, a rush fee or no tent at all. This is a timeline you can paste into your phone.

## Step by step

### 3 to 6 months out: book

Tent companies sell out on peak weekends. In the Chicago area those are June, September and early October Saturdays. Book once your date and site are set, and ask about permit handling, flooring and sidewalls in the same call. See [when to book event rentals](/blog/topic/budget-and-booking). Lock the size early with [what size tent do I need](/blog/what-size-tent-do-i-need).

### 6 to 8 weeks out: check the site and permit

Ask your village what it needs. In the examples we reviewed, Winnetka's form asks for 7 days notice, Oak Park requires electronic application with setup and teardown dates, and Chicago triples fees under 14 days. Start sooner than the minimum. See [Chicago tent permits](/blog/chicago-tent-permit) and [suburban permits](/blog/tent-permit-chicago-suburbs). Also check HOA rules: [HOA and neighbors](/blog/hoa-and-neighbors-tent-party).

### 2 to 4 weeks out: utility locate and details

Illinois law asks you to contact JULIE. According to 811beforeyoudig.com, notice is two working days (excluding weekends) and no more than 10 calendar days before digging. Tickets last 25 days. Ask your tent company whether they request the locate. Details in the [JULIE guide](/blog/julie-utility-locate-tent-illinois). Confirm flooring, lighting, heating or cooling, and sidewalls.

### 1 week out: confirm

Reconfirm the delivery window, gate access, parking for the truck and who will be on site to meet the crew. Mark sprinklers and fragile spots. Book tables and chairs for the same delivery day; see [our blog](/blog) for delivery planning.

### 1 to 2 days before: setup

Crews typically install the tent a day or two before to allow time for the floor, sidewalls, lighting and inspections. Larger tents and floors can take more days. If you need an inspection, schedule it before guests arrive.

### Event day

The tent should be done. Do a final walk: anchors, exits, power, lighting, heater and cords. Have the crew's phone number handy.

### Day after: takedown

Most companies remove the tent the day after, but some do it the same night for small tents. Ask in advance. If you rent the tent over a weekend, the pickup is often Monday.

## Common timeline mistakes

- **Booking tables and chairs separately for a different day.** Match delivery with the tent crew so the floor is ready.
- **Forgetting JULIE.** Staking without a locate puts utilities at risk and can cost you.
- **Skipping a rain plan.** See [rain plan with a tent](/blog/rain-plan-with-a-tent).
- **Booking the same day as a major event.** Marathon weekends and large festivals pull crews. Ask about conflicts.
- **Ignoring the lawn.** Tents left up for days leave marks. See [tent and grass damage](/blog/tent-and-grass-damage).

## Chairs on the same calendar

Delivery of chairs, tables and linens usually happens after the tent and floor are ready. [Garden folding chairs](/chairs/garden-folding) and [cross-back chairs](/chairs/cross-back) can be scheduled for the day of or the day before, depending on your venue. Ask for a time window that matches the tent crew.

## Frequently asked questions

### How far in advance should I book a tent?

For June, September and early October weekends, book 3 to 6 months ahead. Off-season dates may be available with a few weeks of notice.

### When is a tent set up?

Usually one to two days before the event so floors, lighting and inspections are done. Large tents take longer.

### How long before an event do I need the permit?

At least 14 days in Chicago to avoid the tripled fee. Suburban lead times vary, so check your village.

### Who calls JULIE before a tent is staked?

It varies. Ask your tent company if they request the locate and get that in writing. Homeowners can file their own request at illinois1call.com.

### When is a tent taken down?

Usually the day after. Some companies pick up the same night or the next business day. Confirm in your contract.

### Can I get a tent with less than a week's notice?

Sometimes, in the off-season. Expect fewer size choices, possible rush fees and trouble with permits. Call several companies.
