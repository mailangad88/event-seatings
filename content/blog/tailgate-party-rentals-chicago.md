**Quick answer**

For a fall tailgate, rent a 10 by 10 canopy with weights, a couple of folding tables, folding chairs and a heater for the cold, and check the lot's rules before you book. Many lots limit canopy size, grills, propane and the time you can arrive. Ask the lot or team, school or park district what is allowed, because the rules differ from lot to lot and game to game.

A good tailgate in Chicago in October or November is a cold-weather party in a parking lot. Wind comes off the lake, the sun is gone early in the evening, and the cars next to you are closer than you think. Whether you are heading to a Bears game, a college game or a high school Friday night, the setup is the same: a place to sit, a place to cook, and a way to stay warm. This guide covers the rentals and the rules, so the day goes the way you planned.

## Check the lot rules first

This is the part most people skip. Each lot, stadium, school and park district has its own rules, and they can change by game. Check with the team, school, park district or lot operator before you book. Ask:

- **Canopy size and number.** Some lots limit the size or require that tents stay inside the space.
- **Grills and propane.** Are charcoal and gas grills allowed? Is open flame or propane allowed under a canopy?
- **Arrival and departure times.** When does the lot open, and when must you leave?
- **Delivery access.** Can a rental truck get into the lot at all? Many can't.
- **Alcohol and glass.** Rules on drinks and glass differ by place, so check before you rent glassware.
- **Generators and power.** Some lots don't allow them.
- **Space size.** A standard parking space is smaller than you expect, and a setup for 20 people needs more.

## Grills

If the lot allows grills, keep them clear of the canopy, the vehicles and your guests. A grill under a canopy is a fire risk, because the fabric can catch from heat and sparks. Keep a bucket of water or a fire extinguisher nearby, and never leave it alone. Check the manual and the lot rules for charcoal, ash disposal and propane tank storage. [Grill rental for events](/blog/grill-rental-for-events) goes through what to ask a company. ## Cold-weather tailgating

<ol class="flow">
<li><strong>Pick the right heat.</strong> A patio heater needs open space around it, and propane rules for lots vary. Read [patio heater propane](/blog/patio-heater-propane-how-much-and-who-supplies).</li>
<li><strong>Block the wind.</strong> Park so the vehicle is on the windward side, and use canopy side walls if the lot allows.</li>
<li><strong>Feed people hot.</strong> Chili, soup and coffee beat cold sandwiches. A slow cooker needs power, so check before you plan it.</li>
<li><strong>Keep drinks from freezing.</strong> Coolers keep cold things cold, but in a Chicago November they can also keep them from freezing.</li>
<li><strong>Plan for dark.</strong> In late fall the sun goes down early. Bring lights and a way to pack up safely.</li>
</ol>

## What most hosts find out too late

- **Canopy weights matter more than the canopy.** A pop-up canopy catches wind like a sail. Stakes don't work on pavement. Use weights on every leg. See [pop-up canopy vs rented tent](/blog/pop-up-canopy-vs-rented-tent).
- **Rental trucks can't always reach your spot.** A closed lot, traffic and gates may stop a drop-off at your space. Ask about will call or a meeting point.
- **A grill and a canopy are rarely a good mix.** Keep fire away from fabric.

## Frequently asked questions

### What do I need for a tailgate party?

A canopy with weights, a table or two, chairs, a cooler and a grill if allowed. In the cold, add a heater, a hot drink station and lights. Check the lot rules on grills and canopies first.

### Can I rent a canopy for a tailgate in Chicago?

Yes, many rental companies offer 10 by 10 canopies, often with weights. Ask whether delivery to a lot is possible, or pick it up yourself. Check that the lot allows a canopy of that size.

### Are grills allowed at Bears tailgates?

It depends on the lot and the day, so check with the stadium or the lot operator. Rules about charcoal, propane and open flame can change. Check before you bring or rent a grill.

### How do I keep warm at a November tailgate?

Dress in layers, use a heater if allowed, block the wind with your vehicle and canopy walls, and serve hot food. Keep heaters away from fabric and follow the fuel rules.

### How many chairs and tables do I need for a tailgate?

One chair per person, plus a few spares, and one 6 ft table for food and one for drinks or games. A group of 20 needs more space than most parking spots offer.

### Can a rental truck deliver to a stadium lot?

Sometimes, but often not. Lots can be closed to trucks or crowded on game day. Ask the lot and the rental company, or plan to pick up your items yourself.
