**Quick answer**

Winter weddings in Chicago move indoors, so plan seating around the venue's floor plan, coat check and warming areas. Velvet and upholstered chairs feel cozy in cold months, deliveries need clear paths through snow, and booking 2 to 4 months ahead is usually enough outside the holiday weeks.

</div>


In winter, guests arrive in coats and linger indoors. The room has to feel warm and generous, and seating is a big part of that.

## Why velvet works

Velvet reads as warm to the eye and the hand. Emerald, navy, burgundy and deep blush all suit candlelit winter rooms, and an upholstered seat is comfortable through a long dinner.

## Pairing ideas

- **[Velvet Dining Chair](/chairs/velvet-dining) with brass candlesticks.** Rich, warm and photogenic.
- **[Louis Medallion Chair](/chairs/louis-medallion) at the head table.** A formal accent among simpler guest chairs.
- **[Gold Infinity Chair](/chairs/infinity) in a ballroom.** Metallic warmth that picks up candlelight.

## Practical points

- Plan for coats. A chair-back drape can look stylish, but ask your planner about a coat check, since piles of coats on velvet chairs can mark the fabric.
- Upholstered chairs are indoor-only pieces. If any part of your event is outside, plan a different chair for it.

## Frequently asked questions

### What chairs suit a winter wedding?

Velvet, upholstered and dark wood chairs feel warm. Gold Chiavari chairs with rich linens also suit a candlelit winter ballroom.

### Does snow affect chair delivery?

It can. Tell us about stairs, ramps and parking so the crew can plan, and make sure paths are cleared on delivery day.

### Is winter cheaper for event rentals?

Often. Winter weekdays and weekends outside the holidays are less busy, so availability is better and some partners offer lower minimums.
