## 9 to 12 months out: peak dates

Chicago's busiest wedding months run from late spring through fall, with Saturdays most in demand. If your date is a summer or fall Saturday, or you want a specialty style like [velvet dining chairs](/chairs/velvet-dining) or a [Louis medallion](/chairs/louis-medallion) for a large guest list:

- Shortlist two or three styles
- Get a quote with your estimated count
- Ask how long a quote can be held and what deposit secures it

## 4 to 6 months out: most events

Corporate events, showers, milestone birthdays and off-season weddings usually have more flexibility. This is a good time to book and finalize your floor plan with the venue.

## 6 to 8 weeks out: final count

Most rental companies set a date after which counts can only go up if stock allows. Before that point:

- Confirm RSVPs and add vendor seats and a small buffer
- Confirm the layout: rounds or long tables, ceremony flip or separate set
- Send the final venue details: load-in window, dock, COI

The [free seating planner](/seating-planner) does the counting for you.

## 1 to 2 weeks out: last-minute

Classic styles are often still available on short notice, especially on weekdays and in the off-season. Specialty chairs in large counts are harder. Send your date, count and venue address right away. See our [last-minute chair rental guide](/blog/last-minute-chair-rental-chicago).

## Frequently asked questions

### How far in advance should I book wedding chairs in Chicago?
For a Saturday wedding from June through October, start 9 to 12 months out. Off-season and weekday events are usually fine at 4 to 6 months.

### When do I need to give a final chair count?
Most rental companies ask for a final count a few weeks before the event. Plan to confirm yours about 6 to 8 weeks out so you have time to adjust.

### Can I hold a quote without paying?
Policies vary. Some companies hold a quote for a short time; most require a deposit to reserve inventory. Ask before you commit.
