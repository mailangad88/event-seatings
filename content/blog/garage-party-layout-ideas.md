**Quick answer**

A standard two-car garage is about 20 x 20 to 24 x 24 feet, which fits 24 to 36 seated guests at three or four round tables plus a bar and a food table. Open the door, extend onto the driveway with a canopy and clear every vehicle, tool and chemical first. In Chicago weather, plan heat and rain cover.

Garage parties are a Chicago tradition: graduations, birthdays, memorial lunches and neighborhood block parties often take place in a garage and driveway because it is dry, close to the kitchen and easy to clean. With the right layout, it can look surprisingly good. Without one, it feels like a garage with chairs.

## Clear the garage first

Before layout, empty the garage as much as possible. Cars, bikes and storage can move to the street or a neighbor's driveway, if rules allow. Put tools, paint, gas cans and chemicals out of reach. Cover shelves with a drape. A garage that looks tidy feels more like a party.

<ol class="flow">
<li><strong>Clear the floor.</strong> Cars out, storage along one wall at most.</li>
<li><strong>Check the power.</strong> Garage outlets are usually on a single circuit; avoid overloading with heaters and coolers.</li>
<li><strong>Open the door.</strong> Leave the door fully open when people are inside; it ventilates the space.</li>
<li><strong>Set the tables.</strong> Rounds in the center or banquets along the walls.</li>
<li><strong>Extend to the driveway.</strong> Canopy, cocktail tables and a lounge for overflow.</li>
</ol>

Do not run vehicles, generators or charcoal grills in or near a closed garage. Carbon monoxide builds up quickly. Place any generator well away from the door; see [quiet generator placement](/blog/quiet-generator-placement-events).

## Making a garage feel like a party room

The garage floor is hard, cold and usually stained, so cover it. Outdoor rugs or a rented dance floor panel make a clean, level surface, and floor-length tablecloths hide table legs and cords. Light is the second problem: overhead fluorescents are harsh. Turn them off if you can and hang warm string lights or battery lanterns. Check that no lights or cords hang low enough for a tall guest to hit.

Sound carries in a concrete room, so a small speaker is usually enough. Keep the bar and food separate to avoid a bottleneck at the open door, and put the trash and recycling bins outside the garage. Think about the restroom path too: guests will use the house, so set a clear route from the garage door to the bathroom and put a sign on the door to the rest of the house if you want to keep it private.

## Seating capacity

| Layout | Seated | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 3 rounds of 8 (60 in) | 24 | Garage only |
| 4 rounds of 8 | 32 | Tight; bar on the driveway |
| 2 banquets 8 ft + 2 rounds | 28 to 32 | Better for a buffet |
| Garage + 20x20 canopy | 40 to 50 | Dinner in the garage, drinks outside |

Plan 12 to 15 square feet per seated guest; our [square feet per guest](/blog/square-feet-per-guest-by-event-style) chart covers other setups.

## Make it look intentional

A few touches change a garage from storage to a party space. Hang drape or fabric on the back wall, add string lights across the ceiling and put an outdoor rug under the tables. A simple linen on each table, like a floor-length cloth, hides the concrete and grease stains. See [tablecloth size chart](/blog/tablecloth-size-chart). Tape down cords. A few plants or lanterns at the entrance help.

## Weather and heat

In a Chicago winter, a garage is cold, even with the door closed. A propane or electric heater helps, but heaters need proper clearance, ventilation and power; check the manufacturer's instructions. In summer, an open door and a couple of fans are usually enough. See [patio heaters: how many per guests](/blog/patio-heaters-how-many-per-guests) and [fans and coolers for summer events](/blog/fans-and-coolers-for-summer-events).

## Parking, noise and neighbors

A driveway party puts cars on the street. Check your street's parking rules, and tell neighbors in advance. Keep amplified music low after dark. For larger parties, see [hoa and neighbors tent party](/blog/hoa-and-neighbors-tent-party).

## Chairs for a garage party

Hard floors and tight spaces call for compact, easy-to-move chairs. A [garden folding chair](/chairs/garden-folding) is the practical choice; a [bentwood bistro chair](/chairs/bentwood) makes the garage feel like a bistro. ## Frequently asked questions

### How many people fit in a two-car garage party?

About 24 to 36 seated, or 40 to 50 standing, depending on whether you add a bar and a food table.

### How do I decorate a garage for a party?

Clear it first, hang fabric on the back wall, add string lights and cover the floor and tables with rugs and floor-length cloths.

### Is it safe to use a heater in a garage?

Only if it is designed for the space. Follow the manufacturer's instructions, keep clearances and never use fuel burners in a closed garage.

### Can I use the driveway for a party?

Usually yes for a private party, but check parking and noise rules. Tents may need approval.

### What tables are best for a garage?

60-inch rounds or 6-foot banquets. Folding tables in 30-inch width fit side by side along walls.

### How do I keep a garage party dry?

The garage roof helps. For the driveway, use a canopy or tent with sidewalls and a rain plan.
