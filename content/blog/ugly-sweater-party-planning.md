**Quick answer**

For an ugly sweater party, plan for mostly standing with seats for about half your guests, a small stage or spot for the sweater contest, a photo backdrop with good light, and a coat area near the door. A 40-guest party needs about 20 chairs, 6 to 8 cocktail tables, a 6-foot food table, a drink station and 10 to 12 feet of space in front of the contest area.

</div>

Ugly sweater parties are easy to throw and easy to crowd. Everyone arrives in a bulky, itchy, loud sweater, in a Chicago December with coats, boots and scarves. Then there is a contest, photos and a lot of laughing. The layout decides whether it feels fun or jammed. This guide covers the room plan, the contest and photo spots, how much to stand or sit, and where to put 40 coats. For other holiday party ideas, see [December holiday party rentals in Chicago](/blog/december-holiday-party-rentals-chicago) and [holiday party at home rentals](/blog/holiday-party-at-home-rentals-chicago).

## The room plan

Think in four zones: the door (coats), the food and drink, the mingling area, and the contest and photos. Put the contest at the far end so the party moves through the room.

## Standing or seated

An ugly sweater party is a mingling party. People walk around, show their sweaters and refill drinks. If you seat everyone at tables, they stay put. If everyone stands, your older guests and anyone in heels are tired in an hour. A mix works best.

## The contest area

You need a spot where everyone can see the sweaters. It does not need to be a big stage.

- **A small riser** or even a rug and a taped square on the floor.
- **10 to 12 feet** of open space in front of it, with no tables in the sightline.
- **A microphone** if the room has more than 30 guests or music. See [AV and sound](/rentals/av-sound).
- **Categories** such as ugliest, most lights, best homemade and most tacky. Hand out paper ballots or have a clap vote.
- **A prize table** with a small tablecloth. Keep it away from the food.

If you want a real stage, check sizes in [stage and riser sizes](/blog/stage-and-riser-sizes-for-events). A 4 x 8 foot riser is common. Ask what your rental company has.

## The photo area

Photos are half the party. A good spot is a plain wall or backdrop with a bright, even light from the front, not a window behind. A string of lights, a garland and a few props work well. Put it near, but not in, the line for the food.

- **Backdrop.** Choose a solid color or a simple holiday look. Busy patterns fight with loud sweaters. See [photo backdrop ideas](/blog/photo-backdrop-ideas-for-weddings-and-parties) and [selfie station ideas](/blog/selfie-station-ideas-for-events).
- **Light.** A ring light or two lamps at face height. See [lighting rentals](/rentals/lighting) and [lighting for night photos](/blog/lighting-for-night-event-photos).
- **Space.** 6 feet of floor in front for groups. Mark it with tape.
- **A photo booth** can work at a larger party. See [photo booth rentals](/rentals/photo-booths).

## Coat space

In Chicago, from December to March, guests arrive in heavy coats and boots. That is an extra 40 coats at a party of 40, and a pile on a bed is a problem when people leave.

- Plan one **rolling coat rack** for every 25 to 30 guests as a rule of thumb. Ask the rental company how many hangers each one holds.
- Put the rack by the door but out of the main path.
- Set a **boot tray** or mat, and towels for the floor.
- Use a numbered ticket system if you have a coat check volunteer.

See [coat racks for winter events in Chicago](/blog/coat-racks-for-winter-events-chicago).

## What most hosts find out too late

- **Sweaters make people hot.** A room full of wool and a crowd can heat up fast. Keep a window or a thermostat in mind, and put water out.
- **Food and drinks near the contest.** Lines clog the open floor. Put food on the opposite wall.
- **The dark.** Sunset in Chicago is around 4:20 to 4:30 pm in December, so guests arrive in the dark. Light the entry and the path.
- **Peak December weekends fill up.** Ask for tables and chairs early. See [Chicago winter holiday party season booking](/blog/chicago-winter-holiday-party-season-booking).

## Frequently asked questions

### How many chairs do I need for an ugly sweater party?

About half your guest count. For 40 guests, 20 chairs is a good rule of thumb. Add a few more if you have older guests or plan to serve a meal.

### How do I set up an ugly sweater contest?

Pick a spot with 10 to 12 feet of open floor and a plain background. Use categories and paper ballots. Keep it short, about 10 to 15 minutes.

### Where should the photo area go?

Near the party but away from the food line, with a plain wall and bright light on the face. Leave 6 feet of floor in front for groups.

### How many coat racks do I need?

One rolling rack for every 25 to 30 guests is a rule of thumb. Put it near the door, out of the main path. Add a boot tray for snow.

### Is an ugly sweater party standing or seated?

Mostly standing, with seats for about half the guests. That keeps people moving and gives tired guests a place to sit.

### How much space do I need for 40 guests?

Plan about 10 to 15 square feet per guest for a cocktail-style party, so 400 to 600 square feet. See [square feet per guest](/blog/square-feet-per-guest-by-event-style).
