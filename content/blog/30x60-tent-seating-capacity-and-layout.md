**Quick answer**

A 30x60 tent is 1,800 square feet. It seats about 80 to 100 guests at rounds with a dance floor, 110 to 130 at rounds with no dance floor, 120 to 150 standing, or about 180 in theater rows. Plan for 14 to 18 square feet per seated guest and ask your tent company for a drawing.

</div>

The 30x60 is the workhorse tent for a backyard wedding, a large graduation party or a church picnic in the Chicago suburbs. It has room for tables, a dance floor and a bar, and most of the time it fits on a lot that can handle a 30 x 60 plus setup space. Here is how to use it.

## What goes where

<ol class="flow">
<li><strong>Entrance on a short end.</strong> Welcome table and seating chart outside the door.</li>
<li><strong>Tables on the long axis.</strong> Rows along the long sides leave the center aisle.</li>
<li><strong>Dance floor at the far end.</strong> Away from the entrance, near the DJ.</li>
<li><strong>Bar and DJ outside or at the edge.</strong> Keep speaker cables off the aisles.</li>
<li><strong>Catering prep outside.</strong> A separate 10 x 20 canopy or the garage.</li>
</ol>

If you want the dance floor to be larger, shrink to six rounds and add a lounge area on the lawn. See [tent layout for dance floor and bar](/blog/tent-layout-dance-floor-and-bar).

## Where the extra space goes in a 30x60

A 30x60 gives you two choices. If you want a bigger dance floor, drop to six rounds and enlarge the floor to about 18 x 18 feet, which suits a group that loves to dance. If you want a more relaxed dinner, keep eight rounds and use the channel between the rows as a place for a long dessert table or a lounge pair. The channel is 15 feet wide in the plan, plenty for a server lane and a small display.

Think about the entrance as well. Guests who walk in through a 10-foot opening on a short end see the room at once, so place something worth looking at on the far wall, like the bar, a photo backdrop or a sweetheart table. See [photo booth placement in a floor plan](/blog/photo-booth-placement-in-a-floor-plan) for ideas that do not block traffic. A seating chart should sit outside the door, where guests can read it without stopping in the entrance.

## Sidewalls, lighting and flooring

A 30x60 has hardly any slack for decor, so choose the extras that matter. Clear sidewalls keep the view of the yard and let in light; solid walls keep out wind. Lighting hangs from the frame, and string lights or chandeliers need to be rated for the tent. If the ground is uneven, flooring may be needed; see [tent flooring and subfloors](/blog/tent-flooring-and-subfloors). Power for DJ, lights and heaters generally comes from the house or a generator; see [event power needs checklist](/blog/event-power-needs-checklist).

## Frame or pole tent?

A frame tent has no center poles, so you get all 1,800 square feet. A pole tent has center poles that take floor space and break up the layout. See [frame tent vs pole tent](/blog/frame-tent-vs-pole-tent). For most weddings, a frame is a better fit for this size.

## Permits and lot size

A 30x60 tent is 1,800 square feet, a size at which many municipalities require a permit, a fire inspection or both. Rules vary by village and county, and some HOAs add their own. Ask early, since inspections can take days. See [Chicago tent permit](/blog/chicago-tent-permit) and [tent permit rules in the suburbs](/blog/tent-permit-chicago-suburbs). You also need a flat area of about 40 x 70 feet to leave room around the tent, and a utility locate before stakes; see [JULIE utility locate](/blog/julie-utility-locate-tent-illinois).

## Chairs and tables for 80 to 100

An 80-seat tent needs 80 chairs plus spares, eight rounds, and tables for the cake, gifts and bar. A [cross-back chair](/chairs/cross-back) is a natural fit for a backyard tent; a [ghost chair](/chairs/ghost) lets string lights show through. Chairs on grass or flooring need stable legs; see [chairs on grass, sand and gravel](/blog/chairs-on-grass-sand-gravel).

## Frequently asked questions

### How many people fit in a 30x60 tent?

About 80 to 100 seated with a dance floor, 110 to 130 seated without, and 120 to 150 standing.

### How many tables fit in a 30x60 tent?

Eight to twelve rounds of 10, depending on whether you include a dance floor.

### Is a 30x60 tent big enough for 100 guests?

For 100 seated guests with a small floor, it is tight but workable. Consider a 40x60 for comfort.

### How big should the dance floor be in a 30x60 tent?

A 12 x 12 to 15 x 15 foot floor. Larger floors mean fewer tables.

### Do I need flooring in a 30x60 tent?

Not always. Level, dry grass works, but flooring helps on slopes, soft ground or when high heels are involved.

### How much does a 30x60 tent cost?

It depends on the tent company, season and extras like sidewalls and lighting. Ask for a written quote.
