A venue changes its policy, a guest list grows, or a vendor falls through. Here's how to get chairs sorted fast.

## Send everything in the first message

Rental companies answer fastest when they don't need to ask questions. Include:

1. **Event date** and whether it's flexible on delivery day
2. **Exact chair count** (use the [seating planner](/seating-planner) if you're not sure)
3. **Venue name and full address**
4. **Delivery and pickup windows**, plus any loading dock or elevator rules
5. **Two or three acceptable styles**, not just one
6. **Setup needed?** Full setup, or drop-off only

## Be flexible on style

The fastest yes usually comes from widening the list. If your first choice is booked, ask what they have in your count. Styles that suit most rooms:

## Consider splitting the order

If no single company has your full count in one style, consider:

- One style for guest tables and another for the ceremony
- A statement chair for the head table only, with a simpler chair elsewhere
- Two companies, if your venue allows two deliveries

## Watch the extras

Rush delivery, after-hours pickup and same-day changes can carry fees. Ask for the **full total** in writing before you confirm.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I rent chairs for an event this weekend in Chicago?
Often yes for classic styles, especially for smaller counts. Contact several companies at once with your date, count, venue address and delivery window, and stay flexible on style.

### What chairs are easiest to rent last minute?
Folding and other high-volume classic styles are usually easiest. Specialty chairs in large quantities are the first to book up.

### Is last-minute chair rental more expensive?
It can be. Rush delivery, after-hours pickup and late changes may add fees, so ask for the full total in writing.
