June is what most people picture when they think of an outdoor event: long days, green lawns, open patios. It is also the month when afternoons start to feel hot, when thunderstorms roll in off the plains, and when every rental company is busiest. Here is how to plan around all three.

## June weather in Chicago

NWS 1991-2020 normals for O'Hare give June an average high of 80.4°F and a low of 60.8°F, with 4.10 inches of precipitation and no snow. The average hides real variation: some June afternoons stay in the low 70s with a lake breeze, others climb well into the 90s. The warmest weeks usually arrive in the second half of the month.

## The long evening

Sunset is about 8:18 pm on June 1, 8:27 pm on June 15 and 8:29 pm around the summer solstice (calculated for 2027). That is the latest sunset of the year in Chicago. A 6 pm ceremony ends in full sun, and the afternoon sun on a west-facing lawn can be strong. Think about which direction your ceremony faces, because guests squinting into a low sun is a classic mistake.

## What to plan for

### Shade and cooling

By 2 pm a bare lawn is a hot place. Tents give shade, and a fan or misting setup adds comfort. We cover options in our [summer heat plan](/blog/summer-event-heat-plan-chicago). Put drink stations in the shade, bring seating to the shade, and do not seat older guests in direct sun.

### Storms

June has 4.10 inches of average precipitation, and a lot of it falls in short, intense storms. Tents need anchoring suited to the surface. Ask your tent vendor about wind and what the plan is if the weather service issues a warning. Our [rain-on-event-day guide](/blog/rain-on-event-day-chicago) walks through the call.

### Bugs and humidity

Mosquitoes become a real factor in June evenings near water and shade. See our [mosquito and bug guide](/blog/mosquitoes-and-bugs-outdoor-events). Linens in humid weather can feel damp, so choose fabrics accordingly; heavy sequin overlays can be hot to the touch.

## Rentals that work in June

- **Tents** for shade and rain. Ask about sidewalls that can be rolled up for airflow.
- **Chairs** that stay cool: open-weave and slat chairs breathe better than padded ones. The [rattan garden chair](/chairs/rattan-garden) and the [cross-back chair](/chairs/cross-back) both work for lawn dinners.
- **Water and beverage stations** with ice. See [how many beverage dispensers you need](/blog/how-many-beverage-dispensers-do-i-need).
- **Lighting** for the after-sunset hours. Lights begin to matter after about 8:45 pm.
- **Linens** in light colors that shed heat.

## Public spaces and permits

If your June event is in a Chicago park, remember that the Chicago Park District treats outdoor gatherings of 50 or more people, tents, alcohol, amplified sound and inflatables as permit features. Review takes at least 14 days, and alcohol requires an add-on fee, a licensed provider and security. At beaches, the Park District says summer ceremony permits must end before 11 a.m. (see our [beach events guide](/blog/beach-events-chicago-park-district)). Suburban parks differ, so call first.

## Book early

June weekends are the most requested dates for weddings in many Chicago-area venues. If your date is a Saturday, expect rental inventory to tighten, and book as soon as the date is fixed. Our [guide to when to book](/blog/topic/budget-and-booking) lays out a timeline.

## June checklist for hosts

Start with the date and the direction the seating faces, then work outward. Confirm the venue's end time, because a Chicago-area village or park district may set a sound curfew that lands before the sunset glow ends. Ask who supplies power for fans and lights. Decide where the drink station sits and who restocks it. Then build the rental list: tables, chairs, linens, glassware, dinnerware, tent and lighting. A single call is easier than juggling several, which is why we help plan the whole list together.

Finally, add a buffer. June is the busiest delivery month, and a delivery window that looks generous on paper can tighten when roads fill with weekend traffic. ## Frequently asked questions

### Is June hot in Chicago?

June averages an 80.4°F high at O'Hare, which is warm but not extreme. Some afternoons reach the 90s, so plan shade, water and a cooler place to sit.

### What time is sunset in Chicago in June?

About 8:18 pm on June 1 and 8:27 pm on June 15, with the latest sunset near the solstice at about 8:29 pm.

### How much rain does Chicago get in June?

About 4.10 inches at O'Hare, often in short storms. A tent with a clear weather plan protects the party.

### Do I need a tent for an outdoor June wedding in Chicago?

Most planners say yes, for either shade or rain. A tent solves both, and it also gives you a place to put chairs, tables and a bar.

### What chairs are best for outdoor June events?

Open-back chairs that breathe, such as cross-back and rattan styles, work well. Avoid heavy padded chairs in direct sun unless you plan to move them into shade.

### Can I have an outdoor ceremony in a Chicago park in June?

Often yes, but the Chicago Park District requires a Special Event Permit for outdoor ceremonies. Plan well ahead because permit review takes at least 14 days.

### Are there mosquitoes at June outdoor events in Chicago?

Yes, especially near water and shaded lawns at dusk. Plan repellent, fans and a cleanup of standing water.
