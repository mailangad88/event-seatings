Lohri marks the winter harvest in Punjab, and for many families it is the first big gathering of the year. The fire is the center. In January in Chicago, it also means a cold night and a short list of questions. Where can you have a fire? How do you keep 30 people warm? Where do you dance on frozen ground? This guide answers them.

## Check fire rules first

Open fires are not allowed everywhere. A backyard in a Chicago neighborhood, a suburban lot, a park district space and a forest preserve each have their own rules. Condo and HOA rules often add more. Before you plan a bonfire, **check with your village, park district or building**, and ask about permits and distance from a house or fence.

If a fire is not allowed, you have safe options:

- **A contained fire pit,** where allowed, with a screen, on bare ground or a pad, away from fences, trees and eaves.
- **A gas fire table or fire bowl,** which some places treat differently than wood. Ask first.
- **Patio heaters** in a circle, with candles or lanterns in the center for the feel of a fire. See [patio heaters, how many per guests](/blog/patio-heaters-how-many-per-guests).
- **A tent with a heater** for the whole party. See [tent heater vs patio heater](/blog/tent-heater-vs-patio-heater).

Here is a quick way to choose:

<div class="table-wrap">

| Option | Good for | Ask first |
|---|---|---|
| Wood fire pit | Traditional feel | Village or building fire rules |
| Gas fire table | Cleaner, easier to control | Whether it is allowed and who supplies fuel |
| Patio heaters and lanterns | Where open flame is not allowed | Fuel and a spare tank |
| Heated tent | Large groups, wind and snow | Heater type and tent permit rules |

</div>

Keep a hose, a bucket of water or a fire extinguisher close by, and assign one adult to watch the fire at all times.

## What people do on Lohri night

Traditions differ across families, but these are common:

- **The fire.** Everyone walks around it, often three times, and offers popcorn, peanuts and sweets to the fire.
- **Sweet and crunchy food.** Popcorn, peanuts, gajak, rewri and sesame sweets, with sugarcane in some homes. Many families also serve sarson da saag and makki di roti.
- **Songs and dance.** Folk songs about Dulla Bhatti, bhangra and gidda.
- **A first Lohri.** Families celebrate extra for a baby's first Lohri or a newlywed couple's first, with gifts and a special place by the fire.

## Make a clear space for bhangra

Bhangra needs floor. In January the ground is frozen, wet or muddy. A grass lawn becomes a slick patch in minutes. A portable dance floor, or a tent with a floor, keeps dancers safe. For sizes, see [dance floor size by guest count](/blog/dance-floor-size-by-guest-count). A rule of thumb is about 4 to 5 square feet per dancer, and Lohri dances run big and loud, so give more room than a wedding dance floor.

Keep the dance space away from the fire. The dhol or speakers need a power source, and an extension cord must be rated for the load and tape down across the path. See [event power needs checklist](/blog/event-power-needs-checklist).

Heaters need fuel, and propane may not come with the rental. Ask what is included, and plan a spare tank. Place heaters at the edge of the seating, and never inside a closed tent unless the heater is made for it. Check with your rental company about tent heater safety.

Also plan blankets, a hot drink station with chai, hot cocoa or coffee, and a coat rack or a covered porch for coats. See [coat racks for winter events](/blog/coat-racks-for-winter-events-chicago) and [coffee and tea service rental](/blog/coffee-and-tea-service-rental).

## What most hosts find out too late

- **Delivery on frozen ground.** Heavy chairs and heaters need a clear path. Shovel the gangway and tell your rental company about steps and ice.
- **Fuel is a separate item.** Patio heaters, fire bowls and tent heaters often need fuel that is not part of the rental price. Ask.
- **It is dark early.** Without lights, a Lohri party at 5 pm is in the dark. Add string lights or lanterns for walkways. See [backyard party lighting ideas](/blog/backyard-party-lighting-ideas).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I have a bonfire in my Chicago backyard?

It depends on your village or neighborhood and sometimes your building. Check with your village, park district or building before you plan one. If it is not allowed, use a fire pit where permitted, heaters or a tent instead.

### What do you throw in the Lohri fire?

Popcorn, peanuts, rewri and sesame sweets are common offerings. Customs vary by family, so ask your elders. Keep food away from anything that could burn unsafely.

### What is the first Lohri for a baby or newlywed?

Families give a baby's first Lohri or a couple's first Lohri extra attention, with gifts, clothes and sweets. The baby or couple often sits near the fire for photos.

### How do I keep guests warm at an outdoor Lohri?

Use heaters at the edge of the seating, blankets, a hot drink station and a tent or porch. Our guide to patio heaters shows how many to plan for your guest count.

### What do I need for a bhangra dance floor?

A flat, firm surface, because frozen grass is slippery. A portable dance floor or a tent with flooring works. Allow more room than a wedding dance floor.

### How early do I book rentals for Lohri?

Book heaters and tents a few weeks ahead, because January is a busy season for heat. Request a quote and we check dates with the rental companies.
