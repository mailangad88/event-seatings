Downers Grove is a mid-sized DuPage suburb with a busy downtown beside the Metra BNSF line, a strong park district and lots of older neighborhoods. It suits graduation parties, family reunions and small weddings. The tricky part is knowing which pavilion, which rules and which season.

## The pavilion manual is your friend

The Downers Grove Park District publishes a pavilion rental manual, and the 2026 version lays out how pavilion rentals work. Read it before you pick a pavilion, because it answers questions you would otherwise have to call about, such as what is included and what is not. Fishel Park is one of the district's parks, and the district also runs a summer concert series in its parks.

The Village of Downers Grove covers streets, sidewalks and village property. The Downtown Downers Grove organization lists community events around Main Street and the train station.

## Space types

- **Park pavilions.** The most common choice for graduation parties and reunions.
- **Park lawns and fields.** Bigger, less structured, and often shared with the public.
- **Downtown streets.** Village permits apply.
- **Banquet halls, restaurants and hotels.** Common along the main corridors.
- **Private yards.** Lot sizes range from small to large.

## What to do with a pavilion

A pavilion usually has a roof, picnic tables and a limited number of outlets. Think of it as a shelter, not a ballroom. Bring in rounds, linens and a bar setup, but ask first about what may be moved. If you want more shade, a tent alongside may be possible, but ask the district. See [what size tent do I need](/blog/what-size-tent-do-i-need).

## Delivery from St. Charles

Downers Grove is southeast of St. Charles, a longer drive than the Fox Valley towns. Expect to cross I-355 and local arterials, so we plan around rush hour. Downtown loading is tight on weekends, so tell us the pavilion's nearest vehicle access.

## A Downers Grove example

A 100-guest reunion in a pavilion could use 13 rounds of 8 (104 seats). [Garden folding chairs](/chairs/garden-folding) keep the budget low (about $5-7 each, an estimate), while [cross-back chairs](/chairs/cross-back) (about $8-11) look better in photos.

## Putting the list together

Pavilion events look simple, but the details multiply. Walk through them in this order.

1. **Seating and tables.** Count guests, then pick table shapes. A 6-foot banquet table seats 6 to 8 and works for buffets.
2. **Linens.** Floor-length cloths on rounds; plain skirting on the buffet. The [tablecloth size chart](/blog/tablecloth-size-chart) lists sizes.
3. **Food service.** Ask your caterer what they bring and what you must rent.
4. **Weather.** If a storm blows in, the pavilion roof helps, but a sidewall tent adds protection.
5. **Cleanup.** Parks often require you to leave the space as you found it, so plan trash bags and a pickup window.

If your event is in Downers Grove or anywhere around Chicago, tell us the pavilion name and guest count and we can start from there.

## Questions to ask the park district

Pavilion rules are the difference between a smooth day and a stressful one. When you read the manual or call the district, ask:

- How many guests does the pavilion allow, and does that include children?
- Are tables included, and may we bring our own rounds and linens?
- What time can we arrive to set up, and when must we be out?
- Is there power, and how many outlets can we use?
- Are tents, grills, bounce houses or amplified music allowed?
- What happens if it rains: is there a refund or a reschedule?

Write the answers down and send them to your rental company. Setup windows often decide how much help you need, and a 2-hour window with 13 tables means a bigger crew than a 4-hour window.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I reserve a Downers Grove pavilion?

Through the Downers Grove Park District. It publishes a yearly pavilion rental manual, which explains the process and the rules.

### Is Fishel Park rentable?

Fishel Park is a district park. Ask the Downers Grove Park District which spaces in the park are reservable and under what rules.

### Do I need a village permit for a Downers Grove event?

Only for events on village streets or property. Park district events follow the district's rules.

### What can I bring into a pavilion?

That varies. Read the district's pavilion manual, then ask about tables, tents, power and alcohol.

### Do you deliver in Downers Grove?

Yes. First deliveries begin with the April 2027 Founding Season.

### When do pavilions book up?

Summer weekends fill first, so ask the district early and book your rentals as soon as the pavilion is confirmed.
