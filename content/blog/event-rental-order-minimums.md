**Quick answer**

Rental companies set minimums because a truck trip costs about the same whether it carries 10 chairs or 200. An order minimum is the smallest rental total they will book. A delivery minimum is the smallest total that gets delivery at all, or at a lower rate. To meet one, add items you need anyway, combine orders, or pick up the items yourself. Ask each company for its minimums in writing before you compare prices.

You need 20 chairs and two tables for a backyard birthday. The company says the order is too small to deliver. This is common, and it is not personal. Small orders cost as much to deliver as big ones, and Chicago traffic, parking and alleys make each stop slow.

- **Order minimum.** The least the company will book at all. It covers staff time to pull, check and invoice the order.
- **Delivery minimum.** The least that qualifies for delivery. Below it you may pay a higher delivery rate, or you pick up.

Some companies have only one. Some count only the item subtotal, and some count the full total. Ask which.

## Why the minimums exist

Every order has fixed work: pulling items from the warehouse, checking them, loading a truck, driving, unloading, collecting, then washing and restocking. That work does not shrink much for a small order. The minimum protects the company from losing money on a stop. It is the same reason a Chicago restaurant sets a delivery minimum on an app.

## How to meet a minimum without wasting money

<ol class="flow">
<li><strong>Add items you were going to need anyway.</strong> Linens, napkins, a drink tub, a cake table or extra chairs for late guests.</li>
<li><strong>Check what counts.</strong> Some minimums use the item total only, others include fees.</li>
<li><strong>Combine with a neighbor or family member.</strong> Two households on the same block can share one delivery if the company allows it.</li>
<li><strong>Pick one delivery date.</strong> Two small drop-offs are worse than one larger one.</li>
<li><strong>Ask about pickup.</strong> If the order is small, pick it up yourself and skip the delivery line.</li>
</ol>

Do not pad the order with things you will not use. An item you do not need still costs money and still takes space in your yard. See [event rental hidden fees](/blog/event-rental-hidden-fees-checklist) to find what you can cut instead.

## When picking up yourself makes sense

<div class="table-wrap">

| Situation | Pickup tends to work | Delivery tends to work |
|---|---|---|
| Order size | Under about one vehicle load | 100+ chairs plus tables |
| Items | Chairs, folding tables, linens | Glassware, heaters, tents |
| Your vehicle | Van, SUV or truck with space | Sedan |
| Your time | Flexible on pickup day and return day | Busy event day |
| Venue | Easy parking, no stairs | Stairs, dock, alley |

</div>

For the longer comparison, read [full service vs will-call](/blog/full-service-vs-will-call-event-rentals). The short version is that pickup saves the delivery fee, but you take on loading, carrying, counting and the return trip.

## What most hosts find out too late

**Minimums can be lower for pickup than delivery.** Some companies will book a small order for pickup that they would not deliver. Ask for both numbers.

**Minimums can move on busy dates.** On a holiday weekend or a spring Saturday, a company may be less willing to send a truck for a small order. If your date is a peak one, ask early.

**The minimum may apply per delivery, not per order.** If you split a party across two addresses or two days, each stop may need to meet it.

## Questions to ask the company

- What is the order minimum, and what counts toward it?
- What is the delivery minimum, and is there a smaller-order delivery rate?
- Is there a pickup option for small orders?
- Do minimums change on weekends or holidays?
- Are tent, heater and large-item orders handled differently?

## Frequently asked questions

### What is an order minimum for event rentals?

It is the smallest rental total a company will book. It exists because every order needs the same pulling, loading and checking work. Ask what it is before you build a list.

### What is the difference between an order minimum and a delivery minimum?

An order minimum is the smallest order they will book at all. A delivery minimum is the smallest order that qualifies for delivery or the standard delivery rate. Some companies have both, some only one.

### Can I rent just a few chairs and tables?

Often yes, usually as a pickup order or with a delivery fee that is high compared to the items. Combining your list into one order or choosing pickup makes small orders cheaper.

### Do delivery minimums include tax and fees?

It depends on the company. Some use the item subtotal only. Ask which total counts so you do not add items you do not need.

### Is it cheaper to pick up my own rentals?

It can be, because you skip the delivery and pickup lines. You then pay in time and carry the risk of damage and counting. For items like glass or a heater, delivery is often worth it.

### Can I share a delivery with a neighbor?

Sometimes. If two orders go to the same address or the same block on the same day, ask the company whether they count as one stop.
