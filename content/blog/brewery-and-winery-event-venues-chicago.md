**Quick answer**

Breweries and wineries usually give you a room, a bar and their own drinks. You typically still need seating for a dinner, tables, linens, dinnerware, glassware beyond pint glasses and a plan for food. Before you book, ask about food policy, bar minimums, outside vendors, capacity at tables versus standing, sound limits and load-in access.

A brewery or winery event in Chicago has a built-in personality: concrete floors, stainless tanks, a long bar, friendly staff. It is a good fit for a birthday, rehearsal dinner, small wedding, team party or nonprofit mixer. It also has quirks that surprise first-time hosts. Here is what to ask and what to rent.

## What you usually get

Every venue is different, so treat this as a checklist rather than a promise. A taproom typically has a bar, some tables and stools, a stereo or speakers, and restrooms. A winery may add a barrel room, a patio or a tasting room. Beverages are often the venue's own, and rules on outside alcohol vary. Illinois alcohol rules for private events are covered in our [Illinois alcohol rules guide](/blog/illinois-alcohol-rules-private-events); verify details with the venue and with the state or city.

## Seated or standing

A taproom that holds 150 standing may seat 60 at tables. Space per guest is the reason. Standing receptions need about 6 to 8 square feet per person, while seated dinners need about 10 to 12. If the venue lists one capacity, ask whether it is for standing or seated.

## Sound, floors and acoustics

Hard surfaces echo. Linens, rugs and soft seating absorb sound. Ask about the floor too: concrete may need protection for rolling carts and chair feet. Tell your rental company about steps, freight doors and how far from the street to the room.

## Tours, patios and weather

Some wineries host outdoor events on patios or lawns, which brings in the weather questions from our [seasonal event guides](/blog/outdoor-events-chicago-in-september). A patio needs heat in the evening and shade in the afternoon.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I bring my own food to a brewery event?

It depends. Many breweries have no kitchen and allow caterers or food trucks, and some have exclusive partners. Ask the venue.

### Do breweries provide tables and chairs?

Most have tables and stools but not enough seating for a formal dinner. Ask what is included, then rent the rest.

### How many people fit at a taproom?

Standing capacity is higher than seated. A common rule of thumb is 6 to 8 square feet per standing guest and 10 to 12 per seated guest.

### Can I serve my own alcohol at a winery or brewery?

Policies vary and Illinois has rules. Ask the venue, and read our Illinois alcohol rules guide.

### What glassware do I need for a wedding at a brewery?

Venue pints cover beer; add flutes or stemware for toasts and wine.

### Do I need linens at a brewery event?

Not required, but cloths soften the room and cover worn tables.

### What is the best chair for an industrial venue?

Wood, bentwood and velvet styles all work. Choose by the look and the level of comfort you need.
