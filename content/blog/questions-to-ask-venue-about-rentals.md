**Quick answer**

Before you order any rentals, ask your venue what furniture and dinnerware is included, what is allowed from outside vendors, when you can load in and out, what insurance is required and what the rules are for tents, candles, music and decor. Get every answer in writing, and ask for a floor plan with the venue's capacity. These questions prevent double-renting, surprise fees and day-of delays.

</div>

The venue tour is your best chance to find out what the venue really provides. Many "included" tables and chairs turn out to be folding chairs in a closet. Many "outside vendor" rules show up only after you've paid a deposit. Use this list on the tour, or email it to the venue coordinator before you sign.

## Furniture: what is already there

1. **Which tables and chairs are included, and how many?** Get counts by type.
2. **What do they look like?** Ask to see them, not a photo from last year.
3. **What sizes are the tables?** 60 or 72-inch rounds change your linen order.
4. **Is there a ceremony chair set,** or do you need to flip?
5. **Who sets up and tears down?** Venue staff, caterer or you.

## Vendors: who can bring what

6. **Do you allow outside rental companies,** or do you have an approved list?
7. **Do you charge a fee** for outside vendors or for use of venue staff to help them?
8. **What insurance is required,** and does the venue need to be named as additional insured? Many venues, especially in downtown Chicago buildings, ask for a certificate of insurance.
9. **Is your caterer's dinnerware included,** or must you rent it?

## Access: load-in and load-out

10. **When can vendors arrive and leave?** Ask for exact hours.
11. **Where do trucks park or unload?** Is there a loading dock or a curb?
12. **Are there stairs, elevators or long carries?** Freight elevators may be reserved.
13. **Is the venue available the night before or morning after** for setup and pickup?

## Rules: what is allowed

14. **What is the room capacity** seated and standing?
15. **Can we use candles, open flame or sparklers?**
16. **What are the music and sound rules,** including end times?
17. **Are tents, flooring or stakes allowed,** and who pulls permits? Check your village for permit rules.
18. **What about decor,** like taping, hanging, confetti or hanging lights?

## Fees: what else costs money

19. **What is the cleanup fee,** and what must be done before you leave?
20. **Are there overtime charges** if the vendor crew runs late?
21. **What is the damage deposit,** and how is it returned?
22. **Are there minimums** for catering or bar that affect rentals?

## Weather: outdoor sites

23. **What is the rain plan,** and where does the ceremony move?
24. **Is there shade, heat or cooling?**
25. **Who decides** to move indoors, and by when?

## Bring a measuring tape and a camera

On the tour, measure the main doors, the elevator, any stairs and the width of the aisle between the loading area and the room. Take photos of the room from each corner, the coat area, the bar location and the nearest outlets. Note where the lighting switches are and whether the room has a ceiling you can hang from. Back at home, those photos and numbers let a rental planner build a floor plan without a second visit, and they make it easy to spot a problem, like a table size that won't fit through a doorway, while you still have time to change it.

Ask the coordinator for the venue's floor plan and any capacity chart. Many venues have one on file and will send it as a PDF.

## Chicago-specific notes

Downtown Chicago buildings often require insurance certificates, specific loading dock hours and freight elevator reservations. Historic buildings may restrict candles and fixed hanging points. Suburban banquet halls might include chairs, linens and dinnerware but limit outside vendors. Park district facilities, forest preserves and public parks have their own permit and vendor rules; ask the managing office, and do not assume a scenic spot allows a tent or amplified music.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should I ask a venue about rentals?

Ask what furniture is included, which outside vendors are allowed, when you can load in and out, what insurance is required, what rules apply to tents, candles and music, and what fees apply. Get everything in writing.

### Do venues allow outside rental companies?

Many do, some require approved vendors, and some charge a fee. Ask before you sign, because it affects your budget.

### What insurance do rental vendors need?

Venues often ask for a certificate of insurance from vendors, sometimes naming the venue as additional insured. Ask your venue for the exact wording.

### Can I rent chairs if the venue provides some?

Yes. Many couples rent a different chair for the ceremony or head table, or replace the venue's chairs with a better style. Ask the venue whether the supplied chairs can be removed.

### How do I find out the room capacity?

Ask for the seated and standing capacity in writing, and the fire marshal's limit. Compare it to your floor plan.

### When should I ask these questions?

During your venue tour, before signing the contract. Review them again when you book rentals.
