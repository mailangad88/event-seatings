**Quick answer**

A clearspan structure tent has an engineered aluminum frame with no interior poles, so the whole floor is open at any width. It is built for larger events (often 100 to 500 guests), long installs and harsh weather, and it can carry heavier loads like lighting, HVAC ducting and even second-story decking. Rent one when you need more than about 40 x 100 feet of open floor, a hard-floor setup, or a tent that stays up more than a few days.

</div>

Most backyard parties use a standard frame tent, and that is the right answer. But once a guest list passes 200, or a company picnic, festival or wedding needs heating, a stage and a real kitchen nearby, a clearspan structure can be the better tool. This guide explains what you are paying for and how to decide.

## What "clearspan" means

"Clearspan" describes a roof with no support between the outside walls. A standard frame tent also has no center poles, but a clearspan structure uses heavier extruded aluminum trusses sized by engineers. That lets it span wider, hold more weight and handle more wind.

Many clearspan structures are built in bays, usually 5 meters (about 16 feet 5 inches) or 10 feet apart, so the length is flexible. Widths often run 30, 40, 50, 60, 80 or 100 feet. Ask the company for the sizes it stocks.

## When a clearspan structure makes sense

- **Large guest counts.** Past 200 seated guests, a structure is often easier than joining several frame tents. See [what size tent you need](/blog/what-size-tent-do-i-need).
- **Long installs.** Festivals, multi-day weddings and corporate events that stay up for a week or more benefit from the sturdier build.
- **Hard weather.** Engineered structures have published wind and snow load ratings. Ask for them in writing. See [tent wind ratings and weather safety](/blog/tent-wind-ratings-weather-safety).
- **Climate control.** The solid frame carries ducted heating and cooling better than a light frame tent. See [heating a tent in fall](/blog/heating-a-tent-in-fall).
- **Events on pavement.** Structures can be ballasted on parking lots and plazas, and some can be anchored to the ground with specialized fixings.
- **Add-ons.** Hard walls, glass walls, raised flooring, stages and lighting rigs attach more easily to a structure.

## When it is too much

A 20 x 30 tent in a Naperville backyard does not need a clearspan structure. It would cost more and the setup would be much bigger than the yard can take. For a small party, a frame tent is simpler and the smaller crew leaves less damage. Compare in [frame tent vs pole tent](/blog/frame-tent-vs-pole-tent).

## Permits and engineering

Large tents in Chicago over 600 square feet need drawings stamped by an Illinois-licensed architect or structural engineer, and a high winds action plan. Expect that from any clearspan structure. Your tent company should provide engineering documents, a fire certificate for the fabric (NFPA 701 in Chicago) and anchorage details. See [Chicago tent permits](/blog/chicago-tent-permit) and, for suburbs, [permits in the suburbs](/blog/tent-permit-chicago-suburbs).

## Questions to ask the rental company

1. What are the wind and snow ratings, and at what wind speed do you evacuate?
2. Will you pull the permit, and who pays the fee?
3. What is the working footprint, including ballast and staging areas?
4. How do you protect asphalt, pavers or turf?
5. How many days does installation and removal take?
6. What flooring options are available for the surface?

## Seating a structure tent

Open floors reward a strong plan. Round tables of 10 at 12 square feet per guest is the baseline. For a 300-person gala, you might want matching [Chiavari-style alternatives](/blog/chiavari-chair-alternatives) or [ghost chairs](/chairs/ghost) for a modern look under a white ceiling.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a clearspan tent?

A clearspan tent is a structure with no interior support poles. The aluminum frame carries the whole roof, so the floor is fully open at any width.

### How big can a clearspan structure tent be?

Widths of 30 to 100 feet are common, and lengths grow in bays, so one structure can cover tens of thousands of square feet. The stock sizes depend on the rental company.

### Is a clearspan tent better in wind?

Often yes, because it is an engineered frame with a published wind rating. Still, any tent needs a weather plan with a defined wind speed for evacuation. Ask the company for its number.

### Can a clearspan structure be installed on a parking lot?

Yes, usually with ballast. The company must check surface condition, drainage and load, and your venue must approve it.

### Do I need an engineer for a clearspan tent in Chicago?

Chicago requires licensed architect or structural engineer drawings for tents over 600 square feet or over 15 feet high. The rental company typically supplies these.

### Can I heat and cool a clearspan structure?

Yes. They carry ducted HVAC well and sidewalls can be insulated or solid. Ask for the heating capacity and whether it is electric or fuel-fired, since many jurisdictions limit fuel-burning heaters near tents.
