December is the busiest month for corporate parties, family gatherings and holiday fundraisers in Chicago. Everyone wants the same Fridays and Saturdays, everyone needs coat space, and everyone hopes the snow holds off until midnight. This guide covers the weather, the schedule and the rental list.

## December weather

NWS normals for O'Hare list December at a 36.6°F average high and 24.4°F average low, with 2.11 inches of precipitation and 7.6 inches of snow. Wind off the lake makes it feel colder. Most events are indoors, but outdoor elements like a tented patio, a heated entrance or a winter market can work with the right setup.

## Daylight is short

Sunset in Chicago is about 4:21 pm on December 1 and about 4:20 pm on December 15 (calculated for 2027), the earliest of the year. The winter solstice is about December 21. Employees leaving the office at 5 pm arrive in the dark, so signage, entrance lighting and parking instructions matter more than they do in summer. See our [winter event lighting guide](/blog/winter-event-lighting-chicago-sunset).

## Snow and delivery

Seven inches of average snow means some deliveries will be slower. Give your rental company a clear delivery window, a loading dock or door that is shoveled and salted, and a contact who answers. Downtown, remember that holiday traffic and street closures slow crews, so allow extra time. If the party is in a suburb, ask the village about parking restrictions during snow.

## Coats, mats and the entrance

A holiday party in Chicago creates a mountain of coats, boots and wet umbrellas. Plan a coat room with racks and numbered hangers, add mats at the door, and keep the entrance clear. Our [coat rack guide](/blog/coat-racks-for-winter-events-chicago) lists how many racks per guest. Add a table for the check-in and a sign that says where to find the coats.

## Menu and bar equipment

Holiday menus lean on hot drinks. A hot cocoa bar, coffee urns and a mulled cider station add warmth. See [hot cocoa bar ideas](/blog/hot-cocoa-bar-winter-event) and [how many coffee urns for a party](/blog/how-many-coffee-urns-for-a-party). Ask your caterer about warming equipment, and be sure the power plan includes the extra load.

## Corporate considerations

Corporate parties often need a stage or a microphone for remarks, a registration table and a plan for plus-ones. Place the bar away from the entrance to prevent a jam. Offer non-alcoholic choices, and consider a quiet seating area for people who want to chat. If your office is hosting a nonprofit event, ask whether rentals can be donated or discounted; the answer is up to each vendor.

## Frequently asked questions

### When should I book rentals for a December holiday party in Chicago?

Book in October or early November. Popular December Fridays and Saturdays fill quickly, and chairs, tables and linens are the first items to run out.

### What time is sunset in Chicago in December?

Around 4:20 pm for most of the month, the earliest sunset of the year.

### How much snow does Chicago get in December?

About 7.6 inches on average at O'Hare, though it varies a lot from year to year.

### What chairs are best for a holiday party?

Padded and velvet chairs feel warm, while gold and Louis styles feel formal. Choose by the look of the room and the comfort of long dinners.

### How many coat racks do I need for a holiday party?

A common rule of thumb is one rack for about 40 to 50 guests, with hangers. Add more if the party is outdoors or guests bring bulky coats.

### Can I hold a holiday party outdoors in Chicago?

Yes, with a heated tent, flooring and sidewalls, but most hosts choose indoor venues. If you go outdoors, plan heat, lighting and power carefully.

### Do rental companies deliver in snow?

Yes, but delays happen. Give a clear delivery window and a shoveled access point.
