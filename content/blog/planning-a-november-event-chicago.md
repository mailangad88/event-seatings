November is a month of dinners: Friendsgiving, fundraisers, holiday parties started early, quinceañeras and small winter-feeling weddings. Chicago-area weather turns firmly cold, and the clocks change. This guide covers the numbers and the rentals that matter.

## November weather

NWS 1991-2020 normals give November an average high of 48.4°F and a low of 34.1°F at O'Hare. Precipitation averages 2.42 inches and snowfall 1.8 inches. Compare that with October's 62.7°F high: the temperature drops about 14 degrees in one month, and most of that change lands in the second half.

## The clock change

Daylight saving time ends Sunday, November 7, 2027. Before the change, sunset is about 5:46 pm (November 1); after it, about 4:31 pm (November 15). If your event is the weekend of the change, plan guest arrival around the earlier dark, and confirm setup times with the venue and rental company, because delivery crews work in the dark more often.

## Indoor first, outdoor second

A November outdoor event is possible but needs real commitment: a heated tent with sidewalls, flooring, a lighting plan and a coat plan. Most November events are indoors. If the venue is indoors, rentals shift to the room: linens, chairs, tables, glassware and dinnerware. Our guide to [banquet hall transformations](/blog/banquet-hall-room-transformation-ideas) shows what can change a plain room.

## What to rent for November

- **Chairs**: padded and velvet chairs feel warm. A [velvet dining chair](/chairs/velvet-dining) in navy or emerald fits the season; estimated at $18 to $25.
- **Linens**: deep colors and heavier fabrics. See [linen colors by season](/blog/linen-colors-by-season).
- **Lighting**: sunset is before 5 pm, so lighting starts early.
- **Coat racks**: November means coats. See [coat racks for winter events](/blog/coat-racks-for-winter-events-chicago).
- **Dinnerware**: for Friendsgiving and family dinners, see [Friendsgiving dinnerware](/blog/friendsgiving-thanksgiving-dinnerware).

## Thanksgiving week

Thanksgiving is Thursday, November 25, 2027. Rental companies are busy the Monday through Wednesday before, because hosts want tables and chairs for home dinners. Order early and ask about pickup after the holiday weekend. If you are hosting 20 to 40 people at home, see [how many chairs you need](/blog/how-many-chairs-do-i-need) and [tables for your guest count](/blog/how-many-tables-for-50-100-150-200-guests).

## Travel and weather risk

Snow is not common in November but possible: 1.8 inches on average. Guests traveling from out of state may face delays, so build slack into the schedule and the ceremony start. Tell guests about parking and coat check in the invitation.

## Choosing the room and the layout

Winter-feeling events live or die on the room. Count guests first, then match tables to the floor. Round tables of 60 inches seat 8 comfortably and 72-inch rounds seat 10, which changes the footprint of a 100-person dinner by a surprising amount; see [round table sizes](/blog/round-table-sizes-and-seating-48-60-66-72-inch). Leave 4 to 5 feet between table edges so servers and guests can pass with coats, bags and strollers. Add space near the entrance for coats, because 100 coats take real floor.

If the venue is a restaurant, hall or loft, ask three questions: what time you can load in, whether the freight elevator or loading dock is reserved, and what the rules are for candles and heaters. Downtown buildings often limit loading hours, and a missed window means rentals wait on the sidewalk in November wind. Our [Chicago venue rules guide](/blog/topic/chicago-planning) lists the questions to ask.

## Hosting at home in November

Many November events are home dinners for 20 to 40. The challenges are space, parking and chairs. Move furniture to the garage, rent tables that fit the room, and plan a coat closet or rack. Seat older guests near the kitchen if the room is large, and stagger arrival times if the driveway is short. A neighbor's driveway or a street permit can solve parking; ask your village what is allowed.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the weather like in Chicago in November?

The average high is 48.4°F and the low is 34.1°F at O'Hare, with occasional snow and a lot of wind.

### What time is sunset in Chicago in November?

About 5:46 pm on November 1, then about 4:31 pm on November 15 after daylight saving time ends on November 7, 2027.

### Can I have an outdoor event in Chicago in November?

Yes, with a heated tent, sidewalls and flooring. It is more complex and costly than indoor events, so many hosts book a venue.

### When is Thanksgiving in 2027?

Thursday, November 25, 2027.

### What should I rent for a November dinner party?

Chairs, tables, linens, dinnerware, glassware and a coat rack. Warm lighting makes the room feel cozy.

### Does it snow in Chicago in November?

Yes, about 1.8 inches on average at O'Hare, though it varies a lot by year.

### When should I book a November event?

As early as you can. Thanksgiving week and the weekends before December holidays fill fast.

## A short planning checklist for November

Set the guest count first, then the room. Ask the venue about load-in time and the end time, because winter hours can be shorter. Decide on coat storage before the invitations go out. Book rentals for chairs, tables and linens as a package. Add a cold-weather contingency day if you are traveling. And choose a lighting plan that works from 4:30 pm.
