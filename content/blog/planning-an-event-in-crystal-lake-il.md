**Quick answer**

For a Crystal Lake wedding or dinner, long farm tables seat guests family style: an 8-foot table seats 8 to 10, so 24 guests need three tables. Choose sturdy wooden chairs for grass and gravel, and plan for lake wind and a weather backup. Cross-back chairs run about $8 to $11 each, and a full 100-guest reception needs about 105 chairs.

Crystal Lake, in McHenry County, takes its name from the lake at its center, and it has a historic downtown, parks run by the Crystal Lake Park District and plenty of surrounding farmland. Weddings and dinners here often lean rustic or lakeside, with long tables, string lights and outdoor spaces. This guide focuses on the table plan that suits that style.

## Lakeside and park venues

The Crystal Lake Park District runs parks and recreation areas, including lakeside spaces. If you want a lakeside ceremony or picnic, contact the park district for reservation rules, capacity and what is allowed. Some sites allow tents and chairs; others do not. Ask about alcohol, amplified music, parking and restrooms. Also check the weather policy, because lakefront wind can be strong.

Bring layers for guests, since evenings near water are cooler. Weigh down linens, signs and arches. A rain plan is essential.

## Plan the evening light

Country evenings get dark when the sun drops, and few farm fields have street lights. Plan string lights over the tables, lanterns along paths and a light at the parking area. Candles on a runner are lovely, but check venue fire rules. In the Chicago area, summer sunset is around 8:30 pm and falls steadily through fall. See our [string light layouts](/blog/string-light-layouts-for-events) for hanging heights.

## Seasons around the lake

Crystal Lake is in the Chicago region but sits farther out in McHenry County, so open country weather applies. Spring is muddy, summer is warm and humid, fall is cool and bright and winter is cold with snow. Pick the chair and table plan for the season. In spring, use flooring or hard surfaces. In summer, add shade and fans. In fall, bring blankets and heaters (check the venue's rules for open flame). In winter, move indoors and use our [winter seating guide](/blog/winter-wedding-seating).

Sunset affects ceremonies, too. In June the sun sets around 8:30 pm, so a 6 pm ceremony has golden light afterward. In October, it sets before 6:30 pm, so move the ceremony earlier. Check an official sunset table for your date, and share it with your photographer.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many people sit at an 8-foot farm table?

Eight to ten: 4 or 5 on each long side, and one at each end if you want. Allow 24 inches of table edge per guest.

### Are wooden chairs good for outdoor weddings near the lake?

Yes. Wood chairs are stable, and cross-back chairs suit rustic settings. Use foot pads on soft ground.

### Can I use the Crystal Lake Park District for a wedding?

Possibly. Contact the park district for availability, rules and permit requirements.

### What is the best chair for a gravel driveway?

A wide-footed wood chair, with plywood mats under the legs. Avoid thin metal legs on gravel.

### How do I keep linens from blowing away?

Use weights or clips, or choose a lap-length cloth. Lakeside breezes can be strong.

### How early should I book?

For summer and fall weekends, 6 to 9 months out is a good target.
