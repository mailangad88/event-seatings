## Choosing the finish

- **Gold and ivory:** the classic. It works with most palettes and photographs warmly.
- **Silver and white:** cool and bright, good for winter, baby showers and modern events.
- **Maroon and gold:** rich and traditional, a strong fit for certain cultural celebrations and fall weddings.

Always check the room's color. A gold throne in a gold ballroom disappears; the contrast of silver and white is stronger there.

## Staging and photos

1. **Pick the wall first.** A throne needs something behind it: a floral wall, drape, balloon garland, a mirror or a sign.
2. **Add a riser if the room is deep.** A low platform makes the chair visible from the back of the room. Ask your venue what is allowed.
3. **Light the chair.** A warm spotlight or uplights beside the backdrop avoid harsh shadows.
4. **Keep the floor clear.** Dress trains, balloons and floral arrangements should not block the sitters' path.

## Space and fit

A throne is wide, so measure it. A pair takes about 6 feet of width including arms, and the dress or gown needs room in front. Allow 8 feet of clear space for the photographer. If your venue is small, compare with [sweetheart table chair ideas](/blog/sweetheart-table-chair-ideas), which includes options that fit a tighter footprint.

## Mistakes to avoid

1. **Putting the throne against a busy wall.** A patterned wall or a stack of venue chairs behind it ruins the photo. Bring a backdrop.
2. **Forgetting the dress.** Gowns and trains need space. Keep the floor clear and the stage wide enough for a skirt.
3. **Overdecorating.** The chair is already ornate. Choose one accent: a floral swag, a sign or a balloon arch.
4. **Ordering too late.** Statement pieces are limited, and popular dates fill up first.
5. **Skipping the load-in check.** Measure doors and elevators before the chair arrives.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a throne chair rental cost in Chicago?

Our estimate is $75 to $150 per pair. The final price depends on finish, delivery and setup.

### What size is a rental throne chair?

Our throne is a large high-back chair or loveseat, so plan for about 6 feet of width for a pair and 4 feet of depth. Ask for exact measurements in your quote.

### Can a throne chair be used outdoors?

No. It is an indoor piece in our catalog. Use it in a hall, ballroom or a fully covered, floored tent.

### Is a throne chair good for a baby shower?

Yes. It gives the guest of honor a clear spot, and silver and white or gold and ivory suit most baby shower palettes.

### Can I use a throne for a wedding sweetheart table?

Yes. Many couples use a throne pair at a sweetheart table or stage. Keep the table narrow and the backdrop simple.

### Does the throne chair stack?

No. It is a large, individual piece, so plan delivery and a clear path to the stage.

### What goes behind a throne chair?

A floral wall, drape, balloon garland, a mirror or a sign. Pick one so the chair stays the focus.
