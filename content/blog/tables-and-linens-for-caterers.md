**Quick answer**

A catered event needs more tables than the guest count suggests: about one 6 or 8-foot table per 25 to 30 guests for the buffet line, plus 1 to 2 for the bar, 1 for desserts, 1 for beverages and 2 to 3 plain tables for back-of-house prep. Floor-length cloths (90 x 132 in for a 6-foot table, 90 x 156 in for an 8-foot table) hide boxes under the table, and pleated skirting finishes the buffet front.

</div>

Guest tables get all the attention. The service tables, the ones a caterer needs to do the job, are where lists are short and events slow down. A buffet on a card table, a bar with no front or a prep station on a patio chair all signal a gap in the rental list. This guide counts every service table and matches linens to each.

## Table types a caterer asks for

- **6-foot and 8-foot banquet tables,** 30 inches wide, for buffets and prep. An 8-foot table holds 3 chafers, a 6-foot holds 2.
- **60-inch rounds,** for cake and escort cards.
- **Cocktail or highboy tables,** 42 inches tall, for stations and standing guests; see the [tablecloth size chart](/blog/tablecloth-size-chart).
- **Plain utility tables,** for the prep tent, often with no linen.
- **Serpentine or half-round,** for decorative buffets and dessert displays.

## Linen choices for service tables

<ol class="flow">
<li><strong>Buffet front:</strong> floor-length cloth or pleated skirt to hide boxes.</li>
<li><strong>Top layer:</strong> a darker or contrasting cloth where food spills most.</li>
<li><strong>Bar front:</strong> a cloth, a spandex cover, or wood for a rustic look.</li>
<li><strong>Back of house:</strong> plain white cloth or butcher paper over the table.</li>
<li><strong>Spares:</strong> 2 extra cloths per 10 service tables.</li>
</ol>

Sauce stains, wine and grease are hard on linen. Pick a darker color or a pattern for the buffet and keep white for guest tables. Ask your rental company about damage fees for stains from food and fuel. Burn marks from chafing fuel can ruin a cloth, so use a heat pad or trivet under each frame.

## Skirting and finishing

Banquet-style skirting, a pleated fabric strip clipped to the table edge, makes a buffet look finished for the guest-facing side, while the back stays open for access. A skirt usually needs clips and a table cloth on top. For a floor-length cloth, check the height of the table, because older tables may be 29 inches. For cloth sizes, read the [tablecloth size chart](/blog/tablecloth-size-chart).

## Plan space for service tables

Allow at least 36 inches of space around service tables, 48 inches in busy areas. A buffet line wants 12 to 20 feet of table; see [buffet line flow](/blog/buffet-line-flow-and-serving-speed). Do not forget a table for dirty dishes and a table for gifts, since people end up placing them wherever they find space. Check with your venue about their tables; many banquet halls include their own banquet tables but charge for linen.

## Linen color and chairs

Dark service tables around lighter guest tables draw the eye to the food. A neutral service linen leaves room for color on the guest tables. If your guest tables use ivory linen with [ghost chairs](/chairs/ghost), a deep green or charcoal buffet cloth adds contrast. For a casual layout, natural linen goes with [rattan garden chairs](/chairs/rattan-garden).

## Chicago planning notes

Chicago banquet halls vary on whether they supply tables for caterers. Ask early. In a rented loft or gallery, loading elevators and stairs limit table size. In tents, add tables for the caterer's prep zone as described in [catering prep tent and back of house space](/blog/catering-prep-tent-and-back-of-house-space).

## Frequently asked questions

### How many tables does a caterer need?

Plan 3 to 4 buffet tables, 2 prep tables and tables for the bar, desserts and drinks for 100 guests. The exact count depends on menu and staff.

### What size tablecloth for a buffet table?

A 90 x 132-inch cloth reaches the floor on a 6-foot table, and a 90 x 156-inch cloth on an 8-foot table.

### Should the buffet tables be skirted?

Skirting hides what is under the table and looks finished from the guest side. A floor-length cloth works if you do not need access from the front.

### What linen color is best for food tables?

A darker or patterned color hides stains. Keep white for guest tables.

### Do caterers bring their own tables?

Some do and some do not. Ask in the first conversation, and check what the venue includes.

### What is the best table for a bar?

A 6-foot banquet table with a floor-length cloth, or a rented bar. Provide a work table behind for ice and supplies.
