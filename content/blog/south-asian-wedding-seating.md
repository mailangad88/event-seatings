South Asian weddings often span several events, welcome large guest lists and include ceremonial seating. Planning seating well takes more than a headcount.

## What to plan for

- **Multiple events.** Sangeet, ceremony and reception can each have different layouts and chair needs.
- **Large guest counts.** Stackable chairs make delivery and storage far easier at 300 or more guests.
- **Ceremonial seating.** The couple, parents and officiants often need distinct seating at the mandap.
- **Elders first.** Plan comfortable, supportive chairs close to the action for older guests.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many chairs does a South Asian wedding need?

Count each event separately. A sangeet, ceremony and reception may each need a full set, unless one set can be flipped between events at the same venue.

### What chairs go in the mandap?

Throne or ornate chairs for the couple and a few upholstered chairs for parents and the officiant. Confirm the mandap size so chairs fit with room to move.

### Can the same chairs be used for the ceremony and reception?

Yes, if there is time to move them. With large guest counts the flip takes longer, so plan the timeline with the venue and rental crew.
