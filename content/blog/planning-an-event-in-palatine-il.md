**Quick answer**

For a Palatine graduation or open-house party, you do not need a seat for every guest at once. Seat roughly 60 to 70 percent of the guest list at tables, and use cocktail tables for the rest. For 100 guests, that is about 65 to 70 chairs, 8 rounds and 8 to 10 cocktail tables. Teak garden folding chairs cost about $5 to $7 each.

</div>

Palatine is a northwest suburb with a walkable downtown, a Metra station, many parks run by the Palatine Park District and Harper College nearby. Graduation season, from late May through June, packs backyards and park shelters every weekend, and hosts often rent chairs for the first time. The most common mistake is renting one chair for every guest for a party that people drift through for hours. This guide shows a cheaper, better plan.

## Park shelters and tents

The Palatine Park District manages many parks and facilities. If you want to reserve a shelter or pavilion, contact the park district for availability, capacity and rules. Ask what tables are already there, because fixed picnic tables reduce what you need to rent. Ask also about decorations, alcohol, bounce houses and amplified music. For a backyard tent, check your village's permit rules; our [tent size guide](/blog/what-size-tent-do-i-need) and [permit guide](/blog/chicago-tent-permit) show what to ask.

In late May and June, thunderstorms are common in the Chicago area. A 20 x 40 foot frame tent covers roughly 66 guests at rounds, or about 80 for a standing mix. Confirm size with the tent company.

## Make the party comfortable

Heat is the main risk in a June party. Put drinks in the shade, give out cold water and set a few chairs in the shadiest area for older guests. Provide a clear path to restrooms, and a visible trash station to keep the yard tidy. For kids, a small table with games or crafts keeps them from filling adult seats.

## Food and drink for a long open house

A graduation party runs 3 to 4 hours, so food should hold. Choose items that sit well: sandwiches, pasta salads, fruit, a grazing table and a sheet cake. Hot items need chafing dishes or slow cookers, and cold items need ice. Plan about one drink table per 50 guests and replace ice at the midpoint. If you serve alcohol, check Illinois rules for private events and ask about any park district restrictions; a Chicago-area graduation party at a public shelter may not allow it at all.

Set a memory table with photos, a guest book and the graduate's awards, covered with a cloth and clear of the food line. A photo backdrop by the entrance keeps lines from forming in the middle of the party. Put a trash can within 15 feet of every table, since cups and plates pile up fast with a large crowd.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many chairs do I need for a graduation party of 100?

About 65 to 70 chairs if guests come and go over several hours, plus cocktail tables for standing. For a plated meal, order one chair per guest.

### How many cocktail tables for 100 guests?

Plan 8 to 10 cocktail tables, with 4 to 5 guests at each. Place them near food and the bar.

### Can I use a park shelter in Palatine?

Possibly. Contact the Palatine Park District about reservations, capacity and what is allowed in each shelter.

### Do folding chairs look good in photos?

Teak folding chairs look warm and natural, and they photograph well on grass. Pick a color that matches your decor.

### When should I book for graduation season?

As soon as you know the date, ideally 2 to 3 months ahead. Late May and June weekends are busy.

### Can rentals be delivered the day before?

Often yes, but ask. Early delivery can lock your setup in a protected space, though overnight storage must be safe from weather.
