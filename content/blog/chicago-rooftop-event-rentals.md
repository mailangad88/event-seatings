**Quick answer**

For a Chicago rooftop event, confirm four things with the building before you rent: the roof's posted capacity and any weight limits, whether tents, heaters or open flames are allowed, how furniture gets to the roof (stairs or elevator), and what wind plan the building expects. Choose heavier or tied-down furniture, skip tall lightweight decor and have an indoor backup.

</div>

A rooftop is the most photogenic place to host in the city and the most exposed. There is nothing between your guests and the wind except a parapet. Furniture that sits happily in a banquet hall can slide or tip at 30 stories. The good news is that rooftops reward simple, planned setups.

## Start with the building, not the furniture

A rooftop is a building system, not just a view. The owner or management company decides what is allowed. Ask for the rules in writing:

<ol class="flow">
<li><strong>Capacity.</strong> How many guests does the roof allow, and who sets the number?</li>
<li><strong>Load.</strong> Are there weight limits for furniture, a dance floor or a tent?</li>
<li><strong>Access.</strong> Stairs or elevator, and what size?</li>
<li><strong>Equipment.</strong> Are tents, heaters, string lights and generators permitted?</li>
<li><strong>Insurance.</strong> Does management want a certificate from vendors?</li>
</ol>

We cannot give you a citywide rule on rooftop tents or capacity, because we haven't verified one. For a tent, start with our [Chicago tent permit guide](/blog/chicago-tent-permit), then ask your building. A roof tent often needs anchoring that the building must approve.

- **Tie down.** Tablecloths should be weighted or clipped. Cloth napkins and menus fly.
- **Go low.** Short centerpieces beat tall ones. Skip floral arches and light banners unless they are anchored.
- **Heavier chairs help.** Wider, sturdier chairs resist tipping better than very light ones. The [cross-back chair](/chairs/cross-back) and [velvet dining chair](/chairs/velvet-dining) have more presence than a thin folding seat, though always check weight with your provider.
- **Use spandex covers on cocktail tables** where cloths would billow. Our [tablecloth size chart](/blog/tablecloth-size-chart) explains the options.
- **Have a plan B** for strong wind: an indoor space or an early move.

## Weight and surface

Roofs are made of membranes, pavers or decking, and some are fragile. High heels and narrow chair legs can puncture a membrane. Ask what surface you have and whether protective floor panels are needed. A dance floor adds weight, so confirm it with the building first. Our [dance floor size guide](/blog/dance-floor-size-by-guest-count) helps you decide whether you need one.

## Sun, shade and temperature

Rooftops are hot in July with no tree cover. Chicago's normal July high is about 84.5 F, according to the National Weather Service's 1991 to 2020 normals for O'Hare, and a rooftop surface can run hotter than that. Plan shade and water. In spring and fall, a rooftop feels colder than street level. Heat sources need building approval. For seasonal planning, see [Chicago summer event season planning](/blog/chicago-summer-event-season-planning).

## Logistics to confirm

- **Access path.** Stairs mean a smaller, lighter rental order. Elevator access still needs a reservation. See the [high-rise guide](/blog/chicago-high-rise-party-freight-elevator-coi).
- **Pickup time.** Rooftops often end late. Make sure staff can remove items.
- **Restrooms.** Confirm how many are reachable from the roof.
- **Sound.** Neighbors and other tenants care. Ask about quiet hours.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I put a tent on a Chicago rooftop?

It depends on the building and the City. Many roofs ban or restrict tents because of anchoring and fire rules. Ask management first, then check our tent permit guide for the permit side.

### What chairs work best on a windy rooftop?

Sturdier, wider chairs that do not tip easily. Very lightweight chairs can slide in a gust, so ask your provider about weight and use them where they are sheltered. Avoid stacking tall or loose items.

### How do I stop tablecloths from blowing off on a roof?

Use weighted hems, clips or fitted spandex covers, and keep centerpieces low. For cocktail tables, a fitted cover often holds better than a draped cloth.

### Do I need a certificate of insurance for a rooftop party?

Many buildings ask vendors for one. Ask management for the exact requirement and share it with your provider early.

### Can I use heaters or fire pits on a rooftop?

Often not. Open flames and propane are restricted in many buildings. Ask building management and the fire department's guidance before you plan a heat source.

### How many guests can a rooftop hold?

It depends on the posted capacity set by the building and fire code. Do not guess. Ask management for the number and keep seating inside it.
