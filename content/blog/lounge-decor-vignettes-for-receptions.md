A lounge vignette is a small, styled seating group: a sofa, two chairs, a coffee table, a rug and a lamp, arranged like a living room corner. It gives guests a place to sit, talk and take photos, and it makes a big banquet hall feel warmer. Here is how to plan vignettes for a Chicago-area reception without crowding the room.

## What goes into a vignette

- **A sofa or settee** as the anchor.
- **Two accent chairs** across from it. Velvet or cane adds texture.
- **A coffee table** that sits knee height.
- **A rug** to define the zone.
- **A lamp or candles** for warm light (battery candles if your venue bans flames).
- **A side table** for drinks.

Pair sofas with a [velvet dining chair](/chairs/velvet-dining), a [cane-back chair](/chairs/cane-back) or a [rattan garden chair](/chairs/rattan-garden) for different moods.

## How many vignettes do you need?

| Guests | Vignettes | Seats |
|---|---|---|
| 50 | 1 | 4 to 6 |
| 100 | 2 | 8 to 12 |
| 150 | 2 to 3 | 12 to 18 |
| 200 | 3 to 4 | 16 to 24 |

These are starting points. The goal is that about 10 to 15 percent of guests can sit in lounge seating at any time, which is a common rule of thumb for receptions with a dance floor and a cocktail hour. Event style changes it: a cocktail party leans on lounge furniture, a seated dinner needs less.

## Where to place them

<ol class="flow">
<li><strong>Near the dance floor edge</strong> so tired dancers can sit close.</li>
<li><strong>Near the bar</strong>, but not in the line.</li>
<li><strong>In a quiet corner</strong> for older guests.</li>
<li><strong>Near a photo wall</strong> to double as a portrait seat.</li>
<li><strong>Out of exits and aisles</strong> with a 3-foot path.</li>
</ol>

## Style recipes

- **Modern:** white sofa, black accent chairs, glass table, black rug.
- **Boho:** rattan or cane chairs, jute rug, pampas and candles.
- **Glam:** velvet sofa, gold table, mirror, mirrored tray.
- **Winter:** deep green velvet, faux fur throws, candles.
- **Garden:** wicker, wood table, greenery, soft pastel pillows.

## Practical details

Lounge furniture is heavy and white fabric shows spills. Ask the rental company about cleaning policies, how furniture is protected in tents and whether upholstery has a stain guarantee. Check the venue's load-in rules and elevator size. Outdoor lounges need weatherproof fabric and a plan for rain. Chicago evenings cool quickly after sunset, so provide blankets or heaters if the lounge is outdoors, and check venue rules on heaters.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Too many pieces and not enough walking space.
- Furniture too low for older guests.
- White upholstery near the bar.
- No light, so the lounge feels like a dark corner.


## Making a vignette feel finished

The difference between a pile of furniture and a vignette is layering. Start with the rug so the group has an edge. Place the sofa against a wall or a drape so its back is covered, then angle the accent chairs about 20 degrees toward each other instead of squaring them off. Add a low centerpiece that stays under 12 inches so conversation flows, plus one soft light source. Pillows and a throw add color, and a small sign or a framed photo of the couple gives guests a reason to stop.

## Tents, halls and rooftops

In a Chicago tent, lounge furniture needs a firm floor and a rug that keeps legs from sinking into grass. In a banquet hall, lounge groups work best in the corners and along the walls where tables would be awkward. On a rooftop or terrace, ask about wind and weight limits, and confirm the freight elevator can carry a sofa. Whatever the space, ask your rental planner for a scaled layout before delivery so the sofa fits through the door.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a lounge vignette?

A small styled seating group such as a sofa, chairs, coffee table, rug and lamp. It creates a living room feel in a larger space.

### How many lounge areas do I need?

About 1 for 50 guests, 2 for 100 and 3 to 4 for 200, depending on the event style. Cocktail-style parties need more than seated dinners.

### How much space does a lounge vignette need?

About 10 x 12 feet, or 120 square feet, plus a 3-foot walkway around it.

### Where should lounge seating go at a reception?

Near the dance floor edge, the bar and a photo wall, but out of aisles and exits.

### Can lounge furniture be used outdoors?

Yes if it is weatherproof or protected by a tent. Ask about stains, rain and wind.

### Do I need rugs under lounge furniture?

Rugs define the area and cover bare floors. They are optional but make a vignette look finished.
