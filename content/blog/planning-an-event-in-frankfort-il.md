Frankfort, in Will County, is a small-town southwest suburb with a historic downtown, the Old Plank Road Trail and a village green that anchors seasonal events. Its charm draws weddings, rehearsal dinners, bridal showers and anniversaries, often with 30 to 120 guests. At that size, details matter more than volume: the right table size, a thoughtful timeline and a plan for the season. This guide covers each.

## Rehearsal dinners and private party rooms in Frankfort

Many couples want the rehearsal dinner close to the wedding venue, and Frankfort's historic downtown has a few restaurants with private or semi-private rooms that suit a group of 15 to 40. A private room usually comes with its own tables and house chairs, so rentals there are limited to extras like a cake stand, a sign or special linens if the room's own setup doesn't match your colors. Call the restaurant directly to ask about room minimums, a fixed menu option and whether outside decor is allowed.

If no private room fits your group size or date, renting a small tent or a room at a banquet hall and bringing in your own chairs and tables gives you full control over the layout and guest count. This also works well for a rehearsal dinner that doubles as a welcome party for out-of-town guests.

## Downtown and public spaces

Frankfort's historic downtown and village green host community events, and public spaces have their own rules. If you want to hold a ceremony or party in a village property or park, contact the Village of Frankfort or the park district for the site to ask about permits, capacity, alcohol and tents. Downtown events share streets with shops, so ask about loading zones and parking. Do not set up on public property without permission.

## Rehearsal dinners and showers

Smaller events use the same ideas on a smaller scale. A rehearsal dinner for 25 works at a long table or three 60-inch rounds. Showers use rounds of 6 to 8, a gift table and a dessert table. Rent a few special chairs rather than a full set to dress up a small room, and use the venue's basics for the rest.

## Making a small guest list feel full

A 60-guest wedding can feel quiet in a large room and perfect in a smaller one. Match the room to the head count. If the space is bigger than you need, define zones with furniture: a lounge corner, a bar nook and a dance area marked by a rug or floor. Use rounds of 8 rather than 10 so tables feel lively, and pull them toward the center so the edges of the room stay open for photos and service.

Small weddings give you room to be generous where guests notice. Spend on comfortable chairs, good lighting and a longer cocktail hour, and trim what few people see. Keep the program short: a 25-minute ceremony, a few toasts and the first dance, then let the evening run. Chicago-area guests often travel from the city and farther, so offer a clear map, parking directions and a start time that leaves room for traffic.

## What to confirm two weeks before

1. The final head count, with any dietary needs, to the caterer and rental team.
2. The delivery window and the name and number of the on-site contact.
3. The floor plan, including table numbers and the dance floor position.
4. The weather plan: tent, indoor space and the decision time.
5. Pickup timing and who will stack chairs and clear tables.

A one-page sheet with these five items, shared with every vendor, prevents most last-minute surprises.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many chairs for a 60-guest wedding?

About 63 for the reception, plus the same for the ceremony if you flip, or a second set.

### What table size for a small wedding?

Rounds of 60 inches seat 8 and keep conversation easy. Use 72-inch rounds to save tables.

### When is the best time to book in Frankfort?

For summer and fall weekends, aim for 6 to 9 months out.

### Can I hold an event on the village green?

Contact the Village of Frankfort for permits and rules. Do not assume it is available.

### What chair is best for a classic look?

The Windsor spindle chair, with floor-length ivory linens.

### Do I need a tent?

For outdoor events, a tent is a smart backup. Ask the village about permits.

### Where can I have a rehearsal dinner in Frankfort, IL?

A handful of downtown Frankfort restaurants offer private or semi-private rooms for groups of 15 to 40, which suits most rehearsal dinners. Call ahead about room minimums and a fixed menu. For a larger group or full control over the layout, rent a small tent or book a banquet hall and bring in your own chairs and tables.

### Are there private party rooms in Frankfort, IL?

Yes, several downtown restaurants have private or semi-private rooms for rehearsal dinners, showers and small celebrations. Room size and minimums vary, so confirm your guest count and date directly with the restaurant before booking.
