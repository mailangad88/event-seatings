In La Grange, the La Grange Park District runs the village's parks, including Gordon Park, home of the Endless Summerfest, and the Village of La Grange handles streets and public property. Downtown sits on the BNSF line with limited curb space, so plan the truck stop first. Ask the right office about tents, alcohol and noise before you book rentals.

La Grange is a classic Cook County village: a walkable downtown with a Metra station, a mix of older homes and cottages on modest lots, and a calendar of community events. It is close enough to Chicago that many events here pull guests in from the city. Space and parking, not permits, are usually the main challenge.

## Gordon Park and the summer calendar

Gordon Park hosts the village's Endless Summerfest, a long-running summer event, and other community gatherings. When you pick a date for any event near the park or downtown, check the festival calendar first. Parking, noise and foot traffic rise on festival weekends, which can help a public event and hurt a private one.

The La Grange Park District manages park use and shelters. The village handles downtown streets, sidewalks and public property. If your plan touches both, call both and ask which comes first.

## Space types in La Grange

- **Park shelters and lawns.** Through the park district. Ask about capacity, tents and alcohol.
- **Downtown streets and storefronts.** Village rules and permits apply.
- **Restaurants, halls and churches.** Indoor space for receptions and showers.
- **Private backyards.** Older lots can be narrow and deep.
- **Nearby forest preserves and golf courses.** Separate agencies with their own booking processes.

## Narrow lots and tight streets

Many La Grange homes sit on lots where the backyard is a rectangle of 30 to 40 feet wide. That fits about four to six 60-inch round tables with room to walk. Streets near downtown are lined with parked cars on weekends, so a delivery truck may need a different stop than the front door. Tell us the street, the driveway width and any alley access.

For a larger party, a park shelter or a hall is easier than a yard. Our [guide to table counts](/blog/how-many-tables-for-50-100-150-200-guests) shows how many guests different layouts hold.

## Delivery from St. Charles

La Grange is a long drive east and south from St. Charles into Cook County, along I-88, I-294 or arterials. We avoid rush hour on those roads. Downtown loading is tight on weekends, so we ask for the best stop and any restrictions on the block.

## Seasonal notes

Summer is the busiest season, with community events and long evenings. Sunset is about 8:30 pm at the June solstice, and about 4:20 pm in mid-December, so winter parties need lighting early. Fall brings leaf cover on lawns, so check the ground before you place tables.

## A La Grange example

For a 50-guest backyard graduation party, plan six rounds of 8 (48 seats) and one banquet table for food. [Cross-back chairs](/chairs/cross-back) (about $8-11 each, an estimate) make a small yard look finished, while [garden folding chairs](/chairs/garden-folding) (about $5-7) are a lighter budget choice.

## Neighbors, noise and the end time

In a dense village, a party affects the whole block. Tell neighbors the date, the hours and a phone number. Ask the village about noise rules and end times for amplified music. If you plan a tent, ask whether a permit is required, and read [tent permits in Chicago suburbs](/blog/tent-permit-chicago-suburbs). A polite note a week ahead prevents most complaints.

## Choosing a rental list for a village party

Village parties work best with a short, clear list. Start with seating, then add only what the space can hold.

- **Seating first.** One chair per guest who will sit at once. For an open house, plan seats for about 60% of the guest list.
- **Tables second.** Rounds for dinner, a banquet table for food, a small table for gifts or cards.
- **Linens third.** Floor-length cloths on dining tables hide legs and extension cords. The [tablecloth size chart](/blog/tablecloth-size-chart) lists exact sizes.
- **Extras last.** Heaters, fans, a tent or a dance floor, only if the space and the budget allow.

A train-adjacent village like La Grange also means some guests will walk from the station, so a clear sign at the street and a path that is easy on heels helps. If you are hosting guests from Chicago, share the Metra schedule and the walking route in your invitation.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who manages parks in La Grange?

The La Grange Park District. Ask it about shelters, Gordon Park and rules for events.

### What is the Endless Summerfest?

A summer community event held in Gordon Park. Check the village calendar for dates before you plan a nearby event.

### How many tables fit in a small La Grange yard?

About four to six 60-inch rounds in a 30 by 40 foot yard, with room to walk. Measure first.

### Do I need a permit for a block party?

Ask the Village of La Grange. Events that use streets need village approval.

### Do you deliver in La Grange?

Yes. First deliveries begin with the April 2027 Founding Season. Tell us about narrow streets and alley access.

Cross-back chairs look polished; garden folding chairs are the lighter budget choice.
