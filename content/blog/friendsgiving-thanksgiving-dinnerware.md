**Quick answer**

For Thanksgiving or Friendsgiving, plan 2 plates per guest (dinner and dessert), 1 fork, knife and spoon each, and 2 glasses. For 12 guests that is 24 plates, 12 forks and 24 glasses; for 20 guests, 40 plates; for 30 guests, 60 plates. Add platters for turkey, bowls for sides and a pie stand. Book early: November weekends are a peak time for party rentals.

Thanksgiving is the one dinner where a home table suddenly needs to seat 20. The turkey is easy; the plates are the problem. ## Table and seating

Twenty people need two or three tables and 20 chairs. A long table of 8-foot banquet tables set end to end works well in a home with a big room; see [how many chairs do I need](/blog/how-many-chairs-do-i-need). Allow about 24 inches per guest, as in [place setting spacing](/blog/place-setting-spacing-measurements). Add a kids' table for younger guests; see [dinnerware for kids](/blog/dinnerware-for-kids-at-events).

## Serve style

Most hosts serve Thanksgiving family-style or as a buffet. Family-style needs a full set of platters at each table, as in [family-style serving ware](/blog/family-style-serving-ware). A buffet keeps the table clear, which suits a crowded home. Compare options in [dinnerware sizes for buffet, plated and family-style](/blog/dinnerware-sizes-buffet-plated-family-style).

## Style and color

Fall colors suit Thanksgiving: rust, olive, cream and gold. White plates give a neutral base with colorful napkins. A gold-rim plate adds a bit of polish; see [gold-rim vs white china](/blog/gold-rim-vs-white-china). A runner down a long table is easier than a full cloth; see [matching dinnerware to linens](/blog/matching-dinnerware-to-linens).

## Cleanup after the meal

After a long meal, the last thing anyone wants is a sink full of plates. Rental dishes are scraped and stacked rather than washed; see [do you wash rental dishes](/blog/do-you-wash-rental-dishes). Assign two people to clear and crate while the others relax.

## Holiday timing in Chicago

Late November brings cold, early dark and travel traffic. Delivery windows can be tighter near the holiday, and some companies close on the day itself. Ask about delivery and pickup dates around the holiday, and whether you can return on the Friday after or on the following Monday.

## Small touches that make the table

Place cards help with seating a mixed group of friends and family. Small bud vases and candles leave room for dishes better than a large centerpiece. Set out a basket of extra napkins, since this meal is messy. Plan a spot for coats and bags so the table stays clear. If you serve wine, count one glass per guest for water and one for wine, and plan a separate cart for beverages. Early darkness in Chicago in November makes candlelight and warm bulbs part of the look.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many plates do I need for Thanksgiving dinner?

Plan 2 per guest: one for dinner and one for dessert. For 20 guests, that is 40 plates plus a small cushion.

### Can I rent dishes for Friendsgiving?

Yes. Rental dishes make a large Friendsgiving easier, with matching plates and no washing. Ask for a quote with your guest count and date.

### How early should I book for Thanksgiving?

Aim for three weeks out, and earlier if you can. November is busy for holiday parties.

### Do I need a separate fork for dessert?

Not necessarily. A dinner fork works for pie. If you want a formal look, add dessert forks.

### What serving pieces do I need?

A large platter for turkey, bowls for sides, a gravy boat, a bread basket and pie stands. Add serving utensils for each dish.

### How do I handle leftovers?

Plan containers for takeaway, and keep rental dishes separate so they do not leave with guests.
