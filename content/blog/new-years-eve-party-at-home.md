**Quick answer**

A New Year's Eve party is a standing party with a seated pause. Rent seats for 40 to 50 percent of guests, cocktail tables for the rest, a bar with at least one bartender station per 40 guests, and a food table that can be grazed all night. Plan a toast at midnight with a glass in every hand, which means roughly 4 to 5 glasses per guest across the evening.

</div>

New Year's Eve runs longer than most parties, usually 6 hours from first guest to last. People arrive hungry, stay for the midnight toast, and drift out afterward. The rentals you need follow that arc: seating early, standing mid-evening and a clear room at midnight.

## What to rent

### Seating
Chairs fill the edges, not the middle. Put clusters of 4 to 6 in corners, along walls and beside the food. [Velvet dining chairs](/chairs/velvet-dining), estimated $18 to $25, feel like a party. [Ghost chairs](/chairs/ghost), about $10 to $14, stay out of the way of sparkly decor. Lounge pieces add a place to sit with a drink. See [lounge furniture for cocktail hour](/blog/lounge-furniture-for-cocktail-hour).

### Tables
- 42-inch cocktail tables, one for every 8 to 10 standing guests. Read [how many cocktail tables](/blog/how-many-cocktail-tables-for-standing-reception).
- A 6-foot or 8-foot table for food and desserts.
- A separate table for the bar.

### Bar
The bar is where the evening lives. One bar table with one bartender serves about 40 guests comfortably. For 75 guests, build two points of service. See [bar setup for 100 guests](/blog/bar-setup-for-100-guests) for the layout. Put the bar away from the entrance, and put a water station beside it.

### Glassware
Midnight needs a champagne flute or coupe for each guest, in addition to your cocktail glasses. See [champagne flutes vs coupes](/blog/champagne-flutes-vs-coupes). Pre-fill or pre-pour at 11:45 so the toast is on time.

## Chicago notes

December 31 brings cold, so coats are the main concern. See [holiday party at home](/blog/holiday-party-at-home-rentals-chicago) for coat racks and flow. Rideshares and street parking are tight on New Year's Eve, so tell guests to arrange rides and plan for overnight guests. Delivery often has to happen on Dec 30 or earlier, and pickup is likely Jan 2, since holiday schedules vary. Ask when you book. Check noise rules with your village, since midnight noise is often policed lightly but not at all villages.

## Keep the late hours comfortable

A six-hour party puts more wear on the space than a three-hour one. Think about what happens around hour four.

- **Food runs out quietly.** Plan a second wave of snacks at 10:30 or 11, not just the first spread. Cold items such as cheese, fruit and dessert bars last, while hot dishes need warmers.
- **Restrooms get busy.** If you have more than 40 guests and a single bathroom, stock it with extra hand towels and a trash can, and check it every hour.
- **Coats pile up.** Keep the rack near the door so guests can leave without hunting through a bedroom at 1 a.m.
- **Noise travels.** Face speakers into the room, away from neighbors, and turn the volume down after midnight.
- **Rides matter.** Post a note with rideshare tips or a list of friends who can host overnight guests. A table of water and snacks at the door is a kind last stop.

Finally, plan the cleanup. Rental items can be stacked and left for pickup, so you do not need to wash everything at 2 a.m. Ask your rental company how to prepare dishes and linens, and leave tables folded and chairs stacked by the door.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many chairs do I need for a New Year's Eve party?

About 40 to 50 percent of your guests, in small groups along the walls. For 50 guests, 22 to 25 chairs.

### How many drinks per guest should I plan?

A common planning rule is one drink per guest per hour, plus a midnight toast. That is 5 to 6 drinks across a 5-hour party. Our [glass counts](/blog/how-many-glasses-per-guest) give details.

### Do I need a bartender?

For over 30 guests, yes. A hired bartender keeps the line short and lets you enjoy the party. Check Illinois rules for private events before you serve; see [alcohol rules for private events](/blog/illinois-alcohol-rules-private-events).

### How do I handle the midnight toast?

Have flutes ready by 11:45, cleared from the bar. Use trays or a table, not a line, and assign a friend to pour.

### When will my rentals be delivered and picked up?

Most rental companies deliver the day before or the morning of, and pick up the next business day. Holiday schedules vary, so confirm.

### Should I do a sit-down dinner instead?

If you want a dinner, plan a seat per guest and start earlier. Many hosts do dinner at 8, and open the room for dancing at 10.
