Oak Brook is a business-heavy DuPage village with corporate campuses, hotels, a large shopping center and pockets of parkland and forest preserve. Most events here are company parties, fundraisers and conferences. That changes the rental list: fewer picnic tables, more linens and polished seating.

## Rules to read first

The Village of Oak Brook's code includes a Special Events chapter, so outdoor and public-space events need a look at village rules. The Oak Brook Park District runs parks and facilities, including recreation spaces. Graue Mill and Fullersburg Woods sit on forest preserve land managed by the Forest Preserve District of DuPage County, not the village.

## Space types

- **Hotels and conference centers.** The usual home for galas and corporate parties. Ask what furniture is included and what must be rented.
- **Office campuses and lobbies.** Company events often use these. The landlord sets the rules.
- **Park district spaces.** Rentals through the Oak Brook Park District.
- **Forest preserve and historic sites.** Graue Mill is a landmark with its own booking process, so contact the preserve.
- **Private estates and clubs.** Common for weddings.

## Corporate events: what changes

A company party has a different planning rhythm from a wedding. Headcounts firm up late, budgets are approved by committee, and branding matters. Ask for linens in company colors, and ask about spare chairs for last-minute guests. For a 200-guest awards dinner, plan 25 rounds of 8, plus a head table and staging. ## Checklist for a corporate event in Oak Brook

Corporate planners often juggle more than one stakeholder, so a checklist helps.

- **Contract and insurance:** confirm that the rental company can provide a certificate of insurance.
- **Branding:** list items that need logos or colors, such as napkins, linens or signs.
- **Accessibility:** wide aisles and ramp access matter, and venues have rules.
- **Dietary and service:** your caterer's needs affect plates, flatware and chafers.
- **Backup:** have a plan for headcount changes of 10% either way.

## Planning around conference and hotel rules

Hotels and conference centers in Oak Brook usually have in-house banquet teams, and many have policies about outside vendors. Before you hire a rental company:

1. Ask whether outside rentals are allowed and whether there is a fee.
2. Ask for the room's dimensions and a floor plan, so we can check table counts.
3. Confirm load-in hours, dock location and elevator size.
4. Ask who sets up and who clears: the hotel's staff, ours or yours.
5. Confirm insurance requirements in writing.

These answers change the quote more than the chairs do. A venue that charges a fee for outside rentals may make an in-house package cheaper, or the opposite. We do not know your venue's terms, so ask, and share the answers with us on a call.
