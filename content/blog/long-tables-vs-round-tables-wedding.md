**Quick answer**

Round tables win for guest count, flexibility and easy conversation among 8 to 10 people. Long tables win for a communal, family-style look and for narrow rooms. A 150-guest wedding takes about 19 rounds of 8 or about 16 long tables of 10. Pick rounds for ballrooms and mixed groups, and long tables for barns, lofts and tents.

</div>

Planners in Chicago hear this question in the first call: should we do round tables or long ones? Neither is better in every room. The right answer depends on your venue shape, guest list and how you want people to talk. Use this guide to decide.

## Side by side

<div class="table-wrap">

|  | Round tables | Long tables |
|---|---|---|
| Guests per table | 8 to 10 | 10 to 20 or more |
| Conversation | Easy across the table | Mostly with neighbors |
| Room shape | Square and ballroom | Long and narrow |
| Linens | One cloth per table | Fewer, longer cloths |
| Centerpieces | One per table | A continuous runner |
| Photo feel | Classic, traditional | Communal, modern |
| Flexibility | Easy to add or drop one | Harder to change |

## When round tables are the right call

- **Mixed groups.** Eight to ten people can all talk to each other. This is why rounds are the default at weddings with friends and relatives who do not know each other.
- **Ballrooms and banquet halls.** Most Chicago banquet halls and hotel ballrooms plan around 60 and 72 inch rounds. See [round table sizes](/blog/round-table-sizes-and-seating-48-60-66-72-inch).
- **Plated service.** Servers can reach every guest in a predictable pattern.
- **Late RSVP changes.** Dropping one guest from a round barely changes the picture.

## When long tables are the right call

- **Barn, loft and warehouse venues.** Long open rooms suit long rows. Read [barn wedding seating ideas](/blog/barn-wedding-seating-ideas).
- **Smaller guest lists.** Under 60 guests, one or two long tables feel like a dinner party.
- **Family-style meals.** Platters pass down a row.
- **Tent weddings.** Rectangular tents fit rows efficiently. See [farm tables for weddings](/blog/farm-tables-for-weddings).

## The space math

For 100 guests with a dance floor, a 40 x 60 foot room is a rough minimum for rounds at 10 square feet per guest, which is 1,000 sq ft, plus about 300 for the dance floor and bar. Long tables use about 8 sq ft per guest in a row layout, because less floor is lost to aisles between islands. Those are rules of thumb, so always confirm with your venue's floor plan.

<ol class="flow">
<li><strong>Measure the room</strong> length, width and fixed obstacles.</li>
<li><strong>Mark the dance floor</strong> about 3 to 4 sq ft per dancing guest.</li>
<li><strong>Place the rows or rounds</strong> keep 5 ft between table edges.</li>
<li><strong>Check the exits</strong> fire exits must stay clear. Ask your venue about code.</li>
<li><strong>Confirm with the venue</strong> most have a published capacity per layout.</li>
</ol>

## Can you do both?

Yes. Many Chicago-area couples use rounds for guests and one long table for the wedding party or family. See [how to mix round and long tables](/blog/mix-round-and-long-tables). A long head table in front of rounds is the traditional version; a long family table with rounds around it is the newer one.

## Cost drivers

Each approach changes your rental list differently. Rounds need more linens and centerpieces. Long tables need fewer tables, but wood farm tables and special pieces are priced differently from folding banquet tables. ## Chairs

The look of the table changes what chairs read best. Rounds hide most of the chair behind guests, so comfort matters. Long tables show a row of chairs, so a consistent style such as [cross-back](/chairs/cross-back) or [wishbone](/chairs/wishbone) looks deliberate.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are round or long tables better for a wedding?

Round tables are better for mixing groups and for ballroom rooms. Long tables are better for a communal look in a narrow room. Many couples use a mix.

### Do long tables fit more guests?

Not necessarily. Rows use floor space well in narrow rooms, but rounds fit more in square ones. Always compare the same room.

### Are long tables cheaper than round tables?

Not always. Fewer cloths and centerpieces can lower costs, but wood tables can cost more to rent than folding rounds. Request a quote for both layouts.

### How many people sit at a long wedding table?

A 6-foot table seats 6 to 8 and an 8-foot table seats 8 to 10. Join tables for longer rows.

### Can I have a long head table with round guest tables?

Yes, and it is the classic setup. Leave 6 to 8 feet in front of the head table for the dance floor or a sightline. See [head table setups](/blog/head-table-setups-long-u-e-shape).

### Which table style is best for a small wedding?

For under 50 guests, one or two long tables feel intimate and use less room. For 50 to 100, rounds or a mix both work.
