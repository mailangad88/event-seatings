**Quick answer**

A move-in or anniversary party works as a mingle with one short program. Seat 30 to 50% of guests, use cocktail tables and lounge groups, set up a bar and two food stations, and place a small stage or podium for a 10 minute toast. Clear desks, protect carpets and confirm freight elevator times with building management.

</div>

A company milestone is a chance to show employees and clients what you have built. It is also a party in a space designed for work: carpet, desks, glass walls and limited power. The layout should respect both. This guide covers a new-office open house and an anniversary celebration, whether in an office in the Loop, a suburban office park or a warehouse or showroom space.

## Protect the building

Office carpets stain easily and glass walls show every fingerprint. Use table covers and cups with lids near equipment, and add floor protection under bars and food stations if the building asks. Ask building management about freight elevator reservations, loading dock rules and any fees. Many require a certificate of insurance from vendors; see [corporate event COI and vendor requirements](/blog/corporate-event-coi-and-vendor-requirements). Confirm fire code rules on exits and capacity with the building, since an open floor with 150 guests may have an occupancy limit.

## Tours and storytelling

If the party is a move-in, a short tour gives guests a reason to walk the space. Create three stops: the main work floor, the team's favorite spot and the kitchen or lounge. Put a small sign at each, with a team member to explain it. For an anniversary, a wall of real photos, product milestones or customer notes tells the story better than a speech. Stick to facts you can verify with your own records. Give each guest a card or a small keepsake with the date and the company name. These moments cost little, yet they make a party feel personal rather than catered.

## Employees versus clients

Decide who the party is for. An employee celebration can be casual, with music, games and a team photo. A client celebration needs a host for every ten guests, a clear welcome and a tidy workspace. If both groups attend, set a separate area for each, or open with a client hour followed by a team hour. Mixed events work when the program is brief and the seating encourages introductions.

## Seating style

A move-in party reflects your brand. For a modern office, [ghost chairs](/chairs/ghost) keep the space airy. For a warm, creative studio, [bentwood chairs](/chairs/bentwood) or [rattan chairs](/chairs/rattan-garden) soften the feel. For a firm celebrating a major anniversary, [velvet chairs](/chairs/velvet-dining) give a formal touch. ## Food and drink

Stations in the kitchen or breakroom area keep the main floor clear. A caterer can use the building's kitchen if permitted; ask facilities. Drink dispensers and a bar cart cut the line. A cake or dessert table near the podium gives the toast a focus. For catering equipment, see [food station ideas and equipment](/blog/food-station-ideas-and-equipment).

## Chicago note

Many Chicago office buildings schedule deliveries and setup after hours. Ask for the time window and plan on staff or a vendor to receive the order. In winter, plan a coat area at the elevator lobby. Summer events can use a rooftop or terrace if the building allows; confirm access and weather options.

## Frequently asked questions

### What do I need to rent for an office party?

Cocktail tables, chairs or lounge pieces, a bar and food tables, and possibly a podium or mic. Check what the office already has.

### How many guests fit in an office space?

It depends on the floor area and fire code. Use 10 to 15 square feet per person for standing and ask building management for the posted occupancy.

### Should we do a seated dinner or a mingle?

Mingle works for most office spaces. A seated dinner needs much more floor area.

### How do I protect the carpet?

Use lids on drinks, floor mats under the bar and ask caterers to avoid red wine and dark sauces near sensitive areas.

### Who sets up the furniture?

Many rental orders include delivery to the floor and setup. Confirm the scope and the building's rules.

### What do I need for an anniversary display?

A table or two with a cloth, easels and photos or items from the company's real history.
