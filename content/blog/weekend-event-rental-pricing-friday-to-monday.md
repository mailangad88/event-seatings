**Quick answer**

Many rental companies treat a weekend as one rental day: items go out Friday and come back Monday for a single rental price. That is a common practice, not a rule. It depends on the company, the item and the season. Ask whether your quote covers Friday to Monday, what counts as an extra day, and whether delivery and pickup are priced on the day you choose.

</div>

Your party is Saturday. The truck cannot bring everything at 4:00 p.m. on the same day, and you do not want to hunt for a pickup crew at midnight. So you ask for Friday delivery and Monday pickup. Will that cost three or four days of rent? Often not. Many companies price the weekend in a block. Here is how to check yours.

In Chicago the question matters more than it seems. Summer weekends, graduation season and the big holiday weekends fill truck schedules, and a Friday drop in a two-flat backyard or a Monday pickup from a busy block both need a clear plan with the company.

Rental companies often price by "rental period", not by the calendar. Items go out, they come back, and the period is a day, a weekend or a week. Because many businesses are closed on Sunday or run fewer crews, a Friday-to-Monday weekend is a natural block. It is a practice we see described widely, but each company sets its own terms.

## When one weekend price holds

- **Standard items** like chairs, tables and linens at many companies.
- **Events on Saturday** with Friday delivery and Monday pickup.
- **Off-peak dates**, when trucks and stock are open.

## When it may not

<div class="table-wrap">

| Situation | What can change |
|---|---|
| Tents, heaters, generators | These are often priced by day, event or install, not by weekend |
| Peak weekends (June, prom and graduation season, holidays) | Companies may charge extra or limit pickup days |
| Long weekends (Memorial Day, July 4th, Labor Day) | A Monday holiday can mean a Tuesday pickup and an added day |
| Sunday events | Pickup may be Monday morning, but some companies price Sunday-to-Monday differently |
| Friday events | The weekend block may start Thursday |
| Specialty items | Dance floors, games and lighting may use their own terms |

</div>

Always ask by item type. One quote can include some items on a weekend block and others by the day.

## What most hosts find out too late

**Monday pickup is not always Monday.** If Monday is a holiday, the company may be closed and pick up Tuesday. Ask what happens and whether it adds a day. This matters around Memorial Day, July 4th and Labor Day weekends. Also see [late-night and early pickup fees](/blog/late-night-and-early-pickup-fees-event-rentals) for the other direction.

**Friday delivery means a night of rentals sitting at your place.** Items stay in your garage, backyard or venue, and you are responsible for them. Read [keeping rentals safe overnight](/blog/keeping-rentals-safe-overnight-outdoors) before you pick Friday.

**A weekend price does not cover a late return.** If items stay out past Monday, the company may bill another day. Say when you will have everything stacked and ready.

## Questions to ask

<ol class="flow">
<li><strong>What is my rental period?</strong> Ask for the exact days in writing.</li>
<li><strong>Is the weekend priced as one day?</strong> Ask by item type, especially tents and heaters.</li>
<li><strong>What is the pickup window on Monday?</strong> And what if Monday is a holiday?</li>
<li><strong>What counts as an extra day?</strong> And what does it cost relative to the item price?</li>
<li><strong>Are delivery and pickup charged separately?</strong> See <a href="/blog/event-rental-delivery-fees-explained">delivery fees explained</a>.</li>
</ol>

## When to take the weekend block, and when not to

If your event is Saturday and your home or venue can hold the items safely, a Friday delivery and Monday pickup is a calm way to do it. If you want the least handling, a Saturday morning delivery and Saturday night or Sunday morning pickup may cost less in item days but more in timing fees. Compare the totals. ## Frequently asked questions

### Is a weekend rental priced as one day?

Often, yes, for common items like chairs, tables and linens. It is a company policy, so ask. Tents, heaters and other specialty items may be priced differently.

### Can I get Friday delivery and Monday pickup?

Usually, if the company has space on its schedule. Peak weekends fill quickly, so ask early. You are responsible for the items while they are with you.

### What if my event is on Sunday?

Some companies still price Friday-to-Monday or Saturday-to-Monday as one block. Others count Sunday differently. Ask for the rental period in writing.

### What happens if Monday is a holiday?

Pickup may move to Tuesday, and some companies add a day. Ask on Memorial Day, Labor Day and similar weekends.

### Do tents and heaters follow the weekend rule?

Not always. Tents are often priced by size and install, and heaters by event or day, plus fuel. Ask for each one by name.

### How do I ask for a weekend rate?

Say the dates: "Friday delivery, Saturday event, Monday pickup. Is this one rental period?" Then ask what an extra day costs.
