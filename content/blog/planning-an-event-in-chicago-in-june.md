## June weather: what to watch out for

June is warm, green and stormy. NWS normals for O'Hare give June an average high of 80.4°F and a low of 60.8°F. Some afternoons reach the 90s, and summer thunderstorms can build fast in the afternoon.

What to plan for:

- **Storms.** A tent with sidewalls, a wind plan and a clear rule for when to move guests. Lightning means everyone goes inside a building or vehicle, not just under a tent.
- **Sun and heat.** Afternoon ceremonies in full sun are hard on guests. Shade, water and an evening start help. See our [summer event heat plan](/blog/summer-event-heat-plan-chicago).
- **The longest days.** Sunset is about 8:17 pm on June 1 and 8:29 pm by June 28. You can run an outdoor dinner in daylight, then switch on string lights.
- **Bugs.** Evenings near water or long grass bring mosquitoes. Ask your venue about treatment, and keep repellent at the bar.

Read [outdoor events in Chicago in June](/blog/outdoor-events-chicago-in-june) for the full weather picture.

## Venue demand and booking lead times in June

June is the busiest month for many Chicago venues and rental items. Saturdays at popular venues are often booked a year or more ahead, and tents, dance floors and the most popular chairs go months ahead. Our rule of thumb for June: book the venue first, then tents and specialty chairs 2 to 4 months ahead at the very latest, earlier for large weddings.

If your date is fixed and the venue is full, consider a Friday, a Sunday or a backyard with a tent. Our [peak season event rentals guide](/blog/peak-season-event-rentals-chicago) explains how to plan around the rush.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is June the busiest month for events in Chicago?

It is one of them, along with September and October. Weddings and graduation parties fill Saturdays, so popular venues and tents are booked months ahead.

### What is the weather like in Chicago in June?

Warm with storms. NWS normals for O'Hare give an average high of 80.4°F and a low of 60.8°F. Afternoon thunderstorms are the main risk for outdoor events.

### When is the Chicago Pride Parade in 2027?

The parade is traditionally held on the last Sunday of June, which is June 27, 2027. Check the organizers' announcement, and expect large closures on the North Side.

### When is Juneteenth in 2027?

Juneteenth falls on Saturday, June 19, 2027, and the federal holiday is observed Friday, June 18. Community celebrations are often outdoors, so plan shade and a rain option.

### How far ahead should I book a tent for June in Chicago?

At least 2 to 4 months ahead, and earlier for a large wedding tent. June Saturdays are the first dates to sell out.

### What time is sunset in Chicago in June?

About 8:17 pm on June 1, 8:26 pm on June 15 and 8:29 pm on June 28 in 2027, the latest of the year.
