## April weather: what to watch out for

April brings the first warm days, but averages are cool. NWS normals for O'Hare give April an average high of 59.0°F and a low of 40.3°F, a 19-degree swing inside a day. Rain is frequent, and the ground stays soft long after it stops.

What to plan for:

- **A rain plan that is booked, not hoped for.** A tent or indoor room reserved at the same time as everything else. Our [rain plan with a tent](/blog/rain-plan-with-a-tent) guide shows how.
- **Warmth after sunset.** Sunset moves from about 7:14 pm on April 1 to 7:43 pm by April 28, and temperatures drop fast after it. Heaters or a move indoors for dinner keep guests comfortable.
- **Ground protection.** Wet lawns and high heels do not mix. Flooring under a ceremony or a dance area makes a big difference.

For a deeper weather breakdown, read [outdoor events in Chicago in April](/blog/outdoor-events-chicago-in-april).

## Venue demand and booking lead times in April

April is when demand starts climbing. Early April still has open dates, but from late April into May, Saturdays fill with weddings, showers, proms, communions and early graduation parties. Our rule of thumb for peak-season Saturdays is to reserve tents, specialty chairs and dance floors 2 to 4 months ahead, and to book the venue first.

If your event is outdoors in a Chicago park, permits take time. Read the [Chicago Park District event permit guide](/blog/chicago-park-district-event-permit) before you set a date. Suburban park districts have their own rules and calendars, so check with your village.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is April a good month for an outdoor event in Chicago?

Only with a booked backup. April averages a 59°F high and a 40°F low at O'Hare, with frequent rain, so reserve a tent or indoor space at the same time as your rentals.

### When is Passover in 2027?

Passover begins the evening of Wednesday, April 21, 2027. Large family seders at home often need extra tables, chairs and linens, so reserve them a few weeks ahead.

### How far ahead should I book rentals for an April event?

For early April, 4 to 6 weeks is often enough. For late April Saturdays, treat it like peak season and book tents and specialty chairs 2 to 4 months ahead.

### Do I need a permit for an event in a Chicago park in April?

Usually, for organized events and anything with rentals or a tent. Apply well ahead and check the Chicago Park District's current rules, or your suburban park district's if you are outside the city.

### What time is sunset in Chicago in April?

About 7:14 pm on April 1, 7:29 pm on April 15 and 7:43 pm on April 28 in 2027. It cools quickly afterward, so plan heat or an indoor dinner.
