**Quick answer**

A white tent in direct sun can feel hotter inside than the outdoor air. Keep it comfortable by opening the sides for cross-breeze, adding fans or misters, using solid sidewalls on the sunny side, picking a shaded site, and scheduling dinner after the heat of the day. For a long formal event or very hot weather, ask your tent company about air conditioning, which needs real power and sealed sidewalls.

Summer weddings and graduations in Chicago bring humid days where the heat index climbs well past the air temperature. Under a tent, the sun heats the roof, the roof heats the air and the walls hold it in. The Choose Chicago visitor guide puts summer daytime highs between about 78 and 92 F, and lake breezes help near the shore but not inland. Cooling is possible, and it starts with where the tent goes.

The solar load on the roof, closed sidewalls, body heat from guests and heat from cooking nearby add up. A 100-person tent with closed walls on a humid day behaves like a small greenhouse.

## The scheduling fix

Heat peaks mid to late afternoon. A 5 p.m. ceremony in a dark suit under a tent is harder than a 6:30 p.m. ceremony. If your venue allows, schedule the ceremony and the start of dinner for after the hottest hours. Summer sunsets in Chicago come late, so an evening start still leaves plenty of daylight to set up.

## Airflow beats everything for the price

1. **Open opposite sides** to create a cross-breeze. Wind through the tent is the cheapest cooling there is.
2. **Raise the sidewalls** a few feet instead of removing them for a partial screen.
3. **Add vented tops** if your company offers them, so hot air has an exit.
4. **Place fans** at the entries and along the edges, angled toward seating.
5. **Keep cooking outside** and away. Codes often keep cooking 20 feet from the tent anyway. See [permits in the suburbs](/blog/tent-permit-chicago-suburbs).

## Air conditioning in a tent

AC works, but it asks a lot. The tent must be mostly sealed, which means solid or clear sidewalls all around, and the system needs real power, often a generator. The cooling capacity needed depends on the tent volume, the sun, the walls and the crowd. Ask your tent company to size it for your tent, your date and your guest count. Expect it to be a major line item. We cannot quote a price. For generator safety and distances, see [tent lighting ideas](/blog/tent-lighting-ideas).

## Guest comfort extras

- Hand fans or printed programs that double as fans for the ceremony.
- Cold towels or a chilled water station.
- Shaded lounge spots outside the tent.
- Ice-cold drinks at the door. Check [tent layout with a bar](/blog/tent-layout-dance-floor-and-bar).
- Light linens and lighter food.

## Choose seating that stays cool

On hot days, chairs matter: slatted and cane seats breathe, while thick velvet traps heat. A [rattan garden chair](/chairs/rattan-garden) or [cane-back chair](/chairs/cane-back) keeps guests cooler, and clear [ghost chairs](/chairs/ghost) look light. For a hot day, skip heavy chair covers.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you keep a tent cool in summer?

Choose a shaded site, open opposite sides for cross-breeze, use fans, and schedule key moments for evening. For hot, formal events, add air conditioning and sealed walls.

### Is it hotter inside a tent than outside?

Often yes, especially in direct sun with closed walls. The roof and body heat trap warmth, so airflow and shade matter.

### Do misting fans work in Chicago humidity?

They help on dry days and do less on very humid ones, since moist air cools slowly. Fans without water are the safer choice on sticky days.

### Can you air condition a tent?

Yes, with the right units and power, and with the sidewalls closed. Ask your tent company to size the equipment for your tent and guest count.

### What time of day is best for a summer tent event?

Late afternoon into evening, after the peak heat, usually feels best. Aim to start dinner after about 6 p.m. if you can.

### Should I use solid or clear sidewalls in summer?

Neither if you can leave sides open. If you must close one side, use solid white on the sunny side because clear vinyl traps heat.
