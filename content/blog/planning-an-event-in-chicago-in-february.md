## February weather: what to watch out for

February is still winter. NWS normals for O'Hare give an average high of 35.7°F and a low of 21.8°F, and snow is common. Cold snaps can push well below the average for days at a time.

What to plan for:

- **Indoor first.** Treat any outdoor element as optional: a heated tent with sidewalls is the only realistic way to use outdoor space.
- **Snow and deliveries.** Avoid a tight load-in window. If your venue only allows a 2-hour window, ask whether an earlier day is possible for chairs and tables.
- **Coats and boots.** Every guest arrives in a heavy coat. Plan coat racks or a coat check and a mat at the door.
- **Daylight improves.** Sunset moves from about 5:03 pm on February 1 to 5:38 pm by February 28, which helps a late-afternoon event.

For more on the cold-weather side, read [January and February event planning in Chicago](/blog/january-february-event-planning-chicago).

## Venue demand and booking lead times in February

The pressure in February is around specific dates, not the whole month. Lunar New Year weekend, Valentine's weekend and Presidents Day weekend all compete for the same private rooms and banquet halls. If your date is on one of them, book the venue first and the rentals right after.

Round banquet tables, gold chargers and red or gold linens are popular for Lunar New Year banquets, and the most popular items go first. Our [Lunar New Year banquet table guide](/blog/lunar-new-year-banquet-tables) covers sizes and layouts. For a Valentine's or engagement party, [velvet chairs](/chairs/velvet-dining) and low candlelight are popular choices, so reserve them early.

## Frequently asked questions

### When is Lunar New Year in 2027?

Saturday, February 6, 2027. Banquet halls and restaurants that host Lunar New Year dinners book up for the weekends around it, so reserve space and round banquet tables early.

### When does Ramadan start in 2027?

Ramadan is expected to begin around February 7 or 8, 2027, depending on the moon sighting, and Eid al-Fitr falls around March 9 or 10. Iftar events start at sunset, which is about 5:20 to 5:40 pm in Chicago in February.

### Is February a good month for a wedding in Chicago?

It can be, indoors. February is outside peak season, so venue dates are easier to find, but some parishes limit weddings during Lent, which starts on Ash Wednesday, February 10, 2027. Ask your church early.

### What is the weather in Chicago in February?

Cold. NWS normals for O'Hare give an average high of 35.7°F and a low of 21.8°F, and snow is common. Plan indoor space and loose delivery windows.

### How early should I book a Valentine's Day event space in Chicago?

Several weeks ahead at minimum. In 2027 Valentine's Day is a Sunday, so the whole weekend is busy for restaurants and private dining rooms.

### Is Presidents Day weekend a good time for a party?

It is a 3-day weekend for many schools and offices (Monday, February 15, 2027), which helps out-of-town guests but also means some families travel. Send save-the-dates early.
