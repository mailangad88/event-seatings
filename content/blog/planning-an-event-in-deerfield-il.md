Deerfield is a Lake County suburb on the North Shore side of the Chicago area, with a walkable downtown, neighborhood parks run by the Deerfield Park District and many homes that host showers, brunches and family gatherings. Shower season, spring and early summer, fills clubs, restaurants and backyards. Most shower hosts want a refined, easy-to-manage layout that makes guests feel looked after. This guide gives you that layout and a counting method that works for any room.

## Count the room before you count guests

A shower is a seated, social event with gifts, games and a meal, so people stay at tables for longer than at an open house. Use 12 to 15 square feet per guest to leave room for gifts and a buffet. Measure the room and divide.

## Where to host

Shower hosts in Deerfield often choose a restaurant room, a club, a park district facility or a home. Each has trade-offs. A restaurant gives you food and service but limits the layout. A park district room may have tables and chairs but little decor. A home feels personal but needs more rentals. If you consider a park district building, contact the Deerfield Park District for availability and rules on decorations, food and outside vendors. If you host at home, ask neighbors about parking.

## Gift management

Gifts pile up fast. Dedicate a table near the entrance with a floor-length cloth so bags and boxes can sit underneath. Assign a friend to record who gave what. Plan a loading path to the car, and bring a cart if the lot is far. Place a card box or basket on the gift table; a lockable box is a good idea for cash gifts.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many chairs do I need for a 40-person shower?

About 42 chairs. Add extras if you have a games area or a guest of honor lounge.

### What size tables for a bridal shower?

Rounds of 60 inches seat 8 and are the easiest to arrange. Use 48-inch rounds for tight rooms.

### Do I need linens on shower tables?

Most hosts use floor-length or lap-length cloths for a finished look. See the [size chart](/blog/tablecloth-size-chart).

### What chair looks best for a brunch?

Cane-back, bentwood and velvet chairs all suit a brunch. Pick by color and budget.

### Can I rent just a few special chairs?

Yes, many hosts rent a pair of statement chairs for the guest of honor. Ask in your quote.

### How early do showers book?

Spring and early summer weekends fill first, so book 2 to 3 months ahead.
