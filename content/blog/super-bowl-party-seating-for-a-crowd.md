**Quick answer**

Seat a Super Bowl party in rows facing the screen, with the best seats at a distance of 1.5 to 2.5 times the screen's diagonal. Use tiers: sofas or low chairs in front, regular chairs in the middle, and high stools or standing room at the back. For 30 guests, rent about 15 to 18 chairs, 2 to 3 cocktail tables and two food tables, and keep the food behind or beside the seating so no one walks in front of the screen.

</div>

A Super Bowl party looks like a dinner party but behaves like a theater. Everyone faces one direction for three-plus hours, the food moves during commercials, and the crowd stands up at the same moments. The best layout puts sightlines first and food second.

## Sightlines before furniture

Find the screen, then walk the room from every seat. The bigger the group, the more important this is.

## How far from the screen

A common home-theater rule is to sit about 1.5 to 2.5 times the screen's diagonal away. For a 65-inch TV that is roughly 8 to 13.5 feet. For a projector screen of 120 inches, it is 15 to 25 feet. Use this as a starting point for the first and last row, and adjust for the room.

<div class="table-wrap">

| Screen | Closest seat | Farthest seat | Seats it serves well |
|---|---|---|---|
| 55 in TV | 7 ft | 11 ft | 8 to 10 |
| 65 in TV | 8 ft | 13 ft | 12 to 15 |
| 85 in TV | 11 ft | 18 ft | 18 to 24 |
| 120 in projector | 15 ft | 25 ft | 30 or more |

</div>

If your screen is smaller than the crowd, add a second screen on the side or in the next room. It keeps the party from splitting into those who can see and those who cannot.

## What to rent

### Seating
Mix heights. [Garden folding chairs](/chairs/garden-folding) at $5 to $7 are easy to line up for a middle row. [Cross-back chairs](/chairs/cross-back) at $8 to $11 look better and are comfortable for a three-hour game. Bar-height stools at a counter fill the back row. For lounge-style seating, read [lounge layout by guest count](/blog/lounge-layout-by-guest-count).

### Tables
- Two to three 42-inch cocktail tables near the seating for drinks and plates.
- Two 8-foot tables for food, one for hot dishes and one for cold and desserts.
- A drinks table with a tub, a beverage dispenser or both. See [how many beverage dispensers you need](/blog/how-many-beverage-dispensers-do-i-need).

### Food equipment
Game day food is hot, saucy and constant. Chafing dishes keep wings, chili and dip warm. See [how many chafing dishes for a buffet](/blog/how-many-chafing-dishes-for-a-buffet). Napkins matter more than usual; see [how many napkins per guest](/blog/how-many-napkins-per-guest).

## Chicago-area notes

The Super Bowl falls in early to mid February, one of the coldest stretches of the Chicago winter. Plan for coats and boots, and a place to put them. Streets can be icy, so tell guests to leave extra time and give them a spot to park. If the game is at your home and you have a garage or heated porch, it can hold the overflow, but check heater clearances. Winter weather also affects deliveries, so ask your rental company about timing. Our [winter wedding seating guide](/blog/winter-wedding-seating) shows how rentals work in winter conditions.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many chairs do I need for a Super Bowl party?

About half of your guests, because people stand during big plays and food breaks. For 30 guests, rent 15 to 18 chairs on top of your own furniture.

### How far should seats be from the TV?

Roughly 1.5 to 2.5 times the diagonal of the screen. A 65-inch TV works for seats 8 to 13 feet away.

### Where should the food go?

To the side or the back of the room, out of the line between the seats and the screen. Two tables spread people out.

### How do I keep the party from splitting?

Add a second screen if the first is not visible from everywhere, and place seats in the same room as the game. Guests in the kitchen miss the best plays.

### What size tables work best?

42-inch cocktail tables near the seating, and 8-foot banquet tables for food. They are tall enough to eat at while standing.

Ask during your quote. Equipment options vary, and we can help you decide what fits your room.
