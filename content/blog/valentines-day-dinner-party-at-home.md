**Quick answer**

For a Valentine's Day dinner party for 8 to 16, put guests at one long table or two round tables, with about 24 inches of table per person. Use floor-length or lap-length linens, a runner, low candles and warm, dim light. Valentine's Day 2027 is a Sunday, so start dinner around 6 pm and plan to finish early. Set the table the day before and pick chairs that are comfortable for a long meal.

</div>

Restaurants are packed on Valentine's Day. A dinner party at home with a few other couples gives you a quiet table, a menu you pick and no wait for a reservation. In 2027, February 14 is a Sunday, which makes a long dinner easy to plan, but it also means a Monday morning for everyone. The rest is table size, lighting and a clean flow from the first drink to dessert.

## Linens, candles and place settings

- **Linens.** A floor-length cloth or a neutral runner over a bare table. Deep red, ivory and blush work for the date. The [tablecloth size chart](/blog/tablecloth-size-chart) helps you pick the right size.
- **Candles.** Low candles in holders keep sight lines clear. Avoid tall tapers that block faces across the table. Use battery candles if you want no flame, and keep real flames away from napkins and florals. See [candle rules and fire safety](/blog/candles-at-venues-fire-code-rules).
- **Place settings.** A dinner plate, a smaller plate for salad or dessert, a fork on the left, a knife and spoon on the right, a water glass and a wine glass. For the full order, see [place setting spacing](/blog/place-setting-spacing-measurements).
- **Glassware.** Count a water glass, a red and a white wine glass, or a flute for a toast. See [how many glasses per guest](/blog/how-many-glasses-per-guest).
- **Dinnerware.** If you do not own 12 matching plates, rent them. See [dinnerware for a dinner party at home](/blog/dinnerware-for-dinner-party-at-home).

## Lighting for the evening

Light makes the room. February sunsets in Chicago come early, so you will be eating in the dark from the start.

<ol class="flow">
<li><strong>Turn off overhead lights.</strong> Use lamps, string lights or a dimmer.</li>
<li><strong>Put the brightest light on the food.</strong> A small lamp on a sideboard works.</li>
<li><strong>Add candles last.</strong> Place them down the center, low and spaced about 12 inches apart.</li>
<li><strong>Keep it warm.</strong> Choose warm white bulbs, since cool bulbs look flat.</li>
<li><strong>Check faces.</strong> Sit in each seat and see that no one looks lit from below.</li>
</ol>

See [lighting color temperature](/blog/lighting-color-temperature-events) for bulb choices.

## Chairs for a long dinner

A dining chair that is fine for 20 minutes can be tiring after two hours. Pick padded or well-shaped chairs, and leave about 18 inches between the table edge and the wall or the next chair. See [comfortable chairs for older guests](/blog/comfortable-chairs-for-older-guests) for what to look for, or browse [chairs](/rentals/chairs).

## What most hosts find out too late

- **Valentine's weekend sells out.** Rental companies get busy near Feb 14, and Sunday pickups may be limited. Book early and ask about delivery and pickup times.
- **Measure the room.** A 10-foot table in a 10-foot room leaves no walking space. Leave at least 36 inches around the table.
- **Count everything at delivery.** Check plates, glasses and chairs against the quote.
- **Check your heat.** A crowded room gets warm. Turn the heat down a bit before guests arrive.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I seat 12 people for a dinner party at home?

Use one long table of 10 to 12, or two round tables of 6. Allow about 24 inches per person and leave 36 inches of walking space around the tables. Rent a table if yours is too small.

### What is the best table setup for couples?

Seat couples across from each other or at the corner of a round table so they can talk to the group. Many hosts split couples to get more conversation. Pick what feels right for your friends.

### What lighting works for a romantic dinner?

Warm, dim light from lamps and candles with no bright overhead lights. Place candles low so faces are visible. Test the room before guests arrive.

### Are candles safe at a dinner party?

Yes, if you keep flames away from napkins, flowers and sleeves, and never leave them alone. Battery candles are a good option if you are unsure.

### When is Valentine's Day in 2027?

It is Sunday, February 14, 2027. That makes it a good night for a long dinner, but guests will want to finish by 9 pm.
