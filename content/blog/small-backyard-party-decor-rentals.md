## Count the real space

Measure the usable area, not the lot. Subtract steps, grills, play sets, trees and the strip along the fence. A rule of thumb for a seated party is about 10 to 12 square feet per guest, and 6 to 8 for a standing party, so a 20 x 30 foot (600 sq ft) area handles around 50 seated or 75 standing. Allow a 3-foot walkway around tables.

## What to rent first

1. **Tables and chairs** that fit the footprint. See [how many chairs you need](/blog/how-many-chairs-do-i-need).
2. **A canopy or tent** if you lack shade or want a rain plan.
3. **Lighting.** String lights or lanterns give the biggest evening change. Confirm outlets, extension cords and the circuit load.
4. **A backdrop.** A drape on the back fence hides clutter.
5. **Signs and a bar table** to organize flow.

## Decor that works in a small yard

- **Go vertical.** Hang lights, banners or a drape instead of filling the ground with objects.
- **Hide the ugly.** Drape covers a trash area, a dryer vent or a fence.
- **Use one color family.** Small spaces look busier than large ones.
- **Keep centerpieces low** so tables do not feel crowded.
- **Define zones** with a rug under a lounge area.

## Neighbors, rules and cleanup

Tell your neighbors about the date and time, and keep music at a sensible level. Many Chicago-area villages and HOAs have noise rules, and some require permits for tents. Check with your village or city, your HOA and, if you will dig stakes, call JULIE (811) first. Rental delivery trucks need curb space, so ask about parking on your street, and if you live in a building with a courtyard, check your management company's rules.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Counting the entire lot as usable.
- Blocking the house door and the restroom path.
- Forgetting extension cords and outlet capacity.
- Not planning for rain.


## Frequently asked questions

### How much space do I need for a backyard party?

About 10 to 12 square feet per seated guest and 6 to 8 per standing guest, plus 3 feet between tables.

### What decor works best in a small backyard?

String lights, a drape backdrop, low centerpieces and one color family. Go vertical to save floor space.

### Do I need a tent for a backyard party?

Not always, but a tent protects from sun and rain. Check village rules and plan for a tent for 60 or more guests.

### How do I hide an ugly fence?

Hang pipe and drape or a fabric panel, or add a greenery wall. Weight it for wind.

### Do I need a permit for a backyard party?

Often not for a small party, but tents, noise and street parking may be regulated. Check with your village or city and HOA.

### How early can rentals be delivered?

It depends on the rental company's route. Ask for a delivery window and a pickup time in your quote.
