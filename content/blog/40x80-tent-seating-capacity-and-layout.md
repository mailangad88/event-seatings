**Quick answer**

A 40x80 tent is 3,200 square feet. It seats about 150 to 190 guests at rounds with a dance floor and a bar, 200 to 220 with no dance floor, 250 to 300 standing, or about 320 in theater rows. Plan for 16 to 21 square feet per seated guest, and confirm the layout with your tent company.

</div>

A 40x80 is a big tent: bigger than most houses, with room for a seated dinner, a real dance floor and a bar under one roof. It is the standard size for larger weddings, fundraisers and company picnics in the Chicago suburbs, and it is often rented as a frame tent or a clearspan structure. This guide shows what fits.

## Frame, pole or clearspan?

At 40 feet wide, a pole tent needs center poles that break the layout, so most 40x80 rentals use a frame or a clearspan. A clearspan structure has no interior poles at all, a stronger frame and often a higher ceiling. See [clearspan structure tent rental](/blog/clearspan-structure-tent-rental) and [frame vs pole tent](/blog/frame-tent-vs-pole-tent). Ask your tent company which is being delivered.

## Zones inside a big tent

<ol class="flow">
<li><strong>Entrance and welcome.</strong> Short end of the tent with a seating chart.</li>
<li><strong>Dining.</strong> Tables in rows with 5-foot aisles.</li>
<li><strong>Dancing.</strong> One end, with a stage or DJ at the wall.</li>
<li><strong>Bars.</strong> One at the dance end, one near the entrance.</li>
<li><strong>Support.</strong> Catering prep outside with a path to the kitchen side.</li>
</ol>

You can also split the tent into three: cocktails at the entrance, dinner in the middle and dancing at the end, with pipe-and-drape or lounge furniture as dividers. See [lounge layout by guest count](/blog/lounge-layout-by-guest-count).

## Planning the load-in

A tent this size is a construction job. The crew arrives with trucks, lays out the frame, raises it and adds sidewalls, lighting and flooring, often the day before the event. Chairs and tables arrive after the tent is finished and the floor is safe to walk on. Tell every vendor the order of arrival: tent first, then flooring and power, then tables and chairs, then linens and place settings, then the caterer and the DJ.

If the tent sits on grass, the weight of a rental truck and carts can leave ruts, so ask your rental provider how they route deliveries. A path of plywood or mats from the driveway to the tent protects the lawn and helps guests in heels. For a tent that sits on a parking lot, ballast replaces stakes and the crew needs more time. Whatever the surface, walk the site with your vendors before the day. A 10-minute walkthrough with a tape measure prevents most of the surprises.

## Utilities, lighting and climate

A tent this size needs real power: lights, DJ, bars and perhaps heaters or fans. A generator and distribution is often part of the rental plan; see [generator size for events](/blog/generator-size-for-events) and [quiet generator placement](/blog/quiet-generator-placement-events). Heating or cooling is a separate question: see [heating a tent in fall](/blog/heating-a-tent-in-fall) and [cooling a tent in summer](/blog/cooling-a-tent-in-summer). Lighting for the night is covered in [tent lighting ideas](/blog/tent-lighting-ideas).

## Permits, inspections and neighbors

At 3,200 square feet, a 40x80 tent will require a permit or inspection in most Chicago-area municipalities, and many ask for a flame-retardant certificate and an exit plan. Rules differ by village, county and park district, so ask the building or fire department and the property owner. ## How much yard do you need?

A 40x80 needs about 50 x 90 feet of flat ground for the tent and its stakes or ballast, and more for catering and a generator. Parking lots and driveways need ballast rather than stakes; see [tent on a driveway or parking lot](/blog/tent-on-driveway-or-parking-lot). Grass can be damaged by heavy foot traffic; see [tent and grass damage](/blog/tent-and-grass-damage).

## Frequently asked questions

### How many people fit in a 40x80 tent?

About 150 to 190 seated with a dance floor and bar, 200 to 220 without dancing and 250 to 300 standing.

### How many tables fit in a 40x80 tent?

Fifteen to twenty rounds of 10, depending on the dance floor.

### How big is the dance floor in a 40x80 tent?

A 16 x 20 or 20 x 20 foot floor fits at the end with 15 tables.

### Do I need a permit for a 40x80 tent?

Almost certainly. Ask your village or city; they often also ask for fire-safety documents.

### Is a clearspan better than a frame tent at 40x80?

A clearspan has no interior poles and handles weather better, but costs more. A frame tent works if the ground is level.

### Can I put a stage in a 40x80 tent?

Yes. A 16 x 12 foot stage takes about 200 square feet, which reduces the seated capacity by 10 to 15 guests.
