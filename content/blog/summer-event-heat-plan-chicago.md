**Quick answer**

Chicago's hottest month, July, averages an 84.5°F high at O'Hare, and humid days feel hotter. A solid heat plan has five parts: shade for every seat, water within reach, moving air, an afternoon schedule that avoids the hottest hours and a clear way to help someone who feels unwell. Start events after 5 pm or hold them in shade.

</div>

A hot-weather event does not fail because of the thermometer. It fails because no one planned where people would sit, what they would drink and when they would rest. In Chicago the heat comes with humidity, which makes the same temperature feel worse than it does in dry climates. Here is a plan any host or event manager can use.

## What counts as hot in Chicago

The NWS 1991-2020 normals put June at an 80.4°F average high, July at 84.5°F and August at 82.5°F. Those are averages, and individual days can be much hotter. The National Weather Service issues heat advisories based on the heat index, which combines temperature and humidity, so check the forecast for your date rather than the monthly number.

## The five-part plan

<ol class="flow">
<li><strong>Shade:</strong> a seat in shade for every guest at the same time.</li>
<li><strong>Water:</strong> free, cold and within 20 steps of any seat.</li>
<li><strong>Air:</strong> fans or misting, aimed where people sit.</li>
<li><strong>Schedule:</strong> keep the hottest hours light and short.</li>
<li><strong>Response:</strong> a cool indoor space and a person who knows what to do.</li>
</ol>

### 1. Shade

A tent is the most reliable shade. Umbrellas help on smaller patios, and trees help if you've checked where the sun will be at 3 pm. Rent enough shade for the whole group at once, since people do not spread evenly. See [market umbrellas for shade](/blog/market-umbrellas-for-shade) and [cooling a tent in summer](/blog/cooling-a-tent-in-summer).

### 2. Water

Provide cold water in more than one place, not just at the bar. Beverage dispensers with ice are cheap insurance. For counts, read [how many beverage dispensers you need](/blog/how-many-beverage-dispensers-do-i-need) and [how much ice for a party](/blog/how-much-ice-for-a-party).

### 3. Moving air

Fans do more for comfort than lowering the temperature by a few degrees. Misting adds cooling but also adds wet ground and humidity, which can backfire in muggy weather. Both need power; see [fans and coolers for summer events](/blog/fans-and-coolers-for-summer-events) and [misting and shade](/blog/misting-and-shade-for-summer-events). A generator may be required, so read our [generator size guide](/blog/generator-size-for-events).

If your event must be in the afternoon, shorten the program, hold the ceremony in shade and avoid long speeches in sun. Seat older guests and young children first in shade, and give them water before the program starts.

### 5. Response

Know where the nearest air-conditioned room is, who has the phone and who will call 911 if someone shows signs of heat illness. The CDC lists heat stroke as a medical emergency; symptoms include confusion, a very high body temperature, and hot dry or damp skin. Do not rely on a tent as a cool room.

## Corporate picnics and large groups

With 100 or more people, plan shade for about the whole group at the busiest time, put food and drink in the shade and offer a cooling break area. Keep game areas away from the food line. Our [corporate picnic games guide](/blog/corporate-picnic-games-chicago) has layouts.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I keep guests cool at an outdoor summer event?

Provide shade, cold water, moving air and a shorter program during the afternoon. Hold the event in the evening if you can.

### What temperature is too hot for an outdoor event?

There is no single number because humidity matters. Check the National Weather Service heat index forecast, and have a fallback if heat advisories are issued.

### Do I need a tent for shade?

A tent is the most reliable shade for groups. Umbrellas and trees can work for small groups.

### Are misters a good idea in Chicago humidity?

They can help on dry days but add moisture on humid ones. Fans are the safer first choice.

### How much water should I provide?

Offer water at more than one station and do not run out. Counts depend on the number of guests and hours; see our dispenser guide.

### What time of day is best for a summer event in Chicago?

After 5 pm for most events. Sunset is around 8:30 pm in early July, so there is plenty of evening light.

### What do I do if a guest feels sick from the heat?

Move them to a cool place, give water if they are alert and call 911 if they are confused or have a very high temperature.
