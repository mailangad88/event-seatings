Block clubs, condo associations and homeowner associations run on a different rhythm than a one-time party. The same small group of neighbors meets throughout the year, usually in a community room, a driveway or a shared courtyard, and the events repeat on a schedule: an annual meeting, a holiday potluck, maybe a National Night Out gathering in the summer. This is different from a one-day block party that closes a street. If you're planning that kind of event instead, see our [block party rentals guide](/blog/block-party-rentals-chicago) and the [Chicago block party permit guide](/blog/chicago-block-party-permit).

## Recurring events need a repeatable order

Most block clubs and associations don't need a different rental plan every time. The guest list is roughly the same group of households, the space is usually the same community room or driveway, and the furniture needs barely change year to year. Once we know your group's typical headcount and setup, we can save that order and reuse it for the next meeting or event, so your board doesn't have to re-explain the setup every single time.

Ask us for a quote broken out by what's needed for the event, so your treasurer has a clear number to divide among members or pull from the club's budget. If your group runs the same event every year, that quote also gives you a number to plan next year's dues around.

## Furniture that's easy to set up and store

Community room storage closets and garages are usually small, so lightweight folding chairs and folding tables matter here as much as anywhere we serve. They go up fast in a driveway or a shared room, and they come down just as fast when a few volunteers are doing the setup themselves rather than hiring a crew. The [garden folding chair](/chairs/garden-folding) is a common choice for this kind of recurring event because it's light enough for one person to carry and stack.

## Holiday potlucks and annual meetings

A holiday potluck usually needs tables for food along one side of the room and chairs arranged for people to sit and talk, while an annual meeting leans more toward rows or a U-shape facing whoever is leading the discussion. Our [how many chairs do I need guide](/blog/how-many-chairs-do-i-need) can help size either setup once you know roughly how many households usually show up.

## National Night Out and summer gatherings

National Night Out and similar summer gatherings often happen right in a driveway, a shared lot or a courtyard rather than indoors. Tables and chairs set up outside need stable, level ground, and a backup plan if rain shows up. If your event is more like a full block party that closes off a street, that's a different kind of event and permit process, covered in our [block party rentals guide](/blog/block-party-rentals-chicago).

## Frequently asked questions

### How is a block club event different from a block party?

A block club or association event is usually a recurring meeting or annual gathering for members, often in a community room or driveway. A block party is typically a one-day event that closes off a street to the public, with its own permit process covered in our [block party rentals guide](/blog/block-party-rentals-chicago).

### Can we reuse the same rental order every year?

Yes. Once we know your group's typical headcount and setup, we can save it and use it again for your next meeting or annual event, so you don't have to re-explain it each time.

### How do we split the rental cost among members?

Ask us for an itemized quote. Many boards and treasurers use that number to divide across dues or pull from a shared event budget.

### What furniture works best for an outdoor driveway gathering?

Lightweight folding chairs and folding tables that set up quickly on a driveway or courtyard and store easily afterward. Make sure the ground is level and have an indoor backup in case of rain.

### Do we need a permit for a National Night Out gathering?

It depends on where it's held. A gathering entirely on private property, like a shared driveway or courtyard, usually needs no permit, but using a public street or park may require one. Check with your alderman's office or see our Park District permit guide if it's in a park.

### How far ahead should a block club book its annual event?

A few weeks ahead is usually enough for a repeat order, since the setup rarely changes year to year. Book earlier around the holidays, when community rooms and rental schedules get busier.
