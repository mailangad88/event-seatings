**Quick answer**

The Chicago Marathon is run on a Sunday in October, and it closes streets across the city. In 2026 the race took place on Sunday, October 11, with the first start at 7:30 a.m., and Michigan Avenue between Madison Street and Roosevelt Road closed from 4 a.m. to 4 p.m. If your event is on or near that weekend, schedule rental deliveries for Friday or earlier, avoid the course on Sunday morning and tell guests to plan extra travel time.

</div>

If your wedding, fundraiser or family party lands on Marathon weekend, you are not doomed. Thousands of events happen in Chicago that weekend. But the race changes how trucks, guests and staff move. This guide uses what the City and news outlets published for 2026 as a model for what to expect. Always check the race organizers for the exact dates and closure schedule for your year.

## What the closures looked like in 2026

Fox 32 Chicago's closure guide for the 2026 race reported:

- **Grant Park-area streets** such as Jackson Drive, Columbus Drive, Monroe Street and Roosevelt Road segments were closed before Sunday.
- **Michigan Avenue** (Madison Street to Roosevelt Road) closed 4 a.m. to 4 p.m. on race morning.
- **Columbus Drive** (McFetridge Drive to Roosevelt Road) and **Roosevelt Road** (Michigan Avenue to Columbus Drive) closed 4 a.m. to 6 p.m.
- **Along the course**, closures began around 6 a.m. and reopened as the final runners passed.
- **Open roads** included DuSable Lake Shore Drive, inner Lake Shore Drive from Diversey Parkway to Belmont Avenue, and the expressways.
- The course extended north to Sheridan Road, west to Damen Avenue and south to 35th Street.

## Neighborhood effects

The course passes through many Chicago neighborhoods, and the closures can cut off cross streets for hours. Check the course map before you pick a start time. Venues near the course may have limited parking, and guests may be unable to cross certain streets until runners clear. If your event is a church wedding, a brunch or a backyard party near the route, tell guests which side of the course to approach from.

## Guest experience

- **Hotels** fill. Tell guests early and suggest options farther from the course.
- **Travel time.** Add buffer. A short drive can take twice as long.
- **Weather.** October in Chicago can be cool or warm. Pack a cold-weather backup, and see our [winter wedding seating](/blog/winter-wedding-seating) tips if the forecast turns.

## Frequently asked questions

### When is the Chicago Marathon?

It is held on a Sunday in October. The 2026 race was Sunday, October 11. Check the official race site for the date in your year, since we could not verify a published 2027 date.

### What streets close for the Chicago Marathon?

Many downtown and course streets. In 2026, Michigan Avenue between Madison and Roosevelt closed from 4 a.m. to 4 p.m., and Columbus Drive and Roosevelt Road closed until 6 p.m. Course streets closed around 6 a.m. and reopened as the last runners passed.

### Can I host a wedding on Marathon weekend in Chicago?

Yes, but plan around closures and hotel demand. Choose a venue away from the course or an afternoon or evening time, and move deliveries to Friday or Saturday.

### Will my rental delivery be late because of the marathon?

It can be if the truck needs to cross the course on Sunday morning. Schedule delivery before race day or after streets reopen, and give the driver the official closure list.

### Are expressways closed for the marathon?

In 2026, the expressways and DuSable Lake Shore Drive stayed open, according to the Fox 32 report. Check your year's list before you travel.

### Is it cheaper to avoid Marathon weekend?

We do not publish pricing for venues or hotels, so we cannot say. Ask your venue whether the date changes its terms.
