**Quick answer**

The City of Aurora sorts events into six tiers with lead times of 90 days for Tier 1, 60 days for Tiers 2 and 3, 45 days for Tier 4 and 30 days for film. There is a $25 non-refundable application fee, and the City does not provide tables, chairs, tents, restrooms or sound systems. You arrange those yourself.

</div>

Aurora is the second-largest city in Illinois, and it has the clearest published permit system of any town in this series. If you plan a street event, a festival or a large gathering, the tiers tell you how much lead time to allow. If you plan a private party, you can skip most of it.

## The six tiers

According to the City of Aurora, a Special Event Permit falls into one of six tiers based on scale and impact:

- **Tier 1:** multi-day events, street or sidewalk use, temporary liquor licenses, more than 1,000 attendees per day, food vendors, fireworks or carnivals.
- **Tier 2:** gatherings at City property with under 1,000 attendees per day, events needing extra City services, or stationary events affecting up to two blocks.
- **Tier 3:** events with no alcohol that affect one block, need only a temporary street closure or last under five hours on City property without food or drink.
- **Tier 4:** smaller events, such as small runs or vehicle shows.
- **Tier 5:** film productions.
- **Tier 6:** assemblies needing only basic City support, with no traffic control and no sales.

A $25 non-refundable application fee is invoiced after initial review. If Police or Fire staffing is required, the organizer pays for City personnel, with a 25% deposit due at least 10 days before the event.

## What the City does not supply

The City's page says it does not provide equipment such as portable restrooms, sound systems, tables, chairs or tents. That is where rentals come in. For a Tier 2 or Tier 3 event, you will want a rental list that covers seating, tables, shade and any bar or serving equipment, and you will want to book it before your permit is final, since popular dates go fast.

## Where Aurora events happen

- **Downtown Aurora.** The Paramount Theatre, the Fox River and downtown streets host festivals. Street use is a City permit.
- **RiverEdge Park.** The City lists rental forms for events at RiverEdge Park on its event pages.
- **Fox Valley Park District.** The district manages most parks and its own runs-and-walks permit form.
- **Banquet halls and hotels.** Plenty of indoor options. Ask each what furniture is included.
- **Private homes.** Aurora is large; lot sizes vary widely.

## Planning rentals around an Aurora permit

Because the City does not supply tables, chairs or tents, your rental plan and your permit plan depend on each other. Here is a sensible order for a Tier 2 or Tier 3 event.

1. **Confirm the date and tier** with the City's events office.
2. **Sketch the footprint** of the event and count seats, tables, a bar and a stage.
3. **Request a quote** so you know what is available on your date.
4. **Add rentals to your site plan** before final City review, since inspectors may want to see tent locations and fire lanes.
5. **Confirm delivery** access, especially if streets will be closed.

## Frequently asked questions

### How far in advance do I apply for an Aurora special event permit?

It depends on the tier: 90 days for Tier 1, 60 days for Tiers 2 and 3, 45 days for Tier 4 and 30 days for film. Tier 6 has no listed lead time.

### How much is the Aurora special event application fee?

$25, non-refundable, invoiced after initial review. Police and Fire staffing, if required, is billed separately to the organizer.

### Does Aurora provide tables and chairs for events?

No. The City says it does not provide equipment such as portable restrooms, sound systems, tables, chairs or tents. Organizers arrange them.

### Do I need a permit for a private party in Aurora?

Not usually. The tiers apply to events that use City property, streets or services. A private backyard party generally does not, but check local ordinances on tents and noise.

### Who handles park permits in Aurora?

Aurora has both City parks and Fox Valley Park District parks. Ask which agency owns the park you want.

### Do you deliver in Aurora?

Yes. First deliveries begin with the April 2027 Founding Season.
