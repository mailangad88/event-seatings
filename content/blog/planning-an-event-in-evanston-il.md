Evanston is one of the easiest suburbs to fall in love with and one of the trickiest to deliver to. You have Lake Michigan on the east side, a dense street grid with limited curb space, big older homes with narrow side yards, and a university calendar that changes the traffic and hotel picture every spring. This guide covers how to plan the rental list for an Evanston wedding, graduation, fundraiser or backyard party, and what to ask before you sign anything.

## Start with the site, not the chairs

The three most common Evanston setups are very different jobs.

- **Lakefront or public park.** The City of Evanston runs a Parks, Recreation and Community Services permits and rentals office, and it publishes a special event permit application. If your event is outdoors on city property, start there. Ask what is allowed on the day, including tents, amplified sound and food, and ask for it in writing.
- **A private home.** Many Evanston houses have a front porch, a deep rear yard and a shared driveway. The question is how rentals get from the truck to the yard.
- **A hall, church, club or university space.** The space usually provides tables, but not always the chair you want. Ask what is included before you order anything.

## Lakefront and park events: permission and wind

Lake Michigan changes the weather in a way that inland guests do not expect. Shoreline spots are often cooler than the rest of the city in late spring and summer, and the breeze off the water is steady. That affects three things on your rental list.

1. **Chair weight and stability.** Wooden chairs like the [cross-back](/chairs/cross-back) and [Windsor](/chairs/windsor) sit solidly. Light stacking chairs can shift on sand or soft turf if guests lean back. Ask us about foot pads for lawn setups.
2. **Table linens.** Floor-length cloths catch wind like sails. Use clips or weights, or choose a lap-length drop. Our [tablecloth size chart](/blog/tablecloth-size-chart) shows both.
3. **Shade and warmth.** Plan a wrap basket or blankets for evenings, and a shaded waiting area for guests who sit still through a ceremony.

Do not assume you can tent, serve alcohol or play amplified music on city land. Those are separate approvals, and rules change. Call the City of Evanston permits office and your own venue contact, then build the rental list around what you are told.

## Private homes: the delivery path

The most common Evanston surprise is not the yard. It is the path to the yard. Before you order, walk the route a delivery person will carry a rolled-up table and a stack of chairs.

<ol class="flow">
<li><strong>Measure the gate.</strong> Side yard gates and passages can be narrower than a standard banquet table is long.</li>
<li><strong>Note steps and slopes.</strong> Stairs to a back deck mean more labor and more time.</li>
<li><strong>Check the curb.</strong> Can a truck stop, or will it need a few minutes in a loading zone?</li>
<li><strong>Pick the staging spot.</strong> A garage or covered porch protects rentals from rain until set-up.</li>
<li><strong>Mark the layout.</strong> A simple sketch with table positions saves an hour on the day.</li>
</ol>

If you are not sure what fits, send photos of the yard and gate with your [quote request](/quote). Our guide to [how many chairs you need](/blog/how-many-chairs-do-i-need) helps with the count.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a permit for an event in Evanston?

It depends on where you hold it. Events on city parks or the lakefront generally need permission from the City of Evanston, which publishes a special event permit application and a parks permits page. Private homes and halls have different rules, so check with the City and your venue before you order anything.

### How many chairs should I order for an Evanston wedding?

Order one chair per guest, plus about 5% spares. If you hold the ceremony and reception in different spaces and want to flip chairs, see our guide on [ceremony versus reception chairs](/blog/ceremony-vs-reception-chairs). A 100-guest wedding usually lands around 105 chairs.

### Can chairs be used on the beach or lawn near the lake?

Yes, but pick sturdy chairs and use foot pads on soft ground. Wooden chairs like the cross-back or Windsor are stable on turf, and the garden folding chair is a light option for ceremonies. Confirm with the property owner that chairs are allowed on sand or grass.

### What size tent do I need for an Evanston backyard party?

A common rule of thumb is about 10 to 12 square feet per seated guest, so a 20 x 30 foot tent suits roughly 40 to 50 guests at rounds. Tents may need a permit, so read our [Chicago tent permit guide](/blog/chicago-tent-permit) and confirm with the City of Evanston.

### Is parking a problem for rental deliveries in Evanston?

It can be, especially on narrow residential streets. Tell us whether the truck can park at the curb or in a driveway, and share any parking restrictions on your block. That helps us plan the delivery window and the carry distance.

### How early should I book rentals in Evanston?

For spring weddings and graduation weekends, aim for 6 to 9 months ahead, and as soon as you have a venue and a date. Fewer options remain the closer you get, especially for specialty chairs.
