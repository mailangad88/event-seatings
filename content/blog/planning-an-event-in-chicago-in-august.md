## August weather: what to watch out for

August starts hot and eases late. NWS normals for O'Hare give August an average high of 82.5°F, a low of 65.1°F and about 4.25 inches of rain, one of the wetter months of the year. Storms are the main risk, and evenings near water bring mosquitoes.

What to plan for:

- **Storm and rain plan.** Sidewalls on the tent and flooring for wet grass. A lightning rule that moves guests into a building.
- **Heat.** Shade, fans and water, as in July. Read [misting and shade for summer events](/blog/misting-and-shade-for-summer-events).
- **Bugs.** Ask the venue about mosquito treatment, keep tall grass trimmed around the tent, and use lighting that does not attract bugs to the tables.
- **Earlier sunset.** Sunset falls from about 8:10 pm on August 1 to about 7:33 pm by August 28. Plan lighting for the end of dinner.

The full weather picture is in [outdoor events in Chicago in August](/blog/outdoor-events-chicago-in-august).

## Venue demand and booking lead times in August

August is steady rather than frantic. Weddings continue, and company picnics and reunions take many Saturdays. Tents and lawn games remain in demand, so book them 6 to 8 weeks ahead at least, and earlier for a large tent.

Corporate planners should watch the summer vacation calendar: early August is a common time off for staff with kids. Mid-week picnics in August can work well. Our [outdoor corporate summer party guide](/blog/outdoor-corporate-summer-party-chicago) has a full list of what to rent.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is August a good month for an outdoor event in Chicago?

Yes, with a rain plan. August averages an 82.5°F high and about 4.25 inches of rain at O'Hare, so book a tent with sidewalls and a lightning rule.

### When is the Chicago Air and Water Show in 2027?

The 2027 dates had not been confirmed when we checked. In 2026 it ran August 15 and 16, centered on North Avenue Beach. Avoid lakefront deliveries those days.

### When is the Bud Billiken Parade in 2027?

It is traditionally held on the second Saturday of August, which is August 14, 2027. Confirm with the organizers and check route closures before a South Side delivery.

### How far ahead should I book a tent for August?

Six to 8 weeks at least, and 2 to 4 months for a large wedding tent or a popular Saturday.

### How do I keep mosquitoes away from an August event?

Ask the venue about treatment, trim grass near the tent, keep lights away from the dining tables, and have repellent available. Evenings near water are the worst.

### What time is sunset in Chicago in August?

About 8:10 pm on August 1, 7:52 pm on August 15 and 7:33 pm on August 28 in 2027.
