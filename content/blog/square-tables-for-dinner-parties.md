**Quick answer**

A 30-inch square table seats 2 to 4, a 36-inch square seats 4, a 48-inch square seats 4 to 8 (4 comfortably, 8 with a shared meal) and a 60-inch square seats 8. Push square tables together to build a long table or break them into a restaurant-style room. Floor-length cloths are 90-inch squares for a 30-inch table, 96 inch for a 36-inch table, and 108 inch for a 48-inch table.

</div>

Square tables bring a restaurant feel to an event. They give each group a table of their own, they flex into longer rows and they fit tight corners better than rounds. For a Chicago-area dinner party in a loft, a gallery or a restaurant buyout, they are an underused option.

## Square table size chart

<div class="table-wrap">

| Table | Seats | Floor-length cloth | Lap-length cloth | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 24 in square | 2 | 84 in square | 54 in square | Cocktail, intimate |
| 30 in square | 2 to 4 | 90 in square | 60 in square | Bistro seating |
| 36 in square | 4 | 96 in square | 66 in square | Dinner for four |
| 48 in square | 4 to 8 | 108 in square | 78 in square | Shared meals |
| 60 in square | 8 | 120 in square | 90 in square | Large groups |

</div>

These cloth sizes follow the same formula as our [tablecloth size chart](/blog/tablecloth-size-chart): table size plus twice the drop. Square cloths in all sizes are not always stocked, so ask what is available. Some companies use a round cloth on a square table to save money and add a different look.

## Where square tables shine

- **Dinner parties of 8 to 40.** Mix pairs and groups of four.
- **Restaurant-style events.** Fundraisers and corporate dinners use squares so groups form their own tables.
- **Tight corners.** A square fits flush against a wall.
- **Flexible parties.** Push tables together in rows when speeches start.

## Corner seating problem

At a square table, a guest in a corner sits close to the neighbors. That is why a 36-inch square seats 4, not 8. One guest per side is comfortable. Two per side only works at 48 inches or bigger. Check legs too: some squares have corner legs that block knee room.

## Combining squares

<ol class="flow">
<li><strong>Count the guests</strong> 4 per 36 in table; 2 on each long side when joined.</li>
<li><strong>Line them up</strong> Check the seam between tops.</li>
<li><strong>Level the tops</strong> Folding legs can differ by a quarter inch.</li>
<li><strong>Cover the seam</strong> with a runner or a long cloth.</li>
<li><strong>Leave space</strong> for servers to reach both sides.</li>
</ol>

Joined squares form a banquet-style row. Two 48-inch squares make a 96 x 48 table that seats 8 to 10. Compare with [banquet tables](/blog/banquet-table-sizes-6-ft-vs-8-ft).

## Floor plan numbers

For 40 guests at squares of 4, plan 10 tables. Each table needs about 8 to 10 feet of space square including chairs, so the room needs around 800 to 1,000 square feet before bar and buffet. That is rule-of-thumb math; ask your venue to check against the floor plan.

## A dinner-party floor plan for 32 guests

Say you host 32 guests for a fundraising dinner in a gallery in Chicago's West Loop. You want an intimate restaurant feel and a speaker at the front.

1. Pick 36-inch squares at 4 guests each, which makes 8 tables.
2. Arrange them in two rows of four with 5 feet between rows, so servers can pass.
3. Put the speaker at one end, with the nearest tables 8 feet from the lectern.
4. Place a small bar table near the entrance, and a service table near the kitchen door.
5. Add one spare table in case of late RSVPs.

If you need a bigger table for a group of six, join two 36-inch squares to make a 72 x 36 rectangle, seating 6 comfortably. Joining squares gives you the flexibility to change your plan after RSVPs close.

## Mistakes to avoid

- **Squeezing 6 into a 36-inch square.** It looks possible on paper and feels crowded at dinner.
- **Mixing cloth sizes.** A 90-inch cloth on a 36-inch table puddles on the floor. Check the size chart.
- **Forgetting corner legs.** Some squares have legs at the corners, which blocks guests' knees. Ask about leg style.
- **Putting all squares in one block.** Break up the room with a mix of sizes to create zones.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many people sit at a 36-inch square table?

Four, one per side. It is a tight fit for plated dinners with large plates.

### How many people sit at a 48-inch square table?

Four comfortably and up to 8 for shared meals, two per side.

### What tablecloth size fits a 36-inch square table?

A 96-inch square cloth reaches the floor. A 66-inch square gives a lap-length look.

### Can square tables be pushed together?

Yes, to form rows or large squares. Check the seam and level the tops.

### Are square tables good for weddings?

For small weddings and rehearsal dinners, yes. Large weddings usually use rounds. See [long vs round tables](/blog/long-tables-vs-round-tables-wedding).

### Do square tables take less room than rounds?

They seat fewer per square foot at the same width, but they fit corners and walls well. See [table rental for small spaces](/blog/table-rental-for-small-spaces).
