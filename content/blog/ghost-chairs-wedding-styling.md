Ghost chairs are the most polarizing option on the Chiavari-alternative list. Done well, they let your florals, linens and architecture carry the room. Done poorly, they look like a hotel banquet from 2012.

## Where they shine

- **Rooms with something to show.** Exposed beams, a stained-glass window or a dramatic floral installation all benefit from a chair that stays out of the way.
- **Dense, colorful tablescapes.** When the table is the star, a transparent chair keeps the eye on it.
- **Small spaces.** Clear chairs make a tight room feel less crowded.

## How to style them

1. **Add warmth.** Pair them with wood tables, linen runners and candlelight so the look doesn't feel cold.
2. **Mind the floor.** Chair legs are visible, so consider the carpet or flooring color behind them.
3. **Use cushions sparingly.** A thin seat pad in a neutral tone adds comfort without ruining the see-through effect.
4. **Skip the shine overload.** Mirrors, gold flatware and ghost chairs together can read as too much. Pick one reflective element.

## When to choose something else

If your venue is already visually quiet, such as a plain white tent, a transparent chair has nothing to frame. A textured chair like [rattan](/chairs/rattan-garden) or [cross-back](/chairs/cross-back) gives the room more character.
