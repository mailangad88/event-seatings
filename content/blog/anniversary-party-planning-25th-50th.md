**Quick answer**

Start planning a 25th or 50th anniversary party three to four months ahead. Decide who hosts, pick a format (dinner, open house or vow renewal), set a guest list of 40 to 120, and plan a short program with toasts, a photo display and a first dance. Seat every guest at a dinner, make the seating comfortable for older relatives, and book rentals early.

Anniversary parties carry more history than most celebrations. There are decades of friends, family and stories to honor, and usually a mix of generations who all want time with the couple. The planning challenge is balancing a meaningful program with a comfortable, unhurried party. Hosts, often adult children, also need to decide who pays, who speaks and how much surprise to include.

## Who hosts and who decides

Adult children, grandchildren or close friends usually host a 25th or 50th anniversary party, and sometimes the couple hosts their own. If you are hosting, talk with the couple early. Some couples want a big party, some want a quiet dinner and some want a vow renewal. Surprises can be wonderful, but a small preview of the plan can avoid problems. Agree on who contributes to the cost, and set the budget before you book anything.

## Choose a format

**Seated dinner (40 to 120).** Round tables, a plated or buffet meal, a head table or a sweetheart table for the couple, and a program of toasts. See [anniversary party rentals and layout](/blog/anniversary-party-rentals-and-layout).

**Open house (50 to 150).** A casual afternoon event with food stations, seating for most guests and a toast. Works for a church hall or backyard.

**Vow renewal with reception.** A short ceremony followed by dinner. See [vow renewal rentals](/blog/vow-renewal-rentals).

**Dinner at a restaurant private room.** Easy and elegant for 20 to 40 guests.

**Backyard party.** Comfortable and personal. A tent, string lights and a good buffet go a long way.

## 25th vs 50th

A 25th anniversary, the silver anniversary, is often a party for friends, with a feel similar to a milestone birthday. A 50th, the golden anniversary, is a family event with several generations and many older guests. That changes the details. At a 50th, plan comfortable chairs, wide aisles, quieter music and a shorter program. At a 25th, you can plan a longer evening with dancing. See [comfortable chairs for older guests](/blog/comfortable-chairs-for-older-guests).

## Colors and decor

Silver and white for a 25th and gold and ivory for a 50th are traditional. You can use these colors in linens, flowers and balloons. A better idea is to pick colors the couple loves. Display wedding photos and one photo from each decade. A guest book or memory jar is a lovely keepsake. See [guest book alternatives](/blog/guest-book-alternatives-for-weddings).

## Timeline

**Four months out:** talk with the couple, set the budget, pick a date and reserve the space.

**Three months out:** build the guest list and send save-the-dates for anyone traveling. Book the caterer, photographer and music.

**Two months out:** send invitations, plan the menu and order the cake. Collect photos and stories.

**One month out:** build the slideshow or photo display, finalize seating and order rentals.

**Two weeks out:** confirm the headcount, the program order and who is speaking.

**One week out:** confirm deliveries and gather supplies.

## The program

A simple program keeps the evening warm. Welcome, a blessing or grace if the family wants one, dinner, two to four toasts of two minutes each, a slideshow, the cake cutting and the couple's dance. Hand the program to the emcee and the DJ. A [microphone and sound check](/blog/sound-system-for-speeches) avoids awkward moments, particularly for older guests who may be hard of hearing.

## Food and cake

Choose a meal that suits the crowd. Many 50th parties choose a family-style or buffet meal. Build a dessert table with the cake, a few sweets and coffee. See [dessert table equipment checklist](/blog/dessert-table-equipment-checklist).

## Photo display

Build a timeline from the wedding day to today. Frame a few favorites, and set up an easel or table. Ask guests for short notes for a memory book.

## Budget

Set a total, then divide it: food and drink, venue and rentals, cake and decor, music, photos and extras. Get at least two quotes for each. See [event rental budget by guest count](/blog/event-rental-budget-by-guest-count).

## Chicago-area notes

Chicago-area anniversary parties often take place in banquet halls, church or community halls, restaurants, golf clubs or backyards. See [golf club wedding and event venues](/blog/golf-club-wedding-and-event-venues) and [Chicago church hall reception rentals](/blog/chicago-church-hall-reception-rentals). Weather matters in an outdoor party: summer evenings can be hot and humid, and fall evenings turn cool. Winter parties need coat space and a safe path to the door. Older guests may need parking close to the entrance and a ramp or elevator. Ask about the venue's rules for decor, alcohol and music, and check with your village or park district for reservation details and capacity.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I plan a 50th anniversary party?

Talk with the couple, set a budget, pick a format and date, build a guest list, and book a space three to four months ahead. Plan comfortable seating, a short program and a photo display.

### Who hosts an anniversary party?

Usually adult children or close friends. Sometimes the couple hosts their own. Agree on the budget and tasks first.

### How much does an anniversary party cost?

It varies with guest count, food and venue. Many hosts set a per-guest budget and add rentals and entertainment. Get several quotes since Chicago-area prices differ.

### What is a good theme for a 25th or 50th anniversary?

Silver for a 25th and gold for a 50th are traditional, but a theme drawn from the couple's story works better. Use wedding photos, the year they married or a favorite place.

### How many guests should I invite to an anniversary party?

Many are 40 to 120 guests. Invite family, close friends and people who have been part of the couple's life.

### How long should an anniversary party last?

Three to four hours with dinner and a program, or two to three hours for an open house.

### Should an anniversary party be a surprise?

It can be, if the couple likes surprises. Ask a close family member first and keep the details of the plan flexible.
