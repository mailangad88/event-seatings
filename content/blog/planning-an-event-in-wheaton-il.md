Wheaton is the DuPage County seat, with a walkable downtown, a college campus, and a park district known for its parks and zoo. It is a good place for weddings, graduation parties and community fundraisers, and a confusing one if you do not know which office to call. This guide sorts that out.

## Two offices, two kinds of land

Wheaton splits into two permit paths. The City of Wheaton publishes an Event Permit page for events that use public property, such as streets, sidewalks and City-owned spaces. The Wheaton Park District has its own picnic area rentals and a park rentals brochure for its parks. Neither office can approve the other's property.

If you are unsure whether your spot is City or district land, ask the park district first; its staff can usually tell you.

## What Wheaton offers by space type

- **Park picnic areas.** The Wheaton Park District rents picnic areas. The rental brochure lists the options, so check current capacities and rules.
- **Memorial Park.** This park has a bandshell used for concerts. Ask the district what use it allows outside programmed events.
- **Cosley Zoo.** Run by the Wheaton Park District, it is a well-known family destination. Ask the district whether it hosts private events and under what terms.
- **Downtown Wheaton.** Streets and sidewalks are a City matter, and the Event Permit page is the start.
- **Private venues, banquet rooms and homes.** Wheaton has hotels, restaurants and many large homes; rules come from the venue or the owner.

## Alcohol and tents: ask, do not assume

Park districts often have specific alcohol rules, and those rules differ by park and by event type. Do not assume that because a spot allows a picnic it also allows a keg, a bar or a tent. Ask the district directly, and ask the City if your event needs a temporary liquor license. For tents, read our [tent permit guide for Chicago suburbs](/blog/tent-permit-chicago-suburbs).

## Delivery from our St. Charles base

Wheaton is a straightforward drive east from St. Charles, mostly along the arterial roads that cross DuPage County. Plan around rush hour, when Roosevelt Road, Geneva Road and the Main Street area can slow down. Ask the venue for the unloading location, especially downtown. College events around Wheaton College may affect traffic on certain weekends, so check dates.

## Seasonal notes

Spring is busy with graduations and open houses, so rentals go early. Summer picnics are common in the parks; heat and shade are the main questions. Fall is the best outdoor season for weddings. In December, sunset is about 4:20 pm, so indoor lighting matters. For weather tips see [the seasons and venues archive](/blog/topic/seasons-and-venues).

## Counting everything for a Wheaton event

Before you request a quote, walk through your event in order and count each item once.

- **Arrival:** a welcome table, a sign-in or gift table, and cocktail tables if you serve drinks.
- **Ceremony or program:** chairs in rows, an aisle or two, and an area for speakers.
- **Meal:** guest tables, a buffet line or serving tables, and a beverage station.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need an event permit in Wheaton?

If your event uses public property such as streets or City-owned space, the City of Wheaton has an Event Permit process. For park picnic areas, the Wheaton Park District sets the rules. Private events on private property generally do not need either, but check local ordinances on tents and noise.

### Where do I rent a picnic area in Wheaton?

From the Wheaton Park District. It publishes picnic area rentals and a park rentals brochure, so start there for availability, capacity and fees.

### Is alcohol allowed in Wheaton parks?

That is set by the park district and can vary by park and event. Ask the Wheaton Park District before you plan a bar, and ask the City if you need a temporary liquor license.

### Can I hold a wedding at Cosley Zoo or Memorial Park?

Ask the Wheaton Park District, which manages both. They can tell you whether private events are allowed and under what terms.

### Do you deliver in Wheaton?

Yes. First deliveries begin with the April 2027 Founding Season. We avoid peak rush hours on DuPage arterials.

### What chairs work for a Wheaton park party?

Lightweight chairs that sit well on grass, such as garden folding or cross-back chairs. Ask about flooring if the ground is damp.
