## Formal means spacing

The difference between a casual dinner and a formal one is mostly space. A full place setting needs room for a plate, flatware on both sides, glassware and bread plate. Industry rule of thumb is about 24 inches of table edge per guest.

## Lakefront pavilions

Lake Forest Parks and Recreation lists beach pavilion rentals on its website. If a pavilion appeals to you, ask these questions before you plan:

1. How many guests does the pavilion hold, and is the capacity for seated or standing guests?
2. Are tables and chairs included, and are outside rentals allowed?
3. What are the rules for tents, music, alcohol and food service?
4. What are the access and delivery rules, including parking?
5. What happens in bad weather?

Rules change by season, so always confirm directly with the department. Beaches can be windy and cooler than inland areas, so plan wraps for evening events and weight your linens.

## Seating a formal ceremony

Formal ceremonies need an aisle at least 5 feet wide for processions, and rows spaced about 36 inches front to back so guests can sit and stand comfortably. A ceremony for 100 guests usually uses 10 rows of 10, with a center aisle, which takes roughly 30 feet of depth including the altar area. Mark the aisle early and make sure the first row has room for family to step out.

If the ceremony and reception are in the same space, you can flip chairs from rows to rounds during cocktail hour. Plan a crew, a clear route and a start time for the flip. Our guide to [ceremony versus reception chairs](/blog/ceremony-vs-reception-chairs) explains when it makes sense to rent separate sets.

## Pairing linens and florals with chairs

A formal table has layers: floor-length linen, charger plate, napkin, glassware and a centerpiece low enough to talk over. Keep centerpieces either below 12 inches or above 24 inches, so guests can see each other. Match your chair to the linen first. Ivory and white work with almost any chair; deeper colors may need a lighter chair so the room does not feel heavy. A clear chair is the safest bet when the linen has a pattern.

## Frequently asked questions

### What chair is best for a formal Lake Forest wedding?

The Louis medallion chair is the most formal choice, followed by the Windsor and the ghost chair. Many hosts use Louis chairs for the head table only to control cost.

### How much table width does each guest need?

About 24 inches of table edge per guest for a formal dinner. That is why a 60-inch round seats 8 and a 72-inch round seats 10 comfortably.

### Can I rent a beach pavilion in Lake Forest?

Lake Forest Parks and Recreation lists beach pavilion rentals. Check their site for current rules, availability and whether outside rentals are allowed.

### Do I need a rental company for chairs if my venue has some?

Not always. Compare the venue's chairs to your style goals. Many hosts rent upgraded chairs for the ceremony or the head table only.
