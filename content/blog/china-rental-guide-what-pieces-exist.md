**Quick answer**

Rental china comes in a core set and an add-on set. The core set is a dinner plate (about 10 to 11 inches), a salad or dessert plate (about 7 to 8 inches), a bread plate (about 6 inches) and a cup and saucer. Add-ons are soup bowls, charger plates (12 to 13 inches), appetizer plates, platters and serving bowls. Most events need only three or four of these, chosen by the menu.

</div>

## The core pieces

**Dinner plate.** The main course plate, usually 10 to 11 inches. It also works for buffet service and for a plated first course when you want fewer pieces on the table.

**Salad plate.** Around 7 to 8 inches. Salads, appetizers, small desserts and cake slices all land here. If your caterer plates a salad course, you need one per guest, and many couples use the same plate for dessert to cut the count.

**Bread plate.** About 6 inches, set above the forks at the left. It is optional for most casual dinners. Our guide to [bread and butter plates](/blog/bread-and-butter-plates-do-you-need) covers when to skip it.

**Dessert plate.** About 7 to 8 inches. A salad plate often does the job, but a matching dessert plate keeps a wedding cake display consistent. See [how many dessert and cake plates](/blog/dessert-and-cake-plates-how-many) to count them.

**Cup and saucer.** For coffee and tea service after dinner. Count them for the share of guests who will take a hot drink, and ask your caterer how they plan to serve it.

## How to choose by menu

Ask your caterer for the final menu, then match each course to a plate. A plated three-course dinner needs a salad plate, a dinner plate and a dessert plate. A buffet with cake needs a dinner plate and a dessert plate. A cocktail party needs only small plates. Our [plates-per-guest guide](/blog/how-many-plates-per-guest) turns this into counts.

<ol class="flow">
<li><strong>Get the menu</strong> and the service style from your caterer.</li>
<li><strong>List each course</strong> and assign one plate or bowl to it.</li>
<li><strong>Decide on extras</strong> such as chargers and bread plates.</li>
<li><strong>Count per guest</strong>, then add a cushion for breakage and late RSVPs.</li>
<li><strong>Confirm the pattern</strong> and the return rules before you sign the quote.</li>

## Pattern choices

Plain white is the safest choice. It works with any linen color and any food. Gold or silver rims suit formal events, and our comparison of [gold-rim vs white china](/blog/gold-rim-vs-white-china) goes through the trade-offs. Colored and patterned plates show up at garden parties and brunches, covered in [colored and patterned plates](/blog/colored-and-patterned-plates-trend). Whatever you pick, look at a physical sample if you can, because photos change the shade of white.

## What your venue or caterer may already provide

Many Chicago banquet halls include basic white china in the package. Some caterers bring their own. Ask both before renting anything. If the venue provides plates but not chargers, you may only need chargers and bread plates from a rental order. Get this in writing so you do not pay twice.

## Frequently asked questions

### What pieces of china do I need for a wedding?

Most weddings need a dinner plate, a salad or dessert plate, a bread plate if bread is served, and a cup and saucer if coffee is served. Add chargers if you want a formal look. Your caterer's menu decides the rest.

### What size is a standard dinner plate?

A standard dinner plate is about 10 to 11 inches across. A charger is wider, around 12 to 13 inches, and sits under the dinner plate. Always check the exact size of the pattern you pick, because a few run slightly larger.

### Is rental china real china?

Rental china is usually porcelain or bone china built to survive many events. It is heavier and more durable than home china. Ask the rental company about the material if it matters to you.

### Do I need chargers if I rent dinner plates?

No. Chargers are decorative and optional. They add a formal look and protect the linen from sauce, but a dinner plate works fine on its own. See [are charger plates worth it](/blog/are-charger-plates-worth-it).

### Can the caterer use rental china?

Yes. Most caterers plate onto whatever you rent, as long as the sizes work for their kitchen. Tell them the plate sizes before the order is final.

### How far ahead should I order dinnerware?

Order as soon as the guest count and menu are close to firm, especially for spring, summer and fall weekends when Chicago venues book up. Details can change later with a small buffer on counts.
