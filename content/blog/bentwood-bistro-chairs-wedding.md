Bentwood chairs come from the café tradition: curved wood, a round seat and a looped back that has been the same shape for generations. At a wedding they look like a long, candlelit dinner in a Paris bistro. If that is the feeling you want in Chicago, they are one of the least expensive ways to get it. They are not right for every room, though, so this guide covers where they excel and where they fall short.

## The shape and what it means for guests

The bentwood is a light chair. The wood is steam-bent into curves, so there are no hard corners and the back follows the spine gently. The seat is round, which is less roomy than a square seat. Our [bentwood bistro chair](/chairs/bentwood) comes in espresso, natural and black, and has an optional seat cushion.

What guests notice:

- **Light and easy to pull in and out.** Good for older guests who need to move a chair themselves.
- **Smaller seat.** Taller or larger guests may prefer a wider seat. See our guide to [chair weight capacity and seat sizes](/blog/chair-weight-capacity-and-seat-sizes).
- **Open back.** No upholstery, so it feels airy rather than formal.

## Where bentwood chairs shine

Choose bentwood chairs if your wedding is:

1. **An intimate dinner of 30 to 80 guests.** The chair's visual weight is small, so a full room does not look heavy.
2. **A restaurant or wine bar buyout.** Many restaurants already use bentwoods, so yours will blend in.
3. **Vintage or European in theme.** Taper candles, checkered or white linens, a string trio.
4. **A rehearsal dinner.** Casual, quick to set up and low cost.

## Where they do not

Be honest with yourself about the room:

- **Outdoor ceremonies.** In the catalog, the bentwood is indoor-only. Wood bent by steam can be sensitive to moisture, and thin legs sink on grass.
- **Very large receptions.** At 200 guests, the non-stacking chairs add delivery and storage headaches.
- **Black-tie ballrooms.** The look is too casual unless you pair it with luxe details.

If you need an outdoor option that looks similar, the [cross-back chair](/chairs/cross-back) is sturdier and stacks. See also [cross-back chair rental in Chicago](/blog/cross-back-chair-rental-chicago).

## Finishes and what they say

- **Espresso:** the café classic. Works with white linen and gold flatware.
- **Natural:** lighter, warmer and a little rustic. Pairs with greenery and wildflowers.
- **Black:** the Parisian bistro look, with a modern finish.

## Setup, spacing and Chicago venues

Indoor Chicago venues, including loft spaces and historic buildings, often have fixed floor plans. Measure the room before you pick small round tables. A 36-inch round seats two, a 48-inch round seats up to four and a 60-inch round seats up to eight, though the bentwood's round seat makes 8 tight. For counts, use [how many chairs do I need](/blog/how-many-chairs-do-i-need) and add the head table, bar seating and any lounge chairs.

Bentwoods are light enough to move around by hand, but they do not stack. When you ask for a quote, say whether stairs or an elevator are involved, because it changes setup time.

## Pairing with other chairs

A single row of bentwoods can look flat in a large room. Many couples add a [velvet dining chair](/chairs/velvet-dining) for the head table, or a pair of cane-back chairs for contrast. [Mixing chair styles at one reception](/blog/mixing-chair-styles-at-reception) explains how to do it without making the room look random.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are bentwood chairs good for a wedding?

Yes, for intimate or vintage weddings held indoors. They are light and affordable, but not ideal for outdoor ceremonies or very large guest lists.

### How much do bentwood chairs cost to rent in Chicago?

Our estimate is $9 to $12 per chair. The final price depends on quantity, finish, delivery and setup.

### Are bentwood chairs comfortable?

They are comfortable for a normal dinner, but the round seat is smaller than a banquet seat. Add a cushion for longer meals.

### Can bentwood chairs be used outside?

Not in our catalog. We recommend indoor use only, or a covered, dry patio.

### Do bentwood chairs stack?

No, so plan extra delivery and storage space, especially for a large guest count.

### Which bentwood finish is most popular?

We can't point to sales data yet, but espresso and natural are the typical choices for weddings because they go with most linens. Black fits modern rooms.

### What tables go with bentwood chairs?

Round tables from 36 to 60 inches, farm tables and marble-style cafe tables all work. Cloths can be floor-length or left bare with a runner.
