There's no rule saying your ceremony and reception chairs have to match. Here's how to think about it.

## When matching makes sense

- **Same space for both.** If you're flipping the room, one chair keeps things simple.
- **A tight budget.** One style means one rental line and fewer moving parts.
- **A minimalist look.** One clean chair everywhere reads as calm and considered.

## When mixing works better

- **Outdoor ceremony, indoor reception.** Use a weather-friendly folding or garden chair outside and a more refined chair inside.
- **You want a moment.** Changing chairs marks the shift from the formal ceremony to the party.
- **A statement head table.** Guests sit in cross-backs while the couple sits in velvet or a carved sweetheart chair.

## Pairings we love

- Teak garden folding (ceremony) → Cross-back (reception)
- Ghost (ceremony) → Velvet (reception)
- Windsor (ceremony) → Bentwood (reception)
