**Quick answer**

Friendsgiving for 20 works best on two 8-foot tables joined into one long table, or a long table plus a round table, with a separate buffet table so the food doesn't take up seating space. Rent one chair per guest, 20 in all, plus two buffet tables, a drinks table and a dessert table. Set the buffet away from the dining table and serve in two lines so 20 people can fill plates in about 10 minutes.

Friendsgiving is a potluck that grew up. It started as a dinner among friends who couldn't get home, and now it's a full meal with 15 to 30 people, a long table, and a fridge that is somehow always out of room. The real constraint is space: where do the dishes go, where do the people sit, and how do you keep a traffic jam from forming around the mashed potatoes.

## Separate the food from the seats

At a family Thanksgiving, dishes go on the table. At a Friendsgiving of 20, there isn't room. Put the food on its own table and let people carry plates to their seats.

## What to rent

### Tables
- **Dinner table:** two 8-foot banquet tables, end to end. With 5 seats on each long side and one at each end, that seats 12; for 20, use a long table of 3 sections (24 feet) or two parallel tables.
- **Buffet:** one or two 6- or 8-foot tables for hot dishes, with room for 8 to 12 dishes per table.
- **Drinks and dessert:** one smaller table each.

See [how many tables for 50 to 200 guests](/blog/how-many-tables-for-50-100-150-200-guests) for scaling up, and [banquet table sizes](/blog/banquet-table-sizes-6-ft-vs-8-ft).

### Chairs
Mixed chairs are part of the Friendsgiving look, so don't worry about matching. [Cross-back chairs](/chairs/cross-back), estimated $8 to $11, are warm and wood-toned. [Windsor chairs](/chairs/windsor) feel like a farmhouse. [Bentwood chairs](/chairs/bentwood) are light. Use your own chairs where you can and rent the rest.

### Linens
Natural linen, burlap or a plain runner on a wood table suits a fall palette. Colors that photograph well: rust, olive, cream, deep burgundy. For sizes, see the [tablecloth size chart](/blog/tablecloth-size-chart).

### Dishes and serving
Potluck means every dish needs a serving utensil, and you'll run short of platters. Count 1 serving spoon for each dish, plus a second for the turkey and gravy. For plates, glasses and flatware by guest count, see [how many plates per guest](/blog/how-many-plates-per-guest).

## Move people through the buffet

<ol class="flow">
<li><strong>Place the table where people can approach from two sides.</strong></li>
<li><strong>Order the dishes:</strong> plates first, then mains, sides, then sauces, and last, bread and butter.</li>
<li><strong>Put drinks elsewhere</strong> so the line doesn't stop at the cups.</li>
<li><strong>Label each dish</strong> with a tent card, especially for allergens.</li>

## The kids table

Put kids at their own table within sight of the adults. A 48-inch round seats 6 kids, and a 6-foot rectangle seats 6 to 8. Give them paper tablecloths and crayons. Seat a teen at the table as the captain.

## Chicago-area notes

November in the Chicago area can bring cold snaps, early darkness and snow, so indoor space matters. If you plan to use a garage or a heated tent, check your village's rules. Sunset comes before 5 p.m. in late November, so plan lighting from the start. Our [guide to backyard lighting](/blog/backyard-party-lighting-ideas) covers candles and string lights, and for heating, check the manufacturer's clearances. Also tell guests where to park, since a 20-person party can fill a block.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many tables do I need for Friendsgiving with 20 people?

Two 8-foot tables placed end to end, or two 60-inch rounds with 10 each, plus one or two tables for the buffet and one for drinks and dessert.

### How do I seat 20 people in a small house?

Use a long table through the dining and living rooms, remove the sofa for the evening, or use two rooms with a table in each. Rent chairs to fill the gap.

### Should I do a buffet or family style?

A buffet is easier with 20 or more, because family style requires table space for every dish. Buffet lets the food sit on its own table.

### How much food per person?

Plan about a pound of turkey per person with bone-in, and a mix of sides that adds up to a full plate. Potluck makes the total unpredictable, so assign dishes by category.

### What do I rent for a potluck?

Tables, chairs, warmers, serving utensils and extra plates. Ask guests to bring dishes in disposable or labeled containers.

### Can I host Friendsgiving outdoors in Chicago?

Possible in early November with a heated tent or patio heaters, but weather is a gamble. Keep an indoor plan.
