**Quick answer**

Order one dessert plate per guest, plus a cushion of about 10% for seconds, vendors and spills. Cake slices are usually served one per guest, so a 100-guest wedding needs about 100 to 110 dessert plates and forks. If you also serve a plated dessert or a dessert table, count those plates separately. A 7 to 8-inch plate is the usual size.

</div>

Dessert plates are the easiest piece of the dinnerware order to forget, then the first thing to run out. The cake is cut at 9 pm, 120 guests line up, and the caterer discovers there are 80 small plates on the cart. This guide turns the cake, the dessert table and the late-night snack into a plate count you can trust. It applies equally to a wedding in a Chicago banquet hall and a graduation party in a backyard.

## One plate per guest is the baseline

A plated or buffet wedding with cake gives each guest a slice. That is one dessert plate per guest. People who go back for a second slice use a second plate unless the caterer clears and reuses. Because rentals are washed after the event, a plate that returns to the table is not re-washed on site, so count each use as one plate.

## Count by dessert type

<div class="table-wrap">

| Dessert | Plates per guest | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Wedding cake, served by slice | 1 | Plus cake forks |
| Plated dessert course | 1 | Sized to the dessert |
| Dessert table with small bites | 1 to 2 | Mini plates or napkins |
| Cupcakes or cookies | 0 to 1 | Napkins may be enough |
| Groom's cake or second cake | 0.3 to 0.5 | Often a few extra slices |

</div>

If a caterer plates the wedding cake in the kitchen, they use your dessert plates and need them delivered with the dinnerware. If you cut the cake yourselves, ask who is serving. This affects how many plates must be washed and staged.

## Can a salad plate double as dessert?

Yes, and it is a common trick. A salad plate is 7 to 8 inches, which fits a cake slice. If you serve a salad course, the same plate can return for dessert, but only if the caterer clears and re-plates, and the sequence is far enough apart. Many companies advise counting separate plates to keep service quick. Our [plates per guest guide](/blog/how-many-plates-per-guest) explains the count.

## Forks and cake service

Each plate needs a fork or spoon. Dessert forks are smaller than dinner forks. The standard cake cutting set includes a knife and server, which couples often bring or borrow from the venue. See the [cake table checklist](/blog/topic/tables) and for more details on layout, check our table guides.

## A dessert table: smaller plates

At a dessert table, guests take two or three small items. Use 6-inch plates or small paper napkins. If guests walk away holding plates, smaller ones are easier. Plan two plates per guest for variety. For a bigger spread of sweets, see [family-style serving ware](/blog/family-style-serving-ware) for platters and tiered stands.

## Late-night snacks

Pizza, sliders and donuts after the dance floor opens are served on paper plates or napkins most of the time. Rental china at midnight rarely makes sense, because guests carry plates onto the dance floor and plates go missing. Plan for disposable at this stage and note it in your rental order so you do not pay for plates that never return. We compare options in [compostable vs rented china](/blog/compostable-vs-rented-china).

## Chicago planning notes

Winter weddings in Chicago run indoors with plenty of staff, so china works well. Summer parties in parks may have rules on glass and breakable items; check with the village or park district before ordering china for an outdoor space. Venues may also restrict where cake is cut and where plates are washed.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many dessert plates do I need for a wedding cake?

You need one dessert plate per guest, plus about 10% for seconds. For 100 guests, order 110. Vendors who eat cake count too.

### Do I need dessert plates if I have a dessert table?

Yes, small ones. Order at least one per guest and often two. Napkins alone work for cookies, but cake and mousse need plates.

### What size is a dessert plate?

Most are 7 to 8 inches across. Cake slices fit on 6 to 7 inches. Check the pattern sizes before you order forks.

### Can I use salad plates for cake?

Yes, if the salad plates are 7 to 8 inches and the service flow allows it. Plan extra plates if you serve both courses close together.

### Who cuts and serves the cake?

The caterer or venue staff usually cuts and plates the cake. Ask in advance, since some charge a cake-cutting fee. We do not set venue fees.

### Do I need cake forks?

Yes. Each plate needs a fork or spoon. Dessert forks are smaller than dinner forks, and a few extra are useful.
