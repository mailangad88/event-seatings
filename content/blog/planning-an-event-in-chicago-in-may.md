## May weather: what to watch out for

May is mild and green. NWS normals for O'Hare give May an average high of 70.5°F and a low of 50.6°F. Warm afternoons are common, but so are cool evenings and fast-moving storms.

What to plan for:

- **Storms.** Spring storms can arrive quickly. Choose a tent rated for wind, have a plan for moving guests, and keep an eye on the forecast the week of the event. Read [tent wind ratings and weather safety](/blog/tent-wind-ratings-weather-safety).
- **Cool evenings.** A 50°F evening feels cold after a warm afternoon. Keep heaters or blankets available for anything after sunset.
- **Long light.** Sunset moves from about 7:47 pm on May 1 to about 8:14 pm by May 28, which is ideal for evening photos.
- **Ground.** Lawns are drier than in April but can still be soft after rain. Flooring helps under a dance area.

## Venue demand and booking lead times in May

May is peak season for party rentals in the suburbs. Graduation parties cluster on the weekends after ceremonies, and they all want the same tents, tables and chairs. Our rule of thumb: book tents, specialty chairs and dance floors 2 to 4 months ahead for May Saturdays, and confirm your date the moment the school announces the ceremony.

Venues are busy too, with weddings, proms and communions. If you want a Saturday in May at a popular venue, it is often booked many months ahead, so consider a Friday or Sunday. ## Frequently asked questions

### Is May a good month for an outdoor event in Chicago?

Yes, it is one of the first reliable months. May averages a 70.5°F high and a 50.6°F low at O'Hare, but storms and cool evenings still happen, so keep a tent or indoor backup.

### How early should I book a tent for a May graduation party?

Two to 4 months ahead for a Saturday, and as soon as the ceremony date is announced. Graduation parties cluster on the same weekends and tents are the first item to run out.

### When is Memorial Day weekend in 2027?

Saturday, May 29 through Monday, May 31, 2027. Many families travel, so check your guest list before picking that weekend.

### When is Eid al-Adha in 2027?

Around May 16 or 17, 2027, depending on the moon sighting. Community halls and tents are in demand for family gatherings that weekend.

### What time is sunset in Chicago in May?

About 7:47 pm on May 1, 8:02 pm on May 15 and 8:14 pm on May 28 in 2027. Evening events have plenty of light for photos.

### Should I avoid Mother's Day weekend for my event?

Usually, unless the event is a Mother's Day celebration. Restaurants are full and many families already have plans on Sunday, May 9, 2027.
