Nothing ruins a lawn dinner like slapping your ankles through the toast. In the Chicago area, bug season is long, from the first warm evenings in May to the first frost, and the worst of it follows rain and humidity. This guide gives a practical plan that does not rely on a single trick.

## When bugs are worst in the Chicago area

Mosquitoes breed in standing water, so a wet June or a rainy August means more of them. The Illinois Department of Public Health has said West Nile virus cases typically peak in August and September, which is why late-summer events deserve the most prep. Wasps and yellow jackets become more aggressive near sweet drinks and food in late summer too.

## The IDPH three R's, applied to events

<ol class="flow">
<li><strong>Reduce</strong> empty or refresh standing water (birdbaths, buckets, planters, tarps) at least a week before.</li>
<li><strong>Repel</strong> provide EPA-registered repellent with DEET, picaridin, oil of lemon eucalyptus or IR3535.</li>
<li><strong>Report</strong> tell the local health department or village about water that stays stagnant.</li>
</ol>

The IDPH's advice is aimed at households, but it translates well to events. The week before, walk the site and tip out anything that holds water. Cover or move tarps that pool water. If your event is at a park or forest preserve, call the district about any standing water you notice.

Mosquitoes are weak fliers. A steady breeze across the seating keeps them off skin better than most repellents. Put tables on open lawn, away from the pond, creek or storm drain, and upwind of any shaded thicket. Avoid placing the dessert and drinks near trees where wasps nest.

## Fans, lights and food

- **Fans**: a pedestal or tent fan aimed at the table height makes it hard for mosquitoes to land. See [fans and coolers for summer events](/blog/fans-and-coolers-for-summer-events). They need power; read the [event power needs checklist](/blog/event-power-needs-checklist).
- **Lighting**: bright white lights attract insects. Warm lights are less attractive, and placing them away from the dining area draws bugs away from guests.
- **Food**: cover buffet dishes and use lids on drinks. Sweet drinks attract wasps.
- **Trash**: close lids and empty bins during the event. See [trash, recycling and composting stations](/blog/trash-recycling-composting-stations-for-events).
- **Candles**: citronella candles help only a little and may be restricted by the venue. Check [candle fire rules](/blog/candles-at-venues-fire-code-rules).

### Repellent stations

Put a small basket of repellent wipes or sprays at the ceremony and at the restrooms. Keep it simple, label the ingredient and offer at least one DEET-free choice. The CDC advises against oil of lemon eucalyptus products for children under 3. Do not spray near food, and ask guests to use it before they sit down.

## Hiring professionals

Some hosts hire a licensed pest control company to treat the lawn days before an event. If you do, ask what product they use, how long guests and pets must stay off the area and whether it is allowed at your venue. A public park, forest preserve or club will have its own rule; do not apply anything without permission. See our guide to [dog-friendly event rentals](/blog/dog-friendly-event-rentals) if pets will be present.

## Chairs and seating

Choose chairs that do not hold sweat in heat: open-back styles like the [cross-back](/chairs/cross-back) or [rattan garden chair](/chairs/rattan-garden). Avoid seating guests near the lawn edge or under thick trees.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I keep mosquitoes away from an outdoor party?

Remove standing water a week before, choose a breezy open site, run fans at the tables and offer EPA-registered repellent. No single method works alone.

### When are mosquitoes worst in Chicago?

Late summer. Illinois health officials say West Nile cases typically peak in August and September, and wet weather increases breeding.

### Do citronella candles work?

They help a little at best, and some venues restrict open flame. Fans and repellent are more reliable.

### Can I spray my lawn before an event?

You can hire a licensed pest control company, but ask about the product and the waiting time, and get venue permission if the space is not yours.

### Do fans really help with mosquitoes?

Yes. Mosquitoes are weak fliers, and a steady breeze at table height makes it hard for them to land.

### How do I keep wasps away from food and drinks?

Cover food and drinks, empty trash often and keep sweet items away from trees and eaves.

### What repellent is safe for children?

Follow the label and check with a pediatrician. The CDC advises against oil of lemon eucalyptus for children under 3.

## Add bug control to your rental list

When you request a quote, tell the rental company the date, the site and whether it is near water or woods. Ask about tent sidewalls, which keep out wind-blown insects when closed, fans and power. A screened or enclosed tent is an option for sites with heavy pressure; ask the vendor what is available. For more date planning, see our guides to [August events](/blog/outdoor-events-chicago-in-august) and the [summer heat plan](/blog/summer-event-heat-plan-chicago).

A last practical note: tell guests ahead of time. A line in the invitation such as "This is an outdoor evening event; bring a light layer" sets expectations, and a bug-prep line in the day-of materials helps people plan clothing. Light-colored long sleeves and closed shoes keep mosquitoes off skin without any spray at all. Most guests will be glad you thought of it, and the staff will spend less time fielding complaints.
