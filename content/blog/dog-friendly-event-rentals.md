**Quick answer**

To host dogs at an event, first confirm the venue allows them, then plan shade, water, a waste station, a quiet rest area and a way to keep dogs away from food and the dance floor. Rent a few extra chairs and a small table or two near the dog area so owners can sit with their pets, and assign a person to manage the space. Plan on one water station per 10 dogs, a rule of thumb.

Dogs at weddings, birthday parties and fundraisers are no longer unusual. A ring-bearer dog, a "yappy hour" for a shelter benefit or a casual backyard party where everyone brought their pup all need a plan. Done right, dogs bring joy and photos. Done wrong, you get a barking chorus during the vows and a mess near the buffet. This guide covers the rentals and rules that help.

## Start with the rules

Venues, parks and cities set their own rules about animals, and they differ widely. Do not assume dogs are allowed.

1. **Ask the venue first.** Many private venues ban pets or require approval. Get the policy in writing.
2. **Check the park district or forest preserve.** Public lands often have leash rules and restrictions on dogs at events. Ask the specific agency.
3. **Ask the caterer.** Food-service rules may restrict animals near food prep.
4. **Ask your insurer.** Event insurance may need to cover pets.
5. **Service animals are different.** Service dogs have legal access in most public venues, and event hosts should plan for them separately. If you need guidance, check the ADA information for your event and the venue's policy.

Cook County and each suburb may have animal licensing and leash rules, so check with your municipality if the event is in a public space. Do not rely on a general policy.

## Seating and comfort

Chairs matter. Owners want to be near their dogs but not on the ground. Choose light, easy-to-move chairs, such as [garden folding chairs](/chairs/garden-folding), and add a side table for drinks. Avoid long dangling linens near the dog area, since dogs chew and trip on them. Keep chair legs stable, since leashes can wrap around them.

Provide a mat or rug for dogs to settle, a bowl of water and a quiet corner. A dog that can lie down in the shade barks less than one that stands in the sun.

## Keeping dogs and food apart

Dogs and buffets are a bad mix. Set the dog area at least 30 feet from food, and keep it downwind if you can. Do not allow dogs under tables or near the bar. Remind guests not to feed other people's dogs. If a dog sits at a table with its owner, use a tight spot away from the buffet line.

## Heat, weather and pavement

Chicago summers are hot and humid. Dogs overheat faster than people, and hot pavement burns paws. Offer shade, water and a cool grassy spot. Limit the dog time to the cooler hours if possible, and watch for panting, drooling and lethargy. In winter, salt and ice irritate paws, so provide a wipe-down spot at the entrance. See [misting and shade for summer events](/blog/misting-and-shade-for-summer-events) and [seasons and venues guides](/blog/topic/seasons-and-venues).

## Etiquette for hosts

- **Tell guests the rules** on the invitation or website: leash, vaccinations, behavior, quiet time.
- **Cap the number** of dogs, as a few friendly dogs are fun and ten are chaos.
- **Assign a handler.** A friend or pet sitter manages the area and can take a dog out.
- **Plan for the ceremony.** Dogs in the ceremony need a handler to bring them out.
- **Allow an exit plan.** An anxious dog needs a quiet spot or a ride home.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I bring dogs to my wedding?

If the venue allows it. Ask in writing, plan shade and water and assign a handler. Many couples include dogs in portraits and then send them home before dinner.

### Do I need a fence for a dog area?

Not always, but it helps. Temporary fencing keeps dogs away from food and exits. Ask the venue what is allowed.

### How many water bowls do I need?

About one station per 10 dogs, placed in shade, and refilled often.

### Where should the dog area go?

At the edge of the event, away from food and the dance floor, with shade and a gate.

### Are service dogs allowed at events?

Service dogs generally have legal access to public spaces. Confirm with your venue and plan space for them at tables.

### What chairs work best near dogs?

Lightweight, stable chairs without long fabric that dogs can chew. Garden folding chairs suit most setups.
