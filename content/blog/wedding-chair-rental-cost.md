**Quick answer**

Wedding chair rental in Chicago costs about $3 to $4 per chair for white folding chairs and $10 to $16 for Chiavari, cross-back or ghost chairs. For 120 guests at $10 a chair, budget about $1,200 for chairs, then add delivery and setup, a damage waiver of about 10% and sales tax.

Chair rental prices vary widely by style, quantity, date and location. Here is how to think about the budget.

## What affects the price

- **The chair style.** Simple folding chairs cost less than upholstered or specialty pieces.
- **Quantity.** Larger orders often carry a lower per-chair rate.
- **Delivery and setup.** Distance, venue access, stairs and set-up time all matter.
- **Ceremony flip.** Moving chairs from the ceremony to the reception takes labor.
- **Date.** Peak season Saturdays are the most in-demand.

## Estimating your total

A simple formula: guests × per-chair price, plus delivery and setup. For a 120-guest reception at around $10 per chair, that is about $1,200 before delivery. Specialty chairs, a second ceremony set or a flip add to this.

## Ways to save

1. Use a premium chair only where it shows most, such as the head table, and a simpler chair elsewhere.
2. Hold the ceremony and reception in the same room, so no second set is needed.
3. Book early. Popular styles and dates fill first.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does it cost to rent 100 chairs for a wedding?

About $300 to $400 for 100 folding chairs, or $1,000 to $1,600 for 100 Chiavari, cross-back or ghost chairs, before delivery, setup, damage waiver and tax. The quote builder shows the exact total for your style.

### Is delivery included in chair rental prices?

No. Delivery and pickup are priced per order, usually $150 to $400 in the Chicago area depending on distance, stairs and timing. Setup and a ceremony-to-reception flip are quoted separately.

### How can I lower the cost of wedding chairs?

Use a premium chair only where it shows, such as the head table, and a simpler chair elsewhere. Hold the ceremony and reception in the same room to avoid a second set, and book early so the cheaper styles are still available.
