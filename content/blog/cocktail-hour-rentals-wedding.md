**Quick answer**

A wedding cocktail hour needs highboy cocktail tables (about 1 per 8 to 10 guests), seating for roughly a quarter to a third of guests, a bar with fronts and glassware, and napkins and small plates for passed food. Plan about 6 to 8 square feet per guest standing, and add 10 to 15 square feet per lounge group. Place the bar away from the entry so lines do not block the door.

</div>

Cocktail hour is the first hour guests are mixing, and the one that sets the mood while the room flips. It is also the hour when rentals matter least to photos but most to guests: where can I put my drink, where can I sit, and where is the bar? The Chicago couples who plan this well rent fewer pieces than they expect and place them better.

## What guests actually do

People stand to mingle but want a surface within arm's reach and a seat for a few minutes. A good cocktail hour offers three modes: standing at highboys, sitting in small groups, and moving to a bar or food station.

## The rental list

**Highboy cocktail tables.** About 42 inches tall and 30 to 36 inches across, they seat nobody and hold plates and drinks. A common ratio is 1 per 8 to 10 guests. For 120 guests that is 12 to 15 tables. See [cocktail highboy vs lowboy tables](/blog/cocktail-highboy-vs-lowboy-tables).

**Linens.** A 120-inch round cloth tied at the waist, or a fitted spandex cover. See the [tablecloth size chart](/blog/tablecloth-size-chart).

**Seating.** Provide seats for about a quarter to a third of guests. Seniors and guests with heels will thank you. Use lounge sofas and a few [bentwood bistro chairs](/chairs/bentwood) or [rattan garden chairs](/chairs/rattan-garden) in small groups. See [lounge furniture for cocktail hour](/blog/lounge-furniture-for-cocktail-hour).

**Bar.** A bar front (or two), back-bar tables, glassware, ice tubs, a trash can and napkins. Count glasses with [how many glasses per guest](/blog/how-many-glasses-per-guest). Illinois alcohol rules for private events vary by situation, so confirm with your caterer or bartending provider who holds the liquor license.

**Food.** Passed hors d'oeuvres need small plates and napkins. A station needs tables, chafers and signs. The [food station equipment guide](/blog/food-station-ideas-and-equipment) lists common items.

**Extras.** A welcome sign, a guest book table, a photo spot, a small trash and recycling setup and, in winter, a coat check.

## Place things so the room flows

<ol class="flow">
<li><strong>Entry:</strong> leave a 6-foot clear zone inside the door.</li>
<li><strong>Bar:</strong> far wall or corner, with room for two lines.</li>
<li><strong>Highboys:</strong> scatter in a zigzag so people move around.</li>
<li><strong>Lounge:</strong> along the side walls, with a path behind.</li>
<li><strong>Exit:</strong> keep the door to the reception visible but not blocked.</li>
</ol>

## Indoor, outdoor and Chicago weather

An outdoor cocktail hour on a terrace or lawn needs shade in July and heat in October. A tent edge, patio heaters or umbrellas help. For indoor spaces in a Chicago hotel or loft, check elevator and loading rules for furniture. Cocktail hour is also where the ceremony space often flips; if so, plan a quick switch with the crew. A little rain on a patio can push the whole hour inside, so keep a backup room.

## Making the hour feel finished

A few small choices make cocktail hour look planned. Use a consistent linen color across highboys, a small candle or bud vase on each so tables are not bare, and a sign that names the signature drink. Keep trash cans out of sight but reachable, with one near every food station, because empty glasses pile up fast. If a photographer needs the room for portraits, tell the rental crew so tables are placed with that in mind.

For winter weddings in Chicago, a coat check or racks near the entry prevents a pile of coats on lounge sofas. For summer, shade and cold water are the equivalent. Neither costs much and both change how guests remember the hour.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many cocktail tables do I need for a wedding?

About 1 per 8 to 10 guests. For 100 guests that is 10 to 12 tables. Add a few more if the space is large and the group will mingle widely.

### Should I have seating at cocktail hour?

Yes, for roughly a quarter to a third of guests. Provide lounge seating and a few bistro chairs so older guests and anyone in heels can rest.

### How long is a wedding cocktail hour?

Typically 45 to 90 minutes. The length depends on how long the couple's photos take and how long the room flip needs.

### What do guests hold at cocktail hour?

A drink, a small plate and a napkin. That's why a surface within reach matters.

### How big is a highboy cocktail table?

Usually about 42 inches tall, with a top of 30 to 36 inches across. Confirm the size with your rental company.

### Do I need a separate bar for cocktail hour?

Not always. A single bar can serve both hours if the layout supports it, but two bars cut lines at larger weddings.
