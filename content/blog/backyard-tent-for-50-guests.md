**Quick answer**

A backyard tent for 50 guests is usually 20 x 30 ft (600 sq ft) for a seated dinner, or 20 x 40 ft (800 sq ft) if you want a dance floor or a buffet inside. That fits 6 round tables of 8 to 10. You also need about 5 to 10 feet of clear yard on each side for stakes and walkways, a utility locate before staking, and a permit check, since Chicago requires one above 400 square feet.

</div>

Fifty guests is the sweet spot for a backyard party: big enough for a real event, small enough for a regular yard. It is also where hosts underestimate the yard. A 20 x 30 tent is only 600 square feet, but it needs a clear area close to 1,200 once you add working room. This guide walks through the choices.

## Measure your yard first

Measure the flat area, not the whole lot. Subtract steps, trees, planters, utility boxes, the AC unit, grills and slopes. Then compare to the tent size plus working room. A 20 x 30 tent is 600 square feet, and with working room you need an open area of around 30 x 40 feet as a rule of thumb. Ask the tent company what footprint they require. See [how much space around a tent](/blog/space-around-a-tent). Slopes can be fixed with a floor: see [tent flooring and subfloors](/blog/tent-flooring-and-subfloors).

## The rental list for 50

- **Tent:** 20 x 30, with or without sidewalls.
- **Tables:** six 60-inch rounds seat 8 each (48 guests), or five 72-inch rounds seat 10. Add a buffet or bar table, a gift table and a cake table.
- **Chairs:** 50 guests plus 3 spares. [Garden folding chairs](/chairs/garden-folding) sit well on grass, and [cross-back chairs](/chairs/cross-back) suit a rustic yard. See [how many chairs you need](/blog/how-many-chairs-do-i-need).
- **Linens:** floor-length cloths for rounds; see the [tablecloth size chart](/blog/tablecloth-size-chart).
- **Lighting and power:** see [tent lighting ideas](/blog/tent-lighting-ideas).
- **Flooring:** only if the lawn is uneven or wet.
- **Restrooms:** a house bathroom may do; add a portable unit if you want guests out of the house.

## Power and food

Most backyards have limited outdoor outlets. Count lights, music, a coffee station and warmers, and ask your caterer what power they need. Extra circuits might be needed. Keep grills and fryers away from the tent; codes commonly keep open flame and cooking 20 feet away.

## Budget the extras, not just the tent

Hosts tend to compare tent quotes and forget the line items around them. Ask each company to price the tent, delivery, installation, takedown, sidewalls, flooring, lighting, heating or fans and permit handling separately, so you can compare like with like. ## Frequently asked questions

### What size tent do I need for 50 guests?

A 20 x 30 tent (600 sq ft) fits 50 seated guests at rounds. Move up to 20 x 40 for dancing or a buffet.

### How many tables do I need for 50 guests?

Six 60-inch rounds seat 8 each for 48 guests, or five 72-inch rounds seat 10. Add tables for gifts, cake and a bar.

### Do I need a permit for a 20 x 30 backyard tent?

Probably. It is 600 sq ft, over the 400 sq ft line used by Chicago, Oak Park and Winnetka. Chicago's Express Permit Program covers tents up to 600 sq ft. Check with your village.

### How much yard do I need for a 20 x 30 tent?

An open, level area of about 30 x 40 feet is a good rule of thumb, but your tent company gives the exact number for their equipment.

### Can I set up the tent myself?

A rental company should install it, since proper anchoring and permit rules matter. A pop-up canopy is a different case; see [pop-up canopy vs rented tent](/blog/pop-up-canopy-vs-rented-tent).

### What if it rains?

Keep sidewalls on hand, consider a floor and plan guest paths with mats. See [rain plan with a tent](/blog/rain-plan-with-a-tent).
