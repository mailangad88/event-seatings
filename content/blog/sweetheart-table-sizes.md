**Quick answer**

A sweetheart table for two is usually a 30 x 48-inch rectangle, a 30 x 60 or 30 x 72-inch banquet table, or a 36 to 48-inch round. Allow at least 24 inches of table edge per person and about 6 feet of clear space behind the chairs. A 6 ft banquet gives room for flowers, a 48-inch round feels cozier, and a 6 to 8 ft farm table fits a small wedding party.

</div>

A sweetheart table is the couple's own table at the reception, set apart from guests and facing the room. Couples love the quiet and the photos. The mistake is picking the wrong size. Too small and the table is crammed with flowers and glasses. Too big and it takes space from guest tables.

## How much room does a sweetheart table need

- **Table edge:** 24 inches per person at minimum.
- **Behind the chairs:** 4 to 6 feet so servers can pass.
- **In front:** a clear sightline for toasts and photos.
- **Distance from guests:** at least 6 feet to the nearest guest table.

Keep it near the dance floor and visible from every table. Avoid corners next to the kitchen door.

## Styling ideas

- A garland or floral swag along the front.
- A backdrop of draped fabric or greenery.
- Candles on the table and a runner in the wedding color.
- A floor-length cloth with a skirt in the front only. See [pleated skirting](/blog/pleated-table-skirting-head-table-buffet).

## Planning the spot, step by step

A sweetheart table feels special because of where it sits, not just how big it is. Walk your venue with a tape measure and answer these questions before you order.

- **Is there a clear sightline?** Every guest table should see you. Pillars, DJ gear and tall centerpieces on other tables can block the view.
- **Is there power nearby?** Fairy lights and candles are easy to run on batteries, but a string of lights needs an outlet.
- **Is there room for photos?** Photographers need a few feet in front and a clean backdrop behind. A wall of drapery or greenery makes cleaner photos than a busy room.
- **Is there a path for servers?** Servers need a route behind the table that does not cross the dance floor.

## Mistakes that crowd the table

Sweetheart tables get crowded because couples treat them as display tables. A plate, a bread plate, a water glass, a wine glass and a champagne flute for each person already take most of a 30-inch-deep table. Add a bouquet, a candle and a menu and nothing is left. Keep the centerpiece small, or hang flowers on the front of the table instead of on top. If you want a lavish look, choose a 72-inch table and keep the middle only 8 to 10 inches wide.

## What guests see

From the room, the table is a stage. The chairs, the cloth and the backdrop are what people photograph. Test the view from your guests' seats at your final walkthrough.

## Frequently asked questions

### What size is a sweetheart table?

Most are 30 x 48 to 30 x 72 inches. A 48-inch round is another common option.

### How long should a sweetheart table be?

Six feet is a safe choice for two people with a small centerpiece. Go to 8 feet only if you want a long floral runner.

### Do we need a tablecloth on a sweetheart table?

A floor-length cloth is common, but you can use a runner on a nice wood table. See the [tablecloth size chart](/blog/tablecloth-size-chart).

### Can a sweetheart table seat four?

Yes. Use a 6 to 8 foot table or a 60-inch round, if you want parents or the officiant with you.

### Where should the sweetheart table go?

Face the room, near the dance floor, with a wall or backdrop behind it. Avoid doors and kitchen traffic.

### How many chairs do we need for a sweetheart table?

Two, plus one or two extras for guests who stop by. Include them in your [chair count](/blog/how-many-chairs-do-i-need).
