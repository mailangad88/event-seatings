Homer Glen is a Will County village in the southwest suburbs known for larger residential lots, open space and a more rural feel than many Chicago suburbs. That space is a gift for a big party, but it creates its own challenges: long driveways, gates, soft lawns and the question of where in a large yard to put things. This guide helps you stage a party on acreage.

Measure the carry in feet. A long carry costs time and adds labor, and soft ground can make carts impossible. If the lawn is wet, ask about plywood, mats or a hard path. Illinois spring storms can leave low areas soggy for days, so walk the lawn after rain.

## Utilities and septic

Acreage often means buried lines. Before stakes go in, call JULIE, the Illinois utility locating service, and mark private lines such as sprinklers and invisible fences yourself, since the public locator may not mark private lines. If your property has a septic system, ask the owner or a pro where the tank and field are, and avoid driving heavy equipment or parking over them. Check for a well head and avoid blocking it. These cautions protect your property and keep the tent crew safe.

## Power and light on a large property

A DJ, caterer and lights can each need dedicated power, and an outlet near the house may be far from the tent. Use outdoor-rated cords, keep connections off wet grass and bury or cover cords across walkways. A generator may be needed for a large setup; ask a professional how to size and place it safely, away from guests and tent walls. Plan light for the walk from the parking area to the tent, since country properties are dark at night. See our [string light layouts](/blog/string-light-layouts-for-events).

## Parking and guests

Large lots can handle many cars, but only if the ground is firm. Mark parking with signs and a volunteer, and keep a path for emergency vehicles. If the lawn is soft, ask whether a nearby lot or a shuttle is allowed. Tell neighbors in advance. Some country roads have narrow shoulders, so plan carefully.

## Frequently asked questions

### How big a tent for 100 guests?

About 40 x 50 feet for a seated dinner, more with a dance floor or buffet inside.

### Do I need to call JULIE for a tent?

Yes, before stakes go in. Your tent installer should confirm. Mark private lines yourself.

### Can a delivery truck reach the back of my property?

Often not on soft lawns. Share a photo of your driveway and lawn so we can plan a drop point.

### Do I need a tent permit in Homer Glen?

Possibly. Ask the Village of Homer Glen about permit and inspection rules.

### How do I handle a wet lawn?

Use flooring, mats or a hard surface, and walk the area after rain to find soft spots.

### What chair is best for the lawn?

A wide-footed wood chair or a folding chair with foot pads.
