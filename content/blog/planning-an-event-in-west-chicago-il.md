West Chicago is a DuPage County city with a railroad heritage, a growing downtown and a big, diverse community that celebrates in a lot of ways: quinceañeras, weddings, church events and festivals. It is also one of the closest DuPage towns to our St. Charles base.

## The park district and the city

The West Chicago Park District runs the city's parks, including Reed-Keppler Park, a large park with fields and facilities. The City of West Chicago handles streets, sidewalks and public property, and publishes information on its website. If you plan a downtown event, the city is the office to call. If you want a shelter or field, call the district.

The city is not the same as Chicago, and the name causes confusion. West Chicago is about 30 miles west of the Loop, in DuPage County.

## Space types in West Chicago

- **Reed-Keppler Park and other district parks.** Shelters, fields and open space through the park district.
- **Downtown West Chicago.** Streets and plazas under city rules.
- **Banquet halls and event centers.** Many serve large family celebrations. Ask each what is included.
- **Churches and community halls.** Common for quinceañeras, baptisms and showers.
- **Backyards.** Often used for cookouts and family parties.

## Large family celebrations

West Chicago hosts many events that run big: 150 to 250 guests, with long meals, music and dancing. Plan the room around the dance floor and the buffet line, then place tables around them. A 200-guest event typically needs about 25 rounds of 8, a head table or a court table, and a clear path to restrooms and exits. See [dance floor size by guest count](/blog/dance-floor-size-by-guest-count) and [buffet line flow](/blog/buffet-line-flow-and-serving-speed).

## Seasonal notes

Summer is the busy season for parks, with many events on weekends. Spring graduations and church events fill May and June. Fall is calm. Winter events happen in halls, and the days are short, with sunset about 4:20 pm in mid-December.

## A West Chicago example

For a 200-guest celebration in a hall, plan 25 rounds of 8, a dance floor and a head table. [Royal throne chairs](/chairs/royal-throne) (about $75-150 per pair, an estimate) add a focal point, while [ghost chairs](/chairs/ghost) (about $10-14 each) keep the room light.

## Coordinating with vendors

Large celebrations involve a DJ or band, a caterer, a baker and a photographer. Share your floor plan with each, because they need power, space and timing. Ask the hall which vendors are allowed and when each can arrive. A one-page schedule with load-in times prevents conflicts. If you are traveling from Chicago for the event, confirm the hall's address and parking before the day.

## What a good floor plan looks like

Large celebrations need a floor plan before anything else. Draw the room, then place the big pieces in this order.

1. **Dance floor** near the DJ or band, with space around it.
2. **Head table or court table** where guests can see it from most seats.
3. **Buffet or serving stations** away from the dance floor, with room for two lines.
4. **Guest tables** filling the rest, with at least 5 feet between chair backs.
5. **Gift, cake and guest book tables** near the entrance.

Leave clear aisles to exits and restrooms, because crowds form there. If the hall provides a floor plan, mark it up and send it to your rental team. A good plan saves hours on setup day. Whether you plan in West Chicago or somewhere else in the Chicago area, the same order works.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is West Chicago the same as Chicago?

No. West Chicago is a separate DuPage County city about 30 miles west of the Loop.

### Who runs parks in West Chicago?

The West Chicago Park District, including Reed-Keppler Park.

### Do I need a city permit for a downtown West Chicago event?

Ask the City of West Chicago. Streets and public property are city matters.

### How many tables do I need for 200 guests?

About 25 rounds of 8, plus tables for food, gifts and the DJ.

### Do you deliver in West Chicago?

Yes. First deliveries begin with the April 2027 Founding Season, and it is one of our closest DuPage towns.

### What chairs suit a quinceañera?

Ghost, Louis or velvet chairs for guests, and a royal throne chair for the honoree.
