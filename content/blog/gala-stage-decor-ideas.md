**Quick answer**

Build a gala stage in layers: a skirted deck, a backdrop such as drape, a flower wall or LED screen, an off-center podium, uplights and a sign readable from the back row. Keep it simpler than the room, and check the venue's rules for rigging, fire and install labor.

A gala stage is the center of the room for an hour or more: speakers, awards, an auction and sometimes a band. At a nonprofit gala in a Chicago ballroom, the stage also frames the video, the logos and the photos that will be shared afterward. Decorating a stage is less about piling on flowers and more about choosing a few layers that look good from the back row and on camera.

## The layers of a gala stage

1. **Skirted stage deck.** A skirt hides the legs and gives a clean base. See [stage and riser sizes](/blog/stage-and-riser-sizes-for-events).
2. **Backdrop.** Pipe and drape, a fabric wall, a flower wall or an LED screen. Pick a backdrop that reads well on video.
3. **Podium or lectern.** Place it off-center if a screen or sign is centered.
4. **Lighting.** Uplights, wash lights and a spotlight for speakers.
5. **Florals and greenery.** Stage-edge arrangements or tall stands at the sides.
6. **Sign or logo.** Large enough for the back row.

| Backdrop | Mood | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Black drape | Formal, dramatic | Hides cables, works with lights |
| White or ivory drape | Soft, elegant | Shows color from uplights |
| Flower wall | Warm, romantic | Needs frame, adds weight |
| LED screen | Modern, branded | Needs operator and power |
| Brand banner | Corporate | Keep text large |

## Plan for the back row

The back row sets your design rules. Anything smaller than a foot tall will vanish. Logos and text should be readable from about 40 feet away, so make them at least a foot tall for every 10 to 12 feet of distance, a common signage rule of thumb. Lighting should favor the speakers' faces. Dark clothes on a dark backdrop disappear, so match contrast to the likely outfits.

## Auction and awards logistics

For auctions, leave space for an auctioneer, bid spotters and a screen. For awards, leave a path for honorees, steps with a handrail and a table for trophies. Add a stage manager's table at the side with power and lights. Put the sound console in the back of the room, not behind a pillar.

## Safety and venue rules

Stages need handrails above a set height, firm bases and cable covers. Large hotels and convention centers in Chicago may require union labor or approved vendors for stage installs, so ask the venue about their policy before ordering. Confirm ceiling height for tall backdrops and lights, and fire rules for fabric.

## Decor tips

Keep the stage simpler than the room. A single color of fabric with a big sign or screen is more elegant than a busy mix. Add height with tall floral stands at both ends, not the center, so they do not block the screen. Use a low arrangement on the podium front. For ideas on drape and signs, see [pipe and drape](/blog/pipe-and-drape-uses-and-sizes) and [marquee and neon signs](/blog/marquee-letters-and-neon-signs-rental).

## Seating the head table

Many galas put a head table in front of the stage. A [royal throne](/chairs/royal-throne) pair, an [infinity chair](/chairs/infinity) or a [velvet chair](/chairs/velvet-dining) gives the table presence.

## Mistakes to avoid

- A stage too small for the program.
- Text that cannot be read from the back.
- Ignoring camera angles for video.
- Placing the sound console behind a pillar.


## Frequently asked questions

### How do you decorate a stage for a gala?

Use a skirted deck, a backdrop, a podium, lighting and a large readable sign. Add florals at the sides, not the center.

### What backdrop looks best for a gala stage?

Black drape for drama, white for softness, an LED screen for modern events. Choose by lighting and camera.

### How big should text be on a stage backdrop?

At least 12 inches tall, and larger for big rooms, so it reads from the back. Test from the farthest seat.

### Where should the podium go?

Off-center if a screen or sign is centered, with a spotlight and a short path from the steps.

### Do galas need a ramp on the stage?

If anyone using a wheelchair or with limited mobility will use the stage, yes. See our stage guide for ramp slopes.

### Do Chicago venues limit stage rigging?

Many hotels and convention centers have rules about rigging, labor and approved vendors. Ask your venue early.
