## December weather: what to watch out for

December is winter. NWS normals for O'Hare give December an average high of 36.6°F and a low of 24.4°F, with snow likely during the month. Sunset is around 4:20 pm, the earliest of the year.

What to plan for:

- **Snow and deliveries.** Book early load-in windows, confirm who clears and salts the path, and keep a backup window.
- **Coats.** Every guest arrives in a heavy coat. Racks and hangers for everyone, plus a mat at the door. See [coat racks for winter events](/blog/coat-racks-for-winter-events-chicago).
- **Darkness by 4:30 pm.** Even a lunch party ends after dark. Plan warm lighting.
- **Parking and travel.** Holiday shopping traffic downtown is heavy on weekends. Read [guest parking and transit plans for Chicago](/blog/guest-parking-and-transit-plan-chicago).

## Venue demand and booking lead times in December

December Saturdays are the first dates to go. Company party planners book venues months ahead, often by late summer or early fall, and rentals for the first three weekends of December follow. Our [winter holiday party season booking guide](/blog/chicago-winter-holiday-party-season-booking) has the timing in detail.

If you are flexible, Thursdays and Sundays are easier, as is the first week of January for a "new year" party. Rental companies often pause deliveries around Christmas and New Year's Day, so confirm your delivery and pickup dates when you book.

## Frequently asked questions

### When should I book a December holiday party venue in Chicago?

As early as you can, ideally by late summer or early fall for a Saturday in the first three weeks of December. Thursdays, Sundays and early January are easier to get.

### When is Hanukkah in 2027?

Hanukkah begins the evening of Friday, December 24, 2027, the same evening as Christmas Eve. Check your guest list before scheduling events that week.

### What is the weather like in Chicago in December?

Cold, with snow likely. NWS normals for O'Hare give an average high of 36.6°F and a low of 24.4°F, and sunset is around 4:20 pm.

### Do rental companies deliver between Christmas and New Year's?

Many do, with reduced schedules, and most pause on the holidays themselves. Confirm delivery and pickup dates in writing when you book.

### What should I rent for a company holiday party?

Usually cocktail tables, lounge seating, linens, glassware and lighting, plus coat racks. A seated dinner adds round tables, chairs and a full place setting.

### Is New Year's Eve more expensive for venues?

Usually. Many venues price New Year's Eve as a special night with its own minimums. Ask early, because the best spaces book well ahead.
