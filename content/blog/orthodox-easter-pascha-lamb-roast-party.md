**Quick answer**

Orthodox Easter (Pascha) falls on Sunday, May 2, 2027. Many families attend a service late on Saturday night, break the fast after midnight, and gather for a big meal on Sunday with red eggs, bread and roast lamb. Plan long tables for the whole family, a tent for early May weather, and a safe spot for the lamb. Check your village's rules before you roast anything outdoors.

</div>

Orthodox Easter follows a different calendar from the Easter most Chicagoans celebrate in March or April. In 2027, Western Easter is March 28 and Orthodox Easter is May 2. Greek, Serbian, Ukrainian, Romanian and other Orthodox families across Chicago mark it as the biggest day of the year, after a long Lent. Customs differ by country and family, so treat this guide as a starting point.

**The midnight service.** Many parishes hold a Resurrection service late on Saturday night. People carry candles and say a greeting: "Christ is risen" ("Christos Anesti" in Greek, "Hristos a înviat" in Romanian, "Khrystos Voskres" in Ukrainian and "Hristos Vaskrse" in Serbian), with the reply "Truly He is risen." Some families go home to break the fast after midnight, often with a soup or a simple meal, and others wait for Sunday.

**Red eggs.** Dyed red eggs are a symbol of new life. People crack them against each other and the one that stays whole is lucky. This game is known as tsougrisma in Greek and has a version in many Orthodox homes. Plan a bowl of eggs, a few napkins and a spot for the shells.

## The lamb on the spit

Roast lamb is the traditional Pascha meal for many Greek and Balkan families. A whole lamb is cooked on a spit over charcoal for several hours, and some families do this outdoors in a backyard or a park.

- **Check local rules first.** Open fire, large grills and spits are restricted in some villages and parks. Check with your village, park district or building before you plan it.
- **Safety.** Place the spit on level ground away from fences, trees and eaves. Keep children and pets back, and have a fire extinguisher on hand.
- **Food safety.** Cook to a safe internal temperature and keep the meat hot after carving. The USDA lists 145°F for lamb with a rest time, and ground lamb is higher. See [keeping food hot outdoors](/blog/keeping-food-hot-outdoors).
- **Rentals.** Some companies rent grills and spits, and many caterers bring their own. See our [grill rental guide](/blog/grill-rental-for-events). Ask what is included, such as fuel and the cooking stand.
- **Smoke.** Tell your neighbors ahead of time, especially in a Chicago two-flat or a tight lot.

## Seating a big family table

Pascha meals are long and loud, with grandparents, cousins and neighbors. Long tables work best.

<div class="table-wrap">

| Guests | Setup | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 12 to 20 | One long table of 8-foot tables end to end | Everyone at one table |
| 20 to 40 | Two long tables or 4 rounds plus a buffet | Put elders near the food |
| 40 and up | Rounds of 8 to 10, a lamb carving table and a dessert table | A tent or hall helps |

</div>

For long table layouts, see [long tables in a narrow room](/blog/long-table-layouts-in-a-narrow-room), and for guest counts [how many tables you need](/blog/how-many-tables-for-50-100-150-200-guests). Cover the tables in white or ivory with red accents for the eggs. Leave room for candles and the traditional bread: Greek tsoureki, Ukrainian paska, Romanian pasca and Serbian pogaca or kolach, depending on the family.

## What most hosts find out too late

- **The date differs from Easter in March.** Orthodox Easter is May 2, 2027. Rental companies may not have it on their calendar. Check the date early.
- **Book the tent and tables early.** Early May is busy with graduations, Mother's Day and Cinco de Mayo. Ask for a delivery window that fits the service.
- **Plan for the late night.** If the family goes to the midnight service, set the table in advance so that no one is rushing at 1 am.
- **Count everything at delivery.** Check chairs and tables against the quote.

## Frequently asked questions

### When is Orthodox Easter in 2027?

Sunday, May 2, 2027. It follows the Orthodox calendar, so it often falls on a different date than Easter in the West.

### What is the midnight service?

It is a Resurrection service on Saturday night, with candles and a greeting of "Christ is risen." Your church sets the exact time.

### Why are Easter eggs red?

Red eggs symbolize new life and, in many Orthodox traditions, the blood of Christ. People crack them against each other in a game. Families differ on the details.

### Can I roast a lamb in my backyard in Chicago?

Possibly, but check the rules first. Open fire and large cookers are restricted in some villages and parks. Check with your village or park district.

### How much lamb do I need per guest?

A common rule of thumb for bone-in lamb is about 1 pound per person, and more if it is the main dish. Ask your butcher or caterer to size it.

### Do I need a tent in early May?

It is a good idea. Early May can bring rain, wind and cool evenings. A tent keeps tables dry and gives a place to hold the meal.
