## Seating style: pick one

For a modern firm, [ghost chairs](/chairs/ghost) and white linens look clean. For a professional services firm, [velvet chairs](/chairs/velvet-dining) in navy or emerald read as upscale. For a creative agency or a casual brand, [rattan](/chairs/rattan-garden) or [bentwood](/chairs/bentwood) chairs add warmth. Think of the chair as a stage for the experience, then pick a lounge corner as the signature, as in [lounge furniture for cocktail hour](/blog/lounge-furniture-for-cocktail-hour).

## Food format

Stations beat buffets for networking because guests stand and talk in smaller groups. Plated dinners lock people to a table for two hours and make it hard for your team to circulate. A reception with three small stations and a dessert table is a good balance. Ask your caterer what service equipment they bring.

## Budget where clients feel it

Clients feel the greeting, the first drink and the seat. They do not feel the centerpieces. Put your rental budget into seating comfort, a good lounge area and linens for the food and bar tables. Skip decor you cannot explain. If you have to cut, cut the number of chair styles, not the number of seats. Ask your rental company for a quote with two chair options so you can compare the cost of a premium style against a standard one.

## Using the event to start conversations

Put a table or two near the lounge with a small guestbook or a short note card. Name tents and a simple seating chart for a dinner help clients meet the right people. At a stand-up event, give hosts a list of three clients to find and introduce. After the event, send a brief personal note with a photo or a next-step offer. The rental list supports the event, but the follow-up is what turns appreciation into the next project.

## Seasonal notes for Chicago

Summer is the best season for a client evening, with a terrace or courtyard, patio heaters in shoulder seasons, and fans in July. December is crowded with holiday events, so consider January or February for a client night when calendars are open. Weather in spring changes quickly, so keep a backup indoor area. For more outdoor planning, see [outdoor lounge rentals in Chicago](/blog/outdoor-lounge-rentals-chicago).

## Frequently asked questions

### What should I rent for a client appreciation event?

Chairs for about 60% of guests, cocktail tables, a lounge area, a bar, linens and glassware. Add a backdrop or signage if you want branding.

### How many guests should a client event hold?

It depends on your client list. Under 150 stays personal; over 200 usually needs a larger venue and a more structured program.

### Is a seated dinner better than a cocktail reception?

A seated dinner suits a smaller group and a formal tone. A cocktail reception suits mingling and introductions.

### What time should a client event start?

Weeknights from 5:30 to 8:00 pm are typical. Ask your own clients which night works, since schedules differ by industry.

### How much should I spend per guest?

We don't publish catering prices. Set the budget by the total and ask for line-item quotes for rentals and food.

### Can I host a client event at my office?

Yes, if the building allows it. Check your lease, elevator and insurance requirements.
