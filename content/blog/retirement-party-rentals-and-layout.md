**Quick answer**

A retirement party mixes coworkers and family, so seat people at rounds of 8 to 10, put a head table or honoree table where the whole room can see it, and leave a clear spot for a podium and microphone. For 60 guests, rent about 60 chairs, 6 to 8 round tables, a buffet or bar, a gift and card table, a memory display and a microphone. Keep speeches under a total of 20 minutes so the party stays light.

</div>

Retirement parties have an unusual audience. Part of the room is people who worked with the guest of honor for decades, part is family who see a different side of them, and everybody wants to hear a story or two. That means the setup has to do two things at once: feed and seat a mixed crowd comfortably, and give a few people a way to speak to everyone.

## Seating plan: mix, don't silo

Put family at tables close to the front, and let coworkers sit by department, or by who they'd want to sit with. Assigned seating is optional for under 40 guests but helps at 60 or more. If you're using a venue with its own tables, ask for a layout early.

- **Honoree table:** the retiree, spouse, kids and a close friend or two.
- **Family tables:** nearest the front.
- **Work tables:** grouped by team, with the manager or boss at the front.
- **Flex table:** for guests who arrive alone or with unexpected plus-ones.

## What to rent

### Chairs and tables
A comfortable chair for a two-to-three hour event is key. [Cross-back chairs](/chairs/cross-back), estimated at $8 to $11, give a polished look. For the honoree, use a [velvet dining chair](/chairs/velvet-dining) or a [Louis medallion chair](/chairs/louis-medallion). Tables are usually 60-inch rounds seating 8 or 72-inch rounds seating 10. See the [round table size guide](/blog/round-table-sizes-and-seating-48-60-66-72-inch).

### Linens and decor
Floor-length linens in a calm color, with a runner or a small centerpiece. The [tablecloth size chart](/blog/tablecloth-size-chart) shows which cloth fits which table. Use the retiree's favorite color or the company's color for napkins.

### Memory display and tributes
Plan a table or easels for photos, a poster, a timeline or a book to sign. One 6-foot table with a floor-length cloth is usually enough. Place it away from the buffet so people can linger.

### Sound
A podium or lectern, a microphone and a speaker. Test it at the room's quietest and loudest moments. Provide a clear line of sight from every seat to the podium.

## Chicago-area notes

Retirement parties often happen in restaurants, banquet halls, office spaces and backyards. If it's at a workplace, ask your facilities team about rules for outside vendors, load-in times and building access. Downtown buildings often require a certificate of insurance or a freight elevator reservation for deliveries, so ask early. In winter, give guests a coat rack or a closet. Also tell guests where to park: suburban offices have lots, and city locations rarely do. For the corporate side, see [our corporate and nonprofit guides](/blog/topic/corporate-and-nonprofit).

## Frequently asked questions

### How many chairs do I need for a retirement party?

One per guest for a seated dinner. For a cocktail-style party with a short program, rent seats for about two thirds of guests.

### Where should the retiree sit?

At a table near the front with their spouse and closest family, facing the room. A slightly different chair marks the seat without a head table.

### How long should the speeches be?

Three to five minutes each, with three or four speakers. Total tributes should stay under 20 to 25 minutes.

### Do I need a microphone?

For more than 30 guests, yes. Voices carry poorly across a room with clinking dishes, and a mic keeps the toasts from being repeated.

### What should be on the memory table?

Photos from different eras, a card box, a guest book and a few objects from the career. Keep it simple, and put it by the entrance.

### How early should I book rentals?

Four to six weeks ahead for most weekends. Retirement dates often depend on work calendars, so ask us about availability once you have a date.
