**Quick answer**

Plan roughly 10 to 12 square feet per guest for a seated dinner under a tent and 6 to 8 for standing cocktails, plus space for a bar, buffet and walkways. A 20 by 40 foot tent (800 square feet) holds about 55 guests at rounds with a bar and room to move. Add 3 to 5 feet around the tent, so you need about 1,200 or more square feet of flat, usable yard. Measure the usable space, not the lot.

</div>

Chicago backyards come in two sizes: tiny and surprisingly large. A bungalow lot in the suburbs can host a 100-person tent, while a two-flat in the city may have a 20 by 40 foot patch of grass. Before you pick a tent or count chairs, measure the space guests can actually use.

## Start with the rules of thumb

Event planners commonly use a few square-foot rules. Treat them as rules of thumb, not guarantees.

<div class="table-wrap">

| Layout | Square feet per guest | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Seated dinner, round tables | 10 to 12 | Includes aisles |
| Seated with dance floor | 14 to 16 | Add floor area |
| Cocktail reception | 6 to 8 | Standing, highboys |
| Ceremony rows | 4 to 6 | Chairs only |
| Buffet or bar | Add 100 to 200 total | Depends on layout |

</div>

These ranges are widely used in the event industry. Your venue, village or tent company may use slightly different numbers, so confirm with them.

Mark the usable area with stakes or flour: subtract the house, steps, trees, garden beds, fences and setbacks. Leave at least 3 to 5 feet around a tent for stakes and walking, and more for a caterer. Tents need stakes or weights, and the ground may hide utilities. See [JULIE utility locate for tents in Illinois](/blog/julie-utility-locate-tent-illinois) before staking.

A 600-square-foot tent is a small seated dinner or a cocktail party. At 800 square feet, a seated dinner for 55 is realistic if the layout is simple. The figures count tent area only; add yard space for clearance, restrooms and kitchen support.

## What to rent for a small yard

- **Tables**: 48-inch rounds seat 6 and use less floor than 60-inch rounds. See [round table sizes](/blog/round-table-sizes-and-seating-48-60-66-72-inch).
- **Chairs**: stackable, narrow chairs. The [garden folding chair](/chairs/garden-folding) at about $5 to $7 and the [cross-back](/chairs/cross-back) at $8 to $11 both fit.
- **Tent**: a size that matches the layout. Read [backyard tent for 50 guests](/blog/backyard-tent-for-50-guests).
- **Restrooms**: a house bathroom may not handle 50 guests; see [how many portable restrooms per guests](/blog/how-many-portable-restrooms-per-guests).
- **Bar and buffet**: put them outside the seating area if possible.

### When the yard is too small

If the numbers do not fit, there are three choices: shrink the guest list, shift to cocktail style, or use a second space such as the front yard, driveway, garage or a neighbor's yard with permission. See [tent on driveway or parking lot](/blog/tent-on-driveway-or-parking-lot). Some hosts split the event: dinner in the tent for 30 and a standing reception for the rest.

## Village rules and neighbors

Check whether your village requires a permit for tents, noise or large gatherings. Chicago has its own tent permit rules; see our [Chicago tent permit guide](/blog/chicago-tent-permit). Tell your neighbors in advance, plan parking and set a quiet time. HOA rules may apply; see [HOA and neighbors tent party](/blog/hoa-and-neighbors-tent-party).

## Frequently asked questions

### How many people can fit in a backyard?

Roughly 10 to 12 square feet per seated guest at rounds, and 6 to 8 for standing. A 20 by 40 foot tent (800 square feet) holds about 55.

### How big a tent do I need for 50 guests?

A rule of thumb is a 20 by 40 or 30 by 40 foot tent for 50 at rounds, depending on dance floor and bar. See our tent size guide.

### How do I measure my backyard for a party?

Measure the usable lawn, subtracting the house, steps, garden beds, trees and fence setbacks, then add clearance for stakes.

### Can I host 100 people in a Chicago backyard?

Only if you can fit a tent of roughly 1,400 square feet or more, plus clearance. Most city lots are not, so consider a larger tent footprint or a different venue.

### Do I need a permit for a backyard party in Chicago?

Possibly for a tent or amplified sound. Check the City of Chicago and your village. This guide cannot confirm your address.

### Where do the caterers set up?

Plan a prep area outside the seating, with power and cover. A garage or side yard often works.

### What if my yard is on a slope?

Ask your tent vendor about leveling and flooring. Sloped sites often need staging or a different location.
