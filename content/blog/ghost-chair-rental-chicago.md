## What you are renting

Our [ghost chair](/chairs/ghost) is a Louis-style acrylic chair with a molded back. It is available in clear and smoke. It stacks, it is outdoor-friendly in the catalog, and it has no cushion. If guests need one, ask us whether a flat pad is possible for your order.

Acrylic has a few traits you should know about before you order:

- **It shows fingerprints and dust.** Every chair needs a quick wipe before guests sit.
- **It scratches more easily than wood or metal.** A small scuff on a clear chair is visible in photos, which is why rental companies set aside a few spares.
- **It reflects light.** Under a sunny tent, clear chairs sparkle; under a flash, they can glare in photos.
- **It is slick.** Long dresses and satin slip a little. Most guests do not notice.

## Ghost chairs in the sun and the cold

Acrylic warms up fast in direct sun, and a chair left in July sun on a Chicago patio can be uncomfortable to sit on for a few minutes. Cold is gentler, but acrylic becomes stiffer when it is cold, so rough handling matters more in winter.

## Grass, gravel and decks

Ghost chairs have slim legs, and slim legs sink. On a wet lawn, the back legs press into the turf and the chair tilts. Fix it with one of these:

1. Choose a firm, flat area for the seating block.
2. Lay down ground protection, rental flooring or plywood under the aisle and chair rows.
3. Use a different style for the part of the event that sits on soft ground. Our guide to [chairs on grass, sand and gravel](/blog/chairs-on-grass-sand-gravel) explains why.

Hard surfaces such as ballroom floors, patios and tent flooring are ideal.

## How many extras to order

Order your guest count plus the head table, plus **one or two spares per 50 chairs**. That is a common planner's rule of thumb rather than a hard standard. The spares cover guests who spill on a chair, a chair with a visible scuff and last-minute RSVPs.

## Cleaning and what counts as damage

Ghost chairs come back with fingerprints, frosting and the occasional lipstick. Normal dirt is part of renting. What costs money is a crack, a deep scratch or a missing part. Ask any rental company, including us, for its damage policy before you sign, and look at the chairs when they arrive. Our guide to [how to spot a quality rental chair](/blog/how-to-spot-quality-rental-chair) covers what to check.

Do not use abrasive cleaners or paper towels on acrylic. A soft cloth and a damp wipe is all it needs.

## Delivery and timing in the Chicago area

Delivery windows depend on your venue's rules, not just on us. Downtown Chicago buildings often have specific freight elevator and loading dock schedules, and some require insurance paperwork before a vendor can enter. Suburban venues are usually simpler but can have their own load-in times. Ask your venue early, and ask for their load-in rules in writing.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does ghost chair rental cost in Chicago?

Our estimate is $10 to $14 per chair. The final price depends on quantity, delivery location, stairs or elevators and the setup you need.

### Do ghost chairs scratch easily?

Acrylic scratches more easily than wood or metal. Normal use is fine, but dragging chairs or stacking them without care leaves marks, which is why we recommend ordering a couple of spares.

### Can ghost chairs go outside?

Yes, on firm ground. Avoid soft grass, gravel and standing water, and shade them from direct sun until you need them.

### Are ghost chairs comfortable?

They are firm. They suit a three-hour reception if guests are moving around, but for a long sit-down dinner you may want flat pads.

### How many guests can sit on a ghost chair?

Each chair seats one adult. Ask your rental company for the rated capacity if you have guests who need extra support, and see our [chair weight capacity guide](/blog/chair-weight-capacity-and-seat-sizes).

### Are ghost chairs good for a ceremony?

Yes, because they keep the view clear for florals and the aisle. The risk is soft ground, so use flooring or choose a firm area.

### What is the difference between clear and smoke ghost chairs?

Clear is nearly invisible and suits florals and mirror tables. Smoke is a gray tint that shows up a little more and suits modern or moody palettes.
