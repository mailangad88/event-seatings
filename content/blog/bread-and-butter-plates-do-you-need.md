**Quick answer**

You need bread and butter plates only if bread and butter are served to the table. A 6-inch plate sits above the forks at the upper left, with a small butter knife resting across it. Skip it for buffets, casual dinners, cocktail receptions and any menu without a bread basket. Order one per seat for a formal plated dinner with bread, plus a small cushion.

</div>

The bread plate is the smallest piece of a place setting, and it takes up valuable table real estate. At a 60-inch round in a Chicago banquet hall, that 6-inch circle competes with glasses, the napkin and the centerpiece. This guide covers when it is useful, where it goes, and how to decide.

## What a bread plate is for

The bread plate holds a roll and butter so crumbs and spreads stay off the dinner plate. It sits at the upper left of the place setting, above the forks. A small butter knife lies across the plate with the blade facing the center. A guest breaks off a piece of bread, butters it on the plate, and eats it with their hands.

## When to include it

<div class="table-wrap">

| Situation | Bread plate? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Formal plated dinner with a bread basket | Yes | Keeps crumbs off the dinner plate |
| Buffet dinner | No | Bread goes on the dinner plate |
| Cocktail reception | No | Appetizer plates cover it |
| Family-style dinner | Optional | Space is tight with platters |
| Brunch with pastries | Optional | Use the small plate |
| Casual backyard party | No | Skip it |

</div>

## When to skip it

Skip bread plates if bread is not served, if the table is crowded, or if the budget is tight. Each bread plate is another piece to rent, count, carry and wash. At a table with 10 guests and a centerpiece, removing the bread plate frees up room for a wine glass or a menu card. See [place setting spacing](/blog/place-setting-spacing-measurements) for the inches involved.

## The BMW rule

Etiquette teachers use a memory trick, "BMW" for bread, meal, water, to find where things sit from left to right. Bread plate is to the left of the meal plate, and the water glass is to the right. Our [multi-course etiquette guide](/blog/multi-course-place-setting-etiquette) goes through the order of courses, and the [casual place setting diagram](/blog/casual-place-setting-diagram) has a simpler layout.

## Count and size

Order one bread plate per seat, plus a 5 to 10% cushion as a rule of thumb. Bread plates are small enough to be lost in a rental crate, so ask how they are packed. Standard bread plates are 6 inches. Some companies stock 6.5 or 7 inches; confirm the size before you finalize the table layout.

## Alternatives that save space

- Serve bread on the dinner plate in a corner.
- Use a shared bread board in the middle of the table.
- Serve a roll with the salad on the salad plate.
- Use a tiny bread cone or a napkin-wrapped roll on the charger.

Each saves space and money. A shared board also cuts down on plate count for 8 or 10 guests.

## Chicago note

Many Chicago caterers, especially at hotel ballrooms and banquet halls, serve bread and butter to the table for plated dinners and expect a bread plate in the setting. At loft and restaurant buyouts, they may skip it. Ask your caterer what they do by default, then adjust.

## Butter details

Butter can go on the bread plate in pats, in a ramekin, or in a small dish shared by two guests. Shared dishes mean fewer pieces to wash. Whipped butter or olive oil dips are served in small bowls, which counts as additional pieces. List them on the order sheet so nothing is missed. ## Frequently asked questions

### Which side does the bread plate go on?

The bread plate goes on the left, above the forks. The water glass is on the right above the knife. A butter knife rests on the plate.

### Do I need a bread plate at a wedding?

Only if bread is served to the table at a formal plated dinner. Buffets and cocktail receptions usually skip it. Ask your caterer what the menu includes.

### What size is a bread plate?

About 6 inches across. It is the smallest plate in the usual set. Some patterns run a bit larger.

### Can the dinner plate hold bread?

Yes. Casual dinners put bread on the dinner plate, which is why you can skip the extra plate. It simplifies setup.

### Where does the butter knife go?

The butter knife rests across the top of the bread plate, blade toward the center, handle at the right. Some formal settings place it diagonal.

### How many bread plates should I order?

One per seat, plus a small cushion of about 5 to 10%. Count the head table and the kids table if they will use bread.
