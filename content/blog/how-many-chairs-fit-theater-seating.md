**Quick answer**

Plan about 6 to 8 square feet per person for theater seating, or roughly 10 to 15 chairs per 100 square feet after aisles. A 20 x 30 foot room (600 sq ft) holds about 75 to 100 chairs, and a 40 x 60 foot ballroom section (2,400 sq ft) holds about 300 to 400. Rows sit about 32 to 36 inches apart, and aisles should be at least 3 feet wide.

</div>

Theater seating, also called auditorium style, is the densest way to seat a room: rows of chairs facing a stage or screen, no tables. It is the layout for keynotes, annual meetings, church announcements, school assemblies and product reveals. The question every Chicago-area planner asks is simply how many chairs will actually fit, because the capacity chart in the venue brochure assumes a perfect rectangle. This post gives the formula, a size chart and the details that reduce your real count.

## The formula

**Chairs = (usable floor area / 7) x 0.85** is a good first pass. Seven square feet is the middle of the 6 to 8 range, and the 0.85 trims for aisles. Usable area is the room minus the stage, the sound table, columns and walkways along the walls.

## Row spacing and aisles

- **Row depth:** about 32 to 36 inches from the back of one chair to the back of the next. Under 30 inches and guests cannot pass without everyone standing.
- **Chairs per row:** keep rows to 14 to 16 chairs between aisles. Longer rows make latecomers climb over people.
- **Center aisle:** 4 feet is comfortable; 3 feet is the minimum most planners use. Side aisles along walls are usually 3 feet.
- **Front gap:** leave 6 to 8 feet between the stage and the first row so the stage is not looming over guests.
- **Back gap:** 4 feet behind the last row for standing latecomers, the A/V table and staff.

Count the room, then reduce for each of these: a raised stage, a sound table, a camera riser, a wheelchair space (plan accessible spaces in each section, not just at the back), and a reserved front row for speakers. For ADA seating requirements ask your venue or village building department.

## Chair choice for theater seating

Theater chairs are in use for an hour or two, so comfort matters less than size and speed. [Garden folding chairs](/chairs/garden-folding) are the practical choice for big rooms: they stack and set fast. For executive audiences, [cross-back chairs](/chairs/cross-back) or [ghost chairs](/chairs/ghost) look better but are bulkier to store. Ganging chairs (linking them in rows) stops aisles drifting and is worth asking about.

## Chicago examples

A suburban hotel ballroom divided by an airwall often lists theater capacity for the whole room, but the divided section is smaller. A church fellowship hall or a village community room may list a fire-code occupancy lower than the chair math suggests, and the lower number wins. Confirm the posted occupancy before you order. For [conference styles](/blog/conference-seating-styles-theater-classroom-banquet-u-shape) beyond theater, see that guide.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many chairs fit in a 20 x 30 foot room in theater style?

About 70 to 85 with a center aisle. At 600 square feet, the 6 to 8 square feet per person rule gives 75 to 100 before aisles.

### How much space per person for theater seating?

Plan 6 to 8 square feet per person. Use 8 for comfortable seating or when guests carry coats and bags.

### How far apart should rows be in theater seating?

About 32 to 36 inches back to back. Closer rows make it hard to pass without standing.

### How wide should aisles be?

At least 3 feet, with 4 feet in the center. Local fire code may require more, so ask your village or the venue.

### Should I use a center aisle?

Yes for crowds over about 50. It improves access and reduces the number of people stepping over seated guests.

### Is theater seating good for a long event?

It suits sessions up to about 90 minutes. For longer programs, add tables (classroom) or move to rounds.

### How many extra chairs should I order?

About 5% extra, plus standing-room staff chairs. Last-minute attendees at a public event often exceed registration.
