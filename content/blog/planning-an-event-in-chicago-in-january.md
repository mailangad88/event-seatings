## January weather: what to watch out for

January in Chicago is reliably cold, and the averages hide the bad days. NWS normals for O'Hare give an average high of 31.6°F and a low of 18.8°F, and January brings an average of 11.3 inches of snow. Lake wind makes downtown and lakefront sites feel colder than the thermometer says.

What that means for your event:

- **Entrances get wet and salty.** Plan a mat or runner at the door, a place for boots and umbrellas, and a coat rack sized for every guest. Our guide to [coat racks for winter events](/blog/coat-racks-for-winter-events-chicago) has the counts.
- **Deliveries need slack.** A snowstorm can push a delivery window by hours. Book the earliest load-in your venue allows and keep the setup crew's path cleared and salted.
- **Darkness arrives early.** Sunset is about 4:28 pm on January 1 and 4:58 pm by January 28, so an afternoon event is an evening event by the time dinner starts. Plan lighting from the start; see [winter event lighting and Chicago sunsets](/blog/winter-event-lighting-chicago-sunset).
- **Outdoor portions are short.** A fire pit or a 10-minute photo moment is realistic. A ceremony outdoors is not, unless it is under a heated tent with sidewalls.

## Venue demand and booking lead times in January

January is one of the quietest months for event venues in Chicago, which works in your favor. Many venues treat January to March as their slow season, so ask directly whether they have off-peak pricing, lower food and beverage minimums, or Friday and Sunday rates. Do not assume the answer; each venue sets its own.

For rentals, 4 to 6 weeks of lead time is usually enough for a January event, except around Lunar New Year banquets at the very end of the month and into February. Statement items like velvet or [Louis chairs](/chairs/louis-medallion) can still be limited, so reserve those as soon as your venue is set.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is January a good month for an event in Chicago?

Yes, if the event is indoors or in a heated tent. January is a quiet month for many venues, so dates and terms can be easier to get, but you need to plan for cold, snow and sunset before 5 pm.

### What is the weather like in Chicago in January?

Cold. NWS normals for O'Hare give an average high of 31.6°F and a low of 18.8°F, with about 11.3 inches of snow across the month. Wind off the lake makes downtown feel colder.

### How far ahead should I book rentals for a January event?

Four to 6 weeks is usually enough for chairs, tables and linens. Book sooner for specialty chairs, heaters, or anything near Lunar New Year at the start of February.

### Are venues cheaper in January in Chicago?

Often more flexible, but not always cheaper. Many venues treat January as their slow season, so ask about off-peak pricing, lower minimums and Friday or Sunday rates rather than assuming.

### What happens to my delivery if it snows?

Plan for it in advance. Book the earliest load-in your venue allows, confirm who clears and salts the delivery path, and agree on a backup window with your rental team.

### What time is sunset in Chicago in January?

About 4:28 pm on January 1, 4:42 pm on January 15 and 4:58 pm on January 28 in 2027. Plan your lighting as if the whole event runs after dark.
