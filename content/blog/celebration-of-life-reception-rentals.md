**Quick answer**

A celebration of life reception works best with a seat for every guest, round tables of 6 to 8 so people can talk, a memory display near the entrance, a buffet or light food service, and a quiet area for those who need a break. For 80 guests, rent about 80 chairs, 10 rounds of 8, a food table or two, a table for photos and a guest book, and a microphone for a few spoken memories. Order early, but ask your rental company about last-minute flexibility.

</div>

Planning a gathering after a loss is different from planning any other event. You may have days, not weeks, and you may be making decisions for the first time in a week of grief. The rentals should take work off your plate, not add to it. This guide keeps the list short and clear.

## What matters most

People at a memorial reception talk in small groups, sit for long stretches, and need to feel looked after. A few principles guide the layout:

- **Comfort first.** Older relatives and travelers need chairs with firm backs.
- **Conversation space.** Round tables of 6 to 8 let people talk without raising their voices.
- **A place for memories.** Photos, objects and a book to sign give people something to do with their hands and eyes.
- **A quiet corner.** A couple of armchairs or a lounge pocket away from the main room.

### Chairs and tables
Choose chairs for comfort and calm. [Cross-back chairs](/chairs/cross-back), estimated at $8 to $11, are warm and unfussy. [Ghost chairs](/chairs/ghost), about $10 to $14, stay out of the way. For the family table, a few [velvet dining chairs](/chairs/velvet-dining), $18 to $25, add comfort without ceremony. Our [round table size guide](/blog/round-table-sizes-and-seating-48-60-66-72-inch) helps you pick between 60 and 72 inches.

### Linens
Soft, neutral colors suit most families: white, ivory, gray, soft green. Floor-length cloths on every table keep the room tidy. See the [tablecloth size chart](/blog/tablecloth-size-chart).

### Food
Receptions often serve light food: sandwiches, salads, pastries, fruit and a hot dish. Buffet service lets people eat when they are ready. Many families also ask friends to bring dishes, which means serving ware and warmers are useful. See [how many chafing dishes you need](/blog/how-many-chafing-dishes-for-a-buffet) and [coffee urns](/blog/how-many-coffee-urns-for-a-party), since coffee and tea are central.

## Practical help

- **Delegate.** Ask one trusted person to be the contact for the rental company, the venue and the caterer, so you are not fielding calls.
- **Tell the rental company your timeline.** If the date is short notice, say so. Timing depends on availability, and we will tell you plainly what we can do.
- **Confirm access.** Venues such as churches, community halls and funeral homes have their own load-in rules, so ask about them before the truck is on the way.
- **Plan for weather and mobility.** Provide drop-off near the door, a ramp if needed and a coat area in winter.
- **Keep decor simple.** Fresh flowers from the service or family can be moved to tables.

## Chicago-area notes

Many Chicago-area receptions follow a service at a funeral home, a house of worship or a community hall, and others take place in a home or a park. Policies about outside rentals, food, alcohol and amplified sound differ by site, so ask each one directly. In winter, the roads and parking at a house of worship fill quickly, so plan arrival time and a coat area. If you're holding the reception at a park, ask the park district about shelter reservations and tent rules; see [Chicago tent permit rules](/blog/chicago-tent-permit) for the city.

## Frequently asked questions

### What do I need to rent for a celebration of life?

Chairs, round tables, linens and a food table, plus tables for photos and a guest book. A microphone helps for 50 or more.

### How many chairs should I rent?

One for each guest. Memorial gatherings run long, and people sit and talk, so plan seats for everyone.

### How quickly can I book rentals?

It depends on the date and inventory. Tell us your date and venue and we will say what is possible. Many families need delivery within a week.

### Should I have assigned seating?

Usually no. Reserve the front tables for family and let others sit freely.

### What should go on the memory table?

Photos, a favorite object or two, a guest book and a basket for notes. Place it near the entrance and give it room.

### Can the reception be at home?

Yes. Plan chairs, a food table and coats, and tell neighbors about parking. A small tent can help with overflow if the weather allows.
