**Quick answer**

The Sikh wedding ceremony (Anand Karaj) usually takes place at a gurdwara in the presence of the Guru Granth Sahib, with guests seated on the floor and no rental seating needed. Rentals are for the reception, held at a banquet hall or similar venue: rounds of 8 to 10, a head table or stage for the couple, a dance floor and a buffet. Always follow your gurdwara's rules, and ask your granthi or gurdwara committee about the langar hall, the ceremony space and any limits on outside items.

</div>

A Sikh wedding is usually a two-part event: a morning ceremony at the gurdwara, then a reception later that day or the next. The ceremony follows the customs of the Sikh faith and the gurdwara's own rules. The reception is a celebration like many others, with dinner, music and dancing, held in a hall. For rentals, the main rule is to keep the two spaces separate: the gurdwara is a place of worship with its own seating customs, while the reception hall is where rental chairs, tables and linens come in.

## At the gurdwara: no rentals needed for most ceremonies

Guests in a gurdwara typically sit on the floor in the main hall, facing the Guru Granth Sahib, with men and women often seated in separate areas or sections depending on the gurdwara. Heads are covered and shoes are removed. Many gurdwaras provide a few chairs or benches for elders or people who cannot sit on the floor.

If you have questions about the ceremony space:

- **Ask the gurdwara** what is provided, including chairs for elders, floor coverings and any limit on decor.
- **Do not bring outside furniture** into the main hall unless the gurdwara approves it.
- **Ask about the langar hall.** Many gurdwaras offer a communal meal after the ceremony, and some have tables or floor seating in a separate dining room. Ask the committee what is available and what they allow.

This guide does not set rules for the ceremony. Your gurdwara and your family decide those details.

## Reception planning

At the reception, Sikh weddings follow a similar format to many South Asian celebrations: a stage or head table for the couple, family tables, dinner, music and dancing. Guest counts are often large, so plan early.

<div class="table-wrap">

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 150 | 15 | 10 seats | about 20 x 20 ft | gifts, cake, DJ |
| 300 | 30 | 12 to 16 seats | about 28 x 28 ft | add second gift table |
| 500 | 50 | stage with thrones | about 32 x 32 ft | add buffet lines |

For large weddings, check venue capacity carefully. A hall that seats 500 in rows may seat only 300 at rounds with a dance floor. Ask the venue for the maximum at rounds.

## Food, dietary needs and alcohol

Many Sikh weddings serve vegetarian food at the gurdwara langar and sometimes at the reception as well. Families differ on whether alcohol is served at the reception. Ask your family, then discuss it with your caterer and venue. If the reception is vegetarian, tell the caterer so equipment, serving pieces and station layouts match. ## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need to rent chairs for the Anand Karaj?

Usually not. The ceremony is typically held at the gurdwara with guests on the floor. Some gurdwaras provide chairs for elders. Ask the gurdwara what they provide before you rent anything.

### Can I bring chairs into the gurdwara?

Ask the gurdwara committee first. Many have rules about what can enter the main hall. Do not assume outside furniture is allowed.

### What do I rent for the reception?

Chairs, tables, linens and a dance floor for the hall, plus stage seating for the couple. Banquet halls often include basic tables and chairs.

### How many guests will I need to plan for?

Sikh weddings often have large guest lists. Plan from your confirmed count and ask the venue for its capacity at rounds, not just in rows.

### Is alcohol served at the reception?

Families decide. Some serve alcohol and some do not. Discuss it with your family and venue, and follow your gurdwara's and family's wishes.

### What about the langar hall?

Many gurdwaras offer a community meal. Ask the committee whether you can host your guests there and what is provided.
