**Quick answer**

A baby shower is a seated party with a gift-opening moment, so give every guest a chair. Use round tables of 6 to 8, one decorated chair for the mom-to-be, a gift table, a food table and a games or favors table. For 30 guests that is about 30 chairs, 4 tables of 8, 3 display tables and a clear view from every seat to the gift corner.

</div>

Baby showers are small and personal. Most guests are family and close friends, they stay for two to three hours, and most of the time is spent sitting, eating, playing a game and opening gifts. Chairs matter more than at a standing party, and sightlines to the gift area matter more than the centerpiece.

## What to rent

### Chairs
Guests will sit for a while. Choose comfortable ones, with a decent seat and back. [Cross-back chairs](/chairs/cross-back), estimated at $8 to $11 each, suit a rustic brunch. [Ghost chairs](/chairs/ghost), about $10 to $14, keep a pastel color scheme clean. [Rattan garden chairs](/chairs/rattan-garden) look lovely for a garden shower. For the mom-to-be, a [velvet dining chair](/chairs/velvet-dining) or a [Louis medallion chair](/chairs/louis-medallion) with a sash or flower garland becomes the party's focal point. Note that the chair should have a sturdy back and comfortable seat for pregnancy.

### Tables
- Round tables seat 6 to 8. Use 48-inch rounds for 6 and 60-inch rounds for 8.
- One 6-foot table for the gift display, one for food, and a small one for games or favors.
- A cake or dessert table, often a 36-inch or 48-inch round.

### Linens
Floor-length cloths on the display tables hide boxes and bags. For guests' tables, a runner or an overlay on a plain cloth is enough. See the [tablecloth size chart](/blog/tablecloth-size-chart).

## Plan for the gift moment

Gift opening takes 20 to 40 minutes for 30 guests, longer if you pass each gift around. Seating should face the mom-to-be. Put a trash bag within reach, a notebook for who gave what, and a small table next to her. Make sure there's enough floor room behind her for a helper.

## Comfort details for the guest of honor

Place her seat near restrooms and out of the sun. Provide a footstool if she wants one, and a cushion. Keep drinks and food within reach of her seat. Avoid chairs that are very low or that require pushing up from a deep cushion.

## Chicago-area notes

Showers often happen at restaurants, banquet rooms and homes, and in spring and summer in backyards. If you are hosting at a restaurant, ask whether you can bring in rentals or decorations, and whether there's a room fee. At home, check parking for older guests and plan a rain backup. For an outdoor shower, a tent or canopy shields gifts and food from weather. See [Chicago tent permit rules](/blog/chicago-tent-permit) for tents in the city, and check your village for suburbs.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many chairs do I need for a baby shower?

One for each guest, plus one extra. Showers are mostly seated, so plan on seating everyone. For a 30-guest shower, rent 31 to 32.

### What size tables work best for a baby shower?

Round tables of 6 or 8 work best because they encourage conversation. Use 48-inch rounds for 6 and 60-inch rounds for 8.

### How do I decorate the chair for the mom-to-be?

Use a chair that stands out, such as velvet or Louis medallion, then add a sash, balloons or a garland. Keep decorations off the seat itself so it stays comfortable.

### Where should the gift table go?

Near the front of the room, beside the mom-to-be's chair, but away from the food. Give it a cloth to the floor to hide bags and boxes.

### How far in advance should I book?

Four to six weeks ahead for spring and early summer, which are busy. Tell us the date and the guest count and we'll plan around it.

### Is a baby shower better indoors or outdoors?

Either works. Indoors is easier in cold or wet weather. Outdoors gives space and light but needs shade and a rain plan.
