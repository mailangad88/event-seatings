## July weather: what to watch out for

July is hot and humid. NWS normals for O'Hare give July an average high of 84.5°F and a low of 66.4°F. Heat waves push well past that, and humidity makes it feel hotter. Afternoon storms can pop up quickly.

What to plan for:

- **Heat first.** Shade over every seat, cold water before guests ask, and an evening start. Read our [summer event heat plan for Chicago](/blog/summer-event-heat-plan-chicago) and [fans and coolers for summer events](/blog/fans-and-coolers-for-summer-events).
- **Storm rule.** Know where guests go if lightning is near: a building, not a tent.
- **Dark upholstery and metal get hot.** Light-colored, outdoor-friendly chairs stay more comfortable in direct sun.
- **Late light.** Sunset is about 8:29 pm on July 1 and 8:14 pm by July 28, so dinner can run in daylight.

Read [outdoor events in Chicago in July](/blog/outdoor-events-chicago-in-july) for more.

## Venue demand and booking lead times in July

July is busy, but it is less concentrated than June or September. The 4th of July weekend is the main pressure point: backyard rentals, tents and party items for that weekend book early. Our [4th of July backyard party rentals](/blog/fourth-of-july-backyard-party-rentals) guide has a full list.

If your guests are traveling into downtown Chicago, check the festival calendar before you set a date. Big Grant Park festivals fill hotels and raise rates. The city announces each year's dates, so confirm them for 2027. For a block party in the city, you will also need a permit from your alderman's office or the city; check current rules early.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the hottest month in Chicago?

July. NWS normals for O'Hare give an average high of 84.5°F and a low of 66.4°F, and heat waves go well beyond that. Plan shade, cooling and water for any outdoor event.

### When is the 4th of July holiday observed in 2027?

July 4, 2027 is a Sunday, so the federal holiday is observed Monday, July 5. Expect a 3-day weekend and early bookings for backyard party rentals.

### When is Lollapalooza in 2027?

Dates for 2027 had not been announced when we checked. In 2026 it ran July 30 to August 2 in Grant Park. Downtown hotels fill that weekend, so check before setting a downtown date.

### When is the Taste of Chicago in 2027?

The city sets the dates each year. In 2026 it ran July 8 to 12 in Grant Park. Check the city's announcement before planning a downtown event that week.

### What chairs work best outside in July?

Light-colored resin or garden folding chairs. They handle grass and do not trap heat the way dark upholstery or bare metal in full sun can.

### Do I need a permit for a block party in Chicago?

Yes, Chicago block parties need a permit. Rules and lead times change, so check with your alderman's office and the city early.
