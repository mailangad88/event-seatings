**Quick answer**

A 9-foot market umbrella shades a 60-inch round table with its chairs, or about 4 to 6 seated guests at a smaller table. Plan one umbrella per table, anchor each with a base of at least 50 pounds (heavier in wind), and close them whenever wind picks up. For crowds over 50 or for events with food service, a tent is usually a better shade solution.

Chicago summers deliver bright sun, heat and humidity, and guests at an outdoor table notice both. A market umbrella is the quickest shade rental: it opens in a minute, adds style and works on patios and lawns. It also has limits, especially in wind. This guide helps you pick the right size, count and base, and know when to choose something else.

## Sizes and coverage

Umbrella size is measured by canopy width. Coverage depends on the sun angle, which moves through the day, so the shade does not stay centered on the table.

<div class="table-wrap">

| Canopy | Shades about | Good for | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7 ft | 2 to 4 people | Cafe table, lounge side table | Small footprint |
| 9 ft | 4 to 6 people | 48 or 60 in round table | Most common size |
| 11 ft | 6 to 8 people | 60 or 72 in round table | Needs a heavier base |
| 13 ft or cantilever | 8 to 10 people | Lounge sets, bars | Use with strong base |

</div>

These are typical ranges. Ask your rental company for exact canopy sizes and heights. A round table needs a canopy at least 2 to 3 feet wider than the table to shade the chairs when the sun is not overhead.

## Base and wind safety

An umbrella is a sail. In a gust, a light base lets it tip or lift, which can injure guests and damage property.

- **Base weight.** Rental umbrellas typically use weighted bases or water or sand bases. A common guideline is at least 50 pounds for a 9-foot umbrella and more for larger canopies. Ask the vendor for the specific base weight and wind rating.
- **Through a table.** Many umbrellas pass through a hole in a table. This adds stability but not for all table styles.
- **Close it in wind.** Close umbrellas when gusts come up, and tell staff to watch the sky.
- **Never leave them open unattended** in a storm.
- **Ground.** Soft turf needs a heavier or staked base. Concrete needs a weighted base.

Chicago is called the Windy City for a reason, and lakefront or open-field venues can gust hard. For breezy sites, consider a tent with sidewalls or a smaller number of heavier umbrellas. See [outdoor event chairs and Chicago weather](/blog/topic/seasons-and-venues).

## Styling

Umbrellas can match your palette: white and ivory for weddings, bold stripes for casual picnics, black for modern corporate events. Add string lights under the canopy for evening use, but confirm with the vendor that lights are safe to attach. A cluster of umbrellas at different heights looks designed. Avoid mixing too many colors.

## Chicago and suburb notes

Park districts and forest preserves may restrict stakes, anchors and tents, and some beaches restrict structures. Always check with the specific site. For rooftop and terrace venues, ask about weight limits and wind rules, since high-rise rooftops are windier than ground level.

## Frequently asked questions

### What size umbrella do I need for a 60-inch round table?

A 9- to 11-foot umbrella. The canopy should extend at least 2 to 3 feet beyond the table so chairs stay shaded as the sun moves.

### How heavy should an umbrella base be?

A common guideline is at least 50 pounds for a 9-foot umbrella, more for larger ones and in windy areas. Ask the rental company for its recommendation.

### Can market umbrellas be used in wind?

Light breezes are fine, but close them in gusts. Do not leave them open in storms or when wind is strong.

### How many umbrellas do I need for 100 guests?

About 10 to 13 if guests sit at 8 to 10 tables, plus lounge and station umbrellas. For that many, compare the cost to a tent.

### Can I put an umbrella through a table?

Often, if the table has a center hole. Ask the vendor which tables and bases are compatible.

### Do umbrellas protect from rain?

They help in light rain but are not a rain plan. Use a tent with sidewalls for rain.
