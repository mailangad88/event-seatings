**Quick answer**

The item price is only the first line of an event rental bill. Delivery, stairs or a long carry, setup, after-hours or holiday timing, a damage waiver, cleaning, fuel, tax, rush orders, re-delivery and lost items can all be added. Ask about each one before you sign and get the answers in writing, so the total on the quote is the total you pay.

</div>

You find chairs at one price and a tablecloth at another, add them up, and the number looks fine. Then the quote comes back higher. Nothing sneaky happened. Rental companies price the work around the items, and that work shows up as separate lines. In the Chicago area it adds up fast: alley garages, third-floor walk-ups, street parking and winter weather all change the work.

This post is the whole list in one place. Each line links to a longer guide where we have one.

## The checklist, line by line

<div class="table-wrap">

| Line | What it covers | Ask this |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery and pickup | The truck trip, by distance or zone | Is pickup a separate charge from delivery? |
| Stairs or long carry | Crew carrying past the curb, up steps or down a long gangway | What counts as a long carry, and is it by flight or by item? |
| Elevator or dock | Waiting for a freight elevator or dock slot | Who pays if the slot is late? |
| Setup | Placing tables and chairs to your plan | Is setup included, and does it follow my floor plan? |
| Breakdown | Collecting and loading after the event | Do I need to stack and bag items first? |
| After-hours | Delivery or pickup outside normal hours | What hours count as standard? |
| Holiday or peak weekend | Extra cost on busy dates | Is my date treated as peak? |
| Damage waiver | Covers accidental damage | What is excluded, and is it required? |
| Cleaning | Extra washing or stain charges | What condition do I return items in? |
| Fuel | Propane or other fuel for heaters and cooking gear | Is fuel included or billed at pickup? |
| Tax | Sales tax on rentals | Is tax on the quote or added later? |
| Rush order | Short-notice bookings | What is the cutoff for standard pricing? |
| Re-delivery | A second trip if no one could receive the order | What happens if the venue is locked? |
| Lost items | Replacement for missing pieces | What is the replacement price per item? |

</div>

We are not listing dollar amounts because they differ by company and by job. The point is to see every line before the date is locked.

## What most hosts find out too late

**Stairs and carries are priced on what the crew sees, not what you told them.** "A few steps" can mean 20 steps in a Chicago two-flat or a walk from a street spot to a back patio. Give the company the real route. [Measure the doors, gates and stairs](/blog/measure-before-you-rent-doors-gates-stairs) before you request the quote, and the number will not change later.

**Heaters and fryers need fuel.** Many rentals arrive without it, or with a starter amount. Ask whether fuel is included, who refills it mid-event, and what happens to a partly used tank. This matters most from October through March, when a patio heater runs all night.

**Missing items are billed at replacement price.** A chair or a glass rented for a few dollars can cost much more to replace. The count at delivery and pickup is the one thing in your control. [How to count rentals](/blog/how-to-count-rentals-at-delivery-and-pickup) walks through it.

## A five-step way to use the checklist

<ol class="flow">
<li><strong>Write down the route.</strong> Street or alley, steps, elevator, how far to the room.</li>
<li><strong>Write down the timing.</strong> Delivery window, event end, pickup day, and whether it is a holiday weekend.</li>
<li><strong>Send both with your item list.</strong> The quote can then include access and timing charges from the start.</li>
<li><strong>Ask for every line to be itemized.</strong> A single bundled total hides what you can change.</li>
<li><strong>Compare totals, not item rates.</strong> See <a href="/blog/how-to-compare-event-rental-quotes">how to compare event rental quotes</a>.</li>
</ol>

## Ways to lower the total without cutting items

- Pick a delivery and pickup time inside standard hours.
- Move the delivery point to the closest door.
- Stack, bag and group items so the crew loads faster.
- Move off a peak weekend if your date is flexible.
- Skip a waiver only if you have read the replacement prices and are comfortable with them.

## Frequently asked questions

### What hidden fees should I watch for with event rentals?

The most common ones are stairs or long-carry charges, after-hours timing, damage waivers, cleaning, fuel, tax and re-delivery. Holiday and peak-weekend pricing can also apply. Ask for each one by name.

### Is delivery included in the rental price?

Usually not. Delivery and pickup are normally separate lines, and some companies price each trip on its own. Ask whether the quote shows both trips.

### Why did my rental total go up after the first quote?

Quotes change when details arrive that were missing: a new headcount, stairs, a loading dock window or a different date. Sending the access and timing details up front gives you a number that holds.

### Are rental fees negotiable?

Some are. Timing and access charges often shrink if you pick standard hours and a close drop-off point. Damage waivers and tax usually are not negotiable, but you can ask whether a waiver is required.

### Do rental companies charge for lost items?

Yes, usually at a replacement price listed in the contract. Counting at delivery and pickup is the best way to avoid disputes.

### What should be in writing before I pay a deposit?

The item list, the delivery and pickup windows, every fee line, the waiver terms and the cancellation rules. If a line is not on the paper, ask for it to be added.
