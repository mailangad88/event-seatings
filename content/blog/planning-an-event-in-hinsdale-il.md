Hinsdale is an upscale village straddling DuPage and Cook counties, with a Metra-connected downtown, older estates and a village that manages its own parks. That last point trips people up: elsewhere you call a park district; here you call the village.

## Village, not park district

The Village of Hinsdale Parks and Recreation Department runs the village's parks and programs; Hinsdale does not have a separate park district. Burlington Park, near downtown, is the village's central green space and a common spot for community events. For any park reservation or public-space event, start with the village.

## Space types in Hinsdale

- **Village parks.** Burlington Park and others, managed by Parks and Recreation. Ask what is reservable.
- **Downtown streets and storefronts.** Village rules and permits apply.
- **Clubs and restaurants.** Private spaces with their own rules and furniture.
- **Estates and large homes.** Common for tented weddings. Parking, access and neighbors are the main constraints.
- **Churches and community halls.** Used for receptions and showers.

Hinsdale's larger lots make tented weddings and graduation parties common. A tent is more than shade: it needs flooring, lighting and often power. ## Seasonal notes

Spring weddings are popular, and the ground can be soft. Summer evenings are long and warm. Fall is the most reliable season for outdoor events. In winter, a heated tent or club ballroom is the practical choice.

## A Hinsdale example

A 120-guest tented wedding at an estate might use 15 rounds of 8. A [ghost chair](/chairs/ghost) (about $10-14, an estimate) lets the tent and linens show through, while [Louis chairs](/chairs/louis-medallion) (about $20-30) suit a formal look.

## Details that matter at an estate

Estate events succeed or fail on logistics. Think through these before you order.

- **Access:** how wide is the driveway, and where will a truck stop? Heavy tables are easier to carry 30 feet than 150 feet.
- **Ground:** a level, firm lawn is rare. Ask about tent flooring. Our [tent flooring and subfloor guide](/blog/tent-flooring-and-subfloors) covers it.
- **Power:** estates often have limited outdoor outlets. Count lights, a DJ and a bar, then plan a generator if needed.
- **Neighbors:** tell neighbors about the date, and ask the village about noise rules and end times.
- **Parking:** guests need a place to park. Valet or shuttle service may be needed.

## Choosing between a club, a restaurant and a tent

Hinsdale hosts do not lack choices, so decide on priorities first.

- **A club or restaurant** gives you a finished room, a kitchen and a roof. You pay for convenience, and you follow its rules on outside vendors and décor.
- **A tent at a private home** gives you control and a personal setting. You manage permits, flooring, lighting, restrooms and parking.
- **A village park** is the most modest option and the least private. It suits community events, not formal weddings.

Whichever you pick, count guests first, then ask what the space includes. Many people discover late that a club includes chairs but not linens, or that a tent includes walls but not floors. Request a written list so you can compare real totals.

No. Parks in Hinsdale are run by the Village of Hinsdale Parks and Recreation Department. Ask it about park use and rentals.

Burlington Park is a central park in Hinsdale near the downtown area. Ask the village what is reservable.

Ask the village. Tent rules vary by size and use, and a rental company can tell you what the inspector may want to see.
