If you have been to a Chicago hotel ballroom for a fundraiser or a wedding, you have probably seen a gold chair. The Chiavari is the classic, but the infinity chair has become the modern alternative: the same polish, a more contemporary shape and often a lower price. This guide helps planners decide when it is the right choice, how to pair it with linens and how to count seats when the room is full.

## What the infinity chair is

The name comes from the looped shape of the back. Our [gold infinity chair](/chairs/infinity) has a slim metal frame in gold, silver or black. A seat pad, clear or white, is included. It stacks, which is a big advantage in a ballroom that is flipped from ceremony to reception, and it is outdoor-friendly on firm surfaces.

Compared with other formal chairs:

- It is **lighter** than most wood ballroom chairs.
- It has a **thin profile**, so it takes less visual and physical space.
- It is **metal**, so it feels more modern than carved or upholstered options.

## When infinity is the right choice

Pick the infinity chair if:

1. **Your room is a ballroom or banquet hall** with chandeliers, gold or neutral carpet.
2. **You are holding a gala or fundraiser** where donors sit for a long program. The included seat pad helps.
3. **You want a Chiavari look at a different price point.** See our guide on [Chiavari chair alternatives](/blog/chiavari-chair-alternatives).
4. **You need to flip the room** from ceremony to reception. Stacking makes this faster.

Skip it if you want a warm, natural look. A farm-style event or an orchard wedding is better served by [cross-back chairs](/chairs/cross-back) or a [Windsor spindle chair](/chairs/windsor).

## Linens and centerpieces

A slim chair lets the table do the talking. Sequin or satin linens are the usual match for gold, and a tall centerpiece looks balanced against the chair's vertical lines. Keep sashes simple: a thin ribbon or none. A full bow hides the loop, which is the chair's best feature. For linen sizes, use our [tablecloth size chart](/blog/tablecloth-size-chart).

## Counting seats and spacing

Round tables at galas usually seat 10 at a 72-inch round, or 8 at a 60-inch. Infinity chairs are slimmer than many banquet chairs, but you should still allow:

- **About 18 to 24 inches of table edge per guest**, and more for a plated dinner with several courses.
- **60 inches** between table edges in main aisles, 36 inches minimum elsewhere, as a rule of thumb for service and accessibility.
- **Extra width in front of the stage** if there is a program, so people can move without disturbing others.

For tricky counts, use [how many chairs do I need](/blog/how-many-chairs-do-i-need).

## Chicago ballroom notes

Some Chicago ballrooms and hotels supply their own chairs, so a rental makes sense mainly when you want a particular look. Ask the venue before you order whether outside chairs are allowed, whether they need a certificate of insurance and which freight elevator or loading dock you must use. Those rules differ by building, so confirm them in writing.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does it cost to rent gold infinity chairs in Chicago?

Our estimate is $9 to $13 per chair, with the seat pad included. The final quote depends on quantity, delivery and setup.

### Are infinity chairs comfortable?

Yes, for a normal gala dinner. The pad is included. For very long programs or older guests, ask about a thicker cushion.

### What is the difference between an infinity and a Chiavari chair?

A Chiavari has a ladder or tiffany back and a more traditional shape. An infinity chair has a looped back and a slimmer frame. Both look formal; the infinity looks more modern.

### Do infinity chairs stack?

Yes, which helps with delivery, ceremony flips and venue storage.

### Can infinity chairs be used outside?

Yes, on firm ground or flooring. Avoid soft grass, where thin legs sink.

### What color linens go with gold chairs?

Ivory, champagne, burgundy, black and sequin overlays all look good. Avoid yellow linens, which blend with the gold.

### Are infinity chairs good for a corporate dinner?

Yes. Black or silver is neutral, and the chair stacks, so it handles a conference ballroom flip well. See [shell chairs for corporate events](/blog/shell-chairs-corporate-events) for another option.
