**Quick answer**

A typical wood farm table is 8 feet long and 30 to 40 inches wide, seating 8 to 10 guests; a 10-foot table seats 10 to 12. Plan 24 to 26 inches of edge per guest, keep centerpieces low, and skip a full tablecloth in favor of a runner. For 100 guests, expect about 10 to 12 farm tables, plus a head table and service tables.

## Farm table sizes

<div class="table-wrap">

| Length | Typical width | Seats | Good for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 ft | 30 to 36 in | 6 | Head table, small family |
| 8 ft | 30 to 40 in | 8 | Most rows |
| 9 to 10 ft | 36 to 40 in | 10 to 12 | Long banquet-style rows |
| Square or round farm | 48 to 60 in | 6 to 8 | Mixed layouts |

</div>

Handmade and farmhouse tables vary by maker, so ask for exact dimensions, not just the length. Width matters: a 30-inch table is fine for a place setting but crowds a shared platter, while a 40-inch table gives you room for food and flowers.

## Why couples choose farm tables

- **They look finished without linen.** The wood is the decor.
- **They create a communal feel.** Everyone sits in one family-style row.
- **They photograph well.** A long table with candles and greens is a natural fit for rustic and boho themes. See our [boho wedding chairs](/blog/boho-wedding-chairs) and [barn wedding seating ideas](/blog/barn-wedding-seating-ideas).

## The trade-offs nobody mentions

<ol class="flow">
<li><strong>Weight</strong> Wood farm tables are heavy. Venue access, stairs and elevators matter.</li>
<li><strong>Seats vary</strong> Legs and aprons can block some seats. Count usable seats, not the length.</li>
<li><strong>Table runner, not floor cloth</strong> Floor-length cloth hides the wood you paid for.</li>
<li><strong>Centerpieces</strong> A 36 inch width gives you a narrow lane, so keep them low.</li>
<li><strong>Floors</strong> Ask the rental company about felt pads or protective feet for hardwood venues.</li>
</ol>

If a farm table has a trestle base, a guest at the end may find a leg in the way of their knees. A pedestal base or an end-leg table is easier to seat.

## Styling a farm table

1. Start with a linen or burlap runner 12 to 16 inches wide, not a cloth. See [farm table linens](/blog/farm-table-linens-runner-only) for sizing.
2. Add greenery, low vases or candles down the middle.
3. Place chargers, napkins and flatware directly on the wood.
4. Add menu cards on the plates, not on stands.

Keep the center lane clear enough that two plates of family-style food fit beside the flowers. A clutter-free lane lets servers pass dishes without knocking things over.

## Chairs for farm tables

Pick chairs with light visual weight so they do not fight the wood. The [cross-back chair](/chairs/cross-back) pairs naturally with farmhouse wood, and [windsor chairs](/chairs/windsor) add a classic country look. If you want contrast, a clear [ghost chair](/chairs/ghost) lets the table take center stage.

Check seat height too. A standard 30-inch table works with chairs of 17 to 18 inches in seat height. A farm table with a thick top can sit a bit higher, so ask for measurements.

## Questions to ask the rental company

- Exact table dimensions and seat count.
- Weight and number of people needed to carry it.
- Finish: reclaimed, whitewashed or dark wood.
- Whether it is sealed, because red wine on bare wood leaves marks.
- Delivery rules for stairs and loading docks.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many people sit at a farm table?

An 8-foot farm table seats 8 to 10 guests and a 10-foot table seats 10 to 12. Count about 24 to 26 inches of edge per person.

### How wide is a farm table for a wedding?

Most are 30 to 40 inches wide. A 36-inch width balances place settings and a center lane for flowers.

### Do you need tablecloths on farm tables?

No. Many couples use only a runner or bare wood. A full cloth hides the wood you chose the table for.

### Are farm tables good for a plated dinner?

Yes, as long as the table is wide enough for both place settings and a runner. A 36-inch width is the usual minimum for plated service.

### Can farm tables go outside?

Yes, on firm ground with a plan for rain. Check that the finish is sealed and the legs sit level. See [rental for outdoor events](/blog/table-rental-for-outdoor-events-level-ground).

### Are farm tables more expensive than banquet tables?

Usually, because they are heavier and more specialized. Pricing varies by rental company, so request a quote with your table list and venue.
