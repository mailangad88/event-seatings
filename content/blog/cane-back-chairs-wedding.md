## Why cane is indoor-only

Cane is a natural fiber, and it does not like standing moisture, long rain or soft, wet ground. A damp weave can sag or stain. In the Chicago area, humid summers and spring storms make outdoor risk real. If your event is on a lawn, choose a weather-tolerant chair, such as the [Windsor](/chairs/windsor), and keep cane-backs for a dry room. Our [outdoor event chairs and Chicago weather](/blog/topic/seasons-and-venues) guide has more.

## Cane-back vs rattan

The two are often mixed up. Rattan is a sturdier woven frame or seat and works outdoors in our catalog. Cane is a flat, open weave panel and is more formal and more delicate. Compare them in [rattan chairs for a garden party](/blog/rattan-chairs-garden-party).

## Best uses

1. **Head table.** Use cane-backs with a linen runner or flowers across the front.
2. **Full reception for 40 to 100 guests.** The chair is lighter in visual weight than an upholstered one.
3. **Estate or mansion venues.** The shape matches period rooms.
4. **Bridal showers and rehearsal dinners.** A relaxed event gets the texture without needing to hold up outdoors.

Keep sashes off. The weave is the decoration. Instead, tie a small bunch of dried or fresh flowers to one chair at the head table, or put a name card on the seat. For more, read [chair back decor, florals and signs](/blog/chair-back-decor-florals-and-signs).

## Comfort and care

The cane panel has some flex, so it is more forgiving than a rigid back. The seat is wood and firm. Cushions are available as an option, and they help with a long dinner. Tell guests with sharp jewelry or beaded gowns that cane can snag. At setup, handle chairs by the frame, not the weave, and avoid stacking boxes or bags on them.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are cane-back chairs good for weddings?

Yes, for indoor or covered weddings with a vintage, garden or estate feel. They are not meant for lawns or rain.

### How much does it cost to rent cane-back chairs in Chicago?

Our estimate is $12 to $16 per chair. Quantity, delivery and setup affect the final quote.

### Can cane-back chairs be used outdoors?

Not in our catalog. Cane is a natural fiber that does not like moisture, so use them inside or on a fully covered, dry, floored space.

### Do cane-back chairs stack?

No. Plan for extra delivery space and a clear spot where chairs can wait before setup.

### Are cane-back chairs comfortable?

They are comfortable for a normal dinner. Add the optional cushion for dinners longer than two hours.

### What is the difference between cane and rattan?

Cane is a thin, flat woven panel and looks formal and vintage. Rattan is a thicker, rounder fiber used for frames and seats and looks more casual.

### Can I use cane-back chairs with a farm table?

Yes. Natural cane with a bare wood table is a classic combination. Add linen napkins and greenery.
