**Quick answer**

At a housewarming, guests walk through the house and eat while standing, so you need seats for about one third to one half of them, not a full dining set. For 40 guests, rent 15 to 20 chairs, two cocktail or bistro tables, and one or two long tables for food. Place seats in small groups of 3 to 6, away from the food table and out of the walking route from the front door to the tour.

</div>

A new home rarely has enough seats for the party you want to throw. The living room has a sofa and two chairs, the kitchen island has four stools, and the dining table is still being delivered. You do not need to buy anything. A small rental order fills the gaps for one afternoon and goes back on a truck.

## Why a housewarming needs fewer chairs than a dinner

Guests tour, mingle and refill plates. They do not stay in one place, and they bring a drink. Plan your seating around three behaviors:

- **Tour:** guests walk room to room, so hallways and stairs must stay clear.
- **Graze:** they eat from a table or island, then look for somewhere to sit for ten minutes.
- **Talk:** they gather in small groups, usually near the kitchen.

Count your own furniture first. A sofa seats 3, a loveseat 2, an armchair 1, kitchen stools 4 or more. Then rent the difference. The guide to [how many chairs you need](/blog/how-many-chairs-do-i-need) explains the counts in more detail.

## Pick chairs that blend with the house

A housewarming should look like the home, not like a banquet hall. Choose a style that matches your decor and doesn't scream "rental".

- [Cross-back chairs](/chairs/cross-back) at an estimated $8 to $11 suit a modern farmhouse.
- [Ghost chairs](/chairs/ghost) at about $10 to $14 disappear into a modern interior.
- [Bentwood chairs](/chairs/bentwood) look good around a bistro table.
- [Garden folding chairs](/chairs/garden-folding) at $5 to $7 are practical on a patio or lawn.

## Food that suits a tour

<ol class="flow">
<li><strong>Put the main spread in the kitchen or dining room</strong> so guests see those rooms.</li>
<li><strong>Add a second small station</strong> (cheese, dessert or drinks) in another room to spread people out.</li>
<li><strong>Use cocktail tables</strong> beside each station, 42 inches tall, so guests have somewhere to set a drink.</li>
<li><strong>Keep drinks away from the front door</strong> to avoid a crowd at the entrance.</li>
<li><strong>Offer a sit-down spot</strong> with a few chairs, since not everyone wants to stand for two hours.</li>
</ol>

Rent plates and glasses if you do not want to buy more of either. See [how many plates per guest](/blog/how-many-plates-per-guest) and [how many glasses per guest](/blog/how-many-glasses-per-guest) for the counts.

Put a mat or runner down for wet shoes in winter. Set felt pads under chair legs on hardwood. Use coasters, and keep red wine on a washable surface, not the new rug.

## Chicago-area notes

Chicago winters mean coats, so add a rack or a bed in a spare room. If you live in a condo or a two-flat, check your building's rules for deliveries and elevators, and tell neighbors that guests will be coming. Many suburban neighborhoods have street parking limits, so ask guests to carpool. In summer, open the back door and let the patio carry part of the crowd. For lighting after dark, see [backyard party lighting ideas](/blog/backyard-party-lighting-ideas).

## Frequently asked questions

### How many chairs do I need for a housewarming?

Plan seats for one third to one half of your guests, counting your own furniture. A 40-guest party usually needs 15 to 20 rented chairs.

### Do I need a food table for a housewarming?

Yes. One 6-foot table for 20 guests and an 8-foot for larger groups. Set it in the kitchen or dining room, not at the entrance.

### Should I rent plates and glasses?

If you do not own enough, yes. Rented plates and glasses save a shopping trip and a dishwasher run, and they look better than paper for a nice evening.

### How long does a housewarming last?

Two to three hours is typical. Guests come and go, so plan food for the full time and stagger refills.

### Where should I put coats?

In a spare bedroom on the bed, or on a rolling rack by the entrance. Do not pile them on the sofa or the dining chairs you need for seating.

### Can I host a housewarming in a small condo?

Yes. Plan standing room, use cocktail tables, keep the food to bite-sized and use a patio or common room if your building allows it.
