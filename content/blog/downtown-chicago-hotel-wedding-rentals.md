A downtown Chicago hotel ballroom is a reliable choice for a wedding reception: built-in capacity, an events team that runs weddings constantly, and a location guests can find easily. But a hotel ballroom isn't a blank room you can set up however you like. The hotel has its own furniture, its own schedule, and its own rules for anyone bringing things in from outside.

## In-house furniture vs. your own rental chairs

Every downtown hotel ballroom comes with its own chairs and tables as part of the room rental or catering package. These are usually simple banquet chairs and round or rectangular tables, built for volume rather than a particular look. If you want something different, like a [cross-back chair](/chairs/cross-back) or [wishbone chair](/chairs/wishbone) for your head table, or a full room of a specific chair style, many hotels will allow outside rental furniture for an additional fee.

## Loading docks and freight elevators

Downtown hotels are busy buildings with multiple events happening at once, sometimes on the same day in different rooms. Getting rental furniture into a ballroom usually means using the hotel's loading dock and a freight elevator, not the main lobby entrance. Ask your catering manager:

<li><strong>Where the loading dock is</strong> and how a delivery truck should approach it.</li>
<li><strong>Which freight elevator</strong> serves the ballroom floor.</li>
<li><strong>What time window</strong> is reserved for your vendors.</li>
<li><strong>Whether other events</strong> are sharing the dock or elevator that day.</li>

## Working around the hotel's event timeline

A hotel ballroom usually has a tight schedule built around other events, housekeeping turnovers, and the hotel's own staff setting tables and linens. Your rental delivery, setup, and any swap of furniture needs to happen inside a window the hotel assigns, not whenever is convenient for you. Build your wedding day timeline around:

- When the room is actually released to you and your vendors
- How much time the hotel staff needs to set their own tables before your extra furniture goes in
- When everything needs to be out the next morning, if there's another event right after

## Certificates of insurance

Hotels almost always require a certificate of insurance from any outside vendor delivering furniture, décor, or equipment into the building. This is standard for hotel events and protects the property in case of damage. If your hotel requires one, ask whoever delivers your rental chairs and tables for their certificate of insurance well ahead of your date, and send it to your catering manager for approval before the wedding.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I bring in my own rental chairs for a hotel wedding?

Usually yes, but confirm with your catering or events manager first. Many hotels allow outside rental furniture for an additional fee, while their in-house chairs are included in the base package.

### Does the hotel provide a certificate of insurance for my rental company?

No. If the hotel requires a certificate of insurance, it comes from whoever delivers your rental furniture, not from the hotel or from you.

### How does rental furniture get into the ballroom?

Almost always through the hotel's loading dock and a freight elevator, on a schedule the hotel assigns, rather than through the guest lobby.

### Will the hotel remove their own chairs if I rent different ones?

Ask ahead of time. Some hotels remove and store their furniture for your event, while others may charge extra or need more setup time to do so.

### What if another event is using the loading dock the same day?

This is common in busy downtown hotels, so confirm your delivery window early and build in some buffer time in case the schedule shifts.

### How far in advance should I confirm outside rental approval with the hotel?

As early as possible, ideally when you book the ballroom, since it affects your rental budget and your vendor's delivery schedule.
