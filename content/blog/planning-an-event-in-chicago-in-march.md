## March weather: what to watch out for

March in Chicago is unsettled. NWS normals for O'Hare give March an average high of 47.0°F, 2.45 inches of precipitation and about 5.5 inches of snow. That snow often falls on days when the ground is already soft from a thaw.

What to plan for:

- **Soft, muddy ground.** Lawns and gravel lots are often saturated in March. If any part of your event touches grass, ask about flooring or ground protection, and keep heavy deliveries on pavement.
- **Two-season wardrobe.** Guests arrive in coats one week and light jackets the next. Keep coat racks in the plan.
- **Wind.** March is windy. Tents need proper anchoring, and lightweight signs and decor need weights. See [tent wind ratings and weather safety](/blog/tent-wind-ratings-weather-safety).
- **Later sunset after March 14.** Sunset is about 5:39 pm on March 1 and about 6:55 pm by March 15, after the clocks change. Evening events feel brighter and photos are easier.

## Venue demand and booking lead times in March

March is still off-peak for most Chicago venues, with a few exceptions: St. Patrick's weekend downtown, Easter and Holy Week for church halls and restaurants, and Eid gatherings for banquet halls. Outside those, it is a good month to book a venue that is hard to get later in the year.

March is also when spring planning starts. If you are hosting a graduation party in May or June, a spring wedding, or a first communion, this is the month to lock in tents and specialty chairs. Spring Saturdays start filling early. Read our [graduation party tent guide](/blog/graduation-party-tent-rental) if you plan to host at home.

## Frequently asked questions

### When is the St. Patrick's Day parade in Chicago in 2027?

The downtown parade and the Chicago River dyeing are usually held on the Saturday before St. Patrick's Day, which would be March 13, 2027. Check the official parade announcement, and avoid Loop deliveries and load-ins that morning.

### When does daylight saving time start in 2027?

Sunday, March 14, 2027. Sunset in Chicago moves from about 6 pm to about 7 pm overnight, which gives evening events in the second half of March much more light.

### Can I have an outdoor event in Chicago in March?

Only with a solid backup. The average high is 47°F and snow is still common, so use a heated tent with sidewalls or keep the outdoor part short, like photos or a fire pit.

### When is Easter in 2027?

Sunday, March 28, 2027, with Good Friday on March 26. Church halls and restaurants are busy that week, so book early if you plan a brunch or a first communion party nearby.

### When is Eid al-Fitr in 2027?

Around March 9 or 10, 2027, depending on the moon sighting. Banquet halls that host Eid dinners book for the days and weekend that follow.

### What should I book in March for a spring event?

Book the venue, any tent, and specialty chairs first, because those are the items that run out on spring Saturdays. Tables, linens and standard chairs can follow.
