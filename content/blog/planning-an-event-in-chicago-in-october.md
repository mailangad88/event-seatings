## October weather: what to watch out for

October turns cool. NWS normals for O'Hare give October an average high of 62.7°F and a low of 45.4°F. Warm afternoons still happen early in the month, but cold snaps and the first frost are possible later.

What to plan for:

- **Heat for evenings.** Patio heaters or tent heaters for anything after sunset. Read [heating a tent in fall](/blog/heating-a-tent-in-fall).
- **Lighting.** Sunset falls from about 6:34 pm on October 1 to 6:10 pm by October 15 and 5:51 pm by October 28. Plan lighting from the start of the event.
- **Leaves and damp ground.** Leaves are slippery when wet. Clear aisles and paths, and consider flooring for a ceremony on grass.
- **Rain plan.** Keep sidewalls or an indoor room on hold.

More in [outdoor events in Chicago in October](/blog/outdoor-events-chicago-in-october).

## Venue demand and booking lead times in October

October is peak season for weddings, galas and fundraisers, and the Marathon weekend adds pressure on downtown hotels. Book the venue first and treat October Saturdays like June: tents, specialty chairs and dance floors 2 to 4 months ahead at minimum.

Halloween parties cluster on the weekend before October 31, and Diwali celebrations on the weekends around October 29. If you serve South Asian families, banquet halls and decor items like [throne chairs](/chairs/royal-throne) and mandap staging go early. Our [Halloween party planning guide](/blog/halloween-party-planning-guide) covers that side of the month.

## Frequently asked questions

### When is the Chicago Marathon in 2027?

Sunday, October 10, 2027, starting and finishing in Grant Park. Streets close along the course through many neighborhoods, so avoid deliveries in the city that day and expect downtown hotels to be full.

### Is October a good month for an outdoor event in Chicago?

Early October can be lovely, with a 62.7°F average high at O'Hare. Plan heaters and lighting because the average low is 45.4°F and sunset falls before 6:30 pm.

### When is Diwali in 2027?

Friday, October 29, 2027. Banquet halls and caterers book the weekends around it, so plan early if you are hosting a Diwali party.

### When are Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur in 2027?

Rosh Hashanah begins the evening of Friday, October 1, 2027, and Yom Kippur is October 11 (beginning the evening of October 10). Check your guest list before scheduling events on those days.

### What time is sunset in Chicago in October?

About 6:34 pm on October 1, 6:10 pm on October 15 and 5:51 pm on October 28 in 2027.

### When should I have a Halloween party in 2027?

Halloween is a Sunday in 2027, so most parties will be on Saturday, October 30 or the weekend before. Book rentals for those Saturdays early.
