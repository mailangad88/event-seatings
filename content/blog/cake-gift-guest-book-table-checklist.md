**Quick answer**

Plan a separate table for each of these: guest book, escort cards, gifts and cards, cake, and favors or desserts. Use a 30 to 36-inch round or a 6-foot banquet table for most, and a 48-inch round for the cake. Place the guest book and escort cards at the entrance, gifts near the head table or out of the main traffic, and the cake in a spot with a plain backdrop and good light.

</div>

Every wedding has a handful of small tables that nobody counts until the venue's floor plan is due. Then someone asks where the gifts go, and the room has no space left. In Chicago ballrooms and suburban banquet halls, a few of these tables are often provided by the venue, but many are not, so write them into your rental list. Here is a table by table checklist.

## The checklist

<div class="table-wrap">

| Table | Size | Notes | Cloth |
|---|---|---|---|
| Guest book | 30 in round or 6 ft banquet | Near the door, with a pen and a sign | Lap or floor length |
| Escort cards | 6 ft banquet | Cards in alphabetical order, easy to read | Floor length |
| Gifts and cards | 6 ft or 8 ft banquet | A box for cards and space for 20 to 40 gifts | Floor length |
| Cake | 36 to 48 in round | Sturdy and level, with a skirt | Floor length |
| Favors | 30 in round or banquet | One per guest if used | Lap length |
| Dessert or late-night | 6 to 8 ft banquet | Serve from one side | Floor length |
| Memory or photo | 30 in round | Photos of family, optional | Lap length |

</div>

## Guest book table

Guests reach it first. Keep it small: a 30-inch round fits a book, a pen and a framed sign. A banquet table gives room for a sign-in alternative such as a polaroid camera or a prompt card. Make sure the lighting is good and a pen is attached, so you do not lose it. A chair next to it helps older guests.

## Escort cards and seating chart

Escort cards go on a banquet table of 6 or 8 feet. If you have 100 guests, you need about 6 to 8 feet of table to lay them out in alphabetical order with room to read. Large seating charts on easels need floor space, so place them where lines can form without blocking the door. For more ideas see [reception table names and seating maps](/blog/reception-table-names-and-seating-map).

## Gift table

Pick a 6 or 8-foot banquet table, set away from the door, near the head table or in a corner where someone can watch it. Put a card box or locking box on it, since cash and cards do get lost at weddings. Leave a chair for a relative to watch the gifts at the end of the night. Plan roughly 3 feet of table for every 20 gifts, and add a spare table if your guests are generous.

## Cake table

The cake needs a firm table. A 36 to 48-inch round is standard, with a cloth that reaches the floor. Place it where the cake cutting can be photographed, with a clean backdrop. Keep it away from sunny windows and away from heating vents or heaters, because buttercream and heat do not mix, and Chicago summers are humid. Ask your baker for the cake's exact footprint and weight. A multi-tier cake can overhang a 36-inch top. See [tablecloth size chart](/blog/tablecloth-size-chart) for the cloth.

<li><strong>Ask the baker</strong> for the cake's base size and weight.</li>

## Favors and dessert tables

Favors go at the door or on each guest's place setting. A dessert table is best placed away from the buffet, to avoid a double line. A 6 or 8-foot table with tiers or cake stands gives you height. Keep a trash bin and napkins close.

## Linens and skirting

Floor-length linens hide boxes and spare supplies stored under the table. Add a pleated skirt in front of the cake table for a finished look; see [pleated skirting](/blog/pleated-table-skirting-head-table-buffet).

## Frequently asked questions

### What size table do I need for a wedding cake?

A 36 to 48-inch round table works for most cakes. Check the cake's base width and weight with your baker.

### What size is a guest book table?

A 30-inch round or a 6-foot banquet table. Keep it small so it does not block the entrance.

### How many tables do I need for gifts?

One 6-foot table for about 40 guests. Add another if the guest list is over 100. Gift tables fill up more at big weddings.

### Where should the cake table go?

Where the cake cutting can be photographed, with a plain backdrop, out of sunlight and away from heaters.

### Do I need a table for escort cards?

If you assign tables, yes. A 6-foot banquet table suits 80 to 120 cards.

### Who supplies these tables?

Sometimes the venue; sometimes the rental company. Ask your venue which tables are included and add the rest to your list.
