Tent lighting is where a plain white roof becomes a room. It is also where events run out of power, trip breakers or leave half the tent dark. Good lighting is a design plan plus an electrical plan. This guide covers both.

**Layer 1: overhead glow.** Warm white string lights (bistro or festoon style) draped in swags or crisscrossed across the ceiling are the classic. Warm white (about 2700 K) is flattering for skin, food and linens.

**Layer 2: focal points.** Chandeliers over the dance floor or head table, or pendant globes over a bar. Frame tents have rigging points; ask the company what weight each point can carry before you buy a chandelier.

**Layer 3: color and wall wash.** LED uplights placed along the sidewalls add color and shape. Use them sparingly and keep colors close to your palette.

## More ideas that work

- **Starlight ceiling:** a field of tiny lights under a sheer fabric liner.
- **Paper lanterns or globes** at different heights over a casual reception.
- **Edison bulbs on a grid** above the dance floor.
- **Path lights and lanterns** outside the tent for the walk from the parking area.
- **Candles at tables,** in glass or LED. Open flame is restricted near and inside tents in many places, so choose LED flameless unless your village allows open flame.

## Plan the power

1. **Count watts.** LED string lights draw very little; chandeliers, uplights, a DJ and a band add up. Add them in a list.
2. **Know the source.** Is power coming from the house, a tent company generator or a venue panel? A household circuit is typically a 15 or 20 amp breaker, and a caterer's warmers or coffee machines can easily trip it.
3. **Separate circuits.** Keep lights on their own circuit away from kitchen and DJ gear.
4. **Use outdoor-rated cords** and keep them out of walkways, with covers where guests walk.
5. **Generators** need space and distance from the tent. Winnetka's tent form, for instance, asks for 20 feet of separation between generators and the tent. Check your village.
6. **Emergency lighting.** Chicago requires battery-backup emergency lighting for tents that stay in place more than 48 hours. See [Chicago tent permits](/blog/chicago-tent-permit).

## When do you need lights in Chicago?

Sunset moves quickly across the seasons, and a tent without windows gets dark before the sky does. By late October, an evening reception needs full lighting well before dinner. Check an almanac for your exact date and plan lights to come up 30 minutes before sunset. More on cold-weather events in [heating a tent in fall](/blog/heating-a-tent-in-fall).

## Lighting and sidewalls

Clear sidewalls make a lit tent glow from outside, which is lovely and also shows every cord and fixture inside. Solid walls hide the party and give a backdrop for uplighting. See [clear, window and solid sidewalls](/blog/tent-sidewalls-clear-window-solid).

## A simple lighting plan for a 20 x 30 tent

For a small backyard tent, you do not need a designer. Run two or three strands of warm string lights from the center ridge to the side walls, hang one or two lanterns or pendants over the main table, and add a pair of uplights in the corners. Put the strands on a dedicated outlet with a timer or a dimmer. Place path lights from the car or house door to the tent opening so guests can see their steps. Test the whole setup at dusk the evening before and replace any dead strands.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you light the inside of a tent?

Layer lights: warm string lights overhead, a few chandeliers or pendants as focal points, and uplights on the walls. Plan the power first so the circuits can carry it.

### Can you hang string lights from a rented tent?

Usually yes, from the tent's frame or poles, but only at approved points. Ask your tent company what hardware and weight limits they allow before you hang anything.

### How much power does a tent party need?

It depends on the fixtures, the caterer and any DJ or band. Add the watts for each, and keep lighting on its own circuit. Ask the tent company or an electrician for a load calculation if you use house power.

### Are chandeliers safe in a tent?

They can be if they hang from approved rigging points and stay within weight limits. Confirm both with the tent company.

### Is it okay to use candles in a tent?

Often no. Many codes restrict open flame in or near tents. Use LED flameless candles unless your village says otherwise.

### Do I need a generator?

If the tent is far from a house or venue power, or the load is heavy, yes. Generators have distance and safety rules, so ask your tent company and village.
