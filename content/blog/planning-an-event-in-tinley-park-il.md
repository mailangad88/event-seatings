## A big hall needs a clear floor plan

A room for 200 is large enough to feel empty or confusing. A few moves help.

1. **Zone the room.** Dinner on one side, dance floor in the center and lounge on the other.
2. **Mark the main aisle** from the entrance to the dance floor, at least 6 feet wide.
3. **Put the bar away from the entrance.** Lines at the door block arrivals.
4. **Place the cake table** where photographers can reach it without crossing the dance floor.
5. **Check exits.** The hall's layout must respect exit paths, so share the plan with the venue.

For the dance floor size, see our [dance floor guide](/blog/dance-floor-size-by-guest-count). A typical rule is about 3 to 4 square feet per dancing guest, and many halls assume about a third of guests dance at once.

## Venue questions for a large hall

1. What is the seated capacity with a dance floor?
2. What tables, chairs and linens are included, and in what colors?
3. Who sets up and who takes down?
4. What are the rules for outside rentals and the loading dock?
5. Is there a room fee for early setup or a late teardown?
6. Where can the DJ and band plug in?

Do not rely on a venue's website for these answers; get them in writing from the event manager.

## Guest flow in a long evening

A reception in a big hall often runs five hours, and the room has to work at every stage. At arrival, guests need a place to check in, find their table and get a drink without bunching at the door. Plan a welcome table with the seating chart and a bar within sight. During dinner, tables should be reachable by servers on at least two sides. After dinner, the dance floor becomes the center, so keep tables from encircling it too tightly, or the noise will drown out conversation at the closest tables.

Plan a late-night item to keep energy high: a dessert table, a coffee station or snacks near the dance floor. Put a few lounge seats along the edge for guests who need a break. When the night ends, a clear exit path and a coat area keep departures calm, which matters in Chicago-area winters when everyone leaves at once.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many tables for a 200-guest wedding?

About 20 guest rounds of 10, plus 8 to 10 extra tables for the head table, food, bar, cake, gifts, DJ and seating chart.

### How many chairs for 200 guests?

About 210, plus any ceremony, lounge or head table seats.

### Should I upgrade all the chairs?

Not necessarily. Many couples upgrade the head table and keep the venue's chairs for guests.

### What size cloth for a 72-inch round?

A 132-inch round cloth reaches the floor. See the [size chart](/blog/tablecloth-size-chart).

### How big should the dance floor be?

About 3 to 4 square feet per dancer, sized for roughly a third of guests dancing at once.

### Can I bring in my own rentals to a convention center?

Often yes, but ask the venue about fees, insurance and dock access.
