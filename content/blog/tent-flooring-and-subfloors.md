**Quick answer**

You need a floor under a tent when the ground is uneven, soft or likely to turn muddy, or when guests will wear heels. Options range from simple grass mats to full raised subfloors that level the site and keep guests dry. A raised floor adds to the cost and setup time, but on a slope or after rain it is what keeps tables, chairs and high heels stable. Ask your tent company whether your site needs one.

</div>

Grass looks lovely in photos and behaves badly in practice. Chair legs sink, heels stick, tables rock and a rainy week turns the middle of a tent into a swamp. Flooring is the least glamorous part of a tent rental, and the one guests notice most when it is missing. This guide covers the main options and how to choose.

## Floor types, from basic to full build

<div class="table-wrap">

| Floor | What it is | Best for | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bare grass | The lawn | Dry, level ground, casual events | Sinks, muddy after rain, uneven |
| Grass or turf mats | Interlocking plastic mats over grass | Protecting lawn, light traffic | Does not level slopes |
| Carpet or turf rolls | Fabric or artificial turf laid on ground | Look and comfort | Needs a flat site |
| Raised subfloor | Platform on adjustable legs, plywood top | Slopes, wet sites, heels, ADA access | More cost, a day of setup |
| Dance floor | Hard panels over the subfloor or ground | Dancing | Needs a level base |
| Finished floor | Subfloor topped with carpet, wood-look tile or vinyl | Weddings, galas | Highest cost |

</div>

## Why a subfloor matters

A subfloor is a platform built on adjustable legs that sits above the ground. It does four jobs:

- **Levels the site.** Backyards in Chicago suburbs often slope toward a drain or a fence. A subfloor can level that slope, within limits.
- **Keeps guests dry.** Water runs under the floor, not through the party.
- **Protects the lawn.** Weight spreads out so chairs and tables do not rut the grass. See [tent and grass damage](/blog/tent-and-grass-damage).
- **Improves access.** A level surface with a ramp is easier for wheelchairs, strollers and walkers.

## When to say yes to a floor

Add a floor if any of these are true:

1. **The ground slopes or is lumpy.** If a marble rolls, so will a chair.
2. **You expect rain.** Flooring is rain insurance. See [rain plan with a tent](/blog/rain-plan-with-a-tent).
3. **Guests wear heels.** Heels sink 2 inches in soft turf.
4. **You want dancing.** A dance floor needs a flat base. Plan the area; see [tent layout with dance floor and bar](/blog/tent-layout-dance-floor-and-bar).
5. **Older guests or wheelchairs.** Level, firm surfaces matter.
6. **Winter or late fall.** Cold ground radiates up. A floor adds comfort and helps heating.

You can skip the floor when the lawn is flat and dry, the event is casual and short, and the tent covers a small area.

## Flooring over concrete, asphalt and pavers

On a patio or driveway you may not need a subfloor for leveling, but you may want carpet or tile for comfort and a finished look. On asphalt in hot weather, sharp points can dent the surface. See [tent on a driveway or parking lot](/blog/tent-on-driveway-or-parking-lot).

## What flooring costs

We do not publish tent prices. Floor cost is driven by square footage, the site slope, the system type, how many days it stays, access for trucks and whether it needs a ramp. A flat site is cheaper to floor than a hillside. Ask the tent company to quote the tent with and without a floor so you can see the difference.

<li><strong>Measure the tent area</strong> and the slope (a level and a tape will do).</li>

## Chairs and tables on a floor

A solid floor makes chairs behave. Folding and light chairs work well on mats or plywood. [Garden folding chairs](/chairs/garden-folding) are a practical pick for grass-based setups, and [cross-back chairs](/chairs/cross-back) look right on a wood-look floor. On bare turf, chair feet can leave marks, so ask about mats under the rental.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a floor under my tent?

Not always. If your lawn is flat, dry and the event is casual, grass works. Add a floor if the ground slopes, rain is likely, guests wear heels or you want dancing.

### What is a tent subfloor?

A subfloor is a raised platform of plywood over adjustable legs. It levels uneven ground and keeps guests above water, grass and mud.

### Can a subfloor fix a sloped yard?

Within limits. Adjustable legs can level a moderate slope, but steep sites may need more work or a different location. Your tent company will tell you the maximum slope their system can handle.

### Can I put a dance floor directly on grass?

Only if the ground is level and firm. A dance floor over an uneven lawn can rock and separate. Most companies recommend a subfloor under a dance floor on grass.

### Will a floor damage my lawn?

It shades the grass for days, so some yellowing is likely. A floor spreads weight better than chair legs, which makes it gentler than bare use.

### How long does floor installation take?

Often a day for larger floors, since the platform is built before the tent furniture goes in. Ask your company for their crew schedule, and see our [setup timeline](/blog/tent-rental-setup-timeline).
