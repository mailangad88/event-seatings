Lemont is a southwest suburb known for its limestone history, a historic downtown near the I&M Canal and a landscape of bluffs, quarry areas and golf courses, including Cog Hill. Many venues and backyards here use stone, gravel and uneven ground, which looks beautiful and challenges furniture. This guide compares surfaces and shows how to protect your guests and your chairs.

## Which surface is under your chairs?

Chair legs concentrate weight into small points. On firm ground, that is no problem. On soft or loose ground, it matters a lot.

Fixes are simple:

1. **Gravel:** lay plywood sheets or interlocking mats under the seating area, or choose a hard surface.
2. **Wet lawn:** wait, add flooring or move indoors.
3. **Uneven stone:** shim table legs and test chairs on the real surface the day before.
4. **Sloped ground:** pick the flattest area and keep guests away from drops.

Ask us about foot pads for lawn setups. For the details on tables on soft ground, see our [round table guide](/blog/round-table-sizes-and-seating-48-60-66-72-inch).

## Walking distance and shoes

Gravel and stone are hard on heels and wheelchairs. Place the ceremony and reception on the same level if possible, and keep the path from parking to seating short and firm. Offer a drop-off for older guests. Provide a basket of flats for guests who struggle with gravel, and consider a rubber or wood path for the main walkway. Lighting along the path is a safety matter after dark.

## Local rules and places

Lemont has a village government and a park district. For public land, such as canal paths or parks, contact the Lemont Park District or the Village of Lemont for permits, capacity and rules about chairs, tents and alcohol. Historic sites sometimes limit what can touch the ground. Private venues and quarries have their own policies, so ask. Never place chairs or tables on trails or in protected areas without permission.

## Weather around the bluffs

Weather in the Chicago region can shift quickly, and open ground near water and bluffs can feel windier. Weigh down signs, arches and linens. In spring, ground is wet; in summer, thunderstorms arise in the afternoon; in fall, evenings cool fast. Plan clear call times for weather decisions and a backup space.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can chairs go on gravel?

Yes, with plywood sheets, mats or foot pads. Thin legs sink into loose gravel and tip.

### What is the best chair for uneven ground?

A wide-footed wood chair, such as the cross-back or Windsor, with pads where needed.

### How do I make gravel comfortable for guests?

Add a firm walkway, offer a drop-off and light the path.

### Can I hold a ceremony on a canal path?

Contact the Lemont Park District or the Village of Lemont for rules on public paths and permits.

### Do I need flooring under a tent on gravel?

Often yes. Ask the tent company. Gravel is uneven and unsafe for heels.

### How early should I test the surface?

The day before, with a chair and a loaded table, at the same time of day as the event.
