A tent feels perfect at 3 p.m. in September and miserable at 8 p.m. in October. Heat leaves through the roof and the floor, and thin vinyl holds none of it. Heating is a safety question as much as a comfort one, because fire, fuel and carbon monoxide inside a fabric room are serious risks. Here is how to plan it.

## How cold does it get?

The National Weather Service's 1991 to 2020 normals for Chicago in October show an average high of about 62.7 F and an average low of about 45.4 F. That is an average. Evening temperatures after sunset are closer to the low, and a cold front can push them into the 30s. Plan heat for any event that runs past sunset from late September through November.

## What the fire code usually says

Rules are local, so ask your village or the City of Chicago. As an example, Winnetka's tent permit form states that:

- Portable outdoor gas-fired heaters and open-flame heat sources are prohibited inside the tent.
- Portable outdoor gas-fired heaters may not sit within 5 feet of the tent or of an exit.
- Electric space heaters must be listed and labeled and plug directly into an approved receptacle, with no extension cord.

Chicago's tent rules also factor heating devices into the separation distances between tents, and require fire extinguishers where cooking or heating takes place. See [Chicago tent permits](/blog/chicago-tent-permit) and [permits in the suburbs](/blog/tent-permit-chicago-suburbs). Open flames are also commonly banned in or near tents, so skip fire pits under the roof.

## Heater types, compared

<div class="table-wrap">

| Type | How it works | Pros | Cautions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ducted indirect-fired heater | Heater outside, hot air ducted in | Safest and best for large tents; no exhaust inside | Needs fuel, power, and a professional |
| Electric heater | Plugs into dedicated circuit | No fumes | Needs a lot of power; check circuits |
| Portable propane "mushroom" heater | Open flame, radiant | Popular outdoors | Often banned inside tents; CO and fire risk |
| Radiant electric panels | Heat people, not air | Spot warmth by bar or entry | Limited coverage |

</div>

For most events, the best answer is to let the tent company supply the ducted heater, since they know what the tent and your village will allow. Ask:

1. What is the heater's output, and what temperature can it hold in our tent?
2. Is it indirect-fired, so no exhaust enters the tent?
3. Where does the unit sit, and how far from the tent and exits?
4. Who monitors it during the event?

## Hold the heat in

Heating a leaky tent is wasteful. Cut the loss first:

- **Close and secure sidewalls** on every side. Solid sidewalls insulate better than thin clear vinyl, though clear panels still block wind. See [clear, window and solid sidewalls](/blog/tent-sidewalls-clear-window-solid).
- **Add a floor.** Cold ground pulls heat from guests' feet. See [tent flooring and subfloors](/blog/tent-flooring-and-subfloors).
- **Add a liner** under the roof if your company offers one.
- **Limit doors.** One main entrance with a vestibule or draft panel beats five openings.
- **Choose rugs** over bare vinyl in lounge areas.

## Comfort extras that help

Hot cider and coffee stations, wraps for the ceremony and a start time that finishes dinner before the coldest hours all help. Plan the dance floor away from the doors. Layout details in [tent layout with dance floor and bar](/blog/tent-layout-dance-floor-and-bar).

## Sizing: ask the pro

Heater sizing depends on the tent volume, the wall type, the outdoor temperature and the target. Industry rules of thumb exist, but they vary, so we leave the BTU number to your tent company. Give them your date, location and guest count and ask them to size for the coldest likely evening, not the average.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you heat a tent in October?

Close the sidewalls, add a floor and use a heater supplied and sized by your tent company, ideally an indirect-fired ducted unit or electric heat. Check your village's rules on heaters before you book.

### Can you use a propane heater in a tent?

Often not. Winnetka's tent form prohibits portable outdoor gas-fired heaters inside the tent and requires 5 feet of distance from the tent and exits. Ask your village and your tent company.

### What is the safest way to heat a tent?

Ducted heat from an indirect-fired unit placed outside is common because no exhaust enters the tent. Electric heat is the other safe option when you have enough power. Both should be installed by professionals.

### How warm will a heated tent get?

It depends on the heater size, the outdoor temperature, the walls and the floor. Ask your company what temperature they can hold at the coldest evening you might face.

### Do I need a permit for heaters in a tent?

Possibly. Chicago's tent permit process considers heating devices in its separation and fire extinguisher rules, and many villages require inspection of heaters. Ask when you apply.

### How much does heating a tent cost?

It depends on tent size, heater type, fuel, power and how many hours you run it. We do not publish rental prices outside chairs, so ask your tent company for a quote with and without heat.
