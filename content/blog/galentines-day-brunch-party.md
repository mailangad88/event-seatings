Galentine's Day is a brunch with your friends the day before Valentine's Day. It is a popular excuse to get a group together in a Chicago winter, when everyone needs something to look forward to. In 2027, February 13 falls on a Saturday, so you do not have to compete with work. The party is easy to plan if you set up the room well and keep the drinks and the craft table apart.

## Pick the room and the time

A late morning start around 11 am works for most groups. It leaves time to set up and keeps the afternoon free. Plan on a two-hour party, plus 30 minutes after for guests who linger.

- **At home.** A dining room or open living area works for 10 to 12 guests. Beyond that, you will need to move furniture.
- **A rented room or hall.** For 15 to 20, a community room, loft or party room gives you more space for stations.
- **Restaurants.** Some private rooms allow outside decor and a craft table. Ask before you plan around it.

Check the door width and the floor before you order tables, and see [brunch rentals for 20 to 50](/blog/brunch-party-rentals-for-20-to-50) for bigger groups.

A widely used rule of thumb is about 24 inches of table width per person. That puts 8 people at an 8-foot banquet table, and 10 at a 72-inch round.

## The mimosa or mocktail bar

Keep the bar on its own table, away from the food and the craft area.

- **Mimosa bar.** Chilled sparkling wine, 2 to 3 juices, fruit and a tray of flutes. Our [champagne flutes vs coupes](/blog/champagne-flutes-vs-coupes) post helps you pick glasses.
- **Mocktail bar.** A pitcher of sparkling water, juices, herbs and a few syrups. See [mocktail bar ideas](/blog/mocktail-bar-ideas).
- **Ice.** Plan for it. See [how much ice for a party](/blog/how-much-ice-for-a-party).

## The craft station

A craft table gives guests something to do while others arrive, and gives you something to take home. Pick one craft and keep supplies simple.

<ol class="flow">
<li><strong>Choose one craft.</strong> Cards, flower arranging, tote bags or candle decorating.</li>
<li><strong>Put it on a table of its own.</strong> A 6-foot banquet table fits 6 people.</li>
<li><strong>Cover the table.</strong> Use paper or a cheap cloth, and keep wet supplies on a tray.</li>
<li><strong>Set supplies in bowls.</strong> One set per two guests keeps things moving.</li>
<li><strong>Give it a time.</strong> Run it for 30 to 40 minutes, then clear it before dessert.</li>
</ol>

## What most hosts find out too late

- **Chairs are the first thing to run short.** Count every guest, plus a few spare for late arrivals. If you are hosting 15 or more, rent chairs and tables so no one sits on a couch alone.
- **Book early for February Saturdays.** Valentine's weekend is busy, and rental companies may have limits on delivery times. Check the date as soon as you set it.
- **Winter changes the delivery.** Snow, salt and ice make the entry slippery. Clear the walk and ask the crew where to stage things.
- **Dishes count.** A brunch for 15 needs plates, forks, cups and napkins for several rounds. See [dinnerware for brunch](/blog/dinnerware-for-brunch-events).

## Frequently asked questions

### When is Galentine's Day?

Galentine's Day is February 13, the day before Valentine's Day. In 2027 it falls on a Saturday, which makes it a good day for a brunch.

### How many tables do I need for 15 friends at a brunch?

Two 8-foot tables seat 16 at about 24 inches per person. You can also use two 60-inch rounds and a small table. Add a separate table for drinks and one for crafts.

### What should I serve at a Galentine's brunch?

Pastries, eggs, fruit, a savory dish and a sweet dessert cover most groups. Add a drink bar with both alcohol and non-alcohol choices. Ask guests about allergies before you plan the menu.

### What craft works best for a group?

Pick something that takes 30 to 40 minutes and needs little cleanup, such as cards, flower arranging or decorating tote bags. Set supplies in bowls so everyone can reach them.

### How do I keep the drink station from crowding?

Put it on its own table away from the food, and set out a few pitchers instead of one. Keep ice in a cooler under the table and refill as needed.

### Can I host a Galentine's brunch in a small apartment?

Yes, if you keep the guest list to 8 to 10 and use a drop-leaf or folding table. For more guests, a rented room gives you space for tables and stations.
