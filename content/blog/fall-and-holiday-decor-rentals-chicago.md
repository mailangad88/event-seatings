Decor for these seasons is mostly about warmth: light, texture and color that make a room feel like a gathering. Here is a plan for choosing seasonal rentals for October through December events, from fall weddings to holiday parties and nonprofit galas.

## What to rent for the season

1. **Lighting.** String lights, lanterns, uplights and candles. Evenings arrive early, so lighting drives the look.
2. **Heating** if outdoors. Ask the rental company and the venue about heater options and rules. See our guide to [winter wedding seating](/blog/winter-wedding-seating) for more cold-weather planning.
3. **Warm seating.** Velvet, wood and cane feel warmer than glossy plastic. Try the [velvet dining chair](/chairs/velvet-dining) or the [cross-back](/chairs/cross-back).
4. **Linens.** Deep colors and textured fabrics. Table runners in velvet or burlap look right in the fall.
5. **Backdrops.** Drape, flower or greenery walls in seasonal colors.
6. **Lounge pieces and throws** for an after-dinner corner.

## Fall wedding decor

Fall weddings lean on harvest colors, candlelight and natural textures. Rust and burgundy linens, wood chairs and mixed greenery set the tone. Use pumpkins sparingly and pair them with candles so the room does not read like a farm stand. Lounge areas with velvet seating feel right in October. See our guide to [barn wedding seating ideas](/blog/barn-wedding-seating-ideas) for rustic layouts.

## Holiday party decor

Corporate and family holiday parties benefit from a clear theme: winter white and silver, red and gold, or evergreen and brass. Rent a backdrop for photos, a tree stand or garland frames if your venue allows. Mirrors and metallic accents reflect light and brighten dark afternoons. Plan the coat check, the entrance mat and the timing of the first guests arriving in snow.

Holiday dates fill early, especially Fridays and Saturdays in December. Book rentals as soon as you have the date and the venue, and see our [FAQ](/faq) for how booking works.

## Weather planning

Chicago weather in October to December can swing from mild to snow in one week. Keep a plan for rain, wind and cold. If your event uses a tent, ask about sidewalls, heaters and flooring. Delivery trucks need clear access, so ask your venue about snow removal. Check the forecast a week out, but keep the plan flexible.

## Decor you can keep simple

If your budget is limited, spend on lighting and chairs and keep the rest minimal. A candle on every table, a seasonal garland on the bar and a good backdrop do more than many small pieces. Remember that real greens and pumpkins need to be sourced from a florist or farm stand, not from a rental company.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Forgetting how early the sun sets.
- Using too many themes at once.
- Open flames where the venue bans them.
- No plan for coats and wet boots.


## Frequently asked questions

### What colors work for fall event decor?

Rust, burgundy, deep green, gold and cream. Use three colors at most and add texture with velvet, wood and candles.

### What decor do I need for a holiday party?

A clear theme, good lighting, a backdrop for photos, warm seating and a coat solution. Metallics and mirrors brighten dark rooms.

### How early does it get dark in Chicago in winter?

Very early, with sunset in the late afternoon in December. Check an official sunset table for your date and plan lighting accordingly.

### Do I need heaters for an outdoor fall event?

Often yes after sundown. Ask the rental company and venue about heater types and rules, and consider a tent with sidewalls.

### When should I book fall and holiday rentals?

As early as you can, because October weekends and December dates fill first. Our booking timeline guide explains the details.

### Can I rent real pumpkins and greenery?

Those usually come from a florist or farm stand. Rental companies focus on furniture, linens, lighting and structures.
