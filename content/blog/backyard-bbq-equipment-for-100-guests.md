**Quick answer**

A backyard BBQ for 100 guests needs two grills or a 48-inch grill, 4 to 5 serving tables, 10 to 12 round tables of 8 (or 13 tables of 8), 100 chairs plus 10 percent extra, a 20 x 40-foot tent for shade, 8 coolers, 4 drink dispensers, 6 to 8 chafing dishes, 6 waste bins and one covered prep area. Plan a yard of at least 3,000 square feet.

</div>

A backyard barbecue for 100 people is a catering job in cargo shorts. The food is simple, but the logistics are the same as a wedding: seats, shade, power, cold storage and a place to put the trash. Most Chicago-area yards are not big enough for all of it. This list covers each category, with counts, and a layout.

## Cooking: grills and food

A typical BBQ menu for 100 includes burgers or chicken, a vegetarian choice, 2 sides and a dessert. A common planning range is about 1/3 to 1/2 pound of cooked meat per guest, so 35 to 50 pounds of meat. At 160 degrees F for ground beef and 165 degrees F for poultry, the cook needs a probe thermometer. Plan resting and holding space: chafers and insulated carriers for cooked meat. See [grill rental](/blog/grill-rental-for-events) and [keeping food hot](/blog/keeping-food-hot-outdoors).

## Cold: coolers, ice and drinks

Plan about 1 pound of ice per guest for drinks, 100 pounds, plus ice for salads and raw meat coolers. Use separate coolers for raw meat, salads and drinks to avoid cross-contamination and constant opening. Add dispensers for water and one or two flavors; see [beverage dispensers](/blog/how-many-beverage-dispensers-do-i-need) and [keeping food cold](/blog/keeping-food-cold-at-outdoor-events). Keep drinks near the seating, not the buffet.

## Shelter and seating

Plan a tent to cover the seating: 100 guests at tables of 8 need about 13 tables, which fits a 40 x 60-foot tent at 10 to 12 square feet per person, or a 20 x 40-foot tent for a standing-and-sitting mix. Consult your tent company; see [what size tent do I need](/blog/what-size-tent-do-i-need). If the yard is small, use a 20 x 40 tent for the buffet and drinks, and set tables on the lawn with market umbrellas. Casual events can use [garden folding chairs](/chairs/garden-folding) and picnic tables. For wind, rain and ground, read [outdoor event chairs and weather in Chicago](/blog/topic/seasons-and-venues).

## Power, lighting and neighbors

Gather your electrical needs: urns, popcorn, lights and music. Ask about circuit capacity and put the grill's work light on its own circuit. See [lighting and power](/blog/topic/lighting-and-power). For parties past dusk, plan string lights and path lights. Notify your neighbors, check your village's noise and street parking rules, and check whether you need a permit for a tent or for closing a street. Rules vary by village; call your village hall.

## Waste and cleanup

Three stations of trash, recycling and compost handle 100 guests; see [trash, recycling and composting stations](/blog/trash-recycling-composting-stations-for-events). Add a grease can for the grill, and a place to store dirty serving ware.

## Food safety

Keep hot food hot and cold food cold, and use the two-hour rule; see [food safety at home parties](/blog/food-safety-at-home-parties).

## Frequently asked questions

### What equipment do I need for a BBQ for 100 people?

Grills, serving tables, chafing dishes, utensils, coolers, drink dispensers, tables, chairs, a tent, trash bins and a prep area. The counts in the master list above are a starting point.

### How much meat for 100 guests?

About 35 to 50 pounds of cooked meat, or one-third to one-half pound per guest, depending on sides and appetites.

### How many chairs for a backyard BBQ?

One seat for each guest, plus 10 percent extra. Casual events can go with 70 to 80 percent if guests will stand and mingle.

### How big a yard do I need?

About 3,000 square feet for a tent, buffet, grill and seating. Smaller yards can use a driveway or a neighbor's lot with permission.

### Do I need a permit for a backyard party?

Maybe, for a large tent, street parking or amplified music. Check with your village hall.

### How early should I order rentals?

3 to 4 months in summer, and earlier for holiday weekends.
