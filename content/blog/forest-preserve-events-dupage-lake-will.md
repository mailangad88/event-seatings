**Quick answer**

Forest preserve rules differ sharply by county. DuPage prohibits alcohol and inflatables and generally allows private canopies up to 200 square feet; Lake County allows alcohol for guests 21 and older but not within 100 feet of parking; Will County allows 10 by 10 tents, one bounce house per permit and alcohol that is served but not sold. Always read the current rules for your exact shelter before ordering rentals.

Forest preserves are among the prettiest places to host a party near Chicago, and they are the places where rental plans most often collide with the rules. A bounce house that is fine at home can be banned. A wedding-style canopy can exceed the size limit. This guide compares what DuPage, Lake and Will County say, and tells you who to call about Cook and Kane.

## DuPage County: no alcohol, no inflatables

DuPage requires a picnic permit for groups over 25 people, and says to apply at least three full business days ahead. It lists alcoholic beverages and inflatables among the prohibited items. Signs and decorations must be free-standing, with nothing taped or tied to trees or shelters. Generators should sit on platforms to protect grass, and musical performers, DJs and microphones fall under special-use rules. Fees are nonrefundable, even for weather. Weddings and ceremonies need a special-use permit requested at least four full calendar weeks ahead.

## Lake County: alcohol with limits

Lake County's FAQ says alcohol is permitted for drinkers 21 and older, but not on or within 100 feet of any parking area. Caterers must be licensed by the district, and food you bring yourself or have a restaurant deliver does not need a licensed vendor if you serve it. The rental fee covers use until sunset, which matters for evening plans. Dunk tanks, petting zoos and pony rides are not allowed. Music rules vary by site.

## Will County: more amenities, with limits

Will County lists approved amenities for a shelter permit: extra tables and chairs near the shelter, up to two generators, one portable latrine, a food or beverage truck parked in the lot and one bounce house per permit from a district-approved vendor. Tents larger than 20 by 20 feet are prohibited. Amplified music and DJs are limited to eight specific shelters.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I serve alcohol at a DuPage County forest preserve?

DuPage's picnicking page lists alcoholic beverages as prohibited. Check its current rules before you plan.

### Can I have alcohol at a Lake County forest preserve shelter?

Lake County's FAQ says yes for drinkers 21 and older, but not on or within 100 feet of a parking area.

### Can I rent a bounce house at a forest preserve?

DuPage prohibits inflatables. Will County allows one per permit from an approved vendor. Lake County lists dunk tanks and pony rides as prohibited. Always check your district.

### How big a tent can I bring to a forest preserve?

It depends. DuPage generally allows private canopies up to 200 square feet; Will County approves 10 by 10 tents and prohibits tents larger than 20 by 20.

### Do I need a permit for a forest preserve picnic?

DuPage requires a permit for groups over 25 people. Other districts have their own thresholds. Check yours.

### Can I hold a wedding at a forest preserve?

Often with a special-use permit and extra lead time. DuPage asks for requests at least four full calendar weeks ahead.

### Can caterers serve at a forest preserve shelter?

Rules differ: DuPage allows caterers serving within the reserved area but not food trucks; Lake County requires licensed caterers; Will County allows one food truck per permit.
