Commencement is one of the biggest single seating jobs a school runs all year. Hundreds or thousands of chairs go onto a field in one morning, stay out in the sun or wind for an afternoon, and come off again before the field is needed. The ceremony only works if the count is right and the rows are set the way the graduates will walk.

## How to count every chair

Count each group separately. The total is the sum, plus a small buffer for a few late changes.

<div class="table-wrap">

| Group | How to count | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Graduates | Final graduate list | Lock this number with the registrar or counselors |
| Faculty and staff | Everyone who processes | Often a row facing the graduates |
| Platform party | Speakers, board, administrators | Usually on the stage itself |
| Guests on the field | Tickets per graduate × graduates | Subtract guests seated in the bleachers |
| Accessible seating | Guests who need it, plus companions | Keep it on level, firm ground near an entrance |
| Performers | Band, choir, color guard | Only if they sit during the ceremony |
| Buffer | A few extra chairs | For last-minute changes on the day |

</div>

Many schools limit guest tickets per graduate, and some seat all guests in the stands. The ticket policy is what really sets the guest number, so confirm it before you request a quote. The [rental order calculator](/downloads/rental-order-calculator) helps you total each group.

## How to lay out the field

<figcaption>A common field layout. Your stadium, stage position and ticket policy will change the details.</figcaption>
</figure>

1. **Stage at one end** of the field, facing the home stands so guests in the bleachers can see.
2. **Faculty row** in front of the stage, facing the graduates or the stage.
3. **Graduates in the center**, in rows set to match the order names are read, with a center aisle for the procession.
4. **Guest chairs on both sides** of the graduates, with clear aisles to the exits.
5. **Accessible seating** on level, firm ground close to an entrance, with companion chairs beside each spot.

Leave wide cross aisles. Graduates need to move row by row to the stage and back without climbing over anyone, and guests need clear paths for an emergency.

## Protecting the turf and the track

Most Chicago-area high school fields are artificial turf with a running track around them. Before you order, ask your athletic director:

- **Can chairs sit directly on the turf?** Some schools allow it, others ask for floor protection under the stage or in high-traffic areas.
- **How will delivery trucks reach the field?** Many tracks can't take a loaded truck, so chairs often come in on carts through a gate.
- **When is the field free?** Spring sports, practices and the stage build all compete for the same day or two.

On natural grass, ask about soft ground after rain. Our guide to [chairs on grass, sand and gravel](/blog/chairs-on-grass-sand-gravel) covers how to keep rows level.

## The rain plan

Most schools move commencement to the gym or fieldhouse if the weather turns. That changes the count, since indoor capacity is smaller and guest tickets are often cut. Decide three things before you order:

- Who makes the weather call, and by what time
- Whether the field chairs move indoors or the gym uses its own seating
- What the rental company needs to know if the set changes on the day

The [outdoor event weather plan](/downloads/outdoor-event-weather-plan) is a good template for writing this down.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many chairs do I need for a graduation ceremony?
Add one chair for each graduate, faculty member and platform guest, then add guest chairs for the tickets given to each family if guests sit on the field. Add accessible seating with companion chairs and a small buffer. Subtract any guests who will sit in the bleachers.

### Can rental chairs go on an artificial turf football field?
Often yes, but every school sets its own rules. Ask the athletic director whether chairs can sit directly on the turf, whether floor protection is needed under the stage, and how delivery carts can reach the field without damaging the track.

### How should graduation chairs be arranged?
Set the graduates in straight rows that match the order names will be read, with a center aisle for the procession. Put faculty in front, guests on both sides and accessible seating on level ground near an entrance, with wide aisles to the exits.

### When should a school book chairs for a spring commencement?
Book as soon as the ceremony date and location are set, ideally several months ahead. Late May and early June are busy weeks for rentals in the Chicago area, with many graduations and weddings on the same weekends.
