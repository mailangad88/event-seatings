**Quick answer**

A First Communion party is usually held on the same weekend as the church service, in spring, and often in a backyard, hall or restaurant. Plan one chair per guest, tables of 8 or 10 or long family tables, a separate kids table, a cake and treats table, a gift and card box and shade or a tent for the Chicago spring weather. For 60 guests, that means about 6 rounds of 10 or 8 six-foot tables, plus a small table for the cake and a card box.

</div>

First Communion is a milestone in many Catholic and other Christian communities. In Chicago and the suburbs, families usually hold the celebration on a spring Saturday or Sunday after the church service, with relatives, godparents and the child's classmates. Many parties are in backyards, which makes spring weather the biggest planning issue. Rain, wind and chilly mornings are common in April and May, so plan cover and flexibility.

## Plan around the weather

Spring weather in the Chicago area swings widely, so build a plan for sun, rain and cold:

- **A tent or canopy** for shade and rain cover. See [what size tent do I need](/blog/what-size-tent-do-i-need) and [pop-up canopy vs rented tent](/blog/pop-up-canopy-vs-rented-tent).
- **Heaters** for cool mornings and evenings. See [patio heaters](/blog/patio-heaters-how-many-per-guests).
- **A hall or garage fallback** if rain is likely.
- **Check your village's tent rules.** See [tent permits in the suburbs](/blog/tent-permit-chicago-suburbs).

## Seating for the honored child

Many families seat the child and parents at a decorated family table, or give the child a special chair for photos. A [velvet dining chair](/chairs/velvet-dining) ($18 to $25 estimated) in a soft color or a [Windsor spindle chair](/chairs/windsor) ($9 to $12) in white fits the day. For guests, [garden folding chairs](/chairs/garden-folding) ($5 to $7) are easy to set on grass with chair feet protectors. See [chairs on grass, sand and gravel](/blog/chairs-on-grass-sand-gravel).

## The cake, candy and photo corner

A 6-foot table with a floor-length cloth holds a cake, cupcakes or a candy display. Place it in the shade, away from wind, and keep it close enough to the tent or house to carry things quickly. A simple arch, balloons or a sign adds a photo spot. ## Setting up around the church service

Because the party usually begins right after Mass, the setup has to be done before the family leaves for church. Ask the rental company for an early delivery window, and have a relative or neighbor receive the order. Lay out the tables the night before if the weather allows, and keep chairs stacked until the morning so dew and dust do not soil them. Put linens on at the last minute, and weigh down cloths with clips if wind is expected.

Plan the flow of the afternoon: arrivals and greetings, a blessing or toast, lunch, cake and photos, then games for the kids. Put games and the kids table on one side of the yard and the adult tables on the other, with the treats table between them. A shaded spot with chairs near the kids lets grandparents watch without standing.

## Chicago notes

- April and May weekends are busy for First Communions, and rental stock goes quickly. Book early. See when to book.
- Delivery windows are tight if the party starts right after church. Ask for a delivery time the evening before or early that morning.
- Ask your HOA or neighbors about a tent, parking and noise before the day.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many guests come to a First Communion party?

Parties range from 30 to 150, depending on family size and tradition. Count godparents, relatives, family friends and classmates.

### How many tables do I need for 60 guests?

About 6 rounds of 10 or 7 to 8 six-foot tables, plus a kids table, a cake table and a gift table.

### Do I need a tent?

Not always, but spring weather in Chicago is unpredictable. A tent or large umbrellas protect against rain and sun. Check your village's rules for tents.

### What size should the kids table be?

A 6-foot banquet table seats 6 kids on chairs. Use lower chairs and tables for young children if you can.

### Where should the cake go?

In the shade, away from the grill, with a clear path. A floor-length cloth hides storage underneath.

### Can I rent for a parish hall party?

Yes, but ask the parish what is included, whether outside rentals are allowed and what the setup times are.
