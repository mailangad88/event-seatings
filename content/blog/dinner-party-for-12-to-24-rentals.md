**Quick answer**

For 12 to 24 guests, set one long table made of 6 or 8-foot banquet tables, or two to three 60-inch rounds. Allow 24 inches of table length per person, so a single long table seating 16 guests, 8 on each side, needs about 16 feet of length. Rent one chair per guest, floor-length or runner linens, and a full place setting for each course. Add 10 percent to place settings for breakage.

A dinner for 12 to 24 is the sweet spot where your own dining table runs out and a banquet hall is overkill. The room is probably a dining room, a living room cleared of furniture or a covered patio. What you rent depends on how the guests are seated and how many courses you serve.

## How much table you need

Plan 24 inches of table length per seated guest, and 30 inches for a more formal dinner. A 6-foot banquet table seats 6 on two sides, an 8-foot seats 8. For a 16-guest long table, join two 8-foot tables end to end, giving 16 feet of length with 8 on each side. See [banquet table sizes](/blog/banquet-table-sizes-6-ft-vs-8-ft) for dimensions.

Allow 48 inches behind each chair so a server can pass. If your room is tight, set seating on one side only, and put the hosts at the end.

## Pick chairs that go with a long dinner

A dinner sits for two to three hours, so comfort beats everything. [Cross-back chairs](/chairs/cross-back) (estimated $8 to $11) have a sturdy back and a padded option. [Velvet dining chairs](/chairs/velvet-dining) ($18 to $25) feel like a restaurant. [Bentwood chairs](/chairs/bentwood) are light and easy to move. Mix two styles for a stylish look: use velvet at the two ends and cross-back along the sides.

## Set each place

For a three-course meal, each place needs:

- A charger or dinner plate, a salad or soup plate, and a bread plate
- A dinner fork, salad fork, dinner knife, soup spoon and dessert spoon
- A water glass, a wine glass and, if you like, a second wine glass
- A cloth napkin, 20 x 20 inches

The [formal place setting diagram](/blog/place-setting-diagram-formal-dinner) shows spacing and order, and [how many forks, knives and spoons per guest](/blog/how-many-forks-knives-spoons-per-guest) gives the counts. ## Chicago-area notes

Chicago dining rooms and bungalows often fit 12 comfortably, but 24 means using a living room or a sunroom, or a tent in the yard. Measure the room before you order, including doorways, since 8-foot tables need a doorway of 36 inches or a diagonal tilt to pass. Plan for coats in winter and a place to hold deliveries. For a covered backyard dinner, see [how to size a tent](/blog/what-size-tent-do-i-need).

## Mistakes to avoid

- **Tables that are too tight.** A table that fits the room but leaves 20 inches behind chairs traps guests in their seats. Measure the room, then subtract 48 inches behind each chair.
- **A wobble at the join.** When you join two banquet tables, check that the tops are level. A quick fix is a folded card under a short leg. Cover the seam with a runner or a long cloth.
- **Candles at eye level.** Tall tapers block faces across a long table. Keep them under 3 inches or above 14 inches.
- **Too many courses for the crew.** If you have no help in the kitchen, a three-course meal for 24 is a lot. Plate the starter in advance, serve the main family style, and set out dessert on a table.
- **Forgetting the other furniture.** Move the sofa, the rug and any coffee table before the table arrives, not after.

Take a photo of the room from the door and sketch the layout on top of it. It is the quickest way to see whether the plan works.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many people fit at an 8-foot table?

Eight comfortably, four per side, or ten with two at the ends. For a formal dinner, stick with eight.

### How long should a dinner table be for 16 people?

About 16 feet if seated on both sides, joining two 8-foot tables. If you seat on one side only, double that.

### Round or long tables for a dinner party?

Long for a single conversation, up to 20 guests. Round tables mix people better at 16 to 24 guests. A mix works too.

### How many place settings should I rent?

One per guest plus 10 percent extra, rounded up. For 16 guests, 18 place settings.

### How far ahead should I book?

Three to four weeks for most weekends, longer for November and December. Holiday weekends book first.
