In Batavia, the Batavia Park District manages the Batavia Riverwalk and its parks, so reserve park space through the district. Batavia calls itself the Windmill City, and the Fox River runs through the middle of it. A paved Riverwalk, historic limestone buildings and a compact downtown make it easy to host a small wedding or a community party. This guide covers the kinds of spaces here, the questions to ask, and how to plan a rental list for a Batavia date.

## The Riverwalk and the Batavia Park District

The Batavia Park District runs the Batavia Riverwalk, a landscaped stretch along the Fox River that hosts events through the year, including the Windmill City Festival in summer and the Celebration of Lights at the start of the holiday season. If you want to use any part of the Riverwalk or the district's other parks, start with the district. Ask what is reservable, how many guests it allows, and whether tents or amplified sound are permitted.

## Space types in Batavia

- **Riverwalk and riverfront parks.** Public space, public rules. Expect to reserve through the park district, and expect that nearby events may be going on.
- **Downtown streets and storefronts.** Anything on a street or sidewalk is a City matter. Ask City Hall about timing and insurance.
- **Banquet rooms, restaurants and historic buildings.** Indoor spaces, usually with their own chairs and tables. Ask exactly what is included.
- **Private backyards.** Many Batavia homes have deep lots near the river and on the west side. You skip park reservations, but tents and parking still follow local rules.
- **Farm and barn venues on the edge of town.** Kane County has several. Confirm access roads, flooring and power.

## Questions to ask before you reserve

Because we cannot see Batavia's current forms, these are the questions that matter in any town:

1. Is the space owned by the park district, the city or someone else?
2. What is the capacity, and is there a tent or alcohol rule?
3. Where does the truck unload, and how far is the carry?
4. What is the rain plan?

For tents specifically, read our [tent permit guide for Chicago suburbs](/blog/tent-permit-chicago-suburbs).

Batavia is one of the shortest trips from our St. Charles base, a few miles down the Fox River corridor. That makes same-weekend add-ons realistic. The key is the carry: Riverwalk paths are not designed for loaded trucks, so ask the district where a vehicle may stop and whether you may need to hand-carry to the event area. ## Seasons in Batavia

Summer weekends are crowded. The Windmill City Festival and other events add traffic, so check the Park District calendar before you pick a date. Fall is the best bet for outdoor weddings. In winter, the Celebration of Lights marks the start of the holiday season, and you will see families downtown. Sunset is near 4:20 pm in December, so plan indoor lighting.

## Planning a rental list for a Batavia event

Batavia's mix of historic downtown buildings and river parks means most events are small to medium, so the rental list is usually shorter than a suburban banquet hall's.

- **Seating:** one chair per guest, plus a few spares. For a ceremony facing the river, rows of 8 to 10 seats with a center aisle of 4 to 5 feet is a common layout.
- **Tables:** rounds of 8 for dinner; a long banquet table or two for food; a cocktail table or two near the entrance.
- **Linens and napkins:** a floor-length cloth on guest tables hides legs and cords. Our [tablecloth size chart](/blog/tablecloth-size-chart) lists the standard sizes.
- **Weather plan:** a tent or an indoor fallback. If the Riverwalk is the venue, ask the district first, since not every public space allows tents.

Because Batavia is close to our base in Chicago's western suburbs, small changes the week before the event are usually easy to handle. Call early, though, since summer weekends fill up.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who manages the Batavia Riverwalk?

The Batavia Park District manages the Batavia Riverwalk. Contact the district about reservations, capacity and any rules for tents, power or amplified sound.

### Can I hold a wedding on the Batavia Riverwalk?

Ask the Batavia Park District. Public park spaces can have reservation rules and may be shared with the public, so confirm what is allowed for ceremonies and chairs.

### Do I need City permission for a Batavia street event?

Yes, anything that uses a street or sidewalk is a City matter. Call City Hall for current forms and timing.

### When is Batavia busiest for events?

Summer weekends, when festivals like the Windmill City Festival draw crowds. Check the Park District calendar before you pick a date.

### Do you deliver in Batavia?

Yes. Batavia is one of the closest towns to our St. Charles base. First deliveries begin with the April 2027 Founding Season.

### What chairs are best on a Riverwalk lawn?

Lightweight chairs that do not sink are best. Garden folding and cross-back chairs both work, and flooring helps if the ground is damp.
