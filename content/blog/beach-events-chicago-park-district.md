A Chicago beach wedding or party is a great picture: sand, skyline, Lake Michigan. It is also one of the most regulated places to host an event. This guide uses the Chicago Park District's own pages to explain the main rules, then shows what to rent and how to plan around the sand.

## What the Park District says

The Chicago Park District's page on weddings and ceremony permits says outdoor ceremonies, including weddings, baptisms, dedications and memorials, need a Special Event Permit "regardless of location and number of people." Permits cover a designated space for a two- or four-hour window. They do not allow food or beverage service of any kind. Permits are not issued on holidays, and at beaches, summer events must end before 11 a.m. Receptions need a Special Event Venue booking. Engagement or wedding photography needs a separate Filming & Photography Permit.

Fees and rules can change, and the Park District is the authority. Confirm on its website or by phone before you make plans.

## Beach event guidelines

The Park District also publishes Beach Event Guidelines for larger events. Among the rules: only approved vehicles may drive on sand, and only for setup, teardown and event management; grills and open flames are prohibited on sand; smoking is prohibited on all beaches; animals are not allowed except in designated dog areas, with service animals always welcome; speakers must face the lake, away from residences; and participants entering the water require Park District lifeguards. Portable toilets and catering vehicles must be on pavement in pre-approved locations.

## Sand changes the rental plan

Sand is soft, shifts under weight and makes wheeled carts hard to push. Chair legs sink. Ask the Park District about approved ways to place flooring or runners on sand, and plan extra time for crews to carry items from a paved area. Wind is the other big factor: lakefront breezes lift lightweight chairs, signs and fabric. Choose heavier chairs, weights for linens and low decor.

- **Chairs**: wide-footed styles help on sand. Ceremony-only events only need chairs, not tables. See [chairs on grass, sand and gravel](/blog/chairs-on-grass-sand-gravel). The [garden folding chair](/chairs/garden-folding) (about $5 to $7) or [cross-back](/chairs/cross-back) (about $8 to $11) are options.
- **Equipment**: the Park District's page says chairs and trellises can be contracted through its Preferred Professionals. Ask whether outside rentals are allowed at your beach, and who can bring them.
- **Aisle**: a runner on sand needs anchoring. See [aisle runner length and material](/blog/aisle-runner-length-and-material-guide).
- **Sound**: speakers must face the lake.
- **Shade and wind**: tents and canopies are permit features.

## A reception somewhere else

Because food and drink are not allowed with a ceremony permit, most beach ceremonies are followed by a reception at a separate venue. Plan transport and timing, and consider how chairs and decor move. Our guide to [ceremony vs reception chairs](/blog/ceremony-vs-reception-chairs) explains whether to rent one set or two. Plan for a morning ceremony, since summer beach events must end before 11 a.m.

## Larger events and company outings

Larger beach events need Special Event Permits with site plans, and the Park District's guidelines mention beach cleaning, volleyball court rentals and lifeguards for water activities. Tents and canopies are a permit feature; tents larger than 20 by 20 feet need a separate City of Chicago permit application. Allow plenty of time, since the Park District says review takes at least 14 days and its 2027 application opens November 6, 2026.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a permit for a beach wedding in Chicago?

Yes. The Chicago Park District says outdoor ceremonies need a Special Event Permit regardless of location and number of people.

### Can I serve food or drinks at a Chicago beach ceremony?

Not under a ceremony permit. The Park District says no food or beverage service is allowed. Plan a reception at a separate venue.

### What time must a Chicago beach event end?

The Park District says summer events at beaches must end before 11 a.m.

### Can I bring chairs to the beach?

The Park District says chairs and trellises can be contracted through its Preferred Professionals. Ask whether outside rentals are allowed for your permit.

### Are dogs and grills allowed at an event on a Chicago beach?

No. Grills and open flames are prohibited on sand, and animals are not allowed except in designated dog areas, with service animals always allowed.

### Are tents allowed on the beach?

Tents and canopies are a permit feature, and tents larger than 20 by 20 feet need a separate City permit application.

### Are there beaches managed independently?

The Park District lists Millennium Park, Lincoln Park Zoo, Maggie Daley Park, the Lily Pool and Olive Park as independently managed, so call them directly.
