**Quick answer**

King and queen chairs are two oversized statement chairs for the couple, usually at the sweetheart table or ceremony. You can use a royal throne pair (an estimated $75 to $150 per pair), two Louis medallion chairs ($20 to $30 each) or two velvet dining chairs ($18 to $25 each). The right choice depends on your room size, your palette and how tall the chairs need to stand against the backdrop.

"King and queen chairs" is a search phrase, not a product line. Couples type it when they want to be seated differently from everyone else, with a chair that looks like it belongs to the royal court. There are several ways to get that look, and they are not equal in cost or fit. This guide compares the three realistic options for a Chicago wedding, shows how to space the pair at a table and explains how to style them so they photograph well.

A **throne** is the most dramatic. It has a high carved back and wide arms and works when the couple sits on a stage or in front of a backdrop. A **Louis chair** is the elegant middle: upholstered, oval and formal, but fitting at a standard table. **Velvet** is the softest, in colors that echo the room.

## Make the pair look like a pair

The royal feel comes from contrast: the couple's chairs should be clearly different from the guest chairs, and clearly matched to each other. Use identical finishes for both, even if you add signs or flowers to personalize them.

Keep the table narrow, 24 to 30 inches deep, so the couple can see and talk to each other. A narrow table also leaves space for the chairs' arms, which are wider than a normal chair.

## Where to place them

1. **At the sweetheart table.** The classic. See [sweetheart table chair ideas](/blog/sweetheart-table-chair-ideas) for alternatives to a table.
2. **On a ceremony platform.** The pair sits during a reading or a cultural ritual. If it is a South Asian wedding, see [South Asian wedding seating](/blog/south-asian-wedding-seating).
3. **In the photo zone.** A throne pair stays as a prop for portraits, away from the table.
4. **At the head table.** Use matching statement chairs for the couple and a different chair for the wedding party; [Louis chairs for head tables](/blog/louis-chairs-head-table) shows the spacing.

## Signs and decor

"Mr", "Mrs", "King" and "Queen" signs go on the chair backs or on small easels beside them. Keep the sign small, because an ornate chair already has plenty going on. Our [Mr and Mrs chair signs](/blog/mr-and-mrs-chair-signs) guide covers sizes, materials and what to avoid. For florals, a single garland along the table front is enough.

## Space, doorways and delivery

Statement chairs are large. Before you order, check:

- **Doorway and elevator width** for the delivery route.
- **Stage or riser size** if the chairs go on a platform.
- **Floor type.** Heavy chairs on a polished dance floor need felt pads, and on carpet they can tilt.
- **Indoor only.** Our throne, Louis and velvet chairs are all indoor chairs in the catalog.

Ask the venue where the chairs can wait before the reception, and who moves them if the couple dances and returns.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are king and queen chairs?

They are two matching oversized chairs for the couple, often with carved or upholstered backs. They make the couple's seats clearly different from the guests'.

### How much does it cost to rent king and queen chairs in Chicago?

Our estimates are $75 to $150 per pair for a throne, $20 to $30 each for Louis chairs and $18 to $25 each for velvet chairs. The final quote includes delivery and setup.

### Can king and queen chairs go outside?

Not the ones in our catalog. They are indoor pieces. Use a covered, floored space if the event is partly outdoors.

### Where should the couple's chairs go at the reception?

At a sweetheart table, a head table or a stage. Pick the one that fits your room and the way you want to be seen.

### Do I need a throne, or are two nice chairs enough?

Two good chairs are enough for most weddings. A throne is for couples who want a dramatic photo moment or have a cultural ceremony that uses a platform.

### What color should king and queen chairs be?

Match your palette, but keep a contrast with the guest chairs. Gold and ivory fits warm weddings, silver and white fits winter ones and navy or emerald velvet fits moody ones.

### How big are king and queen chairs?

They are larger than a standard dining chair. Ask for exact dimensions in your quote, especially if doorways or a stage are tight.
