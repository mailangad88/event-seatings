Burr Ridge is a southwest suburb of large lots, wooded roads and private clubs. Many weddings, graduations and fundraisers here take place in backyards and tents, not park pavilions. That makes the planning list different: more about ground, access and utilities, less about reservations.

## The village permit page

The Village of Burr Ridge publishes a Special Event Permit page under its permits and licenses section. Read it before you plan an event with a large guest count, amplified sound, a tent or street impact. The page is the village's own word on what needs a permit, so it beats any guess.

## Space types in Burr Ridge

- **Private estates and large backyards.** The most common choice for weddings and graduations.
- **Clubs and golf courses nearby.** Private venues with their own banquet spaces. Ask each what is included.
- **Restaurants and banquet halls.** Along the major roads.
- **Churches and community rooms.** For showers and smaller gatherings.
- **Village-owned spaces.** Ask the village what is available.

## Estate tents: ground, power and access

A backyard tent is more than a roof. Walk the site and ask: Is the ground level? Where are sprinkler lines and septic fields? How many outlets are within reach, and is a generator needed? Can a truck get close, or is it a 150-foot carry? A rental team can plan around almost anything, but only if you know it ahead of time. ## Seasonal notes

Spring weddings are popular and grounds can be soft. Summer is warm, with sunset near 8:30 pm at the June solstice. Fall is the best bet for dry ground. Winter events call for a heated tent or a club ballroom.

## A Burr Ridge example

A 140-guest tented wedding might use 18 rounds of 8, a dance floor, a bar and lounge seating. [Ghost chairs](/chairs/ghost) (about $10-14 each, an estimate) suit a tent with lighting, and [velvet chairs](/chairs/velvet-dining) (about $18-25) work for a head table.

## Being a good neighbor

On large lots, sound still carries. Tell neighbors about the date and hours, and place speakers so they point away from the nearest house. Hire a parking attendant or arrange a shuttle if guests cannot park on the street. If you host from Chicago or another suburb, ask the village for its end-time rules for amplified music, and plan the DJ's last song accordingly.

## Questions to ask before you book a tent

A tent is the biggest single decision at an estate event, so ask these questions in order.

1. **How many guests will be under it?** Count seats, the dance floor, the bar and the buffet. See [what size tent do I need](/blog/what-size-tent-do-i-need).
2. **What is the ground?** Grass, slope, gravel or a patio. Each changes flooring and anchoring. See [tent anchoring](/blog/tent-anchoring-stakes-vs-ballast).
3. **What is the power plan?** Lighting, a DJ, heaters and a caterer all pull power.
4. **What is the permit plan?** Ask the village. Our [tent permit guide](/blog/tent-permit-chicago-suburbs) lists the usual questions.
5. **What is the weather plan?** Sidewalls, heaters and fans extend the season.

Answer these before you order chairs, because the tent shapes the layout. A rectangular tent seats tables differently than a round one, and a dance floor may take a corner. Bring a sketch of the yard to your first call and mark trees, slopes and the house.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Burr Ridge require a special event permit?

The village publishes a Special Event Permit page. Read it and confirm with staff whether your event needs a permit.

### Can I have a tent at my Burr Ridge home?

Often yes, but ask the village about permits and size limits. A rental planner can help with the tent size.

### Which counties is Burr Ridge in?

Burr Ridge sits in both Cook and DuPage counties, near Will County. Rules can differ by parcel.

### Is there a park district?

Ask the village who manages local parks and shelters, since that is the quickest way to find the right office for a park reservation.

### Do you deliver in Burr Ridge?

Yes. First deliveries begin with the April 2027 Founding Season. Tell us about gates and long driveways.

### What chairs suit a tented wedding?

Ghost chairs and Louis chairs look elegant under tent lighting. Cross-back chairs look natural.
