**Quick answer**

Mr and Mrs chair signs are small boards, usually 8 to 12 inches wide, that hang on or lean against the couple's chairs. Pick a lightweight material (wood, acrylic or paper), attach with a ribbon or a clip that does not mark the chair, and match the sign to the chair style. They work best on the sweetheart table, and they take a few minutes to set up.

</div>

His and hers signs are a small piece of decor that appears in nearly every wedding photo. They tell guests where to look, and they are a simple way to personalize a pair of rented chairs. But they have a few practical rules: size, weight, attachment and wording. This guide covers each, with chair-by-chair advice for the Chicago couple who wants the sign to look good without damaging a rental.

- **Small (about 8 x 5 inches):** good for slim backs such as the infinity or cross-back.
- **Medium (about 10 x 6 inches):** the most flexible size for upholstered and wood chairs.
- **Large (about 12 x 8 inches):** for large throne or high-back chairs only. On a smaller chair it dominates.

These sizes are typical of what sellers list, not a standard. Measure the chair back in your quote or ask us for dimensions.

## Materials

<div class="table-wrap">

| Material | Weight | Best for | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wood | Medium | Rustic, cross-back, Windsor | Heavy on thin backs |
| Acrylic | Light | Ghost, infinity, modern | Fingerprints, glare in photos |
| Paper or card | Very light | Short events, budgets | Wilts in damp or wind |
| Fabric banner | Very light | Throne, velvet | Wrinkles |
| Burlap or linen | Light | Boho, garden | Fraying |

</div>

A heavy sign on a slim chair back can tilt the chair, so choose light materials for lightweight chairs.

## Wording ideas

- **Classic:** Mr, Mrs, Bride, Groom.
- **Names:** First names, or last name and date.
- **Same-sex couples:** Mr and Mr, Mrs and Mrs, or the names.
- **Playful:** King and Queen, The Boss, The Other Half.
- **Cultural:** Names in your family's language, or a blessing.

Keep the type large enough to read from 6 to 8 feet away. A simple script on a plain background reads better in photos than a busy design. See [king and queen chairs](/blog/king-and-queen-chairs-wedding) for pairs of statement chairs.

## Attaching signs to rental chairs

The most important rule: never glue, staple or nail anything to a rental chair. Use one of these:

1. **Ribbon loop** over the top rail of the chair, tied behind.
2. **Soft clip** or clothespin on a wire frame, with a piece of felt between metal and finish.
3. **Easel stand** on the table in front of the chair, leaving the chair untouched.
4. **Hook sign** designed to hang over the top rail.

Check the chair back first. Velvet and upholstered backs mark easily, so use an easel stand for those. Carved or woven backs like the [cane-back](/chairs/cane-back) have no flat surface, so a stand is better than a hanging sign.

## Frequently asked questions

### What size should Mr and Mrs chair signs be?

About 8 to 12 inches wide. Ten inches is a safe choice for most chair backs.

### How do I attach a sign to a rental chair?

Use a ribbon loop or a soft clip over the top rail, or put the sign on an easel on the table. Do not use glue, staples or tape on the finish.

### Can I put signs on ghost chairs?

Yes, with a light acrylic or paper sign tied by ribbon. Avoid heavy wood, which can tilt a clear chair.

### Do I need signs on every guest chair?

No. Signs are for the couple, and sometimes the wedding party or reserved family seats.

### Where do the signs go if the chairs have no flat back?

Use an easel on the table or a small stand beside the chair. Carved or woven backs are not good places for hanging signs.

### Are chair signs safe for upholstered chairs?

They are, if the sign is light and has no sharp edges. Use a ribbon hung loosely or an easel, and keep wire away from the fabric.

### Should I buy or make the signs?

Either. A handmade sign is inexpensive and easy to adapt. Buy one if you want a specific material such as engraved wood or acrylic.
