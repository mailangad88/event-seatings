**Quick answer**

Forest preserve districts around Chicago, including Cook, DuPage, Kane, Lake and Will counties, rent picnic groves and shelters for group events, usually by permit. Before you book, check the group size that triggers a permit, whether tents, inflatables, amplified sound and alcohol are allowed, where vehicles can park and unload, and what restrooms exist. Rules differ by district and by site, so read the permit terms before you order rentals.

</div>

Forest preserves are some of the best-value event spaces in the Chicago area: big lawns, shade trees, shelters and parking, often at a fraction of a venue's cost. They work beautifully for family reunions, company picnics, birthday parties, church picnics and community events. The trade-off is that you bring almost everything, under the district's rules.

## Who runs the preserves

Each county has its own forest preserve district with its own permits and rules:

- **Forest Preserves of Cook County**, covering Chicago and suburban Cook County.
- **Forest Preserve District of DuPage County**, including sites near [Wheaton](/chair-rentals/wheaton), [Glen Ellyn](/chair-rentals/glen-ellyn) and [Naperville](/chair-rentals/naperville).
- **Forest Preserve District of Kane County**, including sites around [St. Charles](/chair-rentals/st-charles), [Geneva](/chair-rentals/geneva) and [Batavia](/chair-rentals/batavia), such as Fabyan Forest Preserve.
- **Lake County Forest Preserves**, in the north suburbs.
- **Forest Preserve District of Will County**, in the southwest suburbs.

City parks and suburban park districts are separate systems with their own permits. See our guides to the [Chicago Park District event permit](/blog/chicago-park-district-event-permit) and [suburban park district pavilion rentals](/blog/suburban-park-district-pavilion-rentals).

## Grove, shelter or open lawn

Most districts offer a mix of picnic groves (an open area with some picnic tables), shelters (a roofed structure, sometimes with electricity) and larger areas for big groups. A shelter gives you a built-in rain plan for part of the group. An open grove gives you room for a tent, games and more tables. Ask for the number of picnic tables already on site, because it decides how many you rent.

## The rules to check before you rent

<div class="table-wrap">

| Rule | Why it affects rentals |
|---|---|
| Group size and permit | A permit sets your area, date and hours. |
| Tents | Some sites allow tents with approval; staking may be limited. |
| Vehicles on grass | Usually not allowed, so rentals are carried from the lot. |
| Alcohol | Rules differ by district; some require a permit, some ban it. |
| Amplified sound | Often limited or needs approval. |
| Inflatables and generators | Often need approval or are restricted. |
| Restrooms | Some sites have flush restrooms, some have only basic facilities. |
| Cleanup and hours | Trash, decorations and your end time. |

</div>

Because vehicles usually stay off the grass, the distance from the lot to your area matters. Tell your rental team how far it is, and choose a spot near the lot if you are bringing heavy items. If tent stakes are allowed, Illinois law requires a utility locate before digging; see [JULIE utility locates for tents](/blog/julie-utility-locate-tent-illinois). If staking is not allowed, ask about ballast; see [stakes vs ballast](/blog/tent-anchoring-stakes-vs-ballast).

## Seasons

Forest preserve events run mainly from May to September, with June to August the busiest for reunions and company picnics. Popular groves book early in the year, often as soon as reservations open. Watch for heat, storms and mosquitoes in summer; our [July](/blog/planning-an-event-in-chicago-in-july) and [August](/blog/planning-an-event-in-chicago-in-august) guides cover the weather and calendar.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a permit for a picnic at a forest preserve near Chicago?

For organized group events, usually yes. Each county district sets the group size that requires a permit and the fees. Check the district's website for the site you want before inviting guests.

### Can I put up a tent in a forest preserve?

Sometimes. Many districts allow tents only with approval, and some limit staking. Ask the district, and if staking is allowed, arrange a JULIE utility locate before the tent goes up.

### Is alcohol allowed at forest preserve picnics?

It depends on the district and the site. Some districts require a separate permit, and some do not allow alcohol at all. Check the current rules for your county.

### Can rental trucks drive onto the grass at a forest preserve?

Usually not. Vehicles typically stay on roads and in lots, so rentals are carried to your area. Pick a site close to the lot if you are renting tables, a tent or heavy equipment.

### What is the best month for a forest preserve picnic near Chicago?

June and September are comfortable, with less heat than July and August. Book early, because popular groves fill as soon as reservations open.
