Navy Pier sits right on Lake Michigan and draws big crowds every season, which makes it a popular pick for weddings, galas and company parties. The pier has a few different event spaces, each with its own house rules on setup, vendors and timing. This guide walks through what to expect so your seating and tables show up on time and in the right spot.

## Know your space on the pier

The pier's event venues range from a formal ballroom to a glass-walled garden space, and each one handles furniture and logistics a little differently.

## Wind, weather and the lake

Any outdoor or semi-outdoor space at Navy Pier sits close to open water, so lake wind is a real factor for tents, linens and signage. If your event includes an outdoor component, plan for gusts and have a backup for anything that can blow over. Our [lakefront wind guide](/blog/chicago-lakefront-event-wind-planning) covers what to plan around, and our [winter event planning guide](/blog/chicago-winter-event-planning) helps if you're booking a cold-season date.

## Dock access and loading

Navy Pier has its own service routes for deliveries, separate from guest entrances. Your venue contact will give you a load-in window and tell you where trucks can park to unload. We coordinate directly with that window so chairs, tables and linens arrive and get set up before guests do. If your event also involves a nearby street closure or loading zone downtown, our [parking and loading zone guide](/blog/chicago-event-delivery-parking-loading-zones) is a good reference.

## Parking and getting guests there

Parking at Navy Pier is limited and fills up fast, especially on weekends and during busy seasons. Most planners point guests toward the trolley, rideshare or public transit instead of driving. If your event is downtown-adjacent, check with the venue or the city about any nearby loading zone rules that might affect your own delivery trucks as well as guest drop-off.

## Catering and vendor rules

Navy Pier event spaces often have their own rules about outside vendors and catering, separate from furniture rentals. Check with your venue contact on what's required, including any certificate of insurance. If a certificate is required, that comes from whoever delivers and sets up on site, so ask early so there's no delay on event day.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I rent chairs and tables for an event at Navy Pier?

Yes. We deliver chairs, tables, linens and bar setups to Navy Pier event spaces. Your venue sets the room rules, and we coordinate delivery around their load-in window.

### Does Navy Pier have its own event spaces?

Yes, Navy Pier has a few different venues on site, including a large ballroom area and a glass atrium space, each booked and managed separately through the pier.

### Is parking available at Navy Pier?

Parking is limited, especially on busy days. Most guests do better with the trolley, rideshare or public transit rather than driving.

### How far in advance should I book a Navy Pier event?

As early as you can, especially for weekends and summer dates. Navy Pier is a popular venue and books up months ahead.

### Do I need to worry about wind for an outdoor Navy Pier event?

Yes, if any part of your event is outdoors or near the water. Lake wind can affect tents, linens and decor, so plan for it and have a backup.

### Does Navy Pier require a certificate of insurance for rentals?

Many venues do require one from whoever delivers and sets up. Check with your Navy Pier venue contact on their specific requirement.
