**Quick answer**

Textured linens add depth without adding color. Crinkle (also called crushed or accordion) is casual and cheerful; lace is romantic and vintage; velvet is rich and heavy; jacquard and damask are formal and woven; burlap and linen-look are rustic. Use one strong texture per table, keep the base and napkins smooth, and test the fabric in your venue's light, since texture is invisible in flat lighting.

</div>

Plain polyester is practical, but a room full of it can feel flat. Texture is how planners add warmth and richness without changing the color palette. It also hides wrinkles and small marks, which helps on a busy catering line. This guide covers the main textured linens a Chicago-area event is likely to see, what each is best for, and how to avoid overdoing it.

## What each texture does

### Crinkle (crushed, accordion)
Relaxed and fun, with irregular folds that catch light. It hides wrinkles by design, and it works for garden parties, showers and casual weddings. It is usually a polyester or taffeta-like fabric.

### Lace
Romantic, vintage and delicate. Lace works best as an overlay or runner over a solid base, so the pattern shows. Check for snags before the event. See [overlays and layered linens](/blog/overlays-and-layered-linens).

### Velvet
Rich, soft and warm. Velvet suits fall and winter, and it looks best in deep colors such as burgundy, emerald, navy and plum. It is heavy, so it hangs well, but it can crush or mark. See [Chicago fall wedding linen colors](/blog/chicago-fall-wedding-linen-colors).

### Jacquard and damask
A woven pattern, often floral or geometric, that is part of the fabric. It looks formal and traditional, and it can be a tone-on-tone pattern that gives depth without color.

### Burlap and natural linen
Coarse and earthy. Best as runners on wood tables. Burlap sheds fibers, so keep it away from dark cloths. See [farm table linens](/blog/farm-table-linens-runner-only).

### Linen-look and slub
A smooth cloth with an irregular thread, giving a natural look without heavy wrinkles. A good middle ground.

## Layering texture without clutter

The rule is simple: one texture is the star, the rest are smooth. If the overlay is lace, the base cloth and napkins are plain matte. If the runner is velvet, the cloth is plain and the napkins are the same color family. Also vary the scale. Pair a large pattern with fine texture, not two of the same size.

## Texture and lighting

Texture needs side light to show. Under flat overhead light or bright tent daylight, crinkle and jacquard can look plain. Candlelight and warm uplighting cast shadows that reveal the weave. Ask your venue about the lighting plan, and see [tablecloth colors for photos](/blog/tablecloth-colors-for-photos) and [linen fabrics compared](/blog/linen-fabrics-compared).

## Texture and chairs

A woven chair, such as the [cane-back](/chairs/cane-back) or [rattan garden chair](/chairs/rattan-garden), already carries texture, so a smooth cloth lets it breathe. A smooth, clear [ghost chair](/chairs/ghost) is a good partner for lace, crinkle or velvet. An ornate [Louis medallion chair](/chairs/louis-medallion) needs a plain cloth.

## Care and practical notes

Textured fabrics vary in care. Lace snags, velvet crushes, burlap sheds and crinkle can be hard to press. Ask your supplier how each item arrives and what the damage terms are; see [linen stain and damage policies](/blog/linen-stain-and-damage-policies). In Chicago humidity, soft fabrics can droop on tent tables, so check them before guests arrive.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is crinkle tablecloth fabric?

A crushed or accordion-pleated fabric, usually polyester or taffeta-like, with permanent irregular wrinkles. It gives a casual, shimmery texture and needs no ironing.

### Is lace too old-fashioned for a wedding?

Not if used as an overlay or runner on a modern base. Lace in ivory, white or even black can look romantic or edgy depending on how it is styled.

### Does velvet work in summer?

It can, in the evening or in an air-conditioned room, but it feels heavy in heat. Fall and winter are its natural seasons.

### What texture hides stains best?

Crinkle, jacquard and patterned fabrics hide small marks better than smooth, light cloths. Dark colors also help.

### Can I mix textured linens on one table?

Yes, with care. One strong texture plus smooth items is the safest. Two heavy textures compete.

### Are textured linens more expensive to rent?

Often, since specialty fabrics are stocked in smaller numbers and cost more to clean. Ask for itemized pricing in a quote so you can compare.
