April is the month when Chicago planners get optimistic and the weather corrects them. You can have a 70-degree afternoon on a Tuesday and a 38-degree drizzle on the Saturday you booked. If you are planning a graduation, a baby shower, a spring wedding or a first company picnic of the year, this guide gives you the real numbers and the rental list that makes April work.

## What April weather in Chicago actually looks like

The National Weather Service's 1991-2020 normals for Chicago O'Hare put April's average high at 59°F and its average low at 40.3°F. That is a 19-degree swing inside a single day, and an average hides the spread: warm afternoons in the 70s happen, and so do mornings near freezing. April also averages 3.75 inches of precipitation and about 1.3 inches of snow, so a late flurry is not unheard of.

Treat the number as a starting point and check the forecast for your actual address.

## Sunset and daylight

Daylight saving time is already in effect in April, so you get evening light. Sunset in Chicago is about 7:15 pm on April 1 and about 7:30 pm on April 15 (calculated for 2027). A 4 pm ceremony and a 5 pm cocktail hour finish in daylight, and photographers get warm light around dinner. The catch is that temperatures drop fast once the sun is low, so the coldest part of the day arrives right when the party hits its stride.

## The April rental list

The goal in April is not to pretend it is June. It is to make a cool, possibly wet day feel planned. These are the pieces that matter most:

- **A tent with sidewalls.** A frame tent or pole tent keeps rain off the table and blocks wind. Clear or solid sidewalls can be dropped on the windy side. Read our [tent size guide](/blog/what-size-tent-do-i-need) before you pick a footprint, and check the [Chicago tent permit rules](/blog/chicago-tent-permit) if you are inside the city.
- **Flooring or a dry path.** April ground is soft. Grass that looks fine at noon turns to mud under 100 chair legs. Ask about flooring or a gravel-friendly layout.
- **Heat.** Tent-safe heaters add comfort, but they need clearance, fuel or power and a vendor who sets them up.
- **Chairs that do not sink.** Narrow chair legs push into soft turf. See our guide to [outdoor event chairs and Chicago weather](/blog/topic/seasons-and-venues).
- **A coat plan.** Guests arrive in jackets. A rack or a corner of the tent saves a pile of coats on chairs.

## Permits and venue rules

Public parks follow their own permit rules. For example, the Chicago Park District says a Special Event Permit is needed for outdoor gatherings of 50 or more people, or any size with a tent or canopy (even a 10 x 10), alcohol, amplified sound or inflatables. Its review takes at least 14 days. Tents larger than 20 by 20 feet need a separate City of Chicago permit application. Suburban parks, forest preserves and villages set their own rules, so call the owner of the space first. Our [Chicago venue rules guide](/blog/topic/chicago-planning) lists what to ask.

## Mistakes to avoid in April

- Booking late. Spring weekends fill early; see [when to book event chair rentals](/blog/topic/budget-and-booking).
- Counting on the average. A 59-degree day is a coat day.
- Skipping the tent because the forecast looks clear three weeks out.
- Choosing floor-length linens for a windy lawn without weights.
- Forgetting that grass and tent stakes take longer to set when the ground is wet.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is April too cold for an outdoor wedding in Chicago?

Not if you plan for it. April averages a 59°F high and a 40°F low at O'Hare, so a covered or heated space with an early ceremony works well. Outdoor ceremonies with an indoor or tented reception are common, and a tent with sidewalls and heat makes the whole day comfortable.

### What is the average rainfall in Chicago in April?

About 3.75 inches at O'Hare, based on NWS 1991-2020 normals. That is rain on a meaningful share of days, so build a rain plan into the budget rather than treating it as a maybe.

### What time is sunset in Chicago in April?

Around 7:15 pm on April 1 and 7:30 pm on April 15, using daylight saving time. Sunset moves about 30 minutes later over the course of the month, so check the exact date of your event.

### Do I need heaters for an April party in Chicago?

Usually yes after about 5 pm. The average April low is 40°F, and a tent without heat holds the cold. Ask whether your tent vendor offers heat that fits the tent, and never place a heater against fabric or sidewalls unless the vendor approves it.

### Can I rent chairs for an April outdoor event on grass?

Yes, and you should tell the rental company the surface is grass. Soft April turf can swallow thin chair legs, so ask about chair type, flooring or a gravel and paver base. Our cross-back and garden folding chairs are popular for lawn events.

### When should I book a tent and chairs for April?

Book as soon as you have a date. April and May are popular with weddings, graduations and communions, and tents are the first item to run out. A rental company can hold a date once you pay a deposit.

### What if it snows in April in Chicago?

Average April snowfall is about 1.3 inches, so a dusting is possible. Early April events should have an indoor plan B. Later in the month snow is rare, but cold and wind are not.
