Geneva is the next town south of us along the Fox River, and it is one of the most photographed in the western suburbs: a brick-lined downtown, river views and plenty of green space. If you are planning a wedding, a fundraiser or a family party here, the first thing to learn is how the eight-week rule works.

## Eight weeks is the number to remember

The City of Geneva says its Special Event Application is needed for City-designated festivals, for events that use public property such as streets, sidewalks, five or more parking spaces or parking lots, and for activities that need heavy Police, Fire or Public Works support. It asks you to submit the application and a complete Emergency Action Plan eight weeks before the event. Fully private events, and events on land owned by another government body such as a park district, do not need the City application. Block parties and Tag Days go through the Geneva Police Department instead.

## Where Geneva events happen

- **Downtown shops and streets.** Third Street and the blocks around it are busy on festival weekends, with Swedish Days in June the best known. If your event needs a closure or parking spaces, it is a City permit.
- **Fox River parks.** The Geneva Park District manages parks and rentable spaces. Ask what is reservable and whether tents and power are allowed.
- **Fabyan Forest Preserve.** Fabyan sits along the Fox River on the Geneva side and is run by the Forest Preserve District of Kane County, not the City. Its windmill and gardens are well known, and the district has its own rules for special use. Check with the district.
- **Banquet halls, inns and restaurants.** Geneva has indoor spaces of all sizes. Ask each one what furniture is included.
- **Private yards and farms.** The most flexible option. Rules on tents and parking still apply.

## What to ask the City and the park district

Bring these questions when you call, because they decide whether your plan survives the permit review:

1. Does my event fall under the City application, or is it on land owned by another agency?
2. Is a tent permit needed, and who inspects it? See our guide to [tent permits in Chicago suburbs](/blog/tent-permit-chicago-suburbs).
3. Is alcohol allowed, and does it need a separate license?
4. Where can a delivery truck stop and unload?

## Seasonal planning

Late spring and early fall are the best windows. Swedish Days in late June fills downtown with visitors, so if you want a quiet weekend, check the festival calendar before you pick a date. Summer sunsets are around 8:30 pm near the solstice, so evening ceremonies run long. In winter, sunset is near 4:20 pm in mid-December, so plan lighting from the start.

## A rental list for a Geneva event

Geneva events tend to be photographed, so the details show. Think about what guests will see first and plan the list backward from there.

- **Chairs for ceremony and dinner.** Many Geneva weddings use the same chairs for both, moved between spaces. Ask the venue how much time you have between the ceremony and dinner to flip the room.
- **Linens that match the setting.** Brick and limestone downtown settings suit ivory or natural; a modern loft might want black or sage. Our [tablecloth size chart](/blog/tablecloth-size-chart) shows the correct sizes.
- **Tables by function.** Beyond guest tables, count the cake table, gift table, guest book table and bar fronts. A 150-guest reception can easily have six to eight of these.
- **Cover for the weather.** A tent is a rental and a permit question. Our [tent size guide](/blog/what-size-tent-do-i-need) explains how to size one for Chicago-area guest counts.

When the schedule is tight, a short call beats a long email. Tell us your date, your guest count and whether the space is public, and we can sort out what to ask first.

## Frequently asked questions

### How far in advance do I apply for a special event in Geneva?

Eight weeks before the event. The City asks for the Special Event Application and a complete Emergency Action Plan by then. Events on park district land do not use that application.

### Do I need a City permit for an event at a Geneva park?

Usually not. The City says events on property owned by another government body, such as a park district, do not need its application. The park district or forest preserve district sets its own permit rules.

### Who handles block parties in Geneva?

The Geneva Police Department. Block parties and Tag Days are handled outside the Special Event Application.

### Who runs Fabyan Forest Preserve?

The Forest Preserve District of Kane County. It has its own rules for weddings and group events, so contact the district for current rules.

### Is it hard to deliver rentals to downtown Geneva?

It can be on busy weekends because loading space is limited. Decide the drop point with your venue, and tell us whether a truck can stop close. We are pre-launch, with first deliveries in April 2027.

### What chairs work for a Geneva garden party?

Light chairs that sit well on grass, like garden folding or cross-back chairs, are the best fit. Ask your host whether you need flooring under them in damp seasons.
