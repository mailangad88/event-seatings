**Quick answer**

A mobile or portable bar is the front counter where guests order and the bartender works. A back bar is the shelving or table behind it that holds bottles, mixers, ice and spare glasses. Most events need both: a 6 to 8 foot bar front and a back bar of similar length. Without a back bar, bottles end up on the counter and the line slows down.

</div>

When people say "bar rental" they usually mean one of three things. Understanding which one you are renting stops you from showing up with a front counter and nowhere to put the liquor.

**Bar front (mobile or portable bar).** A freestanding counter, often 6 or 8 feet long and about 42 inches tall. Some have built-in coolers, ice wells or a sink. Many are on wheels. They come in wood, white, black and mirrored styles.

**Back bar.** Shelving or a counter behind the bartender. It holds liquor bottles, mixers, bar tools, spare glasses and signs. It keeps the working surface of the front bar free.

**Prep or ice table.** A banquet table beside the bar for garnishes, ice bins, a cutting board and trash.

## What each one is for

## How to choose

**Beer and wine only.** A simple bar front or a banquet table with a cloth is often enough, plus coolers. A back bar is optional. See [beer and wine only bar setup](/blog/beer-and-wine-only-bar-setup).

**Full bar.** Rent a bar front and a back bar of equal length. A 6-foot bar needs at least a 6-foot back bar; an 8-foot bar works with a 6 to 8 foot back bar. Add a prep table.

**Signature cocktails only.** A smaller bar front and a back bar for the few bottles. See [signature cocktail station ideas](/blog/signature-cocktail-station-ideas).

**Multiple bars.** Only the main bar needs a full back bar. Satellite bars can use a banquet table with a skirt.

## Chicago notes

In Chicago high-rises and loft buildings, a bar often has to travel through a freight elevator and a loading dock, so confirm the width of doors and the booking window for the elevator before you choose a bar that does not fold. In suburban barns and tents, the main question is level ground and distance from a power source.

## Style options

Rustic wood bars suit barns and tents, white bars look at home in modern lofts and gardens, and mirrored or glass bars read as glam in a ballroom. If you want your bar to match your seating, a natural [cross-back chair](/chairs/cross-back) goes with wood and a clear [ghost chair](/chairs/ghost) goes with white or glass.

## Space and power

- **Footprint.** A bar front is about 24 to 30 inches deep. Allow 3 feet behind it for the bartender and 4 feet in front for guests.
- **Floor.** Bars need level ground. On a lawn, ask the rental company about leveling or a platform.
- **Power.** Ice machines, blenders and lights need outlets. Ask your venue where they are. For tents, see our [Lighting and Power](/blog/topic/lighting-and-power) section.
- **Water.** A sink on the bar needs water and drainage. Most portable bars do not have one; bartenders use buckets.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a back bar?

A back bar is the counter or shelving behind the bartender that holds bottles, mixers and spare glasses. It keeps the front bar clear for making drinks and handing them to guests.

### Do I need a back bar for a wedding?

For a full bar, yes. For beer and wine only, a cooler and a table are often enough. Without a back bar, bottles clutter the front counter.

### How long should the bar be?

A 6-foot bar suits up to about 75 guests, and an 8-foot bar suits 75 to 150. Beyond 150 guests, add a second bar rather than a longer one.

### Are mobile bars the same as portable bars?

Usually yes. Both terms describe a freestanding bar that can be set up anywhere. Some are on wheels, some fold flat, and some include a cooler or ice well.

### Can a banquet table be used as a bar?

Yes, with a floor-length linen, a back table and a good layout. It is a budget option for casual events.

### Do portable bars need power?

Only if they include lights, refrigeration or an ice machine. Most basic portable bars are passive furniture. Check the spec before you rent.
