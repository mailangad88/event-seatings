Glen Ellyn is a quiet DuPage County village with a lake in the middle of town, a Metra station and a tight, walkable downtown. It suits small weddings, showers, graduations and neighborhood parties. This guide is for planning one without a surprise.

## Lake Ellyn and the park district

The Glen Ellyn Park District publishes a rental opportunities page, and its parks include Lake Ellyn Park, with a boathouse that the district uses for programs and events. If you want a lake view, start with the district. Ask about capacity, a rain plan, parking, and what furniture is already inside the building.

The village side covers the downtown streets and anything that uses public right-of-way. For a street fair or a block event, start with the village.

## Space types in Glen Ellyn

- **Lake and park pavilions.** Rentable through the park district. They book early for summer weekends.
- **Boathouse and park buildings.** Smaller indoor spaces. Ask what chairs and tables come with the space.
- **Downtown and the train station area.** Public, and tied to village permits and Metra schedules.
- **Churches, halls and restaurants.** Plenty of indoor options for showers and receptions.
- **Private backyards.** Lots in older neighborhoods can be narrow, so measure and plan guest counts around the space.

## Narrow lots and the real constraint

Many Glen Ellyn homes sit on older lots where the usable lawn is smaller than it looks. A 60-inch round table with 8 chairs needs a circle roughly 12 feet across to be comfortable, so six tables fit into a 40 by 30 foot area with room to walk. Mark the space with stakes before you order. Our [guide to tables for 50 to 200 guests](/blog/how-many-tables-for-50-100-150-200-guests) has the numbers.

## Seasonal planning

Lake Ellyn is busy in summer. In spring, the ground near the lake can be soft; in fall, leaf cover makes ground uneven. In winter, a heated tent or an indoor hall is the sensible option. Check our [seasons and venues archive](/blog/topic/seasons-and-venues).

## Making a small space feel complete

Small events succeed on detail. A shower for 40 in a Glen Ellyn yard needs fewer rentals than a banquet, but each one counts.

- **Linens:** floor-length cloths on every table make a lawn feel finished. Use the [tablecloth size chart](/blog/tablecloth-size-chart) to order correctly.
- **A focal point:** a single long table for desserts or gifts draws the eye.
- **Shade:** a small frame tent over the food table helps in July. See [frame tent vs pole tent](/blog/frame-tent-vs-pole-tent).
- **A rain plan:** a garage, a porch or a tent. Decide a week before, not the morning of.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who rents park spaces in Glen Ellyn?

The Glen Ellyn Park District. It lists rental opportunities for parks and facilities, including spaces around Lake Ellyn.

### Can I use the Lake Ellyn boathouse for a party?

Ask the Glen Ellyn Park District. It manages the boathouse and can tell you whether private events are allowed and what is included.

### Do I need a village permit for a Glen Ellyn event?

Only if you use village streets, sidewalks or property. For park district land, the district sets the rules. Private parties generally don't need one, but ask about tents and noise.

### How do I fit tables in a small Glen Ellyn yard?

Measure the usable lawn and plan about 12 feet across per 60-inch round with chairs. Stake the area before ordering.

### Do you deliver in Glen Ellyn?

Yes. First deliveries begin with the April 2027 Founding Season. Tell us about narrow streets or driveways in advance.

### What is the best chair for a small shower?

A lighter-looking chair, such as a ghost chair, takes up less visual space. Garden folding chairs are the budget choice.
