**Quick answer**

For a brunch of 20 to 50, rent one chair per guest, round tables of 6 to 8 or two long tables, a buffet or station table per 25 guests, and a separate drink table for the mimosa or coffee bar. Brunch needs fewer glasses and plates than dinner: plan two plates and two to three glasses per guest. For 30 guests that is 30 chairs, 4 rounds of 8, 2 food tables, 1 drink table, 60 plates and 90 glasses.

</div>

Brunch is a long, relaxed meal that sits between breakfast and lunch. Guests arrive within a half hour of each other, they eat for an hour or two, and they linger over coffee. That makes it close to a seated lunch, with one important twist: the drink table is as popular as the food.

## Two ways to serve

<div class="table-wrap">

| Style | How it works | Best for | Equipment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Buffet | Guests serve themselves from one or two tables | 25 or more | Chafing dishes, platters, serving utensils |
| Stations | Several small stations (eggs, pastry, fruit) | 30 to 50 | One 6 ft table per station |
| Family style | Platters on each table | 12 to 24 | Large platters, extra table space |
| Plated | Servers bring plates | 20 to 40 | Servers, more plates, a kitchen |

</div>

Stations spread people out and shorten lines, so they suit larger brunches. Family style needs wide tables, so choose 72-inch rounds or long tables. Learn more in [buffet line flow and serving speed](/blog/buffet-line-flow-and-serving-speed) and [food station ideas and equipment](/blog/food-station-ideas-and-equipment).

## Place settings for brunch

Brunch uses a simpler setting than dinner:

- A dinner plate, 9 to 10.5 inches, for the main food
- A small side plate if you serve pastries or fruit
- A fork and knife, and a spoon for yogurt or soup
- A napkin, 20 x 20 inches
- A water glass and a second glass for mimosas, juice or coffee, plus a coffee cup and saucer if you serve hot drinks

Use our [casual place-setting diagram](/blog/casual-place-setting-diagram) for spacing. For counts, see [how many plates per guest](/blog/how-many-plates-per-guest), and for glasses see [how many glasses per guest](/blog/how-many-glasses-per-guest). A coffee service for 30 needs roughly one 30-cup urn; read [how many coffee urns for a party](/blog/how-many-coffee-urns-for-a-party).

## Chairs that suit a long morning

Brunch guests sit for 2 hours or more, so comfort matters. [Bentwood chairs](/chairs/bentwood) feel like a cafe. [Cross-back chairs](/chairs/cross-back), estimated at $8 to $11, are warm and sturdy. [Rattan garden chairs](/chairs/rattan-garden) work for a sunroom or patio. [Ghost chairs](/chairs/ghost), about $10 to $14, stay out of the way of floral tables. Light colors reflect the bright morning feel.

## Chicago-area notes

Brunch is easy to hold in a sunroom, a restaurant private room or a backyard in the morning, when it is cooler. In winter, a brunch at home needs coat storage and warm drinks. In summer, start earlier and add shade. Chicago mornings in spring can be chilly even when afternoons are warm, so consider a tent with sidewalls or heaters. If your brunch is at a restaurant, ask about outside rentals and fees. For suburban homes, check parking for 20 to 50 cars and ask neighbors.

## The mimosa and coffee bar

The drink table does most of the work at a brunch, so give it its own space. A 6-foot table holds a tub of chilled sparkling wine or a pitcher, juice in two or three flavors, a bowl of fruit for garnish and a stack of flutes. Put a separate section at one end for coffee and tea, with an urn, cups, saucers, sugar, cream and stir sticks. Keep it away from the food line so people can top off a cup without joining a queue.

Add a no-alcohol option that looks as inviting as the rest, such as sparkling water with citrus or a pitcher of iced tea. Check Illinois rules for serving alcohol at private events if you plan to hire a bartender or sell tickets. Ice buckets, a tray for empties and a trash can under the table keep it tidy. For a table of 30 guests, many hosts set a pitcher of water on every table so the bar is not the only source.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many tables do I need for a brunch of 30?

Four round tables of 8 for guests, two food tables or one two-sided buffet, and one drink table. That is 6 or 7 tables in total, plus a cake or gift table if needed.

### Should brunch be a buffet or plated?

A buffet is simpler and cheaper for 25 or more. Plated service feels more formal but needs servers and a kitchen that can plate quickly.

### How many mimosas per guest?

A common planning rule is 2 to 3 drinks per guest over a 2-hour brunch. A bottle of sparkling wine pours about 5 to 6 glasses, so check with your bartender or supplier.

### Do I need a tent for a brunch?

If it's outdoors, yes for shade or rain. Morning sun is less harsh than afternoon, but weather in the Chicago area changes fast.

### What linens work for a brunch?

White, cream, pastel or natural linen. Use floor-length cloths on food tables and either a cloth or a runner on guest tables. See the [tablecloth size chart](/blog/tablecloth-size-chart).

### How early should I book rentals?

Four weeks ahead for most weekends, and six to eight for Mother's Day and spring weekends.
