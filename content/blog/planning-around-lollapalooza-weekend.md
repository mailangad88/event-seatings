**Quick answer**

Lollapalooza runs four days in Grant Park in late July or early August (July 30 to August 2 in 2026, 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily), but street closures near the park start weeks earlier. In 2026, Balbo Avenue between Columbus Drive and DuSable Lake Shore Drive closed July 15 through August 9. If your event is downtown or in the South Loop that week, schedule deliveries early, choose a venue away from the park and warn guests about crowds and hotel demand.

Late summer is peak wedding and party season in Chicago. It is also festival season. Lollapalooza fills hotels, packs trains and closes the streets around Grant Park. If your event is within a mile or so of the park, the festival touches your plan even if none of your guests are going. This guide shows how to plan around it, using the 2026 closure schedule as a model.

## Who is affected

If you live or host in the Loop, South Loop, Near South Side or along the lakefront, the closures change your route. Even if you are not near the park, festival traffic and train crowds change timing for the whole weekend. Metra reported an expected increase in passengers during the festival.

<ol class="flow">
<li><strong>Find your date.</strong> Compare it with the festival dates and the closure dates.</li>
<li><strong>Check your route.</strong> Look at the closed streets between your supplier and your venue.</li>
<li><strong>Move deliveries earlier.</strong> Aim for before the festival starts, or the days before heavy traffic.</li>
<li><strong>Plan pickups later</strong> or on Monday if the streets reopen.</li>
<li><strong>Tell guests early</strong> about hotel and travel crunch.</li>
</ol>

## Guests and hotels

Hotels near the park fill early. If you have out-of-town guests, send travel advice well before the date and consider a block at a hotel farther out. Guests going to the festival may be dressed for it and heading to different places, and a rideshare pickup can take much longer than usual. Add 30 to 45 minutes to your start time estimate only if your venue is close to the closures, and use the real traffic forecast rather than our rule of thumb.

## Heat, storms and summer

Late July is hot. Chicago's normal July high is about 84.5 F and the normal August high is 82.5 F, per the National Weather Service's 1991 to 2020 normals for O'Hare. Summer storms can also close outdoor events on short notice. Have a shade plan and an indoor backup. Our [Chicago summer event season guide](/blog/chicago-summer-event-season-planning) covers it.

## What to ask your venue

- Do you expect the closures to affect load-in or guest arrival?
- Is there a loading dock or alley that avoids closed streets?
- Can vendors set up the day before?
- What is the rain plan for an outdoor space?

## Frequently asked questions

### When is Lollapalooza in Chicago?

Lollapalooza is a four-day festival in Grant Park in late July or early August. In 2026 it ran July 30 to August 2. We could not verify 2027 dates, so check the official site.

### How long do the Lollapalooza street closures last?

Longer than the festival. In 2026, Balbo east of Columbus closed July 15 through August 9, and several other streets closed July 27 to August 3. Check the City's notices for the current year.

### Can I hold a wedding during Lollapalooza weekend in Chicago?

Yes. Choose a venue away from the park, or schedule around the closures, and warn guests about hotels and travel times. Move deliveries earlier.

### Will my rental delivery be affected by Lollapalooza?

If it must cross or approach the closed streets, yes. Give your provider the closure list and agree on an early delivery time and an alternate route.

### Are hotels more expensive that weekend?

We do not publish hotel prices, so we cannot say. Ask your guests to book early and check availability for your year.

### Do events farther from Grant Park need to worry?

Less, but traffic and train crowds can still affect timing. Add buffer to your plan, especially for guest arrival.
