**Quick answer**

For Christmas dinner for 20, plan on two 8-foot tables pushed end to end for adults (about 16 seats) plus a 6-foot kids table, or one long table and one 60-inch round. Ask for delivery on Dec 22 or 23 and pickup on Dec 26 or later, because Christmas Eve and Christmas Day schedules are tight. Order food at roughly 1/2 pound of boneless roast or 3/4 pound of bone-in ham per adult as a rule of thumb.

</div>

Christmas Day is the one dinner where the whole family shows up at once. In a Chicago bungalow or two-flat, that can mean 20 people and a dining room made for 8. The table is the easy part. The hard part is the calendar, because Christmas Eve is Thursday, Dec 24, 2026 and Christmas Day is Friday, Dec 25. Most rental companies shorten hours or close around then.

## Start with the calendar

Rental companies run on trucks and crews, and those crews want the holiday off like everyone else. Hours on Dec 24 are often short. Many companies are closed on Dec 25. Some charge more for holiday delivery. None of that is the same everywhere, so ask each company for its exact hours.

Two things follow from this. First, you hold the rentals for a few days, so tell us if you want the table set up in advance. Second, if you host on Dec 24, you have the same short-hours problem on the delivery side, so earlier delivery is safer.

## What most hosts find out too late

- **Pickup is the problem, not delivery.** Hosts plan the delivery and forget that the crew still has to come back. If pickup lands on Dec 26 or 27, ask for a window, and have everything stacked and counted near the door.
- **Holiday hours change the price.** Some companies add a holiday or after-hours charge. Ask for it as its own line on the quote.
- **Snow changes the schedule.** Chicago snow from December on can slow a truck, and an unplowed alley or gangway can stop one. Shovel the delivery path the day before.

## Tables for 20 in a house

Count the room before you count the guests. A dining table needs about 2 feet of width per person along the table, and about 3 feet behind each chair for people to move. In a typical Chicago dining room, that is tight for even 12.

Three setups work in most homes:

| Setup | Seats | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| Two 8-ft tables end to end | About 16 to 18 | Long dining room and living room opened up |
| One 8-ft table plus one 60-inch round | About 16 | Two rooms, with the round feeling more social |
| Add a 6-ft kids table | 6 more | Kids at one end of the room |

</div>

Rules of thumb: an 8-foot table seats 8 and a 6-foot table seats 6. A 60-inch round seats 8 to 10. See [round table sizes and seating](/blog/round-table-sizes-and-seating-48-60-66-72-inch) and [banquet table sizes](/blog/banquet-table-sizes-6-ft-vs-8-ft) before you measure.

Measure your doors, your stairs and your turn into the dining room. A 30-inch-wide table leg set will not make a tight corner. Tell us if there are stairs, a gangway or a freight elevator, because that changes how items are delivered. See [alley and gangway deliveries](/blog/chicago-alley-deliveries-event-rentals).

## Serving a ham or a roast

Plan the food to match the table, not the other way around. As a rule of thumb, buy about 3/4 pound of bone-in ham per adult, or about 1/2 pound of boneless roast or ham. Add sides, and the plate fills up.

Some tips that save the day:

- **Serve from the kitchen counter or a side table.** A 6-foot table along a wall works as a buffet, with plates at one end and sides in the middle. A [buffet line guide](/blog/buffet-line-flow-and-serving-speed) shows how to keep it moving.
- **Let the roast rest.** A large roast needs time out of the oven before carving, which gives you a window to fill glasses and sit people down.
- **Plan oven space.** One oven for a ham and sides is a puzzle. Ask a guest to bring a dish that goes in a slow cooker or is served cold.
- **Rent chafing dishes for the sides.** Mashed potatoes, green beans and rolls stay warm while the carving finishes. See [how many chafing dishes you need](/blog/how-many-chafing-dishes-for-a-buffet).

## Kids seating

Put the kids where you can see them from the main table. A 6-foot table seats about 6 kids, or 8 small ones sharing. Kid-height tables and chairs are an option for younger children, and they keep little ones off adult chairs. Use plastic or sturdy plates for the youngest. Our guide to [kids tables and chairs](/blog/kids-tables-and-chairs-for-events) covers the sizes.

Seat one or two older cousins at the kids table as helpers. It makes the kids table a place to be, and it helps parents eat in peace.

## Dinnerware, linens and chairs

For 20 guests, count a dinner plate, a salad or dessert plate, a fork, a knife, a spoon, a water glass and a wine glass per person, plus extras for serving. Our [plate counts per guest](/blog/how-many-plates-per-guest) guide shows how many extras to add. Linens go to the floor on a 6-foot table if you want a dressed look, or lap length if you want chairs to slide in easily. The [tablecloth size chart](/blog/tablecloth-size-chart) has exact sizes.

Chairs are the item most homes run short on. Count one per guest, then add 2 for late arrivals. Folding or padded chairs are easy to store, and you can browse them at [/rentals/chairs](/rentals/chairs).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I get rentals delivered on Christmas Eve?

Sometimes, but hours are often short and a holiday charge may apply. Ask for Dec 22 or 23 delivery instead, which is easier on the crew and on your schedule. We check dates with the rental companies when you ask for a quote.

### Are rental companies open on Christmas Day?

Many are closed on Dec 25. Plan delivery before and pickup after, and ask for the exact hours of each company when you book.

### How many tables do I need for Christmas dinner for 20?

Two 8-foot tables end to end seat about 16 to 18, and a 6-foot kids table adds about 6. A 60-inch round table seats 8 to 10 if you want a second table instead. Measure the room first, because 20 guests at one long table needs a room about 20 feet long.

### How much ham do I need for 20 people?

As a rule of thumb, about 3/4 pound of bone-in ham per adult, so around 15 pounds for 20 adults. Boneless needs about 1/2 pound per adult. Add extra if you want leftovers.

### How long should I keep the rentals?

Most hosts keep them from Dec 22 or 23 until Dec 26 or later. Ask about the daily rate for extra days, because it is often lower than the first day. Your quote shows how holiday days are billed.

### Do I need to wash rented dishes?

Rinse off food and stack them in the crates the company provides. Most companies handle the washing. Ask about rules before you scrape, because some want items returned dry.
