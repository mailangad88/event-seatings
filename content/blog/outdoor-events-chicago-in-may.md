May is the sweet spot for outdoor events in the Chicago area. The grass is green, the days are long and the heat has not arrived. It is also the wettest month in the 1991-2020 normals, which surprises people who assume summer holds that crown. This guide covers what to expect and what to rent.

## May weather by the numbers

At O'Hare, May averages a 70.5°F high and a 50.6°F low, with 4.49 inches of precipitation. Snow is down to a trace. That is the highest precipitation total of any month, a little ahead of August at 4.25 inches and June at 4.10. A pleasant average still includes cool, windy lake-effect days and the occasional storm, so the tent is not optional if the budget depends on the date.

## Daylight in May

Sunset is about 7:47 pm on May 1 and about 8:02 pm on May 15 (calculated for 2027). A 5 pm ceremony leaves nearly three hours of good light. By late May you can serve dinner outdoors in daylight. If you plan string lights or uplighting, they still matter for atmosphere after 8:30 pm, and our [string light layouts guide](/blog/string-light-layouts-for-events) shows how far apart to hang them.

## Cool-night comfort

May evenings drop quickly, and the average low of 50.6°F means guests in light dresses and short sleeves will feel it by 9 pm. Planners handle this with heaters under the tent, a basket of wraps or blankets, hot drinks at the bar and tents with at least one sidewall. Lakefront venues are cooler still.

## Permits and park rules

In Chicago parks, May is a busy permit season. The Chicago Park District needs a Special Event Permit for outdoor gatherings of 50 or more people and for tents, alcohol, amplified sound or inflatables at any size, and says review takes at least 14 days. Its application for 2027 opens November 6, 2026 at 9 am according to its site. For a suburban park or forest preserve, call that district. Our [venue rules guide](/blog/topic/chicago-planning) is a good checklist.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is May a good month for an outdoor event in Chicago?

Yes. May averages a 70.5°F high and long daylight, with sunset after 7:45 pm. It is also the wettest month in NWS normals, so plan a tent or indoor plan B.

### How much does it rain in Chicago in May?

About 4.49 inches at O'Hare in the 1991-2020 normals, the highest of any month. Plan for rain on a share of days and make a weather decision date with your vendors.

### What time is sunset in Chicago in May?

About 7:47 pm on May 1 and 8:02 pm on May 15. Late-May events can serve dinner outdoors in daylight.

### Do I need a tent for a May backyard party?

If rain would ruin your plan, yes. With 4.49 inches on average and unpredictable storms, most planners treat a tent as insurance, even when the forecast looks clear.

### Will it be warm enough for a May wedding reception outdoors?

The average high is 70.5°F, but the average low is 50.6°F, so evenings need heat or wraps. Evening ceremonies by the lake will feel cooler than inland suburbs.

### What are the busiest May dates for rentals in Chicago?

Weekends around Mother's Day (May 9, 2027) and Memorial Day (May 31, 2027), plus graduation weekends. Book as early as you can.

### Are there bugs at May outdoor events?

Fewer than in summer, but mosquitoes begin to appear near standing water and shady lawns. See our guide to [mosquitoes and bugs at outdoor events](/blog/mosquitoes-and-bugs-outdoor-events).
