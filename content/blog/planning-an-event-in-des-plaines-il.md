**Quick answer**

In a Des Plaines hall or hotel ballroom, the seating style changes capacity a lot: rounds of 10 fit about 12 square feet per guest, theater rows about 6 to 8 and classroom tables about 15 to 18. A 150-guest dinner needs about 158 chairs and 15 rounds. Upgrade chairs only where it shows, such as head tables, and order from about $5 to $30 per chair.

Des Plaines sits next to O'Hare International Airport and the Rosemont area, with hotels, banquet halls, a historic downtown and the Des Plaines River running through it. That makes it a practical place for events with out-of-town guests, from corporate meetings and association dinners to weddings and family reunions. Most use hotel or banquet spaces, so this guide focuses on how seating style changes capacity, and where rentals fill the gaps.

## When to bring in rentals

Hotels provide basic banquet chairs and tables, so you rent what the property lacks. The best returns:

- **Head table or stage chairs.** Pair [Louis medallion chairs](/chairs/louis-medallion) (about $20 to $30) with a floor-length cloth.
- **A lounge near the dance floor.** [Velvet dining chairs](/chairs/velvet-dining) (about $18 to $25) in clusters.
- **Ceremony seating** in the same ballroom, with [gold infinity chairs](/chairs/infinity) (about $9 to $13) or [ghost chairs](/chairs/ghost) (about $10 to $14).
- **Extra tables:** cocktail tables, a cake table, gift and card tables.

Always ask the hotel for its policy on outside rentals and fees, and whether a certificate of insurance is required for the rental company. Request the loading dock hours and the freight elevator schedule. If the ballroom is booked until 5 pm, deliveries may need to wait, which affects your setup window.

## Out-of-town guests

Because of the O'Hare area, many events here include travelers. Plan for it.

1. **Welcome info.** A table at the entrance with maps, schedules and local tips.
2. **Early arrivals and late departures.** Flight delays mean guests may arrive after the ceremony begins; keep a few seats open at the back.
3. **Language and dietary needs.** Ask about food restrictions in advance.
4. **Transportation.** Check whether the hotel runs an airport shuttle, and share the schedule.

Do not assume a shuttle exists; confirm with the property. If you are traveling in winter, remember that Chicago-area snow can delay flights, so keep your schedule flexible.

## Using public spaces

Des Plaines has a park district and village-run facilities. If you want to use a park, plaza or river-area space, contact the Des Plaines Park District or the City of Des Plaines about permits and rules. Public events near water may have additional restrictions. Ask about tents, amplified sound, alcohol and parking.

## Corporate and association dinners

Business groups that meet near the airport have a few special needs. Registration can run 30 minutes before the program, so give it a dedicated table and a short line. Name badges, printed agendas and sponsor signs all need table space, so count them. Breakout sessions may call for classroom tables in a second room, so ask the hotel what is available and how long the flip takes between sessions.

Seating charts matter at these events. Assigned tables by company or committee make networking easier, while open seating can leave guests standing with plates. For awards, put a podium on a riser or a small stage, and test the microphone. Keep dessert and coffee stations open after the program, since people linger. If your group travels in Chicago-area winter weather, plan for late arrivals and a flexible start time.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many people fit in a 1,800 square foot ballroom?

About 150 at rounds, 250 in theater rows and 100 at classroom tables, as rules of thumb. The hotel's official capacity governs.

### Can I bring in outside chairs and tables?

Often, but policies vary. Ask the hotel about fees, insurance and loading dock access.

### What seating style is best for a corporate dinner?

Rounds of 10 are standard for dinners; classroom works for training; theater suits presentations.

### How many tables do I need beyond guest tables?

Plan 6 to 8 extras for 150 guests: registration, gifts, cake, DJ, bar and displays.

### Is it easy to deliver near O'Hare?

Traffic can be heavy on some roads, so schedule deliveries outside rush hours when possible.

### What chair is best for a head table?

A Louis medallion or velvet chair stands out against standard banquet seating.
