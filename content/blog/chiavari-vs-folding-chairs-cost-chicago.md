**Quick answer**

Folding chairs are almost always the cheapest chair to rent, and Chiavari chairs usually cost several times more per chair, often with cushions extra. If you're planning an event in Chicago or the suburbs, you've probably seen two kinds of quotes: the bargain folding-chair quote and the "everyone uses these" Chiavari quote. Here's how to compare them fairly, and what you get in between.

## What you're actually paying for

Every rental quote is built from the same pieces:

- **The chair itself**, priced per chair per event
- **Cushions**, which are often a separate line on Chiavari chairs
- **Delivery and pickup**, based on distance, timing and how hard the venue is to reach
- **Setup and strike**, if chairs are placed for you and stacked away after
- **Extras** like stairs, long carries from the truck, after-hours pickup and a damage waiver

That's why two quotes with the same per-chair price can land hundreds of dollars apart. When you compare, compare the **full total** for your date and venue.

## Folding chairs: lowest cost, lowest impact

Basic folding chairs are built to be practical. They stack flat, set up fast and cost the least. They're a great fit for:

- Backyard parties and graduation open houses
- Overflow seating and vendor areas
- Ceremonies where guests are seated for 20 minutes

Where they fall short is the photos. At a reception, guests sit for hours and your photographer shoots the room from every angle. A plain folding chair reads as functional, not designed.

## Chiavari chairs: the familiar upgrade

The Chiavari is the chair most Chicago ballrooms and banquet halls default to. It's light, elegant and stacks well. The downsides are the price relative to folding chairs, the cushion line item, and the fact that nearly every reception looks the same with it.

## How to decide

1. **Seat count first.** Use the [free seating planner](/seating-planner) to get your real number, since the ceremony, cocktail hour and reception may not all need separate chairs.
2. **Split the spend.** Many hosts put a budget chair at the ceremony and a styled chair at dinner, or splurge only on the head table.
3. **Ask for the full total.** Same date, same count, same venue address, all fees included.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Chiavari or folding chair rental cheaper?
Folding chairs are almost always cheaper per chair. Chiavari chairs usually cost several times more, and cushions may be extra. Compare the full quote, including delivery and setup, before deciding.

### Are there chairs that look better than folding chairs but cost less than Chiavari?
Yes. Teak garden folding, cross-back and bentwood chairs are often priced between basic folding and premium styles while photographing far better than a plain folding chair.

### Should the ceremony and reception use the same chairs?
They can. Reusing chairs with a flip during cocktail hour saves money but needs staff and time. A separate, simpler ceremony chair is another common way to control cost.
