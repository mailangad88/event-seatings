A velvet settee seats 2 and a velvet sofa seats 3, and each takes roughly 5 to 7 feet of wall length. Pick one anchor piece per lounge group, then repeat the color in a few chairs or poufs. Velvet is the quickest way to make a corner of any room look finished. A single jewel-toned settee in a warehouse loft or an all-white ballroom says "this is the place to sit." It also asks more of you than a plain folding chair: it needs planning for the size, the color and the care. This guide covers what matters.

## Settee, loveseat, sofa: what is the difference?

The names overlap, but these sizes cover most rental pieces.

Always confirm exact dimensions in your quote. Rental inventory varies, and a few inches decide whether a piece passes through a door or fits a stage.

## Picking the color

Velvet reads as a color statement, so choose it as you would a bridesmaid dress.

- **Deep jewel tones (emerald, sapphire, burgundy):** rich and flattering in candlelight, good for fall and winter. They hide small marks better than pale colors.
- **Blush, dusty rose and champagne:** soft, romantic and photo-friendly, but they show drinks and makeup.
- **Black and charcoal:** modern and low-risk. They show dust and lint.
- **Ivory and cream:** classic and bright. High-contrast photos, high-maintenance fabric.
- **Mustard, rust, terracotta:** earthy and trending for boho and fall events.

## Care and practical questions to ask

Velvet is a delicate fabric. Before you book, ask the rental company:

1. **What is the fabric condition?** Look for clean seams, even pile and no crushed spots.
2. **What is the spill and stain policy?** Many companies charge for damage beyond normal wear.
3. **Can it be used outdoors?** Most velvet should stay indoors or under a tent with a floor. Damp grass and rain can ruin it.
4. **Does it pass through the door?** Measure doorways, elevators and stair turns. Downtown Chicago high-rises often have service elevators with limited dimensions and booked time slots.
5. **Who sets it up and takes it away?** Make sure delivery crews place the furniture, and agree on teardown timing.
6. **Is there a lint roller or steamer on site?** Velvet shows crush marks, and a quick brush before guests arrive makes a visible difference.

- **Choosing by photo only.** Colors vary by screen and lighting. Ask for a swatch or a photo in natural light.
- **Ignoring the venue floor.** Hard feet on wood can scratch it, so use felt pads.
- **No coffee table.** A sofa without a surface leads to drinks on the floor.
- **Over-matching.** Three identical sofas look like a showroom. Vary shape and height.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many people fit on a velvet sofa?

Three adults on a standard sofa and two on a settee. For comfort at a long cocktail hour, plan on a few fewer, since guests like room for a drink and a purse.

### Is velvet a good fabric for outdoor events?

Usually no. Velvet absorbs moisture and fades in strong sun. If you want a soft look outdoors, ask for weather-rated fabric or place the sofa under a tent with flooring.

### What color velvet photographs best?

Mid-tone jewel colors and blush photograph well because they hold detail in both flash and soft light. Very dark and very pale colors can lose texture on camera.

### Can I put a velvet sofa on grass?

Only on a floor, mat or tent flooring. Wet ground will wick into the legs and fabric, and the rental company may refuse delivery to bare lawn.

### Do rental companies charge for velvet stains?

Many do charge for damage or deep cleaning. Read your contract for the cleaning and damage clause before you book, and ask if a damage waiver is available.

### How far ahead should I book specialty sofas?

As soon as your date and venue are set. Specialty furniture is limited, and popular colors go first for spring and fall weekends.
