Museum Campus sits on the lakefront just south of downtown, grouping three major Chicago institutions in one area: the Field Museum, Shedd Aquarium and Adler Planetarium. Each museum runs its own event program, so there isn't one single process that covers all three. This guide walks through how to approach a gala or fundraiser at any of them.

## Three museums, three separate booking processes

Each museum at the campus has its own events office, its own list of approved vendors or catering rules, and its own loading and setup procedures.

## Why galas and fundraisers choose Museum Campus

The lakefront setting and the exhibit halls themselves make a strong backdrop for a gala or fundraiser, which is why nonprofits and corporate groups book these venues regularly. Each museum's main hall or atrium has its own built-in look, so your rental choices usually just need to fill in seating, dining and bar needs rather than carry the whole room.

## Loading restrictions near the lakefront

Museum Campus sits on a narrow strip of lakefront land with limited roads in and out, and each museum controls its own loading dock or service entrance. Expect a scheduled load-in window set by the museum, and expect that window to be tighter than a typical banquet hall. Coordinate your rental delivery time directly against that window. For more general downtown delivery guidance, see our [parking and loading zone guide](/blog/chicago-event-delivery-parking-loading-zones).

## Catering and vendor rules come from the museum

Each museum sets its own rules for catering and outside vendors, and those rules can differ even between the three museums on the same campus. Confirm what's required, including any certificate of insurance, directly with the museum's events office. If a certificate is required, that comes from whoever delivers and sets up on site, so build time into your planning to get that sorted before the event.

## Booking timing

Museum Campus galas are popular for fall and spring fundraising season, and the museums' own calendars fill up well ahead of those windows. Lock in your date and space with the museum first, then bring in your rental order once the room layout and guest count are set. ## Frequently asked questions

### Which museums are part of Museum Campus?

The Field Museum, Shedd Aquarium and Adler Planetarium. Each one is a separate institution with its own event spaces and booking process.

### Who do I contact first to book an event at Museum Campus?

Contact the museum's own events office directly. They confirm your date, space and capacity before any rental planning should happen.

### Can I bring in any caterer I want?

No, each museum sets its own catering and vendor rules. Confirm what's allowed directly with that museum's events office.

### Is parking or loading limited at Museum Campus?

Yes, the lakefront location has limited roads and each museum manages its own loading dock on a set schedule. Plan your delivery window around that schedule.

### Do the museums require a certificate of insurance for rentals?

Many do require one from whoever delivers and sets up on site. Confirm the specific requirement with your museum's events office.

### What kind of events work well at Museum Campus?

Galas, fundraisers and corporate events that want a lakefront setting with a strong architectural backdrop. Each museum's exhibit halls and atriums shape the look of the event.
