**Quick answer**

Decide early who makes the rain call, when and at what trigger: most planners set a decision 48 hours out and a final check 4 hours before guests arrive. A tent with sidewalls, flooring, covered walkways and a coat plan turns a wet day into a good one. Chicago's wettest months at O'Hare are May (4.49 inches), August (4.25) and June (4.10).

Rain is not a disaster at an event if it is a decision you've already made. The problems come from guessing at the last minute: guests in wet shoes, chairs in mud, a tent not anchored properly. This guide gives a plan you can set before the day.

## What to rent for a rain plan

- **A tent**: a frame or pole tent keeps the party dry. Read [what size tent do I need](/blog/what-size-tent-do-i-need) and [rain plan with a tent](/blog/rain-plan-with-a-tent).
- **Sidewalls**: wind-driven rain comes in sideways.
- **Flooring**: grass turns to mud under 100 chairs.
- **Covered walkways**: a path from parking to the tent.
- **Umbrella stands and mats**: see [umbrella stands and rain plans](/blog/umbrella-stands-and-rain-plans).
- **Extra lighting**: rainy days are dim.

### Chairs in wet weather

Wood chairs handle damp air, but ground moisture can stain them. Velvet and padded seats should stay under cover. Ghost chairs are easy to wipe. [Cross-back chairs](/chairs/cross-back) and [garden folding chairs](/chairs/garden-folding) are practical choices for a tent on grass. Read outdoor event chairs and Chicago weather.

A tent is not a lightning shelter. The National Weather Service's rule is simple: when thunder roars, go indoors. Ask your tent vendor for the wind speed at which they require evacuation or sidewall changes.

Read the weather language in your venue and rental contracts before you sign. Some rentals are non-refundable once set; some venues have a weather clause. ## Guest experience in rain

Greeters with umbrellas, mats at the door, a covered drop-off and a place for wet coats make a rain day feel generous. Move the ceremony under the tent or indoors, and push photos to the next dry window. Use the weather as part of the story instead of fighting it.

## The rain-day setup, hour by hour

On the day, start with drainage. Walk the site and look for low spots where water collects, then ask the tent crew to place the tent, the bar and the buffet on the highest ground available. Lay flooring or runners on every path guests will walk, including the one from the car to the tent. Put mats at the door and a stand for wet umbrellas just inside. If your caterer needs a prep area, give them a covered one with a dry floor, since wet floors and hot equipment are a bad pairing.

In the hour before guests arrive, check that sidewalls are down on the windward side, that gutters on the tent drain away from the seating and that cords and power strips are off the ground and covered. Test the lights, since a gray sky means the party needs them earlier than planned. Brief your greeters on where umbrellas, coats and wet shoes go, and who can call you if something goes wrong.

After the event, drying matters. Rental linens, chairs and tables come back damp, so let your rental company know about spills or soaked items at pickup. Ask about pickup timing too, because a rainy teardown can run long.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should I rent a tent in case of rain?

Yes, if you are outdoors. Chicago gets precipitation in every month, and May averages 4.49 inches.

### When should I decide whether to move indoors?

Most planners set the decision at 48 hours out and do a final walk about 4 hours before guests arrive.

### What if it rains on my outdoor ceremony?

Move it under the tent or inside. Ask your rental company about adding sidewalls and flooring.

### Will the rental company refund me for rain?

It depends on the contract. Read weather and cancellation language before you sign.

### Are chairs ruined by rain?

Wood and plastic chairs handle a damp day, but velvet and padded seats should stay under cover.

### Can I use a tent in a thunderstorm?

No. A tent does not protect from lightning. Move guests to a building.

### Do I need flooring in a tent?

On grass in wet weather, yes. Flooring protects chairs and shoes.
