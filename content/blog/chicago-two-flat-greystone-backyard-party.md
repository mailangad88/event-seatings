**Quick answer**

A typical Chicago city lot is 25 feet wide, so a two-flat or greystone backyard is narrow. After the garage and a walk lane, expect room for roughly 20 to 40 guests seated, more if you stand or use a deck. Use a single row of round or long tables down the length of the yard, keep a 4-foot walk lane, and plan deliveries through the alley or gangway.

A Chicago city lot is a small, specific space. Many are 25 feet wide, with the building taking up most of the front and a garage at the back. What remains is a long, skinny rectangle of grass, deck or patio. That shape rewards a plan more than it rewards a big rental order. This guide shows how to use it.

## Know the space you really have

Measure your yard and subtract what is there already: the deck, stairs, grill, planters, a back porch and the walk lane to the gate. Rules of thumb: allow about 10 to 12 square feet per seated guest and 6 to 8 per standing guest, plus a walk lane at least 4 feet wide. These are planning figures, not code.

## What fits: three sample plans

<div class="table-wrap">

| Guests | Seating | Layout idea |
|---|---|---|
| 20 | Seated | Two 60-inch rounds or one long table plus a lounge corner |
| 40 | Seated, mixed | Four 60-inch rounds, a bar and a small lounge on the deck |
| 60 | Standing and sitting | Cocktail tables, a food station and seats for about half |

</div>

A 60-inch round seats 8 comfortably, so four rounds hold 32 and up to 40 tightly. For 60 guests on a narrow lot, no one should expect a full seated dinner. Mix seating with cocktail tables, and let the deck serve as a second room. Our [table count guide](/blog/how-many-tables-for-50-100-150-200-guests) and [round table sizes](/blog/round-table-sizes-and-seating-48-60-66-72-inch) help with the numbers.

## Long tables or rounds?

In a skinny yard, one long table down the center looks intentional and uses the space well. Rounds are more flexible and let people talk across them. A 96-inch banquet table seats 8 to 10; a farm-style table can feel more relaxed. If the yard is under about 18 feet wide, a single long table with a lane on each side may fit where rounds will not. Our [long tables vs round tables guide](/blog/long-tables-vs-round-tables-wedding) covers the trade-offs.

## Getting items in

Gangways and alley gates decide what you can order. Check our [alley delivery guide](/blog/chicago-alley-deliveries-event-rentals) for measuring the route, and confirm gate width before you choose 8-foot tables. If the only path is through the house, ask for chairs and 48 to 60-inch tables, which are far easier to carry in than long banquets.

## Decks, grass and uneven ground

Chicago backyards often mix a wood deck, patio pavers and a patch of grass. Chairs with narrow legs sink in grass and catch in deck gaps. Pick sturdier seats for soft ground and test one before the party. The [garden folding chair](/chairs/garden-folding) is a practical choice for a crowd, while the [rattan garden chair](/chairs/rattan-garden) suits a relaxed table of friends. If the yard is shady and damp after rain, consider a few boards or mats under the legs.

## Neighbors, noise and permits

A backyard party on private property usually does not need a Park District or special event permit. A tent over 20 x 20 feet may change that, and other rules can apply. See our [Chicago special event permit guide](/blog/chicago-special-event-permit-guide) and [Chicago tent permit guide](/blog/chicago-tent-permit). Tell neighbors about the date, the end time and the alley deliveries. Sound carries along the alley, so set a speaker facing into your yard, not toward the next building.

## Lighting for a narrow lot

Run string lights down the length of the yard, anchored to the garage and the building, to create a ceiling. Use low lights on the walk lane. Our [backyard party lighting ideas](/blog/backyard-party-lighting-ideas) have layouts that suit a skinny lot.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many people fit in a Chicago backyard?

Many city lots hold 20 to 40 seated guests, and more when people stand. Measure the yard, subtract the garage, deck and walk lane, then use 10 to 12 square feet per seated guest. Your real number depends on the lot.

### Do I need a permit for a backyard party in Chicago?

A normal party on private property generally does not need a special event permit. Large tents, street use or serving alcohol to the public can trigger rules. Check the City's special events guidance, and ask your alderman's office if unsure.

### Should I use round or long tables in a narrow yard?

Rounds are flexible and sociable; a single long table fits very narrow yards. If your yard is under about 18 feet wide, long tables are often easier. Check the gate and delivery path before you decide.

### How do I get rentals into a backyard with no alley?

Through the gangway or house. Measure widths and tell your rental provider. Smaller tables and stacking chairs travel best.

### What should I tell my neighbors?

The date, start and end times, delivery times and a contact number. Neighbors accept a loud afternoon more easily when they know when it ends.

### Can I put a tent on a two-flat backyard?

Sometimes. Size, anchoring and fire rules matter, and the space may not fit one. Read our tent size and permit guides first.
