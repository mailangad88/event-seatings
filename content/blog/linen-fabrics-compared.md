**Quick answer**

Polyester is the workhorse: durable, wrinkle resistant and available in the most colors. Lamour (a matte satin finish) looks more upscale and drapes well. Satin is shiny and glam but shows wrinkles and snags. Velvet and sequin are statement fabrics for accents. Real linen and burlap are textured and rustic and wrinkle easily. For a large guest list on a tight timeline, start with polyester or lamour and add one specialty piece.

</div>

Rental linens are sold under confusing names. Two cloths both called "ivory" can look nothing alike in a photo because one is flat polyester and the other is a satin with a sheen. Knowing the fabric lets you read a rental catalog, plan how it will look in a Chicago ballroom or tent, and avoid surprises on the day.

## Fabric by fabric

### Polyester
The default rental cloth. It resists wrinkles, takes a lot of washing and comes in a wide palette. It can look flat in photos, which is why planners add napkins, runners or overlays in a second fabric.

### Lamour
A soft, matte satin-finish polyester. It reads as upscale without the shine of satin, which makes it a favorite for weddings. It drapes in long lines on floor-length cloths.

### Satin
Shiny and light. It looks glam but shows every crease and can snag on rough table edges. Satin napkins and runners are common; full satin cloths work best when pressed on site.

### Velvet
Plush with a soft depth of color. Velvet suits Chicago fall and winter events and goes with gold or dark wood. It can crush or show marks, so handle it carefully and fold it gently. See [textured linens](/blog/textured-linens-for-events).

### Sequin
A statement. Used as an overlay or runner it catches light; as a whole cloth it can overpower. See [sequin linens for glam events](/blog/sequin-linens-for-glam-events).

### Linen
True linen has a lived-in texture and wrinkles easily, and that is part of its charm. Linen-look polyester gets the appearance with fewer wrinkles. Ask which one a supplier means.

### Burlap
Natural, coarse and rustic. It sheds fibers, so avoid dark tabletops and black clothing nearby, and use burlap runners instead of full cloths. See [farm table linens](/blog/farm-table-linens-runner-only).

## Chicago considerations

- **Humidity and heat.** Summer tent events soften lamour and satin; lightweight fabrics also blow more in the wind off the lake.
- **Cold.** Velvet and heavy overlays feel warmer in winter dinners.
- **Photos.** Matte fabrics photograph more evenly than shiny ones under flash.
- **Cleaning.** Delicate fabrics usually carry stricter care terms; read the policy in [linen stain and damage policies](/blog/linen-stain-and-damage-policies).

## Ask the right questions at the quote

Fabric names are marketing as much as description. When you compare rental quotes, ask each supplier these four questions so you are comparing like with like.

1. What is the fabric content, and is it 100 percent polyester or a blend?
2. Does it come pressed, or does it arrive folded with creases?
3. What is the thread count or weight, and does it show the table through the cloth?
4. Do you have a photo of the exact color on a table, not only a swatch?

Weight is worth a closer look. A thin cloth may show the table seams through it, particularly with white or ivory. A heavier cloth drapes well but is harder to press, so ask whether steaming is included. For floor-length cloths on a 72-inch round, heavier fabric hangs straight and moves less in a draft from a door or tent flap.

## Fabrics for the head table and details

Many planners use a different fabric on the head table, the cake table or the sweetheart table. A velvet runner or sequin overlay marks the couple's table without changing the rest of the room. Satin napkins at the head table are a subtle upgrade. Limit this to one or two special spots, so the room reads as a set. ## Frequently asked questions

### What is the difference between polyester and lamour linen?

Both are polyester, but lamour has a matte satin finish and softer drape. Basic polyester is flatter and more utilitarian. Lamour usually looks more expensive in photos.

### Which tablecloth fabric wrinkles least?

Polyester and lamour. They resist creasing and recover quickly, which is why most rental companies use them as standard.

### Is satin or lamour better for weddings?

Lamour for most tables, satin for accents. Satin's shine suits glam but shows wrinkles and snags more easily.

### Are real linen tablecloths worth renting?

If you want the natural look, yes. Expect wrinkles and ask whether the supplier irons or steams them. Linen-look polyester gives a similar appearance with fewer wrinkles.

### Can I mix fabrics on one table?

Yes. Use a matte base with a satin napkin, or lamour with a burlap runner. Keep to two or three textures per table.

### What fabric hides stains best?

Darker colors and patterned or textured fabrics hide marks better than white or ivory satin. If your menu includes red wine or sauce, avoid pale, shiny fabrics.
