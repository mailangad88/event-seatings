**Quick answer**

Plan 1 to 1.5 cups of hot cocoa per guest, served from a 50 or 100-cup urn or insulated dispensers kept above 140 degrees F, with 6 to 8 toppings and mugs or lidded cups. For 100 guests that is about 100 to 150 servings, or roughly 6 to 9 gallons of prepared cocoa. Put the bar near the entry or exit of a Chicago winter event so guests warm up as they arrive or leave.

A hot cocoa bar is a winter classic for winter weddings, holiday office parties, school fundraisers and church socials. It is simple, a crowd-pleaser for adults and kids alike and easy to scale. The key is keeping the cocoa hot, the line moving and the toppings tidy.

## Servings and quantities

A "cup" of cocoa at an event is usually 6 to 8 ounces. A gallon is 128 ounces, so a gallon makes about 16 six-ounce servings.

Gallons are a calculation at 16 six-ounce servings per gallon, so check your cocoa mix and urn sizes with the supplier. Mix cocoa hot, not in the urn, so it does not scorch or stick.

## Toppings and extras

Offer 6 to 8 toppings so guests can build their own:

- Mini marshmallows and big marshmallows
- Whipped cream
- Crushed peppermint or candy canes
- Chocolate shavings or chips
- Caramel or chocolate sauce
- Cinnamon sticks
- Sprinkles for kids
- Dairy-free milk and a nut-free option, labeled

Label allergens clearly, since toppings often contain nuts, dairy, gluten or soy. A signature touch, such as a flavored syrup or a pair of spiked options, needs a bartender; see [Illinois alcohol rules for private events](/blog/illinois-alcohol-rules-private-events). Many winter events keep it kid-friendly and use a separate [signature cocktail station](/blog/signature-cocktail-station-ideas) for adults.

## What to rent

1. **A 6-foot banquet table.** Use a floor-length cloth; see [tablecloth sizes](/blog/tablecloth-size-chart).
2. **A hot water or cocoa urn,** and an extension cord rated for it. See [coffee and tea service rental](/blog/coffee-and-tea-service-rental).
3. **Mugs or lidded paper cups.** Mugs look better and stay warmer; lidded cups are easier for walking.
4. **Jars, bowls and scoops** for toppings.
5. **Spoons or stir sticks,** napkins and a trash bin.
6. **A sign** with the cocoa recipe and allergens.
7. **A heater or heat lamp** for an outdoor station, with venue approval.

## Where to put it

At a winter wedding, put the cocoa bar near the entry, so guests warm up as they arrive, or near the exit as a send-off treat. For an outdoor ceremony, a station next to the seating keeps guests comfortable. Safety matters: a hot urn needs a stable table, kept away from children and aisles, with a drip tray underneath. See [bar placement in your floor plan](/blog/bar-placement-floor-plan) and [winter wedding seating](/blog/winter-wedding-seating).

## Keeping it hot and safe

Hot cocoa should stay above 140 degrees F, the general food safety threshold for hot holding. Do not leave a dairy-based drink out for hours. If your event is long, fill the urn in batches. Never leave a hot urn unattended near children, and keep a few towels nearby. For a school or community event, check the facility's food rules.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much hot cocoa do I need per person?

Plan 1 to 1.5 servings of 6 to 8 ounces. For 100 guests, that is 100 to 150 servings, or about 6 to 9 gallons.

### How big an urn do I need for a hot cocoa bar?

A 50-cup urn for 50 guests and a 100-cup urn for 100 guests. For larger crowds, use two urns so one can be refilled.

### What toppings should a hot cocoa bar have?

Marshmallows, whipped cream, peppermint, chocolate shavings, caramel and cinnamon sticks. Add sprinkles for kids and label allergens.

### How do I keep hot cocoa warm?

Use an insulated urn or dispenser and keep it plugged in. Cocoa should stay above 140 degrees F.

### Can I make a hot cocoa bar for adults?

Yes, with spiked options served by a licensed bartender. Keep the kids' and adults' versions separate and labeled.

### Where should a cocoa bar go at a winter wedding?

Near the entry or exit, away from the dance floor, with a stable table and a clear line space.
