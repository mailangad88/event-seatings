**Quick answer**

VFW posts, American Legion halls, fraternal lodges, parish halls and union halls around Chicago are some of the most affordable event spaces for birthdays, showers, graduation parties, anniversaries and celebrations of life. Before you book, ask what tables and chairs are included and how many, whether you must use the hall's bar, whether outside food and rentals are allowed, the setup and cleanup times, parking and accessibility. Community halls are the hidden backbone of Chicago-area celebrations. Many neighborhoods and suburbs have a VFW post, an American Legion hall, a Knights of Columbus council, an Elks or Moose lodge, or a cultural or union hall that rents its main room. They are practical rooms: a kitchen or serving area, a bar, folding tables and stacking chairs. With the right rentals, they can look like a different place.

## What these halls usually include, and what they do not

Every hall is different, so get a written list. Typical inclusions are the main room, folding or banquet tables, stacking chairs, a serving or kitchen area, restrooms and parking. What halls often do not include: table linens, upgraded chairs, decor, lighting beyond the overheads, AV, a dance floor and someone to set up and clean.

## Events these halls suit

Community halls are popular for [birthday parties](/events/birthday-parties), [showers](/events/showers), [graduation parties](/events/graduation-parties), anniversaries, [memorials and celebrations of life](/events/memorials), fundraisers and [quinceañeras](/events/quinceaneras). ## Timing and booking

Halls are busiest on Saturdays from May to June for graduations and showers, and in December for holiday parties. Many halls have regular member events, meetings or fish fries on set nights, so ask what else happens in the building that week. Check your date against the [Chicago event planning calendar](/blog/chicago-event-planning-calendar-month-by-month), and ask about access the day before so rentals can be delivered and set without a rush.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are VFW and American Legion halls open to non-members for events?

Many rent their halls to the public, though rules differ by post. Call the post directly and ask about availability, rates, deposits and any member requirements.

### Do I have to use the hall's bar?

At many halls, yes. Halls with a liquor license often require you to use their bar and bartenders. Ask before you plan drinks or hire a bartender.

### Can I bring my own caterer to a community hall?

Often, but not always. Some halls have an in-house caterer or kitchen rules. Ask about outside food, kitchen access and any fees.

### What size tables do community halls usually have?

Many have 6 or 8 foot rectangular folding tables, and some have rounds. Ask for the sizes so you can rent linens that fit; floor-length cloths hide folding legs.

### How do I make a VFW hall look nicer?

Start with floor-length linens, then upgrade the chairs or add covers, then add warm lighting and one backdrop. Those three layers change the room more than scattered decor.

### When should I book a hall for a graduation party?

As soon as you know the ceremony date. Halls and rentals fill on May and June Saturdays.
