Oswego sits on the Fox River in Kendall County, with a small historic core, big new neighborhoods and farmland on its edges. Its park district runs popular community events, from river floats to concerts. If you plan a wedding on a farm or a party at home, you will meet both village and county rules.

## Oswegoland Park District

The Oswegoland Park District runs the town's parks and a calendar of events that includes Oswego250 Floatin' Down the Fox, community concerts and picnics, and Boos at the Bridge in October. Because the district runs so many events, ask for its calendar when you pick a date. A shelter reservation near a district event can mean more traffic and parking pressure.

The Village of Oswego handles streets, sidewalks and village property. Ask the village what applies for block parties or events on public ground.

## Space types

- **Riverfront parks.** Check which agency manages the specific park you want before you book.
- **Community parks and shelters.** District reservations.
- **Downtown Oswego.** Village rules apply.
- **Farm and barn venues.** Common on the edge of town, in unincorporated Kendall County.
- **Private backyards.** Often large, with HOA rules in newer subdivisions.

## Farm venues and rural roads

Farm and barn venues can be lovely, and they come with logistics. Gravel roads and long driveways limit truck size. Grass lots can turn soft after rain. Power may be limited. Ask the host about the drop point, the distance to the setup, and whether a generator is needed. Our [barn wedding seating ideas](/blog/barn-wedding-seating-ideas) post covers layouts.

## Delivery from St. Charles

Oswego is a drive south through the Fox Valley, farther than Aurora and over rural roads at the end. We plan for a longer window, and we ask for GPS coordinates on farm venues. Rain can change access, so keep a backup plan.

## Planning a farm or backyard event around Oswego

Rural and semi-rural events reward preparation. A few habits help.

- **Walk the site a week before.** Look for slopes, soft spots, sprinkler heads and low branches.
- **Mark the drop point.** A flag or cone at the spot where trucks should stop prevents confusion.
- **Plan lights early.** Farm lots can be very dark. String lights and path lighting need power or a generator. See [string light layouts](/blog/string-light-layouts-for-events).
- **Bring flooring.** Grass under chairs and tables sinks and tilts. Flooring or leveling keeps tables steady.
- **Have a bug and weather plan.** Evening bugs and sudden storms are common in summer.

If you are driving from Chicago for the day, build in a rain plan with a covered space, and let your guests know whether to wear shoes for grass. Oswego events are often more relaxed than banquet-hall events, and a simple, well-lit setup often looks better than a crowded one.

## Keeping guests comfortable

Comfort decides whether an outdoor event feels good. Provide shade in the afternoon, fans or heaters for the shoulder seasons, and a place to sit for every guest who wants one. Bug control matters near the river and fields, so ask your host about citronella or a spraying service. Clear signs to restrooms and parking help rural events run smoothly.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who runs parks in Oswego?

The Oswegoland Park District. Ask it about shelters, riverfront parks and event calendars.

### What events does the district run?

It runs community events such as Oswego250 Floatin' Down the Fox, concerts in the park and Boos at the Bridge. Check its calendar before you pick a date.

### Is Oswego in Kendall County?

Yes, mostly, with some parts in Kane County. County rules apply outside village limits.

### Can I hold a wedding on a farm near Oswego?

Yes, many farm venues host weddings. Ask the venue about access, power and flooring.

### Do you deliver in Oswego?

Yes. First deliveries begin with the April 2027 Founding Season. Farm venues need GPS coordinates and a contact.

### What chairs suit a farm wedding?

Cross-back and rattan chairs suit rustic settings. Check ground conditions for any chair.
