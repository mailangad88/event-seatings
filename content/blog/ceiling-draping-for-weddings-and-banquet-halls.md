## What ceiling draping does

- **Hides the mess**: ductwork, pipes, tile and fluorescent panels.
- **Lowers a huge room** so it feels cozy, or creates the look of a tent.
- **Hosts lighting**: uplights, string lights and chandeliers sit in the folds.
- **Defines zones**: a drape over the dance floor tells guests where to look.

## Three common styles

| Style | Look | Good for | Notes |

| Center-point tent | Fabric rises to a peak | Dance floors, ceremony areas | Needs a strong center anchor |

| Panel canopy | Parallel strips | Large ballrooms | Needs a grid of anchors |

## What the venue has to allow

This is the part people miss. A rental drape is hung from something. Possible anchors include ceiling grid, trusses, I-beams, or freestanding frames. Many venues prohibit nails, tape, staples or hooks in the ceiling. Some require their own rigger or certified installer. Ask your venue these questions before you price anything:

<ol class="flow">
<li><strong>What is the ceiling height</strong> at the lowest and highest points?</li>
<li><strong>Where can we attach?</strong> Grid, rigging points, beams or none.</li>
<li><strong>Who may install?</strong> Venue crew, approved vendors or our own.</li>
<li><strong>What fire rules apply?</strong> Flame-retardant fabric, sprinkler clearance and heat limits.</li>
<li><strong>When can we load in?</strong> Ceiling work often takes hours before guests arrive.</li>
</ol>

## Fire and sprinkler rules

Fabric near sprinklers and smoke detectors is a safety issue. Venues and local fire departments set clearances and fabric requirements. Do not guess. Ask your venue for its written decor policy, and ask the rental company for fabric certification. In Chicago and suburban buildings, the rules vary by building and by village. Check with your venue and the village or city fire office.

## Sizing and fabric

Rental companies often quote ceiling draping by the square foot of ceiling covered or by the number of panels, so give them the room's dimensions. A rule of thumb is that fabric should be several times the covered area, because it is gathered. Ask how many yards of fabric the plan uses. White, ivory and champagne sheers brighten a room, while black and dark colors hide ceilings and make light stand out.

## Lighting inside the drape

Warm white uplights and string lights behind sheers create a glow. Chandeliers need a hanging point. See our [lighting and power resources](/blog/topic/lighting-and-power) and ask about power runs to the ceiling before you design.

## Pairing with the rest of the room

A drape works as a frame for the floor, so plan it with your [dance floor size](/blog/dance-floor-size-by-guest-count) and the [room transformation plan](/blog/banquet-hall-room-transformation-ideas). Chairs with a lot of detail, like the [Louis medallion](/chairs/louis-medallion), look best under soft, simple fabric.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Ordering without a site visit.
- Assuming you can hang from the ceiling grid.
- Using dark fabric in a low room.
- Forgetting that heat from lights can scorch cheap fabric.


## Frequently asked questions

### How much does ceiling draping cost?

Cost depends on the area covered, ceiling height, fabric, rigging and venue rules. We do not publish a number here. Request a quote with room dimensions.

### What is ceiling draping for weddings?

It is fabric hung across the ceiling to hide the room's structure and create a softer look. It is also used to hold lights and define a dance floor.

### Can ceiling draping be done in any venue?

No. The venue must allow attachment points, and some require their own rigger. Ask for the written decor policy.

### Is ceiling draping a fire hazard?

It can be if it is not flame retardant or if it blocks sprinklers and detectors. Ask for fabric certificates and confirm clearances with the venue and local fire office.

### How long does ceiling draping take to install?

Often several hours, depending on the room and design. Plan the install before other vendors enter the room.

### What colors work best for ceiling draping?

White, ivory and champagne make a room feel bright and soft. Black or dark colors hide ceilings and make lights stand out. Choose by room height and lighting.
