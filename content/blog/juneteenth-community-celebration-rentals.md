**Quick answer**

Juneteenth, observed on June 19, is celebrated in Chicago with cookouts, block parties, parades and festivals. For a community celebration, plan shade (tents or canopies), long tables for food and family seating, a stage or small riser for speakers and music, a kids zone, enough chairs for elders and a permit check with the Chicago Department of Cultural Affairs, the park district or your village before the day. Rule of thumb: one seat for about half your expected crowd at any one time.

</div>

Juneteenth commemorates June 19, 1865, when enslaved people in Galveston, Texas learned of their freedom. It is a federal holiday, and communities across Chicago and the suburbs mark it with parades, festivals, family cookouts and educational events. The City of Chicago lists Juneteenth events on its website, and many neighborhoods host their own. Whether you are planning a family cookout for 30 or a block party for 300, the planning questions are about shade, seating, food and permission.

## Plan around heat and shade

June in Chicago can be hot, humid or stormy. Plan for all three:

- **Shade.** Tents or canopies for the eating area and elders. See [what size tent do I need](/blog/what-size-tent-do-i-need) and [market umbrellas for shade](/blog/market-umbrellas-for-shade).
- **Cooling.** Fans, misting and water stations. See [misting and shade for summer events](/blog/misting-and-shade-for-summer-events) and [water station dispensers](/blog/water-station-infused-water-dispenser).
- **Storm plan.** A place to go if lightning appears, such as a building or a vehicle. See [rain plan with a tent](/blog/rain-plan-with-a-tent).

## Seating, tables and chairs

Plan seats for about half the crowd at any time, since people move between food, music and conversation. For a seated meal, plan one chair per guest. [Garden folding chairs](/chairs/garden-folding) ($5 to $7 estimated) are budget friendly for large crowds. [Cross-back chairs](/chairs/cross-back) ($8 to $11) work well for a head table or honored guests. 8-foot tables seat 8 to 10, and 6-foot tables seat 6 to 8. Read [chairs on grass, sand and gravel](/blog/chairs-on-grass-sand-gravel) if you set up on turf or gravel.

## Stage, sound and program

Block parties often include speakers, performers and a DJ. A simple 8 x 12-foot riser is enough for speakers, and live bands need more. Check power and noise rules. See [stage and riser sizes](/blog/stage-and-riser-sizes-for-events), [sound systems for speeches](/blog/sound-system-for-speeches) and [generator size for events](/blog/generator-size-for-events).

## Food, trash and restrooms

Cookouts mean grills, coolers and long lines. Keep the cooking zone away from tents and walkways, and plan trash and recycling stations. For 150 guests you will need more than one portable restroom, so see [how many portable restrooms per guests](/blog/how-many-portable-restrooms-per-guests) and [trash and recycling stations](/blog/trash-recycling-composting-stations-for-events).

## Chicago notes

- The City of Chicago posts Juneteenth events on its website, and neighborhoods such as Bronzeville host their own. Check dates, street closures and parking before you plan.
- Park district permits, village special event rules and alley use all differ. Ask early.
- Late June thunderstorms are common. Have a plan.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Juneteenth?

It is a holiday on June 19 that commemorates the end of slavery in the United States, based on events in Galveston, Texas in 1865. It is a federal holiday and is celebrated with community gatherings, food and music.

### Do I need a permit for a Juneteenth block party?

Often yes. Block parties on public streets usually need a city or village permit. Contact your municipality well in advance.

### How many chairs do I need for a cookout?

One chair per guest for a seated meal, or seats for about half your crowd for a drop-in event. Add extras for elders.

### How much shade do I need?

Plan shade for the eating area, the elders and the kids zone. A 20 x 20-foot tent covers about 25 to 30 seated guests.

### Can I rent a stage?

Yes. A riser or stage works for speakers and performers. Confirm power, permits and weight limits.

### How early should I book rentals?

As soon as your date and site are confirmed. June weekends are busy. See when to book.
