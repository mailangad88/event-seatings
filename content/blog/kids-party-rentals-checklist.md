**Quick answer**

A kids party needs five zones: a seating and eating area (child-size tables and chairs plus a few adult chairs), an activity zone (bounce house, games or crafts), a cake and gift station, a shaded rest spot for parents and a clean-up and restroom path. Plan roughly one adult for every 4 to 6 young children, and one chair for every child at the eating tables.

</div>

A kids party looks chaotic from the outside, but a good one runs on a quiet plan. Children eat in short bursts, move constantly and need a place to sit that fits their size, while the adults who came with them need a comfortable place to stand back and watch. This checklist covers the rentals that make a party for 20 to 60 children work, whether it is in a backyard in Naperville, a park shelter in Evanston or a banquet room anywhere in the Chicago area.

## Zone 1: Eating and seating

The eating zone is the heart of the party. Children need furniture sized for them, and an adult or two at each table to help.

- **Child tables.** Rectangular or round tables around 20 to 24 inches tall are common for young children. See [kids tables and chairs for events](/blog/kids-tables-and-chairs-for-events).
- **Child chairs.** Seat height of roughly 10 to 12 inches suits toddlers and preschoolers; ask your rental company for the exact sizes.
- **Adult chairs.** One for every adult, plus extras for grandparents. A few [garden folding chairs](/chairs/garden-folding) are an easy, light option.
- **High chairs.** For babies and young toddlers. Read about [high chairs and baby stations](/blog/high-chairs-and-baby-station-at-events).
- **Linens.** Paper or washable table covers; plan on spills.

A good rule of thumb is one child seat per child at the eating tables, with up to 6 children per table so an adult can reach everyone.

## Zone 2: Activity

Spread activities out so the most energetic area is away from the eating tables and the quiet corner. For inflatables, read [bounce house rental safety and space](/blog/bounce-house-rental-safety-and-space). For game stations, see [carnival games for parties](/blog/carnival-games-for-parties).

## Zone 3: Cake, gifts and favors

Plan one table for the cake and one for gifts and cards, plus a small station for favors on the way out. A cake table is often a 6-foot rectangular or a 48-inch round; check the exact fit for your cake and decor. See [card box and gift table ideas](/blog/card-box-and-gift-table-ideas) for a layout.

## Zone 4: Parent comfort

Parents who can sit in the shade relax, and relaxed parents make a smoother party. Provide a lounge corner with chairs and a table for coffee or drinks. A [market umbrella](/blog/market-umbrellas-for-shade) or tent helps in summer sun. For long parties, a few [lounge pieces](/blog/lounge-furniture-for-cocktail-hour) keep adults near the action.

## Zone 5: Restrooms and cleanup

Short trips to the bathroom happen often with children. If the party is at a park or field, check the facilities. If it is at home, plan the route, a hand-washing station and a spot for coats and bags. For larger gatherings, see [how many portable restrooms per guests](/blog/how-many-portable-restrooms-per-guests).

## The weather plan

Chicago spring and fall parties are often cooler than guests expect, and summer ones can be hot. Keep a covered area ready, whether a tent, pavilion or garage. A tent lets you keep the cake out of the sun and tables dry. For heat and sun, see [misting and shade for summer events](/blog/misting-and-shade-for-summer-events).

## Frequently asked questions

### What do I need to rent for a kids birthday party?

Child-size tables and chairs, adult seating, a cake table, shade and one or two activities such as a bounce house or lawn games. Add high chairs if babies are coming.

### How many tables do I need for 20 kids?

Plan 4 tables with 5 to 6 children each, or a few more if you use smaller tables. Keep an adult near each table.

### Do I need a tent for a kids party?

It is helpful, because it gives shade and rain cover, and it keeps food safe. Even a pop-up tent for the cake table helps.

### How much space does a kids party need?

A rule of thumb is about 20 to 25 square feet per child for a mix of seating and play, more with a bounce house. Confirm your venue's capacity.

### What is a good seating plan for toddlers?

Seat toddlers close to parents at low tables, with high chairs for babies. See our post on [high chairs and baby stations](/blog/high-chairs-and-baby-station-at-events).

### Should I rent adult chairs too?

Yes. Parents and grandparents stay for the whole event, so give them comfortable seats near the action.
