Cinco de Mayo is popular across Chicago, from Pilsen and Little Village to the suburbs and downtown offices. It also gets mixed up with Mexican Independence Day, so it helps to know what the day is. It marks the Mexican army's victory over French forces at Puebla in 1862, and it is celebrated more widely in the United States than in most of Mexico. Mexican Independence Day is September 16, and El Grito is the night of September 15. Share that with your guests, and keep the party respectful.

## Pick the date and place

May 5, 2027 falls on a Wednesday. A weeknight backyard party needs an early start. Many hosts pick the nearest weekend, or an office lunch on the day itself.

- **Backyard.** Room for a taco bar, music and kids' games. See [backyard party layout plan](/blog/backyard-party-layout-plan).
- **Office.** A lunch event in a conference room, a lobby or a parking lot with a tent. Check building rules for food and music.
- **Hall or park.** Check with your village or park district for any rules.

## Aguas frescas and drinks

Aguas frescas are light fruit drinks, such as horchata (rice and cinnamon), jamaica (hibiscus), tamarindo and fruit waters like watermelon or pineapple. Glass dispensers with a spigot commonly hold 2 to 5 gallons each. Ask the rental company for the size, and whether it has an ice core.

## Early May weather: shade and heat

Early May in Chicago can bring a cool, windy day near the lake or a hot afternoon inland. Plan for both.

<ol class="flow">
<li><strong>Check the forecast a week out.</strong> Then again two days before.</li>
<li><strong>Add shade.</strong> Market umbrellas or a tent for the food and the seating.</li>
<li><strong>Add warmth.</strong> A heater or sidewalls if the evening is cool.</li>
<li><strong>Keep food in the shade.</strong> Food left in the sun spoils faster.</li>
<li><strong>Have a rain plan.</strong> A tent or a garage.</li>
</ol>

## Music and dancing space

Mariachi, banda, cumbia and Latin pop all work. Choose the sound and plan for the space.

- **Clear a dance area.** About 10 square feet per dancer, away from the food line.
- **Place the speaker or DJ at one end.** Point it away from neighbors.
- **Check noise rules.** Ask your village about hours and limits.
- **Power.** Plan outlets and cords. See [event power needs checklist](/blog/event-power-needs-checklist).

## What most hosts find out too late

- **Deliveries on a weekday.** A Wednesday event means the rental crew drops off in the morning and picks up in the evening or the next day. Ask what time and where they will stage.
- **Book early.** Cinco de Mayo falls near graduations and Mother's Day. Check your date as soon as you can.
- **Count at delivery.** Verify chafing dishes, dispensers and tables against the quote.
- **Plan trash.** Taco bars create a lot of waste. See [trash and recycling stations](/blog/trash-recycling-composting-stations-for-events).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Cinco de Mayo Mexican Independence Day?

No. Cinco de Mayo, May 5, marks the Battle of Puebla in 1862. Mexican Independence Day is September 16, and El Grito is the night of September 15.

### What equipment do I need for a taco bar?

Chafing dishes for hot food, a tortilla warmer, cold pans for toppings, serving spoons and tongs. Plates, napkins and trash bins complete the line.

### How many tacos should I plan per person?

A common rule of thumb is 2 to 3 tacos per adult. Add rice, beans and chips, and plan more for a hungry crowd.

### How many aguas frescas dispensers do I need?

One dispenser for every 30 to 40 guests, with 2 or 3 flavors. Add water and ice for hot days.

### What is the weather like in early May in Chicago?

It varies from cool and windy to warm and sunny. Plan for shade and for a cool evening with a tent or heater.

### Can I host Cinco de Mayo at the office?

Yes. A lunch with a taco bar and drinks is easy. Check building rules for food, tents and music, and ask your landlord about outdoor space.
