Park Ridge is a close-in northwest suburb on the Chicago border, known for a charming downtown, a park district with many neighborhood parks and tree-lined blocks of single-family homes. That mix means a lot of parties happen at home, in modest rooms and small yards. The challenge is fitting guests, food and furniture into the space without feeling crowded. This guide gives you a square-footage method that shows what fits.

## How many people fit in your room

The method is simple: multiply length by width, then divide by the space per guest.

Furniture that stays in the room, such as sofas, bookcases and a fireplace, cuts the usable area. Measure only the clear floor. If a living room is 15 x 20 feet but a sofa takes 20 square feet, subtract it. For a buffet, add 40 to 50 square feet. For a dance floor, add 100 or more.

## Split the party across zones

Most Park Ridge parties work best when guests flow between zones.

1. **Indoors:** seating for older guests and the buffet.
2. **Porch or patio:** cocktail tables and a drink station.
3. **Yard:** rounds under a tent or umbrellas, plus kids' games.
4. **Garage:** a bar or a staging area for coats and strollers in cold months.

This turns a 30-person living room into a 70-person party. Our [guide to Northbrook backyards](/blog/planning-an-event-in-northbrook-il) shows a zone map, and the [chair count guide](/blog/how-many-chairs-do-i-need) helps you add up the seats.

## Using a park

Park Ridge has a park district with many parks and facilities. If you plan to host in a park or reserve a shelter, contact the Park Ridge Park District for rules on reservations, capacity, tents, alcohol and music. Most public sites limit what you can bring and when you can arrive. Delivery may require a short carry from a parking lot, so plan for extra time.

## Winter and holiday parties

Many Park Ridge parties happen in cold months. Plan a coat solution, such as a rack in a bedroom or the garage, and a boot tray at the door. Keep the walkway clear of snow and ice, and salt it before guests arrive. Our [winter seating guide](/blog/winter-wedding-seating) shows how to arrange a room when everyone arrives in coats.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many people fit in a 20 x 30 foot room?

About 50 seated at 12 square feet each, fewer with a buffet or furniture. Measure only the clear floor.

### How can I fit more guests in a small house?

Use more than one zone: porch, yard and indoors. Stagger arrival and food.

### What chair is best for a tight space?

A slim, stackable chair, like the bentwood or wishbone. Folding chairs store flat.

### Do I need a tent for a Park Ridge backyard party?

Not always, but it is a good weather backup. Check with the city about permits.

### Can I rent in a Park Ridge park?

Contact the Park Ridge Park District for reservation rules and what is allowed.

### How should I handle parking?

Share a parking plan with guests and neighbors, and check posted rules on your block.
