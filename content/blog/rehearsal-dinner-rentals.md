**Quick answer**

A rehearsal dinner usually hosts the wedding party, immediate family and out-of-town guests, often 20 to 60 people. Rent one chair per guest, long or round tables sized to the room, linens, dinnerware and glassware if the venue doesn't supply them. Choose a calmer, more casual look than the wedding, and book the pickup and delivery for the day before the wedding so it doesn't collide with your main setup.

In the Chicago area, the rehearsal dinner is the one event of the weekend where you can sit with everyone. It is smaller, less formal and often held at a restaurant, a family home or a rented room in the same suburb as the ceremony. This guide covers what to rent when the dinner is at home or in a space without furniture, and what to ask when it's at a restaurant.

## What to rent

**Seating.** One chair per guest, plus two spares for late arrivals. A casual wood chair suits the mood: a [cross-back](/chairs/cross-back), [bentwood](/chairs/bentwood) or [Windsor](/chairs/windsor) chair keeps the look warm and different from your wedding. If you plan to use the same style at the reception, consider renting the full set once and flipping it. See mixing chair styles for ideas.

**Tables.** Eight-foot banquet tables seat 8 to 10; 60-inch rounds seat 8. For 24 guests, three 8-foot tables placed end to end, or three rounds.

**Linens.** Floor-length is optional at this event. A lap-length cloth, a runner on a bare table or a neutral cloth with a cloth napkin looks relaxed. Use the [tablecloth size chart](/blog/tablecloth-size-chart).

**Dinnerware and glassware.** Many restaurants and caterers include these. For a home dinner, count a dinner plate, salad plate, forks, knife, water and wine glass per guest, and a dessert fork or spoon if you serve dessert. See [dinnerware for dinner parties at home](/blog/dinnerware-for-dinner-party-at-home).

**Extras.** A small bar table, a welcome sign table, a slideshow screen if you plan toasts with photos, and lighting for evenings that run late.

## Where rehearsal dinners are held

- **A restaurant private room:** the restaurant provides tables, chairs, plates and glassware. You usually rent nothing, or only decor.
- **A home or backyard:** you rent almost everything. Read the [backyard wedding rental checklist](/blog/backyard-wedding-rental-checklist) for the tent and power side.
- **A park district or community room:** furniture varies by facility. Ask what's included, and whether you can bring in outside caterers and rentals.
- **The wedding venue:** sometimes the venue offers the night before for a lower rate. Ask whether the chairs and tables stay in place between events.

## Layout and sightlines

Seat the couple and both sets of parents where toasts are easiest to hear. A long head table at one end, or two tables at right angles, lets speakers face the room. Leave 36 to 42 inches behind each chair so servers can pass.

## Chicago weekend notes

Friday evenings in Chicago and the western suburbs bring traffic, especially on the expressways, so build a buffer for deliveries and for guests driving in. If the dinner is downtown, ask the building about loading dock hours and elevator reservations. If it is in a suburb, ask your village or park district whether the room can be set up the afternoon before. Out-of-town guests often arrive hungry and tired, so keep the room easy to find, with a sign at the entrance and a small coat rack in colder months.

A good rule is to treat the rehearsal dinner as a dress rehearsal for your rentals. Notice which chairs are comfortable for a two-hour dinner, which linen color photographs well in your light, and whether the table width leaves enough room for plates and candles. Then adjust the wedding order if you need to.

If the same rental company handles both events, ask whether you can keep rehearsal chairs for the wedding and avoid two deliveries. Rehearsal dinner timing is also a good moment to settle how to flip a ceremony to reception.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who pays for the rehearsal dinner?

Traditionally the groom's family, but many couples and both families share the cost now. Decide early, because it affects the guest list and the rental budget.

### How many people attend a rehearsal dinner?

It usually includes the wedding party, both sets of parents, immediate family and out-of-town guests, often 20 to 60 people. Some couples invite everyone traveling in.

### Do I need to rent chairs for a rehearsal dinner at a restaurant?

Usually not, because the restaurant supplies seating. Rent only if you want a different look or the private room has no chairs. Always confirm in writing.

### Should the rehearsal dinner match the wedding style?

No. A more relaxed look sets it apart. Lap-length linens, wood chairs and simple candles are common choices.

### How many tables do I need for 30 guests?

Four 60-inch rounds seat 32, or four 8-foot tables seat 32 to 40 at a tighter squeeze. Add a small table for drinks.

### Can I keep the chairs for the wedding?

Often yes. Ask your rental company about a single delivery that covers both events. It can save delivery time, but check your pickup date.
