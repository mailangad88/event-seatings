A church or pastor anniversary is part worship, part homecoming and often part fundraiser. The planning pain is the number of volunteers and the long list of moving parts: guest choirs, visiting pastors, a program book, dinner for hundreds, and a sanctuary that has to be ready for service and then flipped for a meal. Without a committee and a clear timeline, the same five people end up doing everything. Here is a manageable plan.

## What the celebration is for

A church anniversary marks the founding of the congregation, and a pastor's anniversary honors the years of a pastor's service. Many Black churches and other congregations hold a week of events culminating in an anniversary Sunday, often with a guest preacher, a visiting choir and a dinner. Some churches combine it with a homecoming, welcoming back former members. Other denominations mark the occasion with a Mass or special liturgy and a parish dinner. Because each congregation has its own customs, begin by asking church elders what past celebrations looked like and what the leadership hopes this one will be.

Set the purpose in a sentence. Is it a worship celebration, a fundraiser for the building, a thank you to the pastor, or all three? The answer shapes the budget, the guest list and the format.

## Roles to fill

**Chair and co-chair.** Own the timeline and the budget. **Program committee.** Plan the order of service, music, scripture readings and tributes. **Hospitality.** Greeters, ushers, parking and the guest speaker's needs. **Food.** Menu, cooks or caterer, servers and cleanup. **Journal.** Collect ads, tributes and photos and print the souvenir book. **Finance.** Track tickets, donations and expenses. **Decor.** Colors, flowers and the fellowship hall. **Communications.** Invitations, flyers and social media.

## The souvenir journal and fundraising

Many congregations print a souvenir journal with the order of service, a history of the church, tributes and paid ads from members, families and local businesses. It can fund much of the event. Set ad sizes and prices early, give a deadline and offer a template. Sell tickets for the dinner at a price that covers cost, and offer a donation option for those who cannot attend. Be clear that donations are voluntary and follow any rules your church has.

## Service and dinner flow

The sanctuary and hall have to be turned over smoothly. If the dinner is in the same building, recruit a team to set tables during the service, or schedule the dinner for a later hour. Seat honored guests, the pastor's family and visiting clergy at a head table or near the front. Plan a quick blessing before the meal and a short program with tributes and gifts. ## Details that save the day

Send a program of the day to everyone involved so timing is clear. Provide a green room or quiet space for the guest speaker, with water and a snack. Arrange parking and wheelchair access, and add shuttle service if needed. Plan a professional or volunteer photographer. Count the offering carefully with two people. Thank guests and volunteers in writing afterward. Keep a binder for next time, with contacts, costs and lessons, so the next anniversary starts ahead.

## Chicago-area notes

Chicago churches range from neighborhood storefronts to large cathedrals, and many fellowship halls are small, so a large dinner may need a rented banquet hall or a tent. Check capacity and parking, since street parking near churches on Sunday is often tight, and some neighborhoods have restrictions. In winter, plan for a coat room and for a service that runs long, which can push back dinner. In summer, a tent outside with fans can add space; read the [Chicago tent permit guide](/blog/chicago-tent-permit) and check with your own village or park district. ## Frequently asked questions

### How do you plan a church anniversary celebration?

Form a committee, set the date and theme, invite the speaker and choirs, plan the service, journal and dinner, and assign leaders. Start six to nine months ahead.

### What is included in a church anniversary program?

Often a processional, praise and worship, scripture, a welcome, music from guest choirs, a message from the guest speaker, tributes and a closing. Order varies by denomination.

### What is a souvenir journal?

A printed book with the program, church history, tributes and paid ads from members and local businesses. It is a common way to fund an anniversary.

### How much does a church anniversary dinner cost?

It depends on the guest count, menu and venue. Ask for quotes, price tickets to cover costs and add a cushion for the unexpected.

### How many people should I plan for?

Look at past attendance and add the guests and visiting congregations you expect. Many anniversaries draw 100 to 500, so be ready for a range.

### Who sits at the head table at a church anniversary?

Usually the pastor and spouse, the guest speaker, visiting clergy and honored guests. Ask your leadership for the order.

### Can we hold the dinner in the sanctuary?

Some congregations do, but many use a fellowship hall or an outside hall. Check your church rules and fire code.

### What rentals do I need?

Tables, chairs, linens, serving ware, a head table, a podium and sometimes a tent. Count by dinner guests and ask your church what you already have.
