## Where the chair comes from

The Chiavari chair is named for Chiavari, a town on the coast of Liguria in northwest Italy. According to published histories, cabinetmaker Giuseppe Gaetano Descalzi reworked a French Empire-style chair in 1807 at the request of Marquis Stefano Rivarola. The classic version had a seat woven from strips of purple willow and a frame in wood such as cherry or maple. It was meant to be light enough for one hand to carry and sturdy enough for regular use, which is why it later became a staple of ballrooms and weddings around the world.

Most Chiavari-style chairs rented for events today are made from modern materials, and they come in several colors. The look, a slim frame with a ladder or tiffany back and a padded seat, is what matters for design.

1. **Material.** Wood, resin and steel versions exist. Ask what you are getting, because weight, finish and feel differ. Don't assume a rated capacity; ask for it. See [chair weight capacity and seat sizes](/blog/chair-weight-capacity-and-seat-sizes).
2. **Cushion.** Usually sold with the chair or separately. Ask whether it is included in the price, and what color and thickness it is.
3. **Color.** Gold, silver, white and black are the typical options. Ask to see photos of the real chairs.
4. **Count and spares.** Order your guest count plus the head table and spares. See [how many chairs do I need](/blog/how-many-chairs-do-i-need).
5. **Delivery.** Ask about stairs, elevators, docks and the delivery window. Downtown Chicago buildings often have freight elevator and loading dock rules, so ask your venue.
6. **Damage terms.** What counts as damage and how it is charged.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a Chiavari chair?

It is a lightweight chair with a slim frame and a ladder or tiffany-style back, named for Chiavari, Italy. It is common at weddings and galas.

### Does Beyond Chiavari rent Chiavari chairs?

Not currently. Our catalog offers alternatives, including the gold infinity, Louis medallion and ghost chairs, designed for formal rooms.

### How much does Chiavari chair rental cost in Chicago?

It varies by company, quantity, cushion and delivery. Ask for an itemized quote, and see our cost guide for what to compare.

### Are Chiavari chairs comfortable?

They are comfortable with a cushion for a normal dinner, and less so without one. Ask whether a cushion is included.

### Are Chiavari chairs good for outdoor weddings?

They are used outdoors often, but the legs are slim, so they need firm ground. See [chairs on grass, sand and gravel](/blog/chairs-on-grass-sand-gravel).

### How many Chiavari chairs can I stack?

It depends on the model and the company's handling. Ask for the recommended stack height.

### What is the difference between Chiavari and tiffany chairs?

The terms are used loosely, and many companies use them for similar ladder-back chairs. Ask for photos to see exactly what you are getting.
