Marquee letters are big light-up characters with bulbs, often spelling LOVE, BAR or the couple's initials. Both are popular for Chicago weddings, quinceaneras, birthdays and brand events, and both need power, a good location and a clear plan for placement.

## Neon, LED neon and marquee: the difference

<div class="table-wrap">

| Type | How it works | Look | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Glass neon | Gas in glass tubing | Classic glow | Fragile, gets warm |
| LED neon | LED strip in silicone | Similar glow | Lighter, safer to move |
| Marquee letters | Bulbs in a metal or plastic frame | Vintage carnival | Tall, heavy |
| Light-up backdrop | LED panel | Bold, bright | Needs frame |

</div>

Most rental neon signs today are LED neon, because it is lighter and survives transport. Ask for the type in the quote.

## What can you spell?

Common choices: couple's names or initials, LOVE, MR and MRS, BAR, CHEERS, HAPPY BIRTHDAY, a hashtag, or a company name. Custom neon takes time to make. Rentals are usually a fixed catalog, so check which words are in stock before you design the rest of the room around a phrase.

## Power, safety and setup

Neon needs a nearby outlet. Glass neon can be fragile and warm, and LED neon draws less power. Ask the rental company what the sign draws, then check with the venue about circuits, since a DJ, a band and food stations can share one circuit. Use cord covers where guests walk. Do not drape fabric over a sign. Mounting options include a frame or stand, a drape, or a wall mount if the venue allows it. Never hang from a weak wall or a tent pole without checking the weight rating. See our [lighting and power resources](/blog/topic/lighting-and-power) for planning outlets and generators.

## Marquee letters

Marquee letters stand on the floor or on a table. They are heavy at the large sizes and need two people to carry. Large sets like LOVE or BAR look best against a darker backdrop or in a dim room, because daylight washes out the bulbs. If you use them outdoors, check whether the bulbs are rated for rain, and plan for wind.

## Pair with a backdrop and stage

A neon sign over a [flower wall](/blog/flower-wall-rental-guide) or on a [pipe and drape backdrop](/blog/pipe-and-drape-uses-and-sizes) is the classic photo corner. Chairs: a [velvet chair](/chairs/velvet-dining) in front makes a good portrait seat. For corporate events, neon with the company name is a simple way to brand a lobby or reception.

## Chicago note

Winter events in Chicago have long evenings, so lit signs show well even in the late afternoon. In summer, an outdoor 7 PM event will be bright for a while, so plan neon for after dusk or inside a tent. Check your local sunset time rather than guessing.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Placing a sign near a window with strong light.
- Running cords through guest traffic.
- Ordering letters that do not fit the room's ceiling.
- Assuming the venue has a free outlet.


## Frequently asked questions

### What is the difference between neon and marquee letters?

Neon is a thin glowing line, often in a word or name. Marquee letters are large light-up characters with bulbs. Neon is smaller, marquee letters can be taller than a person.

### Are rental neon signs real neon?

Most are LED neon, which looks similar but is lighter and safer. Ask your rental company which type you get.

### How tall are marquee letters?

Common sizes range from about 48 to 96 inches. Larger letters need two people to carry and a dim background.

### Do neon signs need electricity?

Yes, each sign needs an outlet. Check with the venue about circuits and use cord covers.

### Can marquee letters be used outdoors?

Only if they are rated for it. Wind, rain and daylight all affect them. Ask the rental company.

### Where is the best place for a neon sign?

Behind the head table, over the bar, or in a photo corner with a drape or flower wall.
