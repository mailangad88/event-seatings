**Quick answer**

A brunch needs fewer plates but more cups than dinner. Plan 1 to 2 plates (a 8 to 10-inch plate and a small plate for pastries), 1 to 2 coffee cups or mugs, 1 juice or water glass and 1 fork and knife per guest. Add small bowls for yogurt or fruit if you serve them. Brunch buffets work best with plates at one end, hot items in the middle and drinks on a separate table.

</div>

Brunch is the easiest meal to host and the easiest to under-supply. People hold a mug, a mimosa and a plate, so the table needs room and the count needs cushion. In Chicago, brunch is a way of life, from bridal showers in the suburbs to baby showers in a Lincoln Park apartment, and this guide helps you rent the right pieces.

## Buffet layout

Brunch buffets are best arranged in the order guests will eat: plates, hot dishes, cold dishes, pastries, flatware and napkins. Put beverages on a separate table so the line does not stall. For 40 or more guests, set up two sides of the same table so two lines move at once.

<li><strong>Plates</strong> at the start of the line.</li>
<li><strong>Hot dishes</strong>: eggs, potatoes, bacon.</li>
<li><strong>Cold dishes</strong>: salads, fruit, yogurt.</li>
<li><strong>Pastries</strong> and bread.</li>
<li><strong>Flatware and napkins</strong> at the end, so hands stay free.</li>
</ol>

## Mugs, cups and saucers

Mugs are casual and hold more. Cups and saucers look more formal and work for showers and tea parties. If you serve both coffee and tea, add a second round of cups or let guests reuse. Count about 1.5 cups per guest as a rule of thumb. See [china rental guide](/blog/china-rental-guide-what-pieces-exist) for the other pieces.

## Mood and color

Brunch is a good place for color, as in [colored and patterned plates](/blog/colored-and-patterned-plates-trend). Light linens, fresh flowers and simple glassware give a bright feel. Chairs matter too: lighter chairs suit daytime light, such as [ghost chairs](/chairs/ghost) or [rattan garden chairs](/chairs/rattan-garden).

## Tables and seating

A brunch can be seated or stand-up. For a seated brunch of 30, plan 4 rounds of 8 at 60 inches. For a standing one, add cocktail tables for plates and drinks. Count extra tables for the buffet, drinks and cake. See our [tablecloth size chart](/blog/tablecloth-size-chart) for cloths.

## Chicago timing

Chicago brunch parties are popular in warm months and cozy in winter. Patio brunches in the summer need shade and wind plans, and you should check any park permit or venue rules for glass and alcohol. If you serve alcohol, confirm your venue's policy and any local rules.

## Cleanup

Brunch plates carry syrup, eggs and cheese, which harden fast. Scrape and stack as you clear, and follow the return instructions in [do you wash rental dishes](/blog/do-you-wash-rental-dishes).

## Brunch extras worth renting

Brunch benefits from a few small pieces: a pitcher for juice, a tiered tray for pastries, a bowl for fruit, a cream and sugar set, and a tea basket. A coffee station with urns, cups, stir sticks and napkins frees the host. Use risers to give the table height, and keep warm dishes on trivets. These extras take little space and make a spread look finished.

## Weather and venue notes

Spring and fall brunches in the Chicago area can swing from sunny to chilly in an afternoon, so plan an indoor fallback for plates and drinks. Light plates and napkins blow around outdoors, so use weights or heavier pieces on patio tables. Ask your venue about start times, because many halls need setup by 8 or 9 am for a late-morning brunch, and delivery must fit before that.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many plates do I need for a brunch?

Plan 1 to 2 plates per guest, plus a small plate for pastries. Add a cushion of 5 to 10%.

### How many mugs or cups per guest?

About 1 to 2. People refill, set down cups and grab another, so a cushion helps.

### Do I need flutes for mimosas?

Flutes are traditional, but a wine glass works. Check what your rental company offers.

### How should I set up a brunch buffet?

Plates first, then hot dishes, cold dishes, pastries, flatware and napkins. Put drinks on a separate table to keep the line moving.

### Can I use paper plates for brunch?

Yes, for casual brunches. Compare in [compostable vs rented china](/blog/compostable-vs-rented-china).

### How early should I order?

As early as the date and headcount are set. Spring weekends fill quickly.
