**Quick answer**

Match the setup to the guest list, not the number. A 30th usually works as a cocktail party with lounge seating and a few high tops, a 40th as a seated dinner with a bar, a 50th as a seated dinner with a toast and a dance floor, and a 60th as a seated dinner or brunch with a slideshow and comfortable chairs. Plan one seat per guest for dinners and about 50 percent for cocktail-style parties.

</div>

Adult milestone birthdays are the least standardized parties there are. The guest list can be 20 college friends or 100 relatives across four generations. The guest of honor might want a rooftop, a backyard or a private room. The number on the cake is a hint about who's coming and what they need, and rentals follow.

## What each decade usually needs

<div class="table-wrap">

| Birthday | Typical vibe | Seating share | Key rentals |
|---|---|---|---|
| 30th | Cocktail party, mingling | 40 to 50% | High tops, lounge sofas, photo wall |
| 40th | Dinner plus drinks | 80 to 100% | Round tables, bar, dance floor |
| 50th | Dinner, toasts, dancing | 100% | Round tables, linens, stage or mic |
| 60th | Dinner or brunch, family | 100% | Comfortable chairs, long tables, slideshow |

These are tendencies, not rules. A 60th that is a golf outing and burger night needs far fewer chairs than a 30th with a seated tasting menu.

### 30th: cocktail and lounge
Keep the room open. A few sofas and accent chairs make conversation areas, and 42-inch cocktail tables give people somewhere to put a drink. Read [lounge furniture for cocktail hour](/blog/lounge-furniture-for-cocktail-hour) for ideas, and the [cocktail table guide](/blog/how-many-cocktail-tables-for-standing-reception) once you've counted guests. Add a photo backdrop and string lights.

### 40th: dinner with a bar
A seated dinner with a real bar suits a 40th. Use 60-inch rounds with 8 guests or 72-inch rounds with 10. [Cross-back chairs](/chairs/cross-back) (estimated $8 to $11) look relaxed and polished. Set a dance floor if there will be dancing. For how big, read [dance floor size by guest count](/blog/dance-floor-size-by-guest-count).

### 50th: a toast and a stage moment
Fifty usually means friends from different decades in one room. Assign tables so cousins and college friends mix or stay together by choice. Plan a spot for the toast, with a microphone and a clear sightline. Tables closest to the microphone should hold the guest of honor's family.

### 60th: comfort and sightlines
Comfort rules. Choose chairs with a firm back, wider seats and, if possible, arms. Cushions help with long dinners. Keep the distance from the door to tables short, and avoid steps. If you're doing a slideshow, place the screen where every table can see it, and keep the lights low only after dessert.

## Chicago notes

Rooftops, restaurants and breweries host plenty of Chicago milestone parties, and each one sets its own rules on outside rentals. Ask the venue what furniture and linens it provides, and what it allows you to bring. At home, think about weather and plan for a tent or an indoor fallback. Sunset arrives late in June and early in December, so decide whether lighting will matter before you order. See [backyard lighting ideas](/blog/backyard-party-lighting-ideas).

## Frequently asked questions

### How many chairs do I need for a 40th birthday party?

For a seated dinner, one chair per guest. For 50 guests that means 50 chairs plus a couple of spares. For a cocktail-style party, rent about half as many.

### What is the best setup for a 50th birthday?

A seated dinner with round tables, a bar, a short toast and a dance floor. Add a slideshow or photo display for a personal touch.

### Should I do a cocktail party or a dinner?

Choose a cocktail party if the group is under 40 and likes mingling. Choose dinner if guests are traveling, if families are coming, or if you want speeches.

### How much space do I need per guest?

About 10 to 12 square feet per guest for a seated dinner, and 6 to 8 square feet for a standing cocktail party. These are common planning rules of thumb.

### What chairs are best for older guests at a 60th?

Chairs with firm backs and a wide seat, and padded or cushioned if possible. Avoid chairs that tip easily or have low seats. Ask during a quote about cushions.

### Can I mix lounge and dinner seating?

Yes. Put lounge seating at one end for early arrivals and late-night chat, and use the dinner tables as the core. Lounge sofas can also hold people who don't want to dance.
