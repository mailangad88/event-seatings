## Using Arlington Heights parks and public spaces

The Arlington Heights Park District runs many parks and facilities. If you plan to rent a pavilion or shelter for a graduation, shower or reunion, contact the park district for reservation rules, capacity and what is allowed, including tents, alcohol and decorations. Park shelters can have fixed picnic tables, so ask what is already there before you order extra tables.

For ceremonies on open lawn, ask whether chairs can be set on grass, how early you can arrive, and where deliveries can park.

## Common mistakes in Arlington Heights party planning

1. **Ordering by guest count only.** Add chairs for head tables, lounges and ceremonies.
2. **Skipping the venue's list.** You may already have tables and chairs included.
3. **Not asking about stairs.** Upstairs rooms add time and cost.
4. **Forgetting weather.** An outdoor party in Chicago needs a rain plan.

For colder dates, our [winter wedding seating guide](/blog/winter-wedding-seating) has ideas.

## Stretching the budget without losing the look

Chairs are the one rental every guest touches, so spend where people look. A common approach is to use a lower-cost chair for the ceremony and cocktail hour, then a nicer chair for the reception. Another is to upgrade only the head table and keep the rest simple. Either way, the same chair can be used twice if you plan the flip: ceremony guests sit in the garden folding chairs, then staff move them to the reception tables while guests have cocktails.

Linens are the next lever. A plain floor-length cloth in white or ivory with a colored napkin gives more variety than changing every cloth. Our [tablecloth size chart](/blog/tablecloth-size-chart) shows which cloth goes with which table. For dishes and glassware, the cost depends on the quantity and the pieces, so ask for a quote instead of guessing from a list.

Finally, count what is already included. Many banquet halls and park shelters supply tables, and some supply basic chairs. Renting only what the venue lacks can cut the order by a third or more, though the real saving depends on your venue.

### Do Arlington Heights park shelters include tables and chairs?

Many have fixed picnic tables, but it varies by site. Ask the Arlington Heights Park District what is included, and tell us what you need so we can fill the gaps.

### What is the best chair for a backyard wedding?

The cross-back chair is a favorite for backyards because its warm wood suits grass, string lights and farm tables. Use foot pads on soft ground.

### Can I mix chair styles?

Yes. Many couples use one style for guests and an upgraded style for the head table. Keep a common color or material so it looks planned.
