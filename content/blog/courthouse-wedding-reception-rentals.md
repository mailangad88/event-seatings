**Quick answer**

A courthouse wedding reception is a dinner or party after a short civil ceremony, usually for 10 to 40 guests at a restaurant private room or at home. At a restaurant you may rent nothing but decor. At home, rent one chair per guest, a table for each 8 to 10, simple linens, dinnerware and glassware, and plan about 10 to 12 square feet per guest. Check the courthouse and county clerk for ceremony rules before you plan the timing.

</div>

Many Chicago couples marry at a courthouse or a civil ceremony office and then celebrate the same day. The ceremony takes minutes, so the reception carries the day. That calls for a rental list that is short, flexible and easy on the budget. This guide covers both common options: a restaurant and a home.

## Plan the day around the ceremony

Ask the county clerk or courthouse about ceremony hours, how many guests can attend and what you must bring. Rules, hours and fees differ by county and building, so confirm directly with the Cook County Clerk or the clerk for the county where you marry rather than relying on a blog. Then pick a reception time with a buffer, such as 2 to 3 hours after the ceremony, for photos and travel.

## Option 1: a restaurant private room

The restaurant provides tables, chairs, plates, glassware and servers. What you might add:

- A different chair for the couple's seat or a long table. See [sweetheart table ideas](/blog/sweetheart-table-chair-ideas).
- Linens, if the restaurant uses bare tables and allows overlays or runners.
- A welcome sign, easels and a guest book table.
- A photo spot with a backdrop.

Ask the restaurant what they provide, whether they allow outside decor, the cake or dessert policy and their final headcount date. Many restaurants set minimum spends for private rooms. This option often needs no rental truck at all.

## Choose a chair that works for photos

Because the ceremony was brief, the reception photos carry the memory. A wood [cross-back chair](/chairs/cross-back), clear [ghost chair](/chairs/ghost) or [velvet dining chair](/chairs/velvet-dining) all photograph well at a long table. For a home dinner, keep the chairs consistent across the table and avoid mixing in kitchen chairs.

## Layouts

For 10 to 14 guests, a single long table. For 20 to 30, two long tables or three rounds. For 40, five rounds. Leave a 36 to 42 inch gap behind each chair so people can pass. See [micro wedding rentals](/blog/micro-wedding-rentals) for layouts.

## A realistic budget

A reception after a courthouse wedding costs less than a full wedding because the guest list and rental list are smaller. The largest costs are usually the dinner itself and the room. For rentals, the minimums and delivery fees matter more than the per-piece price, so ask about both. See our [wedding rental budget breakdown](/blog/wedding-rental-budget-breakdown) for how to compare quotes.

## Chicago notes

Downtown courthouses and nearby restaurants make a short trip, but traffic and parking can stretch the travel time. If you are renting a home or a hall, check parking, building rules and delivery windows. Winter courthouse weddings need coat racks at the reception and a warm entry. Confirm the venue's rules on decor and candles.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I have a reception after a courthouse wedding?

Yes. Many couples go straight to a restaurant, a home or a rented room. Plan the timing around the courthouse ceremony and travel.

### How many guests can attend a courthouse wedding?

It depends on the courthouse and county. Check with the clerk. Many couples bring two witnesses and add a larger gathering at the reception.

### What do I need to rent for a home reception?

Chairs, tables, linens, dinnerware, flatware and glassware, plus a bar table if needed. Rent one chair per guest and a few spares.

### Do I need to rent anything at a restaurant?

Usually not. Add decor or linens if the restaurant allows. Always ask what they provide.

### How far ahead should I book rentals?

Two to four weeks is workable for small orders, longer for peak seasons. Confirm delivery for the morning.

### Is a courthouse wedding cheaper?

The ceremony costs less, and a small reception lowers the total. Rental minimums can still apply.
