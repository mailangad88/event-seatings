**Quick answer**

Start talking to a rental company 6 to 9 months before a wedding, and as early as you can for peak dates in September and October in the Chicago area. Reserve standard colors by then, pick exact colors and fabrics 2 to 3 months out, and lock final counts about 2 to 4 weeks before the event. Smaller or off-peak events can order closer, but specialty fabrics and colors sell out first. This is general planning guidance; ask your supplier for their own deadlines.

</div>

Linens feel like a last-minute detail, which is why they often are. But popular colors, specialty fabrics and large quantities are limited, and the busiest weekends fill early. A simple timeline keeps you from scrambling. The advice below is a planning guide, not a rule from any one rental company, so always confirm cutoffs in writing.

## Why timing matters

### Peak season fills early
In the Chicago area, weekends in late spring, summer and early fall are the busiest for weddings and events. Venues, florists and rental companies all book those dates first. See [booking guides](/blog/topic/budget-and-booking) for the same logic applied to chairs.

### Specialty items are limited
Velvet, sequin, lace and unusual colors are stocked in smaller quantities than white and ivory. Ask early what is in stock for your date and how many are available. A plan for 20 sequin runners might need a backup if the supplier has only 12.

### Changes need a window
Counts change as RSVPs come in. Most suppliers allow reductions and additions until a cutoff. Ask for the cutoff date and put it on your calendar.

## Off-peak and small events

A weekday dinner or a small backyard party can often be ordered a few weeks ahead. Still, ask early for any color or fabric that is not basic white, black or ivory, and give the supplier time to prep. For short-notice needs, see [Budget & Booking guides](/blog/topic/budget-and-booking), which covers the same pressure for chairs.

## What can go wrong when you order late

- **The color you wanted is gone.** You end up with a close substitute.
- **Not enough of a size.** Large banquet cloths or 132-inch rounds can be limited.
- **Rush fees or limited delivery windows.** Late orders may not get the time you want.
- **No time to view swatches.** You choose from a screen.
- **Mismatched house and rental linens** because the venue's palette was not checked.

## Ask these questions

1. What is your cutoff to add or remove items?
2. Do you hold a date with a deposit, and what does it cover?
3. When do you deliver, and what access do you need?
4. How soon can I see swatches?
5. What happens if an item is out of stock?

Put the answers in writing. See the [linen ordering checklist](/blog/linen-ordering-checklist) for the full list of items to order.

## Chicago timing tips

Hotels and downtown venues often schedule deliveries to loading docks and freight elevators, and the windows can be narrow. Suburban banquet halls and barns may have more flexibility, but weekend demand is strong. For outdoor and tent events, add time to confirm the setup order: tent, tables, chairs, linens. Tell your supplier the day and hour linens can first enter the space.

## Frequently asked questions

### How far in advance should I order wedding linens?

Talk to suppliers 6 to 9 months before your wedding, sooner for peak dates, and finalize colors and counts closer in. Ask each company for its own deadlines.

### Can I order linens the week of my event?

Sometimes, for basic colors and small orders, but you have fewer choices. Specialty fabrics and large counts are hard to find on short notice.

### When do I need my final guest count for linens?

Most suppliers want it 2 to 4 weeks before the event. Ask for their exact cutoff and plan RSVPs to hit it.

### Is it too early to order linens a year ahead?

Not for a conversation. You can ask about availability and hold a date, but many details, like exact counts, will not be set until closer to the event.

### What if my guest count changes after I order?

Most suppliers allow changes until a cutoff. Reductions and additions after that may not be possible or may cost more, so ask in advance.

### Do I need to order linens before choosing flowers?

Not necessarily, but your palette should be chosen with both in mind. Flowers and linens often follow the same color decisions, so talk to both vendors early.
