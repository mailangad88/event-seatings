If you host on a normal Saturday in June, you compete with weddings. If you host on the Saturday of a long holiday weekend, you compete with weddings, graduation parties, family reunions, block parties and every backyard in Chicagoland. The rental companies only have so many trucks, so many crews and so many tables. Those weekends fill up.

This guide explains why, how the schedule works when the Monday is a holiday, and what to do to get what you want. It pairs well with our guides on [peak-season event rentals in Chicago](/blog/peak-season-event-rentals-chicago) and [Fourth of July backyard party rentals](/blog/fourth-of-july-backyard-party-rentals).

## Why holiday weekends sell out

Three things stack up.

- **Everyone wants the same days.** The Saturday, Sunday or the holiday itself are the favorites. A rental company can only deliver so many orders in a day.
- **Stock is fixed.** A company that owns 400 chairs cannot rent 600. Tents are scarcer still, because each one needs a crew and a truck.
- **Long weekends take a day away.** A rental company has fewer working days to get everything out and back. If the Monday is a holiday, many are closed or running a short schedule, so the Tuesday becomes the big pickup day.

## Delivery and pickup on a holiday weekend

On a regular weekend, many rentals go out Friday and come back Monday. On a long weekend, the pattern changes.

- **Delivery** is often Thursday or Friday. Some companies add a Wednesday option for tents.
- **Pickup** is often Tuesday, not Monday, because the Monday is a holiday.
- **Windows are wider.** You may get a 4-hour delivery window rather than a one-hour slot. Fit your setup around it.
- **Fees can change.** Some companies charge more for holiday delivery or for any pickup on the holiday itself. Ask for the fee in writing. Our guide to [event rental delivery fees](/blog/event-rental-delivery-fees-explained) shows what drives them.

Since the items stay with you for about 4 days, you need a place to keep them dry and safe. Stack chairs in a garage and keep linens off the grass. Rain can come on any of those days, and wet linen is a charge on a damage waiver. See [damage waiver explained](/blog/damage-waiver-event-rentals-explained).

## When to book

These are rules of thumb. Peak weekends can sell out earlier in a busy year, and smaller orders are easier to place than big ones.

| What you need | Start looking |
|---|---|
| Tables and chairs for under 50 guests | 4 to 6 weeks ahead |
| Tables, chairs and linens for 50 to 150 | 6 to 8 weeks ahead |
| A tent, a heater or fans | 8 to 10 weeks ahead |
| Anything on the exact holiday Saturday | As early as you can |

If you have a flexible date, ask what is open on the Sunday or the Friday. A Sunday party on a holiday weekend often has more availability than the Saturday. A tent for July 4th is the hardest item, so start there.

## What most hosts find out too late

- **The rental company may close on the holiday.** Many shut Memorial Day, July 4th and Labor Day, or run a skeleton crew. If you need a pickup on the holiday itself, that can cost extra or not be possible.
- **Alley and street access is harder on a holiday.** Chicago block parties, parades and street closures can block the truck. Tell the rental company about alley garages, narrow gangways and any street closed on your block. See [alley deliveries](/blog/chicago-alley-deliveries-event-rentals).
- **Count everything at delivery.** Count chairs and tables when the crew drops them off, not on Tuesday. A short count is easy to fix on Thursday and hard to fix after the party.
- **Heaters need fuel.** Propane may not be included. Ask before you assume. This matters most for a cool Labor Day evening.
- **Measure doors and stairs.** If your tables have to go up a flight or through a narrow gate, a 6-foot table will not turn a tight corner.

## Weather across the three weekends

- **Memorial Day** can still be cool in Chicago. A heater or a jacket plan is smart for the evening.
- **July 4th** is hot, with storms in the forecast some years. A tent helps for both shade and a pop-up shower. See [heat plan for Chicago summers](/blog/summer-event-heat-plan-chicago).
- **Labor Day** can be warm in the day and cool at night, and sunset comes earlier than in July.

## Frequently asked questions

### How far ahead should I book rentals for Memorial Day weekend?

Book 4 to 6 weeks ahead for tables and chairs, and 8 to 10 weeks for a tent. Smaller orders can sometimes be placed closer in, but the exact Saturday sells first. If you wait until the week before, you may be choosing from what is left.

### Do rental companies deliver on holidays?

Some do and some do not. Most deliver the Thursday or Friday before a holiday weekend and pick up on the Tuesday after. If you need anything on the holiday itself, ask early, since it may cost more or not be offered.

### Why is pickup on Tuesday after a long weekend?

The Monday is a holiday, and many rental companies are closed or short-staffed. Pickup moves to Tuesday so the crew can work through all the orders that went out. Plan to store the items safely for about 4 days.

### Will I pay more for a holiday weekend?

Maybe. Some rental companies charge more for holiday delivery, for a longer rental period or for after-hours service. Prices depend on the company and the items, so ask for the full cost in a quote, including delivery and pickup.

### What is the hardest rental to get for July 4th?

Tents are usually the first to go, followed by tables and chairs in large counts. Heaters and fans also move quickly depending on the weather. Start with the tent and build the rest of the order around it.

### Can I move my party to the Sunday of a holiday weekend?

Yes, and it can help. Sunday often has more open stock than Saturday, and guests may be glad of a day with no rush. Ask the rental company which days are open before you send out invitations.
