**Quick answer**

Golf clubs near Chicago usually include a ballroom or patio, in-house catering and tables and chairs, but the details vary by club. Ask what the package includes, whether outside rentals are allowed, whether tents may go on the grounds, how cart paths and parking affect deliveries, and what the rain plan is. Rent what the club does not offer, such as ceremony chairs, specialty seating, linens, lounge pieces or a dance floor.

</div>

A golf club looks like a finished venue: a clubhouse, a view, a kitchen. For many couples and corporate planners in the Chicago suburbs, that is exactly the appeal. But club packages differ a lot, and the line between what the club provides and what you rent is not always obvious. This guide covers the questions that matter and where rentals fit.

## Private, semi-private and public clubs

Some clubs are members-only and host outside events only for members or through a member sponsor. Others are public or semi-private and host weddings, outings and fundraisers openly. Policies, minimums and seasons differ, so ask early. We cannot name or characterize specific clubs here; use this as a conversation guide.

## What the club usually provides, and what to verify

<div class="table-wrap">

| Item | Often included | Ask |
|---|---|---|
| Ballroom or tent space | Yes | Capacity seated vs. standing |
| Tables, chairs, linens | Often a standard set | Upgrades allowed? Outside rentals? |
| Catering and bar | Usually exclusive | Outside caterers or bars allowed? |
| Ceremony site | Often a terrace or lawn | Chairs included? Rain location? |
| Staff | Setup and service | Teardown time |
| Parking | Large lot | Delivery access |
| Grounds rules | Cart paths, greens | Where tents and vehicles may go |

</div>

Never assume. If the package says "chairs included," ask what they are and how many. If you want a different chair for the ceremony, you may need to rent it, and some clubs have rules about outside vendors and insurance certificates.

## Grounds, carts and deliveries

Golf clubs protect turf. Heavy trucks may not be allowed on the lawn or cart paths, and tent stakes may be restricted. Ask where vehicles can park, what time deliveries are allowed and whether the club's maintenance schedule affects your date. Some clubs operate on early morning schedules and close the grounds to events at certain hours. Deliveries may need to use a service entrance and walk items across.

## Corporate golf outings

For an outing with lunch or dinner after the round, plan a space for registration, awards and a dinner. Typical needs include a check-in table, scoreboard easel, tables for 100 and a stage or podium. Read about [stages and risers](/blog/stage-and-riser-sizes-for-events) and [signage and easels](/blog/signage-and-easels-for-events). Outings run on tight schedules, so confirm times for check-in, lunch, dinner and teardown.

## Seasons and weather

Golf season in the Chicago area typically runs roughly spring through fall, and course conditions depend on weather. A club may offer lower availability on weekends in summer. Read our monthly guides for [June](/blog/outdoor-events-chicago-in-june) and [September](/blog/outdoor-events-chicago-in-september) to see what weather to expect. A late sunset in June lets ceremonies run later; a September sunset around 7 pm means lighting by dinner.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do golf clubs allow outside rental companies?

Some do and some require the club's own or preferred vendors. Ask the club for its vendor policy and any insurance requirements.

### Can I put a tent on a golf course?

Only if the club allows it. Clubs protect greens and may restrict stakes, so ask early.

### What should I rent for a golf club wedding?

Ceremony chairs, specialty seating, linens, lounge furniture and a dance floor are common. Ask the club first what is included.

### What if it rains at a golf club event?

Ask about the ballroom or covered patio backup, and whether a tent is allowed.

### Can I have a ceremony on a golf course?

Many clubs have a ceremony lawn or terrace. Ask about availability and rain location.

### Is a golf club cheaper than other venues?

Not necessarily. Packages vary, and food and beverage minimums often drive cost. Ask for a full quote.

### Are golf cart paths a problem for delivery?

They can be. Ask the club where trucks can drive and park.
