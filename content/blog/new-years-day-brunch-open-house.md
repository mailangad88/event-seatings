**Quick answer**

For a New Year's Day open house, plan seats for about half your guests, because people come and go over 3 to 4 hours. Use chafing dishes and warming trays for a buffet that holds, set up a self-serve coffee station, and ask for rental pickup on Jan 2 or later. Order food in smaller batches you can refill, rather than one huge tray at the start.

An open house is the easiest way to host a crowd on Jan 1. People wake up late, stop by for an hour, eat, and head home to the couch. It works well in a Chicago apartment or a house in January, when everyone wants somewhere warm to go. The details are different from a dinner party, though. Food has to hold, seats have to turn over, and rentals have to leave after a holiday.

## Plan the hours and the headcount

Pick a window of 3 to 4 hours, such as 11 am to 2 pm. List the people you invite, and expect most, not all, to come. Some will bring a friend you did not count. Plan food for about the number you invited.

The trick is that guests arrive in waves. At a peak, maybe two thirds of your list is in the room at once. You do not need a chair for each of them.

## Seat about half

A drop-in party works with fewer seats than guests. People stand with a plate, sit for 20 minutes, then move on. A rule of thumb for a casual open house is seating for 40 to 50 percent of the guest count, and a mix of heights.

Round 30-inch cocktail tables give people a place to set a plate while standing. Small 36-inch or 48-inch rounds seat 4 to 6. See [round table sizes](/blog/round-table-sizes-and-seating-48-60-66-72-inch).

## A buffet that holds for hours

A brunch buffet sits out for a long time. Choose food that stays good, and keep hot food hot.

<div class="table-wrap">

| Food | Holds well? | Tip |
|---|---|---|
| Egg casserole, strata | Yes | Cut in squares, refill from the oven |
| Bacon, sausage | Yes | Warm in a chafing dish |
| Potatoes | Yes | Cover the tray |
| Scrambled eggs | Fair | Make in two batches |
| Pastries, bagels | Yes | Serve at room temperature |
| Fruit, yogurt bar | Cold only | Keep on ice or in a cold bowl |

</div>

Use chafing dishes with fuel for hot items, and swap in fresh trays every hour or so. See [how many chafing dishes](/blog/how-many-chafing-dishes-for-a-buffet) and [buffet line flow](/blog/buffet-line-flow-and-serving-speed). For food safety, hot food should stay hot and cold food cold. Follow the basics in [food safety at home parties](/blog/food-safety-at-home-parties). Do not leave perishable food out for more than 2 hours, the widely used guideline.

## Coffee that does not run out

Guests drink more coffee than you expect, especially on Jan 1. A rule of thumb is 1 to 2 cups per guest over a brunch. A 100-cup urn covers a crowd of 50 or more, and a smaller one fits a party of 20. Offer hot water for tea and cocoa, and keep milk and sugar in small bowls.

Read [how many coffee urns for a party](/blog/how-many-coffee-urns-for-a-party) and [coffee and tea service rental](/blog/coffee-and-tea-service-rental). Place the coffee away from the buffet, so one line does not block the other.

## What most hosts find out too late

- **Holiday pickup is slow.** Many rental companies close or run short hours on Jan 1, and the day after is busy. Ask for pickup on Jan 2 or later, and check the exact window.
- **Delivery needs a plan for Dec 30 or 31.** Order early for New Year's week, because it is busy. Deliver on Dec 30 and keep the items boxed until the morning of the party, or ask about earliest options.
- **Guests bring their coats and their kids.** A coat rack at the door saves a bed pile. See [coat racks for winter events](/blog/coat-racks-for-winter-events-chicago).

## Frequently asked questions

### How long should a New Year's Day open house last?

Three to four hours is typical, such as 11 am to 2 pm. Guests come and go, so the room stays comfortable. A longer window means more food refills.

### How many seats do I need for an open house?

About half your guest count is a rule of thumb. Mix small tables with a few standing spots so guests can eat and move. Add a few extra chairs for older guests.

### What food works for a drop-in brunch?

Casseroles, bacon, potatoes, pastries and fruit hold well. Eggs are best made in batches. Use chafing dishes to keep hot items warm.

### Are rental companies open on New Year's Day?

Many are closed or have short hours. Ask for delivery before and pickup after, such as Jan 2 or later. We check dates with the rental companies when you request a quote.

### How much coffee do I need?

Plan 1 to 2 cups per guest. A 100-cup urn covers about 50 guests. Add a hot water option for tea.

### Can I serve a mimosa bar?

Yes, with rented flutes or cups, ice and a few juices. Keep it on its own small table, and have water close by.
