**Quick answer**

For a Noche Buena of 40 to 60, plan one 8-foot table for the main dishes (lechon and ham on their own table), a second for sides and dessert, a drink station, and a karaoke corner with power. Plan seats for most guests at once, with padded chairs near the food for lolas and lolos, and a kids table close to the action. Order delivery for Dec 22 or 23, since rental companies close early on Dec 24.

</div>

Noche Buena is the Filipino Christmas Eve feast, eaten after midnight Mass or after the last Simbang Gabi, the nine dawn or evening Masses held Dec 16 to 24. Families across Chicago, from Albany Park to the suburbs, host for the whole clan after church. It is a late night, with grandparents, cousins, babies and neighbors all in one place. Christmas Eve 2026 falls on Thursday, Dec 24. Customs vary by family and by region of the Philippines. This guide helps you set the space.

## Plan the night around the feast

Many families go to Mass in the evening or at midnight, then eat when they get home. That means the table is empty until late, then full, then busy for hours. Food should be ready to heat, not cook, when guests arrive.

## Buffet tables

A Noche Buena buffet is long, with many dishes: pancit, lumpia, spaghetti, rice, ham, queso de bola, fruit salad, leche flan, bibingka and more. Each family has its own list.

<div class="table-wrap">

| Guests | Buffet tables | Seats | Drink station |
|---|---|---|---|
| 30 | 2 (8 ft) | 22 | 1 (6 ft) |
| 50 | 3 (8 ft) | 35 to 40 | 1 to 2 |
| 80 | 4 (8 ft) plus 1 dessert | 56 to 64 | 2 |

</div>

Seating at about 70 percent of the headcount is a rule of thumb for a party where people eat in shifts, sing and visit. If most guests sit to eat together, plan a seat for every guest. Two lines, one on each side of the buffet, move twice as fast as one. ## Space for lechon and ham

A whole roast pig, or lechon, is a centerpiece and takes space. Give it its own 6-foot or 8-foot table, with a cutting board or a tray, a place for the cleaver or knives and room around it so the carver can work. Put it at the start or the end of the line, not in the middle. Many families order lechon from a caterer who delivers it hot. Others buy lechon belly or a smaller roast. A glazed ham needs a carving spot too.

- Cover the table with a plastic-backed cloth or a rented linen with a runner. Grease spots happen.
- Keep paper towels, gloves and a small trash bin at hand.
- Plan for leftovers. Foil pans and a cooler at the door make sharing easy.

## Karaoke corner

Videoke is a big part of a Filipino party. It takes space, power and some care.

- **Screen and speakers** on a stand, with a clear 8 to 10 foot space in front.
- **Power**: use a separate circuit from the warming trays. See [event power needs](/blog/event-power-needs-checklist).
- **Mics**: two is enough. Keep one for kids.
- **Neighbors**: late-night singing in an apartment, condo or two-flat can bother people. Check building and village noise rules.

See [AV and sound rentals](/rentals/av-sound) and [sound system basics](/blog/sound-system-for-speeches).

## Seating many generations

Noche Buena puts a toddler and a great-grandmother in one room. Seat people by what they need.

- **Lolas and lolos** get chairs with arms and a firm seat, close to the food and away from the speakers. See [comfortable chairs for older guests](/blog/comfortable-chairs-for-older-guests).
- **Kids** get a low-key table with a view of the room, plus paper plates and a few games. See [kids tables and chairs](/blog/kids-tables-and-chairs-for-events).
- **Teens and young adults** will move between the karaoke and the table. A few high-top tables near the singing corner work.
- **Round tables of 8 to 10** keep conversation easy. A 60-inch round seats 8 and a 72-inch seats 10. See [round table sizes](/blog/round-table-sizes-and-seating-48-60-66-72-inch).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Noche Buena?

Noche Buena is the Christmas Eve feast in the Philippines. Families eat after midnight Mass or after the last Simbang Gabi. Dishes vary but often include ham, queso de bola, pancit and sweets.

### How many tables do I need for a Noche Buena buffet?

For 50 guests, plan three 8-foot buffet tables, one for the main meats, one for sides and one for desserts. Add a drink station. Use two lines if you can.

### How much space does a whole lechon need?

Give it its own 6-foot or 8-foot table with room to carve. Put it at the start or the end of the line. Ask your caterer what size you are getting.

### Where should the karaoke go?

At the far end of the room from the buffet, with a clear space in front, and its own power. Check noise rules if you live in a condo, apartment or two-flat.

### Can I get rentals delivered on Dec 24?

Possibly, but many rental companies close early or limit hours on Christmas Eve. Ask for Dec 22 or 23, and ask about pickup after the holiday.

### How do I seat grandparents and kids together?

Put grandparents in chairs with arms near the food, away from loud speakers, and put kids at a nearby table where adults can see them. Round tables help conversation.
