A head table needs a different chair from the guest tables, because everyone is looking at it for hours. It is where the couple, the wedding party and sometimes the parents sit, and it is the focal point of every photo. A Louis chair solves that in one move: it is taller, wider and more upholstered than the rest of the room, so the table reads as the center. This guide shows how to plan the table, count the seats and decide how much is enough.

## The chair

Our [Louis medallion chair](/chairs/louis-medallion) follows the French Louis XVI tradition: an oval upholstered back with carved trim and a padded seat. The finishes are white and gold, and grey and silver. The cushion is included. It does not stack and is meant for indoor use.

Why it works at a head table:

- **Presence.** It is visually heavier than a Chiavari or ghost chair, so the table stands out.
- **Comfort.** The upholstery is a real benefit during toasts and long dinners.
- **Formality.** It suits ballrooms, hotels and historic halls.

## How to count

A head table is simple math, but people still get it wrong. Count:

1. The couple (2).
2. The wedding party (maid of honor, best man, bridesmaids, groomsmen).
3. Any partners of the wedding party, if they sit at the table.
4. Parents, if you seat them with you (they often sit at a nearby family table instead).

A one-sided head table needs about 30 inches per person, so a table for 10 is about 20 feet long, which means multiple banquet tables placed end to end. Ask whether your venue can supply or if you need to rent them. Our [sweetheart table chair ideas](/blog/sweetheart-table-chair-ideas) covers the smaller option.

## Styling the front of the table

Because a head table is seen from the front, the table skirt and the chair do all the work.

- **Floor-length linen** with a pleated or ruched skirt hides legs and cables. See the [tablecloth size chart](/blog/tablecloth-size-chart).
- **A long floral garland** along the front, low enough that guests can see the couple.
- **Candles** in a row, with a fire-safe holder.

On the chair, less is more: the carved trim is the decoration. Avoid wide sashes that cover the oval. A small sign on the couple's chairs is fine; see [Mr and Mrs chair signs](/blog/mr-and-mrs-chair-signs).

## Mixing with the room

A common plan is Louis at the head table, a [gold infinity chair](/chairs/infinity) or [Chiavari-style chair](/blog/chiavari-chair-rental-chicago) at guest tables. The head table then looks upgraded, but the room still feels like one set. If you want another approach, see [mixing chair styles at one reception](/blog/mixing-chair-styles-at-reception).

## Room fit for Chicago ballrooms

The Louis chair is wider than a typical banquet chair. In a room with fixed head table risers or a small stage, measure the width of the stage before you order. A standard stage may not leave enough space behind seated guests. Ask your venue for the stage width, the riser height and whether the head table must sit on the floor.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many Louis chairs do I need for a head table?

One per person who sits at the table. Count the couple, the wedding party and any partners, then add one spare in case of a last-minute change.

### How much does it cost to rent Louis chairs in Chicago?

Our estimate is $20 to $30 per chair, with the cushion included. Delivery and setup are quoted separately.

### Are Louis chairs comfortable?

Yes. The padded seat and upholstered back are more comfortable than most banquet chairs, which helps over a long dinner.

### Can Louis chairs go outside?

No. They are indoor chairs in our catalog. Use them in a ballroom, hall or fully covered, floored tent.

### Do Louis chairs stack?

No. Plan for each chair to be handled individually at setup and breakdown.

### Should every guest have a Louis chair?

Not usually. The cost is higher than other styles, and the effect works best when the head table is clearly different from the rest.

### What color Louis chair should I pick?

White and gold for warm, traditional weddings; grey and silver for cool, modern or winter ones.
