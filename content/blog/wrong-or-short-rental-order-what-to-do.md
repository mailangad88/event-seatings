You open the truck and the count is off. Maybe 8 chairs are missing from 100. Maybe the napkins are ivory and you ordered white. Maybe three chairs have cracked legs. In Chicago, it often happens at the worst time: a Saturday morning, a loading dock with a 30 minute limit, or a bungalow with guests arriving at 5. Here is how to handle it calmly and get a fix.

This post picks up where [what to expect on delivery day](/blog/event-rental-delivery-day-what-to-expect) leaves off. That guide covers the normal count. This one covers what to do when the count is wrong.

<ol class="flow">
<li><strong>Ask the crew to wait.</strong> Keep the lead at the site while you finish the count. They can radio the office.</li>
<li><strong>Count by item, not by stack.</strong> Chairs are often racked in tens, but check that the racks are full.</li>
<li><strong>Compare to your contract,</strong> not your memory. Style, color, size and quantity are all on it.</li>
<li><strong>Write it on the delivery sheet</strong> before you sign. "Received 92 of 100 chairs" is clear. "See notes" is not.</li>
<li><strong>Take photos</strong> of the problem, the delivery sheet and the truck's label if one is visible.</li>
<li><strong>Call the office</strong> from the driveway and ask what they can do today.</li>
</ol>

## What to check, by item

<div class="table-wrap">

| Item | Check for | Common problem |
|---|---|---|
| Chairs | Count, style, wobbly legs, cracked backs | Short count, mixed styles |
| Tables | Size, shape, folding legs that lock | Rounds sent instead of rectangles |
| Linens | Color, size, stains, burn marks | Ivory for white, wrong drop length |
| Dinnerware | Count, chips, spare pieces | Chipped plates in the racks |
| Glassware | Cracks, cloudy glass, full racks | Broken glasses at the bottom |
| Heaters, lights | Fuel or cords, switches, bulbs | No propane, missing extension cords |

</div>

A color that looks "off" in a truck can look different in the room. Hold a napkin or cloth up next to your contract line and the swatch, if you have one. [The tablecloth size chart](/blog/tablecloth-size-chart) helps you spot a wrong drop length fast.

## Who to call

Call the number on your contract or delivery sheet first. That is usually the dispatcher or the office, and they can send a second truck. If you booked through us, call or text us too. We check the date and the rental list with the rental companies and can help push for a fast fix, but the rental company has the trucks and the stock.

Say three things: what is wrong, how many, and when you need it fixed. For example: "We are short 8 chairs and the party starts at 5. Can you bring them by 3?" A clear request gets a clear answer.

## What the company can offer

Be ready for any of these. Policies vary, so ask which applies.

- **Bring the missing items out today.** This is best for short counts if a truck is free.
- **Swap the item.** Wrong color or damaged pieces get replaced from stock.
- **A substitute.** A different style that serves the same job. Say yes only if you can live with how it looks.
- **A partial credit.** If they can't deliver, they credit those items. Get the amount and the date in writing.
- **A late fee waiver or a free pickup change** if they ran behind.

A credit does not fix a missing chair at the party. That is why the backup plan matters.

## What most hosts find out too late

- **Photos beat memory.** After the event, a rental company checks returns against its own count. Photos taken at delivery, with time stamps, show what you got and what shape it was in. See the [return condition rules](/blog/returning-event-rentals-condition-and-cleaning).
- **Signing means "received in good order."** If you sign a clean sheet and find 6 missing chairs at 4 pm, you can still call, but it is harder. Note problems before the signature, or write "count not finished" if the crew insists on leaving.
- **Order spares.** A few extra chairs and napkins cost little compared to a hole in the seating. Ask about [the damage waiver](/blog/damage-waiver-event-rentals-explained) too, so a broken chair at the party does not become a surprise bill.

## Backup plans for the things you can't skip

Think about the items that would stop the party if they did not show.

- **Seats:** Know where you can borrow folding chairs from a church hall, a neighbor or a family member. Fill the gap with benches or a kids' table.
- **Linens:** If the color is wrong, use the wrong color on the guest tables and put the right color on the head table or cake table. Or go with it.
- **Dinnerware:** Have paper or disposable plates for dessert and kids.
- **Fuel and cords:** Keep a spare propane tank source, and a long outdoor-rated extension cord.

## After the event

Photograph how everything is stacked at pickup. Email the company a short note within a day: the date, order number, what was wrong, what they did and what credit you expect. Most companies work from a written record. If you were charged for something you never received, [the contract guide](/blog/how-to-read-an-event-rental-contract) shows where to find the terms for disputes and credits.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should I do if my rental delivery is missing items?

Count before the crew leaves, write the shortage on the delivery sheet and call the company right away. Ask for the items to be brought out that day. If they can't, ask for a credit in writing and use your backup plan.

### Can I refuse a rental delivery?

Yes, you can refuse items that are the wrong style, badly damaged or too dirty to use. Say so before the crew unloads or signs, and tell the office. Ask them to replace the order or credit it. Refusing the whole delivery close to the party leaves you short, so refuse only what you can't use.

### Will I get a refund if the order is short?

Usually you get a credit for the items that were not delivered, and sometimes a refund, depending on the contract and how the company handles it. Ask for the amount and the method in writing. A credit for missing items is normal if you note it on the delivery sheet.

### What if the wrong color of linens arrives?

Call right away and ask if the right color is in stock and can come the same day. If it can't, ask what the closest color is and whether they will credit the difference. You can also use the wrong color in a smaller area, like the buffet or the cake table, and keep the main tables on plan.

### What if I notice a problem after the crew leaves?

Call the office as soon as you see it and send photos by text or email. You may still get a swap or a credit, but it is harder to prove the problem did not happen after delivery. This is why counting at the door matters.

### Who is responsible for damaged chairs that arrive broken?

The rental company is, because the damage was there before you used them. Photograph the damage at delivery and write it on the sheet so it is not billed to you at pickup.
