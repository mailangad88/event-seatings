**Quick answer**

A Sweet 16 needs a dance floor, a place for the teens to hang out, and a place for the adults to sit. For 100 guests, rent about 60 to 70 chairs, 6 to 8 round tables, a lounge area for 10 to 12, a dance floor of about 12 x 12 feet, and a photo backdrop. Teens stand and dance most of the evening, so you need fewer chairs than a wedding, but more open floor.

</div>

A Sweet 16 sits between a birthday and a wedding reception. The guest of honor wants it to feel like her party, her friends want to dance and take photos, and the family wants a place to sit with a plate. The rental list splits into those three groups.

## What to rent, zone by zone

### Dinner and family seating
Sweet 16 dinners are often a plated or buffet meal for family. Use 60-inch rounds with 8 seats and floor-length linens. A good mid-priced option is the [cross-back chair](/chairs/cross-back) (estimated $8 to $11) or a [ghost chair](/chairs/ghost) ($10 to $14), which fades into any color scheme. For the guest of honor, a [velvet dining chair](/chairs/velvet-dining) ($18 to $25) or a [Louis medallion chair](/chairs/louis-medallion) ($20 to $30) at the head table makes her seat stand out.

### Dance floor
Dance floor size depends on how many dance at once. For teens, plan for most guests to hit the floor at the same time. Use roughly 3 to 4 square feet per dancer. See [dance floor size by guest count](/blog/dance-floor-size-by-guest-count) for the numbers by headcount.

### Lounge
A lounge gives teens a place to sit that isn't a dinner chair. Two sofas facing each other with a low table is a good unit, and you can add a pair of accent chairs. See [lounge layout by guest count](/blog/lounge-layout-by-guest-count) to size it.

### Photo area
A backdrop, props and good lighting are what teens will use most. Leave 8 to 10 feet in front of the backdrop so the photographer can step back. For equipment options, read about [photo booth types](/blog/photo-booth-types-open-air-360-mirror).

## Chicago-area planning notes

Most Sweet 16 parties in the Chicago area happen in banquet halls, restaurants and private rooms, and some at home or in a tent. Each venue has its own rules about outside rentals, load-in times and sound limits. Ask about all three before you pay a deposit. If the party is at home, noise and parking will come up. Tell neighbors, and check your village's noise rules. Our guide to [tent permit rules in Chicago](/blog/chicago-tent-permit) covers the city, and you should check with your own village or park district if you're in a suburb.

## Frequently asked questions

### What do I need to rent for a Sweet 16?

You need seating for dinner, tables, linens, a dance floor if the venue doesn't have one, and lounge furniture. Add a photo backdrop and a cake table. A venue may already include some of these, so ask first.

### How many chairs do I need for a Sweet 16?

For 100 guests, plan about 60 to 70 dinner chairs, because teens dance and move around. If you serve a seated plated dinner, plan one chair for each guest.

### How big should the dance floor be?

For 100 guests, a 12 x 12-foot floor is a common starting point. For 150, use 15 x 15 feet. Teens fill it faster than adults do, so lean larger.

### Can I do a Sweet 16 at home?

Yes, with a backyard or a finished basement. Count on a tent for weather, a rented dance floor on the lawn, and a plan for parking and noise. Check your village's rules for amplified music.

### What chairs look best for a Sweet 16 head table?

A velvet or Louis medallion chair feels special without looking like a throne. Use the same chair for the guest of honor and her closest friends or family, or a unique one just for her.

### How early should I book rentals?

Book 6 to 8 weeks ahead for spring and fall weekends, which are busy. Earlier is better if your date is near prom or graduation season.
